The CardChoiceGrid catalog demo wraps its grid in `containerType: "size"`
without establishing a container height. Its `cqh`-based site card width
therefore resolves to zero, collapsing all four demo cards to 0×0 geometry.

The Transfiguration Button catalog demo fixes its specimen stage at 560px
with two equal columns. At a 390px viewport, the priced controls overlap their
labels and Essence values instead of adapting to the available width.
`npm audit` reports a high-severity quadratic-CPU advisory in the transitive
`js-yaml` dependency (GHSA-5p4m-2wfm-xmqj) and a moderate out-of-bounds-read
advisory in the transitive `re2` dependency (GHSA-j4r3-hg7j-8chg). Fixed
dependency releases are available, but updating their parent dependency chains
is outside the LToDD image-publishing change.

`npm run opponent-coherence-metric` produces zero for every corpus, generated,
greedy, and random coherence score, causing `beatsRandomFloor` and
`beatsOldRandom` to fail. The same failure occurs on the unchanged master
worktree, so it predates the `opponents.toml` configuration work.
