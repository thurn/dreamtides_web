Tango convergence - AtlasHoverCard (desktop Dream Atlas hover reveal)
====================================================================

src/tango/components/atlas/AtlasHoverCard.tsx is a screen-local fork of the
InfoCard fullBleed treatment, built for exploratory design while the desktop
atlas hover card is still in motion (the "requested divergence" the tango design
system allows; see the tango skill, "Requested divergence must converge"). It
reuses InfoCard's glass material and press engine and is structured as a future
InfoCard variant body.

Convergence decision (deferred, not skipped): once the box measures and the
information-hierarchy proposal settle via the Dream Atlas dev tweaks panel, the
card promotes into a strict `InfoCard variant="atlasReveal"`, and the tweaks
scaffolding is removed per the tango tweaks-panel cleanup contract:

  - delete src/tango/screens/devtools/AtlasHoverTweaksPanel.tsx;
  - delete the `hoverTweaks` plumbing threaded through AtlasScreen -> AtlasMap ->
    AtlasNodeReveal, and the AtlasHoverTweaks / ATLAS_HOVER_DEFAULTS config;
  - bake the settled values in as the variant's plain, commented box-measure
    constants (mirroring FULL_BLEED_FIGURE_HEIGHT et al. in InfoCard.tsx), with
    the chosen hierarchy either fixed or kept as one enumerated `hierarchy` prop.
