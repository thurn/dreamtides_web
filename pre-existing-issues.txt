Battle card play does not mark a character exhausted

In the playable battle at `?goto=battle&ai=0`, dragging a character from the
player hand into an empty back-rank slot spends its energy and moves it into
play, but the resulting battle instance retains `status.isExhausted = false`.
This conflicts with the documented battle behavior that a character enters
play exhausted. `planCardPlay` in `src/rules/battle/basic-automation.ts`
currently plans the energy adjustment and move without a status edit.
