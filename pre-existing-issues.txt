Tango convergence - AtlasHoverCard (desktop Dream Atlas hover reveal)
====================================================================

src/tango/components/atlas/AtlasHoverCard.tsx is a screen-local fork of the
InfoCard fullBleed treatment for the desktop atlas hover reveal. It reuses
InfoCard's glass material and press engine, with settled box-measure constants
for the selected layout: place-forward main card, standard site companion card,
and text-only affiliation companion card.

Convergence decision (deferred, not skipped): if this treatment becomes a shared
popover vocabulary beyond the Dream Atlas, promote it into a strict
`InfoCard variant="atlasReveal"` with the current box-measure constants carried
as commented component constants.
