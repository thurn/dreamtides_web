The CardChoiceGrid catalog demo wraps its grid in `containerType: "size"`
without establishing a container height. Its `cqh`-based site card width
therefore resolves to zero, collapsing all four demo cards to 0×0 geometry.

The Transfiguration Button catalog demo fixes its specimen stage at 560px
with two equal columns. At a 390px viewport, the priced controls overlap their
labels and Essence values instead of adapting to the available width.
