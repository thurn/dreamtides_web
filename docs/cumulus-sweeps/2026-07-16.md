## Findings

- **Two Atlas boss-badge literals bypassed live semantic bridge tokens.**
  Evidence: the boss badge used the exact values owned by `--dt-enemy` and
  `--dt-bg-0` at `src/cumulus/components/atlas/atlas.css:239-245`, while both
  tokens remained named in the orphan baseline. The badge now reads those
  tokens, and both baseline entries are absent from
  `scripts/cumulus-orphan-tokens.test.mjs:73-86`. Disposition: fixed in
  `793a1b3a`.

- **Atlas and Draft carried screen-owned HUD builders beside the router-owned
  journey chrome.** Evidence: `buildAtlasHudView` and `buildDraftHudView` had zero
  non-test callers; both complete view builders accepted run state that did not
  contribute to their returned screen models. `src/components/CumulusJourneyChrome.tsx:44`
  owns the live shared HUD. The current builders accept only inputs used by the
  rendered screens, and their adapters have narrower memo dependencies.
  Disposition: fixed in `5f12851c`.

- **BattleStartScreen exposed an unreachable portrait framing.** Evidence: the
  local `OpponentPortrait` wrapper accepted `"cutout" | "standing"`, while its
  only two callers selected `cutout`. The current wrapper owns the production
  cutout framing directly at `src/cumulus/screens/BattleStartScreen.tsx:179-193`.
  Disposition: fixed in `5f12851c`.

- **Battle results and Draft repeated existing layout infrastructure.**
  Evidence: `BattleResultSurface` declared a local 440px content cap matching
  `JOURNEY_RESULT_CONTENT_MAX_WIDTH_PX` in
  `src/cumulus/screens/journey-result-layout.ts:4-5`; `DraftScreen` carried a
  match-media hook matching the custom-threshold contract in
  `src/cumulus/screens/use-is-desktop.ts:18-37`. Both screens now consume the
  shared sources. Disposition: fixed in `793a1b3a`.

- **Component doctrine overstated ownership or carried stale consumer lists.**
  Evidence: `ResourceChip` claimed every economy number; `DreamAvatarPortrait`
  claimed every Dream Avatar art render; `CardTermDefinitions` and
  `GlossaryDefinitionCard` claimed all glossary reveals; `PipBadge` claimed card
  corners owned by `CardStatOrb`; `RulesText` enumerated three of nineteen
  consumer files; `InfoCard` described its icon disc as identical to SiteNode;
  and `glass-surface.ts` omitted direct consumers including AtlasMap,
  DreamsignGalleryPanel, GalleryActionCard, and SpeechBubble. Current comments
  and generated references state each component's actual role and avoid
  enumerated consumer inventories. Disposition: fixed in `3734c32d`.

- **Tide and resource-mark docs conflated distinct semantic roles.** Evidence:
  the TideDisc callout said every tide chip used `tide-spec`, while
  `TideSelectionButton` colors selection provenance at
  `src/cumulus/components/hud/TideSelectionButton.tsx:9-16`; GlowIcon and
  PipBadge each claimed sole ownership despite glyph vocabulary living in
  `src/cumulus/primitives/glyph.ts` and compact pip fills living in
  `src/cumulus/components/controls/pip-colors.ts`. Current references distinguish
  tide identity from selection role and card-stat glyphs from compact pips.
  Disposition: fixed in `3734c32d`.

- **`card-aspect.ts` was an undocumented 14-consumer workhorse.** Evidence: its
  full-card, battlefield, art-region, radius, and draft-offer geometry contract
  is defined at `src/cumulus/components/card/card-aspect.ts:1-57` and imported by
  fourteen non-test modules. The GameCard reference now names that module as the

entry, record its intended second consumer or its fold/delete destination;
CardTermDefinitions receives one sweep of incubation and becomes a deletion

renderers. Acceptance: every stable catalog entry has either two real consumers

production-owned surface, and generated references expose the policy.

through `GuideGallerySiteLayout`, compose Dreamsign Bazaar replacement through

normal player flow. Acceptance: one revelation composition, one replacement
dialog shell, one travel geometry implementation, and no screen-local mirrored
geometry constants.
