# RD-hv-umm2.154-1: "When an enemy scores ⍟, return this card from your void to your hand" works from the void, once per scoring enemy, and returns the card once
- Ladder: 1 (rules § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring: "When the opponent scores ⍟" responds once per scoring character; rules § Ability Types → Where triggered abilities work: an ability naming another zone works in that zone), then 5 (simplest reading consistent with the card text)
- rules.md: § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring
- Affects: 8a8af982-d1a1-42a0-9229-5e3af13e8dbe (base and amplified)
- Why: "An enemy" is a character the opponent controls, so "When an enemy scores ⍟" is the per-character scoring event that "When the opponent scores ⍟" already responds to once for each scoring character; the engine encodes it as `whenScores(enemyCharacter())`. The ability names your void, so it works only while Eruption is there (`zone: "void"`), never from play, hand, or the stack. "This card" names the card while it is in your void, as for "When you discard this card": with several scoring enemies the card returns once, and any trigger resolving after it left the void does nothing (`returnFromVoid(thisCardIn("void"))` filters to cards still in the void). The return is a zone move, not a draw. Covered by `src/content/specs/cards-42.scenarios.ts`.
