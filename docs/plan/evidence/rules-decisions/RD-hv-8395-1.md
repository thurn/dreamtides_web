# RD-hv-8395-1: Unshuffled deck placements retain exactly inferable positions

- Ladder: 5, the simplest reading consistent with hidden deck order and public ordered movement; refines the positional boundary of RD-hv-umm2.63-1.
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Put on top or on the bottom of a deck.
- Affects: the `putInDeck` library contract; the authored consumer `ffa4fa56-8897-465d-b812-3798e3cf5a1f` moves public void cards and preserves their positions.
- Why: a placement shows both players its count, ordered slots and source zones, and shows each identity only to a player who could identify it as it moved. A known mover's new slot follows from those public facts even when other movers are unidentified. Remembering a nonmover's exact rank requires evidence for how many cards were removed before it; authoritative unidentified origins provide no such evidence.

Original known movers split each viewer's original deck into intervals. Their ordered placement slots determine how many unidentified cards moved from each interval. For a known nonmover in one interval, let U be that removal count, P the number of unidentified original slots before it and Q the number after it. The possible removals before it run from `max(0, U-Q)` through `min(P, U)`. Its new rank is inferable exactly when the bounds agree. Original knowledge and deck positions are captured before any forgetting or ordinary movement. Known movers and exact unfiltered top-prefix relocations remain tracked. Insertions from other source zones have visible counts and produce deterministic rank shifts. A shuffle ends all deck tracking.

This inference uses valid game states. Deck entries belong to their owner and controller (`testing/invariants.ts:52–60`); created cards are forbidden there. Status starts ordinary (`state/create.ts:12–13`), and Reclaim sets its status only after entering the stack (`steps/kinds/play.ts:86–87`). Stack resolution puts characters into play and sends events through ordinary relocation (`steps/kinds/resolve-top.ts:40–51`); prevention applies the same replacement (`rules/stack.ts:31–39`). Every attempted deck entry through `rules/zones.ts:157–168` makes a created card cease and sends a reclaimed card to Banished. Recovery copies knowledge into the committed state, preserving its zones and statuses (`view/knowledge.ts`, `keepDisclosures` and `keepPublishedKnowledge`). Loading JSON preserves those states; arbitrary corrupted JSON is outside this valid-state premise because `state/hash.ts:38–39` trusts its input.

Synthetic public-flow contracts cover top and bottom placements, proper-subset removals below a known prefix, removal of every unidentified slot, known movers among unidentified movers, interval constraints from known movers, and viewer-specific ambiguity with actual removals above and below a known nonmover. They assert ordered redacted events, each viewer's identities and exact deck indices, and unaffected deck/hand knowledge.
