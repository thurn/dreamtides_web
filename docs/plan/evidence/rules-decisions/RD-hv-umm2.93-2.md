# RD-hv-umm2.93-2: "Return this character to play with awakened" puts the character into play without the exhausted status, only when that effect puts it into play, and grants no Awakened keyword
- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with every affected card text), agreeing with 1 (rules.md § Keywords and Effects → Awakened: the keyword's only effect is that the character enters play without the exhausted status; § Exhaust and Awaken: awakening clears the exhausted status; § Zone Changes and § Keywords and Effects → Veil: changes to a card's keywords travel with it across zones)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Materialize ("with awakened")
- Affects: 3a59cd3d-08a9-4a75-a5ab-c91b19d2d8c1 (Graywatch, amplified); the same clause, for authoring by later beads: fd355778-abea-4513-844c-91bf75929ebe (Immortal Reaper, amplified), 7def28c4-d75a-47e1-ae86-5b7093cc0419 (Duskwall Delver, amplified), 1b341984-d32e-42a7-ae56-b22059c59737 (Wreckheap Lookout, amplified), c32ef131-d283-4bf1-bf65-2d5e5d232168 (Titan of Forgotten Echoes, amplified)
- Why: rules.md did not define "with awakened" on a returned or materialized character. Awakened matters only as a character enters play, so "with awakened" makes the character enter as an awakened one does, ready to challenge, block, and pay ☾ that turn. Granting the keyword itself would be a lasting keyword change that travels with the card, so a later return or play would also skip the exhausted status, which the text does not say. The awakening belongs to this return: when the character is not in the void as the effect resolves (an earlier ability put it into play or elsewhere) or the back rank is full, the effect does nothing, and does not awaken a character that another effect put into play exhausted.
- Mechanics: composed from existing primitives, `ifThen(sourceIn("void"), sequence(materialize(thisCardIn("void")), awaken(self())))`: the character enters play as any materialized character does and is awakened by the same effect before any triggered ability resolves or any player receives priority, so the board shows an `exhaustionChanged` after its `materialized`.
