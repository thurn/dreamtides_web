# RD-hv-umm2.110-3: Thief of Memory chooses a player, then a character in that player's void, as its ability resolves, and gains only while still in play
- Ladder: 5 (rules.md § Durations: an effect lasting while its source is in play does not happen if the source is not in play when it would begin; § Targeting for void targets)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities
- Affects: e72245f2-c153-4e79-ba8e-956d18202b48
- Why: "A player's void" is a choice of player, as for Augur Crow (ffa4fa56-8897-465d-b812-3798e3cf5a1f), made as the ability resolves (`choosePlayer`, whose branches choose their targets on resolution), followed by a void target in that player's void; a void with no character leaves nothing to banish and so nothing to gain. "That character" is the card the banish moved: `gainAbilities` resolves its banish and gains from each card that entered a Banished zone meanwhile, so a target no longer legal gives nothing. The banish does not depend on the source, but the gain lasts while the character is in play (RD-hv-umm2.110-2), so a character removed in response still banishes and gains nothing, as "while this is in play" effects do when their source has left.
