# RD-hv-umm2.473-1: Duration-bound chosen hand banishment returns to the owner's hand

- Ladder: 1 (the Hand ownership rule and the effect-start source-presence boundary in Durations and RD-hv-7x4l.6-4), then 4 (the Magic Comprehensive Rules analog, 610.3 and 400.3, adapted to Dreamtides' persistent physical identity and its explicit permission to hold another player's card).
- rules.md: § Zones → Hand; § Keywords and Effects → Banish; § Durations; § Zone Changes; § Created Cards.
- Affects: 1bae969b-0bc4-4777-86f3-1e860d601c5c and ea118471-164b-4d7f-bb02-044c862ed07e, and other chosen hand banishment with a duration.
- Why: Both affected texts choose a card in a hand, and the unrestricted form can choose an event. A return to the original zone is a hand return for either card type. Ownership stays fixed when another player holds a card; an ordinary return goes to its owner's hand. The from-play return remains the prior controller's materialization under F3. A created card ceases to exist instead. The return identifies the exact physical card and the recorded entry into the Banished zone, so leaving that zone and entering it again does not revive the earlier return. Private matching-card selection and public banishment follow RD-hv-umm2.74-1.
- Precedent: The prototype's actual Silent Winter rule (`src/rules/battle/dreamwell-effects-table.ts` and `battle-events.ts`) returns a character banished from play to its prior controller's back rank; it supplies no chosen-hand duration rule. The read-only Rust reference's Banish paragraph (`~/dreamtides/docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md`) names from-play and void variants, and `StandardEffect::BanishCharacterUntilLeavesPlay` has parser/ability declarations rather than a chosen-hand resolution rule. The [official Magic Comprehensive Rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260619.pdf), 610.3, return an object to its previous zone; 400.3 assigns an ordinary hand return to its owner. Dreamtides' explicit Hand rules supply the owner-versus-holder adaptation.
- Source boundary: The source's presence is checked when the effect would begin, as Durations and RD-hv-7x4l.6-4 require. A registered duration ends at the exact physical source's next departure. Returning that source and resolving a fresh trigger starts a separate registration. Dreamtides keeps physical identity across zone changes; the engine does not give queued triggers a Magic-style source-object epoch.
- Reconstruction: The typed start log records the effect id, physical source and chosen instance, expiry and Banished-zone entry. Effect-end and hand-return logs correlate by effect id and physical instance. Init, action and prompt-answer logs identify the catalog UUIDs behind those instances; the start event itself carries physical identities rather than an extra catalog UUID field.
