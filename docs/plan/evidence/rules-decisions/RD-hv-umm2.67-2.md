# RD-hv-umm2.67-2: An abandon cost's "that character's cost" is captured before payment
- Ladder: 1 (C13: a figment's cost is 0● for "that character's cost", and a figment copy has the copied cost), then 4 for the capture (MTG analog: CR 601.2h pays costs before the spell or ability is put on the stack, and CR 608.2h reads a sacrificed permanent's characteristics from its last known information)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X
- Affects: 77f31db4-0397-49a7-8886-f5459ee960e9
- Why: "Abandon a character: A player erodes X, where X is that character's cost" names a value of the card that pays the cost, and the character is in the void, or no longer exists as a figment, by the time the ability resolves. The rules already capture a discarded card's cost before payment and keep it with the stack item; the abandon cost uses the same capture (`abandonCostCapture`), read from the character's copiable values as it is chosen. A figment, which ceases to exist when abandoned, captures 0● (C13); a figment copy captures the cost it copied. "Abandon a character" names any character its controller controls, so the source may abandon itself and its ability still resolves with the captured X. When the ability also pays X●, the captured cost and the chosen X are one shared value. "A player" is chosen as the ability resolves (`choosePlayer`), so either player may erode.
