# RD-hv-7x4l.16-2: An additional cost with alternatives is paid by choosing one payable alternative as the card is played
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with the card text; steps 1–4 are silent: rules.md defines mandatory and optional additional costs but not alternatives, and the MTG analog of a choice among costs is the same reading)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X
- Affects: 7ea13d02-9d97-492e-9edb-f326eb3b4d1c
- Why: Abandon Constraints reads "To play this card, abandon a character or discard a card." The player picks one alternative they can pay while playing the card, alongside the other play-time choices, and pays only it; with no payable alternative the card cannot be played, like any unpayable additional cost. An optional additional cost is likewise offered only when the whole of it can be paid. Whether an optional cost was paid travels with the item on the stack, so a copy counts it as paid without paying it (D15, rules § Copies on the stack).
