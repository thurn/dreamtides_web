# RD-hv-umm2.534-1: Look-back "when … is dissolved" and "when you abandon …" abilities read disabled triggers and "Once per turn" use as the character left play
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 603.10a: leaves-the-battlefield abilities look back in time, so whether an ability exists and can trigger is determined by the game state just before the event; a permanent whose abilities are suppressed while on the battlefield does not trigger on its own departure, and an ability whose suppression begins only because of the departure still triggers). Steps 1–3 are silent: decisions.md has no D-, C-, or P-entry on disabled triggers or once-per-turn limits for look-back triggers.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When a character is dissolved and → When you abandon a character (amended), → Disabled triggers, → Once per turn, § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities; RD-hv-umm2.65-1, RD-hv-umm2.65-2, RD-hv-umm2.333-1, RD-hv-umm2.532-1
- Affects: no authored card combines these today; it governs any character whose triggered abilities are disabled while it is in play or under a condition its own departure changes (`disableTriggers`), and any gained "Once per turn" look-back ability (gain-abilities cards).
- Why: the look-back abilities matched the character as it was in play but read what could stop them only at the `dissolved` or `abandoned` event, after leaving play had ended its gained abilities (dropping their once-per-turn records) and expired "while this is in play" effects. So a gained once-per-turn ability already used that turn triggered again on its own card's departure, a character whose triggers were disabled only while in play triggered on its own dissolve or abandon, and a disable whose condition the departure made true suppressed an ability that could trigger as the character left. Reading both as the character left play matches the rest of the look-back rule: the ability sees the world as it was just before the character left.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts` `armLookbacks` records each armed card or emblem ability's `TriggerBlocks` (`disabled`, `used`) as `ArmedAbility` (`src/engine/steps/types.ts`); `enqueueAbility` reads them in place of the current state for armed abilities and records a once-per-turn use unless the records past the card's own abilities were dropped since it was armed (`gainedRecordsDropped`), so the use never limits an ability the card gains after returning that turn. Contracts: `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/abandon-lookback.contracts.ts`, `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/dissolve.contracts.ts`.
