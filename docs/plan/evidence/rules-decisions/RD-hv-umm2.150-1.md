# RD-hv-umm2.150-1: conditional void permission and current deck-top composition

- Ladder: not invoked; the current normative rules and RD-hv-umm2.489-1, RD-hv-umm2.513-1, RD-hv-umm2.514-1, RD-hv-umm2.515-1 and RD-hv-umm2.59-2 settle these clauses.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Static abilities; § Zones → References to cards in a zone; § Playing Cards and the Stack → Playing from the void, Playing from the top of a deck and Continuously revealing a deck top.
- Affects: 041fafeb-f159-4aba-90c2-3ce4b3038bdc (base/amplified), 1521c06c-f5ac-4d29-a6bd-7030b63a3c3e, 28c3ef90-c0dc-4b85-92c8-40758b99e0ce (base/amplified).
- Why: the void permission applies while its controller holds at most one card, or two amplified, and shares one committed-play allowance across eligible characters for each source ability and arrival object. Turning the condition off and on preserves an already spent allowance. Ordinary costs and timing apply. The next turn renews the allowance.
- Why: the Materialized effect reads the current top at resolution. A character of copiable cost at most two is materialized exhausted when capacity permits. An occupied battlefield leaves that matched card in the deck; it does not take the otherwise branch. A nonmatching top is drawn. An empty deck supplies neither branch a card, so this effect causes no Fatigue. The top reference introduces no target or choice.
- Why: three controlled characters, or two amplified, continuously reveal the current top; the source counts itself. The independently stated permission covers an identified matching own top for normal play while the source applies, including a learned tracked top after the reveal condition stops holding. Unknown replacements require a proper look or reveal. Movement events precede the disclosure of the next top.
