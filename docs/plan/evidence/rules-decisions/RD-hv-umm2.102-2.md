# RD-hv-umm2.102-2: "Reduce its cost by N●" is a permanent own-card reduction on the card the trigger names, wherever it is as the ability resolves, applied with every other reduction by every route of play
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 601.2f: cost reductions apply to the total cost after increases), then 5 for the permanent own-card reduction, the simplest reading consistent with the text. rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X → Cost changes and A card's own cost, § Zone Changes, RD-hv-umm2.101-1 and RD-hv-umm2.422-1 supply the normative cost composition and persistent identity rules.
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → A card's own cost; § Ability Types → When you return a character to hand
- Affects: 87aba308-00b4-40d8-a46f-4e6f58a664a8 (Breach Artist), ad76c77a-e456-4e59-bbf7-6bcb56f892ee (Ferryman of the Falls)
- Why: the rules had "It costs N●" (a set) and "It costs N● more" but no own-card reduction. A set to "printed − N" is wrong for cards cheaper than N, does not stack, and is ignored by replacement routes, so the reduction is its own mode that joins the reductions exactly as an own-card increase joins the increases: two reductions add up, the total floors at 0● once, and it applies by hand, permission, Reclaim and Offering alike ("Reclaim 5●" reduced by 2● costs 3●). "Its" is the returned card. A card keeps its identity across zones, so the change names it wherever it is as the ability resolves; in practice it is still in the hand it was returned to, since triggered abilities resolve before anyone receives priority. No duration is stated, so the change is permanent and travels with the card. The card's cost for conditions is unchanged.
- Mechanics: `CardCostMode` gains `"reduce"` (`src/engine/state/types.ts`); `costModifier` (`src/engine/continuous/costs.ts`) adds it to the reductions; the saved-state validator for `cardCost` (`src/rules/battle/fold.ts`) accepts it; the primitive `changeCardCost(triggeringCard(), { reduce: N })` (`src/engine/effects/primitives/modify-card-cost.ts`) starts a floating layer-6 `cardCost` change on the trigger's subject, announced by `effectStarted`; `modifyCardCost` also accepts `{ reduce: N }`. The status copy reads "Costs N energy less" (`src/runtime/battle-prompt-messages.ts`). The cost-composition table gains `cardCost.reduce` rows.
