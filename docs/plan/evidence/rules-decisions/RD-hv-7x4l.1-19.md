# RD-hv-7x4l.1-19: "Cannot be targeted by effects" covers every effect, including its controller's own
- Ladder: 1 (C17 targeting); non-targeting effects still apply by step 4 (MTG 115.10 analog: only targeting is restricted)
- rules.md: § Targeting
- Affects: 516dbd2d-dae4-4873-937e-adfeeee4444d, 2708c635-9332-4cfb-b43d-889ba2e329b6, bd43b120-2503-406c-8b72-ea3dc55198a0
- Why: C17 makes the restriction apply to every effect, the controller's included. The sentence that non-targeting effects ("Dissolve all characters") still affect the card follows from the text restricting targeting only, and keeps the rule from being read as general protection.
