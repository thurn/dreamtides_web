# RD-hv-umm2.73-1: A ⍟ cost is payable only in full, lowers only the payer's total, and is a loss of ⍟
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: CR 119.4, a player can pay life only if their life total is at least the payment), then 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the card texts)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Paying ⍟
- Affects: 78599234-6054-4df1-8e79-c38782517f9a; later pointsCost cards 97b359ee-5a7a-4f54-bd65-e6221a893208 (the "or pay 5⍟" alternative) and f4c570d4-c500-4b66-ad97-e8ccbef19392
- Why: He Who Lies Dreaming prints "To play this character, pay 4⍟." The rules define a floor of 0 for losing ⍟ (Objective, C14) but no ⍟ cost, and the Rust engine has none. Letting a player with fewer ⍟ "pay" and stop at 0 would make the cost free at 0⍟, which the card text cannot intend; as with life in Magic, the cost is payable only when the player has the whole amount, and otherwise the play, alternative or optional cost is unavailable. Paying lowers the payer's own total by exactly the amount and changes no other player's, so it can never produce a win or a draw. It is announced as a loss of points (the existing pointsScored event, negative amount), as other ⍟ losses are. Energy cost changes do not reach a ⍟ cost, since they change "the energy a player pays".
