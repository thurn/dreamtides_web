# RD-hv-umm2.58-1: "Draw K of the cards" after a look at the top of the deck draws the chosen looked-at cards, and the rest stay on top in their order
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: looking at cards moves none of them, so a card a resolving instruction does not move stays where it is; "draw" names the draw action, whose triggers and counts follow), then 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the text) for a short or empty deck
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Look at the top N cards
- Affects: 01d53e49-7833-4122-a970-b72c73ad2c28
- Why: rules.md had no look-at-top effect. No D, C, or P entry covers it; the prototype implements no look-at-top card (`docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md`), and the Rust engine has none under unchanged text. The text says "draw", where other look-at-top cards say "put … into your hand", so each chosen card is a draw: it fires "when you draw" abilities, counts toward the turn's drawn cards, and is announced as drawn (`cardDrawn`, private to the drawer as every draw is). "They gain ephemeral" is the existing Ephemeral status of a card drawn with ephemeral. The text gives the cards it does not draw no destination and no reorder, so they stay on top of the deck in their order; Foresee's reorder is part of Foresee, not of looking. The looker chooses which cards to draw in a `chooseCards` prompt private to them (role `lookAtTop`), which teaches them every looked-at card, and they keep knowing the ones left on top until the deck is shuffled; the opponent loses track of those cards and sees how many were looked at (`lookedAtTop`, public, naming each card only to a side that can identify it). A deck with fewer cards than the draw has all of them drawn (a lone answer is given automatically); the cards are drawn from among the looked-at cards rather than from the deck, so an empty deck looks at nothing, draws nothing, and causes no Fatigue, as for "the top card of your deck" (§ Zones → References to cards in a zone).
