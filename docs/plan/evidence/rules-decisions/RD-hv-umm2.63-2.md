# RD-hv-umm2.63-2: "Put one on top, one on the bottom, and one into your hand" is one private arrangement of the looked-at cards; a short deck fills the places the player chooses
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Ponder-style effects let the player choose each card's place as they resolve, privately; a card put into the hand from among looked-at cards is not drawn), then 5 for a short deck
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Look at the top N cards
- Affects: f4b34207-2ad4-421b-8798-9c20320607ae (base and amplified)
- Why: The player decides where every looked-at card goes as the event resolves, in one `arrange` prompt private to them (role `putInDeck`) whose destinations follow the printed order, each taking exactly its number and "the rest" (amplified: "Put the rest into your void") the leftovers; cards put together on top or on the bottom are ordered by the player, as CR 401.4 lets an owner order them. "Put one into your hand" is not "draw" (as for Discover and "from among those cards"), so it fires no draw abilities and is announced as `cardTaken` from the deck. With fewer cards than the named places take (a deck of 2 for "one on top, one on the bottom, one into your hand"), the player chooses which places get a card, each at most its number, and "the rest" gets none, so no card is forced into the void while a named place is empty; an empty deck does nothing, without Fatigue, as for any look. The opponent sees how many cards were looked at and went to each place, and the cards that went into the void, which is public; the looker keeps knowing the cards it left on top and put on the bottom until the deck is shuffled.
