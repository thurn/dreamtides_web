# RD-hv-umm2.464-1: A ✦ bound reads a card's final spark outside play, and a played card's spark as it was played
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analogs: 613.8 layer dependency, where an effect whose result changes what another applies to is applied first, so a characteristic-defining read sees the dependent layer's result; and "whenever you cast a spell with [quality]" checking the spell as it becomes cast, with last known information afterwards), adapted by rules.md § Spark → Spark outside play, which applies ordinary static "have +N✦" abilities in play and the bounded original journey-deck spark bonus in every battle zone under [binding C4](../../decisions.md#c4-your-deck-on-a-dreamsign-means-the-journey-deck) and [RD-hv-umm2.589-1](RD-hv-umm2.589-1.md)
- rules.md: § Spark → Spark outside play; § Ability Types → Order of continuous effects; § Ability Types → Triggered abilities
- Affects: general; the `CardFilter` `sparkAtMost` / `sparkAtLeast` bounds read by static abilities (`grantCardKeyword`, `countCards` zone counts in static values and selector bounds, every `StaticEnv.read` consumer) and by "when you play" / "when the opponent plays" triggers, nth-play counts included. No printed card reaches either path yet.
- Why (static abilities): RD-hv-umm2.452-1 makes a ✦ bound compare a card's current spark wherever it is, gained spark and unexpired spark with a duration included. A static ability ordinarily reads the layers before its own, so a layer-3 keyword grant or a layer-5 spark modifier would read a hand or void card's printed spark and miss the spark it gained (layer 5). That would give one ✦ two meanings. In MTG a dependent effect applies after what it depends on. Here the dependency is one-way: ordinary static base-spark and spark forms affect characters in play. Outside play, final spark comes from copiable values, floating base-spark settings, gained spark, floating spark changes, and the bounded C4 `journeyDeckSparkModifier` contribution to matching original physical journey-deck cards in every battle zone. C4 eligibility reads current character type and copiable cost after variant and D39 deck-entry modifications at layer 1, without reading spark. Its supported optional gates (`energyAtLeast`, `turnOf`, `sourceIn`, `sourceWasReclaimed`, and unfiltered `cardsIn`) have no characteristic dependency; characteristic-dependent gates are rejected. Reading final outside-play spark expands that bounded form without consulting keyword or spark layers for qualification, so an ordinary static ability whose output changes spark never reads itself through this exception and no dependency cycle is introduced. A character in play keeps the layer-in-progress reading for `CharacterSelector` bounds, where statics do change spark.
- Why (played records): the turn log records the type, subtype, "has all character types" and copiable cost a card had as it was played (RD-hv-7x4l.34-1), so a later change, or a move to a hidden zone where no side can identify the card, never revisits an earlier play. Spark joins those records on the same footing. Reading the played instance's current spark, as RD-hv-umm2.452-1 did, let an nth-play ✦ trigger fire in the real state but not in a determinization once the card was shuffled into a deck: the view drops the instance link and determinization mints a history-only identity with no spark to read. The recorded spark is non-identifying public information: the card was played face up.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/continuous/layers.ts` `staticReader` gives each static ability the layer-in-progress reader, with the base spark and spark of a character card outside play replaced by `sparkOutsidePlay` (floating layer-4 and layer-5 changes, `status.gainedSpark`, and the bounded C4 contribution over layer 1); `changes(4|5)` applies `assertInPlay` to ordinary static changes, throwing if they name a card outside play, with the explicitly expanded `journeyDeckSparkModifier` as the all-zone exception. `PlayedCard.spark` (`number | null`) is written by `rules/turn-log.ts` `recordPlayed`; `continuous/characteristics.ts` `playedMatchesFilter` compares it, and a saved record without it reads the existing instance's current spark, else matches no ✦ bound. The view carries it with each play record (recovering a legacy record's spark only from a card the viewer sees), determinization copies it, and `rules/battle/fold.ts` `isTurnLog` validates it.
- Contracts: `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/card-filter-spark-layers.contracts.ts`.
