# RD-hv-umm2.78-1: Gained Reclaim is chosen as a target, permanent and traveling without a duration, and its latest stated cost replaces a printed one
- Ladder: 2 (prototype: Firmament Mirror's granted Reclaim plays from the void for the card's energy cost and marks it reclaimed, as printed Reclaim does), then 4 (MTG analog: CR 702.34 Flashback granted by an effect, "target card in your graveyard gains flashback", chooses its card as a target; CR 613.7 timestamp order decides between characteristic-changing effects), then 5 (simplest reading consistent with every affected text) for duration and cost precedence; step 1 is silent (no D-, C-, or P-entry covers a granted Reclaim during play; D39 covers deck-entry Reclaim only), and step 3 is silent (the `~/dreamtides` Rust engine parses `GainsReclaim` but implements no mutation for it)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: db4618f6-121b-4c97-98d3-c29849c5824e ("Choose a card in your void to gain reclaim"), 8951e112-92d2-4246-9859-39320620231b ("Choose an event in your void to gain reclaim"), 7e4cca36-5134-415a-b30a-c4876b58976c ("Erode 1. That card gains reclaim 0●"); and every later entity the content inventory routes to grantReclaim, among them Path to Redemption ("Until end of turn, cards in your void gain reclaim")
- Why: rules.md defined printed Reclaim only. "Choose a card in your void" reads like "a character from your void", which rules.md already treats as a target chosen as the ability is activated, so an activation needs a matching card and a chosen card that left the void before resolution gains nothing. The rules already say an effect with no stated duration is permanent and lasts wherever its cards are, and that gained properties travel across zones, so a gained Reclaim stays with the card in a hand or deck; once the card is played by it, the reclaimed status banishes it as it would leave play, so the gain is never used twice. "That card" after Erode names the card the Erode put into the void; Fatigue puts none there, so it names none. A stated cost ("reclaim 0●") is the price of that Reclaim, so it replaces a printed or deck-entry Reclaim cost, as a deck-entry Reclaim replaces a printed one (D39). Among several gained costs the most recent applies, as layer-3 changes apply in timestamp order. A plain gain states no cost and so changes none; giving a card plain Reclaim never makes it costlier. A loss of Reclaim removes the keyword and every cost gained before it. The engine records the cost on the floating keyword change (`reclaimCosts`) and reads it in `routeCosts`, so the grant is announced by its `effectStarted` event, ends with its duration, and survives a reload as plain state.
