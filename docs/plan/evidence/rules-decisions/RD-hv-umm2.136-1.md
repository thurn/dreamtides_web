# RD-hv-umm2.136-1: "Return a character to its controller's hand" returns it to its owner's hand
- Ladder: 3 (Rust `return_from_battlefield_to_hand` moves the card to `card_properties::controller`, which is the card's owner, since the Rust engine has no control change), supported by 4 (MTG Snap, the card's `mtgName`: "Return target creature to its owner's hand")
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Return to hand; § Zones → Hand
- Affects: 25173cac-333f-4efc-9a03-b532d5783419 (Stratospheric Voyage, base and amplified); a9b5afaa-e149-4bf4-9007-077e8e9f12e6 (Riftwalker, already composed with `returnToHand` in cards-02)
- Why: rules.md said where a returned cost character goes ("its owner's hand") but not where an effect's "Return a character to its controller's hand" puts a character whose controller is not its owner, which only happens after Gain control. The reference engine and the MTG analog both send it to its owner's hand, rules.md § Symbols and Notation already reads "you control" phrasing as ownership language, and § Zones → Hand sends every card bound for "its owner's hand" to its owner's. Reading "controller's hand" as the owner's hand keeps one return effect for every "Return … to hand" card and leaves putting a card into a player's hand who does not own it to instructions that name "your hand". The two readings differ only for a character under Gain control.
- Reference evidence: read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12, `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/effects/apply_standard_effect.rs:275–292` and `rules_engine/src/battle_queries/src/battle_card_queries/card_properties.rs:24–26` (`controller` returns `owner`).
- Mechanics: `p.returnToHand(target(anyCharacter()))` through `returnToHand` in `src/engine/rules/zones.ts`, whose `moveInstance` relocates to the instance's owner; no engine change.
