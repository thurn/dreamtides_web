# RD-hv-umm2.485-1: "You may play this character from your void" is a lasting play permission covering only its source while that card is in its owner's void
- Ladder: 3 (Rust `StandardStaticAbility::PlayFromVoid` read from the card's own abilities while it is in the void, played for its normal energy cost and not banished on leaving play), supported by 4 (MTG Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis, the card's `mtgName`: cast from the graveyard by its own static ability, it goes to the graveyard as usual when it later dies, and can be cast from there again) and the existing rules.md text for play permissions
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Playing from the void; § Costs, Requirements, and X → Banish void cards as a cost, → Banish X cards from your void
- Affects: 3e802ecd-a83b-491b-9528-a4672d0e986f (Abomination of Memory, base; it has no amplified text); the `playSelfFromZone("void")` builder
- Why: rules.md described static play permissions that cover every matching card while their source is in play, and permissions that name a card, but not a card's own ability working from the void. The simplest reading consistent with the card and with the existing permission rules makes it a play permission, not Reclaim: the card is played for its normal costs, its additional cost included ("To play this character, banish 7 cards from your void" excludes the card being played, as every void-banish cost does), it is not reclaimed, so it goes where it normally would (a dissolved character to its owner's void), and it can then be played from the void again. The ability works only while the card is in the void its controller owns, so it covers nothing while the card is on the stack, in play, in a hand, or in a deck, and never lets the opponent play it. It is a lasting permission, so a play it covers uses up no one-play permission, and when the card also has Reclaim the player chooses the route as with any permitted card.
- Reference evidence: read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12, `rules_engine/src/battle_queries/src/legal_action_queries/can_play_cards.rs:158–180` (`PlayFromVoid` and `PlayOnlyFromVoid` price a void play at the card's converted energy cost) and `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/card_mutations/move_card.rs:290–313` (only `banish_when_leaves_play`, set by an explicit effect, banishes a card leaving play).
- Mechanics: `src/engine/effects/primitives/play-from-zone.ts` evaluates a static `playFromZone` naming `thisCardIn("void")` through `StaticEnv.source`, adding a layer-6 `playFrom` change with `instance` set to the source only while it is in its controller's void; `staticAbility(..., { zone: "void" })` makes the ability apply there. Play routing, legality, costs, and the view use the existing `permission` route unchanged.
