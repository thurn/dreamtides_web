# RD-hv-umm2.53-1: A card's static ability that names another zone works while the card is in that zone, timestamped as it entered it
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 113.6 and 113.6b: abilities function on the battlefield unless they state the zones they function from, and then only from those; MTG 613.7a and 613.7d: a static ability's effect has its object's timestamp, which the object receives as it enters a zone). rules.md § Ability Types → Where triggered abilities work supplies the normative zone rule.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Static abilities ("Where static abilities work"); § Ability Types → Order of continuous effects
- Affects: 455ef341-8a26-44e1-b287-19e53bdc6158 ("If this card is in your void, characters you control have +1✦"), and the self-cost cards that work from a hand or a void (6497d8b1-85b8-486d-99e2-5c141486d508, 8bf53fe6-8a25-401e-9a99-84343a026bb0, bbdc6ee7-e9b1-488b-90b9-19337b65fee6, f7c68226-def4-4eb0-bdfe-b028482e1351) as their beads author them
- Why: rules.md said a static ability applies while its source is in play and an emblem's always, but card text such as "If this card is in your void, characters you control have +1✦" names another zone, and rules.md gave triggered abilities that reading only. Static abilities now follow the same rule: one that names a zone works while its card is in that zone and stops as the card leaves it ("any" covers play, void, hand, and deck). Its controller is the side whose void, hand, or deck the card is in, which is the hand's holder for a hand card and the owner otherwise, as the engine keeps those lists. Its timestamp in the order of continuous effects is when the card entered that zone, as a static ability in play is timestamped when its source entered play.
