# RD-hv-7x4l.7-3: Cost changes apply to a card's whole energy cost, X included; "next card" changes are used up when that card's costs are paid
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 601.2f: cost increases, then reductions, applied to the total cost including X; MTG practice for "the next spell you cast costs less": used when that spell is cast)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Cost changes
- Affects: 21965e95-0c8c-470c-a1e1-06d7b87a8d00 ("Events cost you 1● more") and every card, avatar, and dreamsign that makes cards cost more or less, including "the next … costs N● less"
- Why: engine-design said "increases, then reductions, with a minimum of 0" and "next card modifiers are consumed on use" without saying what the floor applies to or what "use" is. Applying the changes to the total of the fixed part and X lets a reduction pay for X, as in MTG, and gives a single floor at 0. The legal range of X widens accordingly. A "next card" change applies to the next matching card its player plays and ends when that card's costs are paid, after the commit point, so legality checks, views, a play cancelled or stopped before the commit point, and non-matching plays leave it in place; it is used up even when the cost was already 0●, because it applied to that play. Cost conditions ("≤2● cost character") read the printed cost (C4, C13), since cost changes modify only the act of playing a card.
