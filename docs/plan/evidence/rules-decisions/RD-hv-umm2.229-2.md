# RD-hv-umm2.229-2: Enemy removal retains its effect controller or opposing challenge character

- Ladder: 5 for Dreamtides causal attribution and explicit created-character banishment, after steps 1–4. Existing rules and RD-hv-7x4l.8-1 settle opposition, replacement order, Veil, and Reclaim; neither D/C/P entries nor an actual prototype implementation settle causal actors. Rust's Judgment-era rules/text differ. Official MTG CR effective 2026-09-25 supports lookback (603.10a), actual replacement outcomes (614.6), token departure timing (111.7), and explicit destruction rather than arbitrary graveyard movement (701.8b). These are adapted foundations, not a direct MTG rule for Dreamtides causal removal or its created-card zone replacement.
- rules.md: § Zone Changes → Causing an enemy's removal; § Materialize (captured delayed cohorts); § Ability Types (delayed Ending timing); § Vengeful; RD-hv-umm2.499-2.
- Affects: dreamsigns a98f468b-5e76-4041-83ee-69c0871a6bf0, c92438fc-8e1f-4f97-9dcf-8ca0edaa8607, 1ccd8d69-e14c-4788-be1d-cb6a1b703225. Their content remains owned by their composition beads.
- Why: an effect's controller causes its confirmed opposing in-play dissolve or banishment, including an actual Reclaim replacement of a return or deck movement. Printed delayed banishment retains its creating controller and checks opposition when it resolves. Challenge removal belongs to the opposing character in the lane, whose identity, controller and copiable cost are captured before either participant departs; ties and Vengeful count. The default removal subject is the victim, whose cost is captured immediately before its departure. The challenge-only subject is the removing character, and an absent "it" receives no spark. Created dissolves and explicit created banishments count, but cessation replacing a return/deck movement does not. Veil prevention, ordinary returns/deck movement, pile banishment, abandon, Offering/Ephemeral cleanup and unrelated cessation qualify for neither causal removal nor a challenge dissolve. Reclaim banishment qualifies for default removal and never for challenge-only dissolution.
