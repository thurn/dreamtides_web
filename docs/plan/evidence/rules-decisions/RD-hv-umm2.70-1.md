# RD-hv-umm2.70-1: Challengers and blockers are the designated characters the Challenge phase would resolve now

- Ladder: 2 (existing engine behavior: `resolveLane` pairs a designated challenger in any front lane with a designated blocker now opposite it), after 1 (rules § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring: designations made at the end of Day and Dusk, which Night repositioning can change).
- rules.md: § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring → Holding a role.
- Affects: 3b5f5686-f63b-4998-b713-2f6777ecbb5f (base and amplified), and every later text that reads challengers or blockers, such as 55340736-7fc5-4555-8f47-081d770a6e8d, 98f16d26-be71-49ad-9034-5fafb5d3f053, 1b341984-d32e-42a7-ae56-b22059c59737, and 989b55bd-139f-40dc-b1fd-eb2f7b1c091a.
- Why: The rules define both roles by designation and say Night movement can change who holds them, without saying how an effect reads a role between designation and the Challenge phase. Reading a role exactly as `resolveLane` would resolve the lane at that moment keeps one meaning for "challenger" across effects and scoring: a designated challenger anywhere in the active player's front rank, and a designated blocker in the opposing front rank directly opposite such a challenger. A character that leaves the front rank, changes control (gain control places it in its new controller's back rank), or loses the challenger opposite it holds no role; designations exist from the end of Day (challengers) and Dusk (blockers) until the turn ends. "Draw a card, then discard a card unless you control a challenger" checks its condition as the discard would happen, after the draw and after any trigger that resolved earlier changed the board (D14 order), the source itself counting when it is a challenger.
