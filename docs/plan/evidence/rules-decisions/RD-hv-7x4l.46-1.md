# RD-hv-7x4l.46-1: Only a gain of at least +1✦ is a gain, and an additional gain triggers no "when … gains ✦" ability
- Ladder: (a) 4 (MTG CR 119.9: a player who gains 0 life has had no life-gain event, so "whenever you gain life" does not trigger), consistent with rules § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring ("a character that scores 0⍟ does not score"); (b) 1 (C17: "the additional gain does not retrigger it. Several sources stack, each adding 1")
- rules.md: § Spark → Gaining ✦ and Additional spark
- Affects: 26a05558-4692-43d2-ae6c-a6eb385a6d22 (card), 1a21186c-cafd-4e0f-9304-1ac0ef55340a (dreamsign) — the only catalog entries whose text triggers on gaining ✦; gains that can be +0✦ and so never trigger them: 89c0d03c-2199-415a-bc66-89e451034328, 31501d09-5dde-44f4-a938-7ca95ad988a0 (cards), 73efd845-6009-4686-860c-01c7f5b1c074, d4b18cc4-caeb-40b3-b0e2-f1c8b1c8354c, 2788cdad-2735-4cdf-89a8-dcecaca87369 (dreamsigns)
- Why: rules § Spark said an additional-spark ability applies to each "gains +N✦" event without saying (a) whether +0✦ or a lowering of spark counts, or (b) whether the additional gain is itself a gain for other "when … gains ✦" abilities.
  - (a) "Gains ✦" reads as an increase. A "+X✦" or "+1✦ for each …" gain whose amount is 0 changes nothing, and granting 1 additional ✦ for it would turn a no-op into +1✦; MTG treats a 0 life gain as no gain, and the rules already treat a 0⍟ score as no score. No catalog card lowers a character's spark through a gain, and no effect that lowers spark reads as gaining it. The engine matches `sparkGained` only with `amount > 0` (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`).
  - (b) C17 fixes that each source adds exactly 1. If an additional gain triggered other sources, Emberfang and Gray Feather together would trigger each other without end, and two sources would add more than 1 each. So no additional gain triggers any "when … gains ✦" ability, its own or another. The engine marks the additional gain `additional: true`, which the matcher ignores for every `gainsSpark` trigger (engine-design § Triggers, hv-7x4l.41). Example: with both in play, a character that gains +2✦ gains 2 additional ✦, 4✦ in all.
  - No catalog card has a non-additional-spark "when a character gains ✦" payoff that reading (b) would starve.
