# RD-hv-umm2.81-1: "You may play an event from your void" in a resolving effect is one permission that lasts until it is used
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Echo Technician's source card, Emry, Lurker of the Loch, grants "you may cast that card this turn", a permission used after its ability resolves rather than a cast during resolution), then rules.md § Durations ("An effect with no stated duration is permanent")
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Playing from the void
- Affects: cd3d8e4a-618e-4c2f-be60-0363b81887f1 (Echo Technician)
- Why: rules.md did not say whether the ability plays an event during its own resolution or lets its controller play one afterwards. The engine plays a card only through the play step, at its timing, never in the middle of another effect, and the ☾ ability has the same Standard timing a card played next would have, so a permission loses nothing a nested play gives. "An event" names one card, so the permission covers one play and ends as that card is played (a `next` play permission, `playFromZone("void", { cardType: "event" }, { next: true })`). The text states no duration, so rules § Durations makes it last until it is used; several activations give several plays. A play covered by a lasting permission uses none, and otherwise the earliest one-play permission is used (continuous/permissions.ts).
