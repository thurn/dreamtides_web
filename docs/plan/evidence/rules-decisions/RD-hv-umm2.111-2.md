# RD-hv-umm2.111-2: A character loses its abilities only while it stays in play; the loss and an "until the opponent pays" effect left with nothing to end end as it leaves
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 400.7: an object that leaves the battlefield becomes a new object, so a "loses all abilities" effect ends then; MTG 603.10a: a creature that has no abilities as it leaves triggers none of its own leaves-the-battlefield abilities), adapted as RD-hv-umm2.110-2 adapted the matching gain
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Losing abilities; § Durations; § Playing Cards and the Stack → Paying to end an effect; RD-hv-umm2.110-2 (gained abilities last while the character is in play)
- Affects: 9e9efbc0-d438-48a9-9551-85c93fb33f3e (base, amplified), 890970bd-475f-4da4-b835-2fb75882a84d; general for every "loses all abilities" effect
- Why: rules.md § Durations makes Signal Hijacker's undated loss permanent and § Zone Changes keeps identity across zones, which would leave a returned or dissolved card without Reclaim, void abilities, additional costs, or ▸Materialized for the rest of the battle on a change no player sees once the card is hidden. "An enemy" is a character in play, and the gain this loss pairs with already lasts only while its character is in play, so the loss mirrors it: `expireAt` ends a character's `loseAbilities` changes as it leaves play (the `sourceLeavesPlay` boundary of that character), whatever their expiry, and fold validation rejects a loss naming a card outside play. Gaining control is not a zone change and keeps the loss. The expiry happens as the card departs, after `leftPlay` is announced, so the trigger matcher sees the character without abilities as it leaves: its own leaves-play and ▸Dissolved abilities do not trigger. A payable effect none of whose floating effects is left (its character left play or ceased to exist) ends unpaid with `payableEffectEnded`, so the opponent is never offered to pay for a change that no longer exists; paying removes the payable effect first, so a paid one reports `paid: true` once. A view keeps a loss whose source it cannot see (`keptWithoutSource`), so every determinization agrees on what the character can do, and its `effectStarted` event is shown to every side that identifies the public character, with the source redacted for a side that cannot identify it (a created source that ceased to exist before its trigger resolved), so both players see the loss begin.
