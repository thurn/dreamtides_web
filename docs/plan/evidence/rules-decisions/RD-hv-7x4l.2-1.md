# RD-hv-7x4l.2-1: Characters on the stack count toward back-rank capacity when playing a character
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with every affected card text)
- rules.md: § The Play Area → Battlefield Capacity
- Affects: general (every character card)
- Why: The capacity rule forbids playing a character when the back rank is full, and says a trigger that cannot place a character leaves it in its previous zone. A played character resolving from the stack has no previous zone to stay in. Without reserving positions, a player could play a character with one open position, respond to the opponent's response with an Interrupt character that takes that position, and leave the first character with nowhere to enter. Counting a player's own characters already on the stack as occupying positions keeps every legal play resolvable and matches the intent that a full back rank blocks character plays.
