# RD-hv-umm2.169-1: A conditionally gained Awakened acts only as the character enters play

- Ladder: not invoked for the keyword itself: rules.md § Keywords and Effects → Awakened ("enters play without the exhausted status") and § Exhaust and Awaken define Awakened as an entry rule and awakening as a separate effect, and RD-hv-umm2.93-2 records that Awakened matters only as a character enters play. RD-hv-umm2.513-1 supplies the live reading of the conditional static. The amended rules.md sentence states the composition explicitly.
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Awakened; § Exhaust and Awaken; § Ability Types → Static abilities (conditional static abilities, RD-hv-umm2.513-1).
- Affects: 29c4c45d-33df-42ac-bf28-387eb00264aa (Inheritor of Sorrows, base and amplified).
- Why: "this character has +4✦ and gains awakened" is a conditional static that grants the keyword while its void-event count holds. The keyword's only effect is that the character enters play without the exhausted status, checked as it takes its position with in-play static abilities, its own included, applied. So Inheritor of Sorrows enters ready when the void already holds enough events, and enters exhausted otherwise. Reaching the count later raises its spark at once but does not ready it, and falling below the count removes the spark but does not exhaust it. Readying a character mid-turn is the separate awaken effect, which this text does not name.
- Mechanics: two `staticAbility` entries sharing one `conditionCardCounts("void", N, { filter: { cardType: "event" } })` condition: `p.sparkModifier(self(), 4)` and `p.grant(self(), "awakened")`. `enterPlay` in `src/engine/rules/zones.ts` reads the keyword through the layers as the character takes its position. Scenarios in `src/content/specs/cards-57.scenarios.ts` cover entry with and without the count and a later count change.
