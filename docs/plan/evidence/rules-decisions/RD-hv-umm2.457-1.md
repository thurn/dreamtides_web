# RD-hv-umm2.457-1: "Reclaim – Discard X cards" binds X to the discarded count, at least 1

- Ladder: 1 (rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X: the contextual X rule, the shared X binding of a discard-X cost, RD-hv-umm2.61-1, and the least X of RD-hv-umm2.68-1; § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim), then 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the card text)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Discard X cards; § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: 79b37916-c05d-4818-9d67-3b90e340926e
- Why: Embrace the Infinite prints "Gain X⍟. / Reclaim – Discard X cards." with an X● cost. Played from hand, it costs X●, and the contextual X rule already makes that X at least 1 (the card's X energy minimum). Explicit Reclaim replaces the ● cost with its printed costs, so from the void the only cost is the discard, and X is the number of cards the player chooses to discard, spending no energy. Discarding zero cards would gain 0⍟ and banish the reclaimed event for no effect, which the contextual X rule disallows ("an event cannot generally be played for no effect"), exactly as for "Banish X cards from your void". So the Reclaim X is at least 1, and the card cannot be played from the void while its owner's hand holds no card. The player may discard any number of hand cards from 1 up to the whole hand; X equals that number and travels with the stack item, and the reclaimed card is banished as it leaves the stack. Discarded cards become public as they enter the void; no other hidden information changes. A variable discard with no stated least X keeps admitting zero, for cards such as Abyssal Plunge where zero is meaningful (RD-hv-umm2.61-1).
