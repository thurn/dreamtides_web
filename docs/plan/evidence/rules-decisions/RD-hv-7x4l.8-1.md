# RD-hv-7x4l.8-1: Zone-change replacements apply in order; Veil answers only a dissolve by the opponent's effect, and a lost Veil stays lost
- Ladder: 1 (engine-design § Zones and zone changes lists the replacements in order; rules § Keywords and Effects → Veil, Reclaim, Abandon), then 5 for what the order implies
- rules.md: § Zone Changes; § Keywords and Effects → Veil
- Affects: 7ffb1179-6c94-4f80-9613-ece2b799f19d, 01298848-1ebb-48a1-b00b-d7e0af890870, bc1c7ea9-e47d-49b8-a93a-8185b9cb40f2, 4b20e66e-2466-4518-8ef9-67f580bd6f14, cea847ef-2d1f-45d2-a0e0-fe9ce3fec55c, bbf316f6-1fd4-40f5-aacd-d9ed8a5c58ea (Veil), and every Reclaim card and figment
- Why: rules.md stated the three replacements separately but not how they combine. They apply in the listed order, each to the zone change as the earlier ones left it. A dissolved figment or other created card ceases to exist instead of entering the void but still fires ▸Dissolved, so its move is still a dissolve and Veil can still replace it: a Veil figment dissolved by the opponent's effect only loses Veil. A reclaimed card's move is replaced by banishment, which fires no ▸Dissolved and is no longer a dissolve, so Veil cannot replace it: a reclaimed Veil character dissolved by the opponent is banished. "An effect the opponent controls" excludes a challenge, whose losers (Vengeful's opponent included) are dissolved by the challenge rules rather than an effect, and an abandon, which only its controller performs. Losing Veil is a permanent change to the card, like gained spark, so it travels with the card across zones (rules § Zone Changes).
