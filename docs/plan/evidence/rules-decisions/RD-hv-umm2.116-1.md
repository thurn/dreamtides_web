# RD-hv-umm2.116-1: "When you discard a card" triggers for every card discarded from your hand, whatever made you discard it
- Ladder: 4 (MTG keyword action "discard": discarding moves a card from a player's hand to the graveyard, and a "whenever you discard a card" ability triggers on each discard from its controller's hand, whether a spell, a cost, the opponent, or the cleanup hand-size discard caused it)
- rules.md: § Zones → Hand ("When you discard")
- Affects: 9a18d375-1b6d-4df4-95ec-c7c5c3f36379 (Pattern Seeker), and every "When you discard a card" ability
- Why: rules.md used "When you discard a card" as an example but did not say which discards count. The player who discards is the one whose hand the card leaves, so a discard counts whether the player's own effect, the opponent's effect (including a chosen discard), a discard cost such as "Reclaim – Discard a card", or the Ending hand-limit discard caused it. A created card that is discarded triggers the ability even though it ceases to exist instead of entering the void, as the discard is announced while it is still in hand (RD-hv-7x4l.50-1). The engine encodes this once: every discard goes through `discardCard` (`src/engine/rules/resources.ts`), which emits the `discarded` event the `discard` trigger matches.
