# RD-hv-umm2.116-2: A card drawn with Ephemeral after the Ending's banishment step is banished in the next turn's Ending
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 513, the end step: a delayed "at the beginning of the next end step" ability created during an end step does not trigger until the next turn's end step)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Ephemeral; § Turn Structure → Ending
- Affects: 9a18d375-1b6d-4df4-95ec-c7c5c3f36379 (Pattern Seeker), b8a66998-f2d8-401d-8262-d5a658563936 (Consumed Marksman), and every "draw … with ephemeral" effect that can resolve during the Ending phase
- Why: rules.md banishes Ephemeral cards in Ending step 2 but did not say what happens to a card drawn with Ephemeral after that step. The common case is Pattern Seeker: the hand-limit discard (step 1) triggers it, and the triggered draw resolves after the Ending phase's steps have run. The banishment is an action of a particular step, like an MTG end-step trigger, so a card drawn after it waits for the next Ending's step 2 (in the next turn, the opponent's unless an extra turn follows) and is banished there if it is still in hand. This differs from "until end of turn" durations that begin late in Ending, which end as the turn ends (RD-hv-7x4l.6-4): those are durations, while Ephemeral is a status the banishment step acts on. The engine banishes every Ephemeral card in a hand in each Ending's step 2 (`banishEndOfTurnStatuses`, `src/engine/rules/turn.ts`), which is this behavior; the cards-04 scenario module pins it.
