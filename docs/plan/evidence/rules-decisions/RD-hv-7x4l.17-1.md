# RD-hv-7x4l.17-1: A required choice of N targets with fewer than N available has no legal option
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 603.3d: a triggered ability for which no legal choice, such as a target, can be made is removed from the stack; "two target creatures" with one available is no legal choice), consistent with rules § Targeting at play time
- rules.md: § Targeting → Required choices with no legal option; § Ability Types → Choices
- Affects: general; every triggered ability whose effect requires a set number of targets greater than one, and the same rule for cards and activated abilities when they are played
- Why: rules.md said a required choice with no legal option makes that part of the effect do nothing, but not whether a choice of two targets with one candidate has a legal option. At play time the engine already treats it as having none (the card cannot be played), and a mode with too few candidates is not a legal mode; taking only the available targets at resolution would make triggered abilities alone read "two" as "up to two". A choice of a set number of targets has no legal option while fewer than that many are available, so that part of a triggered ability does nothing, raises no prompt, and reports noLegalTarget; an "up to N" choice always has a legal option, possibly choosing none.
