# RD-hv-umm2.77-1: A "when … is put into your void" ability sees each card once it is in the void, by any route
- Ladder: 3 (read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12: `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/card_mutations/move_card.rs:244–246` pushes `Trigger::PutIntoVoid` after every move whose new zone is the void, whatever the old zone, and `rules_engine/src/battle_queries/src/card_ability_queries/trigger_queries.rs:85–94` matches the trigger's predicate against the card as a `VoidCardId`, in the void; `docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md` is silent on the trigger), agreeing with 4 (MTG 603.6c: an ability that triggers when a card is put into a zone "from anywhere" is never treated as a leaves-the-battlefield ability, so it does not look back and reads the card in its new zone; 603.6a/603.6d: a zone-entry trigger looks at the object after the event)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When a card is put into a void
- Affects: b6bad2a1-b857-4dd7-8b22-050eb68433a5 (base and amplified); the `whenPutIntoVoid(player, filter)` trigger and every later entity that uses it
- Why: rules.md defined "when … is dissolved" (look-back, RD-hv-umm2.65-1) and "leaves your void", but not a trigger on cards entering a void. The Rust engine and MTG agree that it is a zone-entry trigger: every route into the void counts (dissolve, abandon, discard, erode, foresee, "put the rest into your void", a prevented or resolved card leaving the stack), and the card is matched as it is in the void, so its type, subtype and copiable cost are read there (RD-hv-umm2.318-1), never types it had only from effects in play. "Your void" is the ability controller's void; a card always enters its owner's void, so the trigger fires for the owner's abilities whoever controlled the card. The ability must work where its card is as the card arrives: one that works in play never sees its own card enter a void, and one that works in the void sees its own arrival. A created card ceases to exist and a reclaimed card is banished instead of entering a void, so neither triggers it. Cards an effect moves together enter one at a time, each triggering on its own, consistent with RD-hv-umm2.65-2. The void is public, so the trigger's subject is named to both players even when it came from a hidden deck or hand.
- Mechanics: `relocate` (`src/engine/rules/zones.ts`) emits the public `enteredVoid { instance, side: owner, from }` once the card is in the void, the single point every route passes through; `TRIGGER_EVENTS.putIntoVoid = ["enteredVoid"]` and the matcher checks the void owner and the filter against the card in the void, after the ordinary functional-zone check. No arming or step-context record is needed.
