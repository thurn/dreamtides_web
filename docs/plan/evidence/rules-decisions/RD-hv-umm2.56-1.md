- Why: rules.md named the eroded cards but said nothing about choosing among them. No D, C, or P entry covers it; the prototype implements neither card, and the Rust engine has no "from among those cards" effect under unchanged text. Erode resolves first, so "those cards" are the cards it just put into the void: a card already in the void is not among them, and a card that Fatigue kept from being eroded does not exist to choose. The void is public, so the choice is not private and both players see which card goes to the hand (`cardTaken`, which names the card to both sides). "≤2● cost" compares the copiable cost with X counting as 0, as the existing cost selectors and Discover do. "You may" lets the controller choose no card: one card prompt that accepts none, rather than a separate yes-or-no question before it, so a player is never asked to confirm a take they then cannot make. With no eroded card that matches, nothing is asked. "Return … to your hand" (Vizier of Portents) and "put … into your hand" (Wildpath Companions) read the same here: the eroded cards are their controller's own. Putting the card into a hand is not a draw, as for Discover.
