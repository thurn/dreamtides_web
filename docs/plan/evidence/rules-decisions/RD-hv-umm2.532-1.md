# RD-hv-umm2.532-1: "When you abandon a character" looks back: it matches the character as it was in play, so a character's own ability sees its own abandon
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 603.10a: abilities that trigger when a permanent is sacrificed are leaves-the-battlefield abilities and look back in time, so Mayhem Devil and Korvold, Fae-Cursed King trigger on their own sacrifice). Steps 1–3 are silent: decisions.md has no D-, C-, or P-entry on abandon triggers; the prototype had no "when you abandon" rule (only a fixture-table row in `src/rules/battle/battle-card-effects-table.ts` before 4cdea6b55); the read-only Rust engine at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 parses `TriggerEvent::Abandon` and maps `Trigger::Abandonded`, but never fires it.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When you abandon a character (new), § Ability Types → Where triggered abilities work ("Abilities that trigger when a card leaves play … see that card as it was just before it left") and → When a character is dissolved (an ability that works in play sees its own card dissolved, abandons included), § Keywords and Effects → Abandon, § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities, § Spark; RD-hv-umm2.65-1, RD-hv-umm2.65-2, RD-hv-umm2.333-1, RD-hv-umm2.110-2
- Affects: 4ccb9bb6-e4db-48c3-a534-b0ab454f0f15 (Specter of Silent Snow, authored by hv-umm2.167), 0d867086-8413-40c3-820f-1a5de486577e (Fathomless Maw, base and amplified), 0637b453-4362-4f01-b3e6-07c27af9c847 (Pyreling, pending), 8624f9c7-dc92-4f4a-9c79-319b9c121b19 (Cinderheart, pending), a6866438-3632-408d-b464-c10f8a43a5c2 (Blight Weaver, pending), 16b579fe-c15b-4df6-8262-d45ce44732ae (Kragg avatar, pending)
- Why: rules.md said that abilities triggering on a card leaving play see it as it was just before it left, and that an ability working in play sees its own card dissolved, abandons included, but it did not say whether "When you abandon a character" sees its own card. The engine announced `abandoned` once the character was already in its owner's void, so a play-zone ability on the abandoned character was no longer working and Specter of Silent Snow ("▸Dawn: Abandon a character. When you abandon a character, draw a card and this character gains +1✦.") never saw itself abandoned. Abandoning is MTG's sacrifice adapted to Dreamtides, and sacrifice triggers look back, so "When you abandon a character" works like "When a character you control is dissolved": as the character leaves play the ability is armed if it works where its card is and its filter matches the character as it is in play, its own ability among them, with the abilities the card had in play (gained ones included, RD-hv-umm2.110-2); the `abandoned` event then fires exactly the armed abilities whose "you" is the abandoning player, for the side that controlled each ability's card then (RD-hv-umm2.333-1), the abandoned card ordered by the zone it went to. "This character" names its card only in play (interpreter `namesCharacter`; rules § Spark makes gained spark travel with a card but gives no rule for an effect gaining it outside play), so the Specter abandoning itself draws a card and gains no ✦. A reclaimed character abandoned is banished instead and a created one ceases to exist, but the engine still announces each as abandoned, so their look-back covers those destinations too. Characters abandoned together are abandoned one at a time, so one abandoned earlier is not armed for later ones, as with dissolves. Abilities that work only from the void are not armed for their own card's abandon, as with dissolves.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts` `armLookbacks` arms `abandon` abilities on a `leftPlay` to the void, the Banished zone, or nowhere (`LOOKBACK_DESTINATIONS`), evaluating the trigger filter against the character still in play; `matchKind` matches `abandon` only when armed and the event's side is the trigger's player; `departure` orders the abandoned card among the abandoning side's listeners; `matchListener` reads the departed card's own abilities from the origin recorded as it left. The step-local records are `StepContext.armedTriggers` (`dissolved`, `abandoned`), never battle state. Contracts: `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/abandon-lookback.contracts.ts`.
