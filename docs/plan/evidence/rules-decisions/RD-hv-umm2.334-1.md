# RD-hv-umm2.334-1: "Discard your hand" as a cost discards every eligible hand card without a choice, and an empty hand pays it
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: "Discard your hand" costs on the cards' own `mtgName`s, Diamond Lion and Bomat Courier, whose rulings allow activation with no cards in hand: a cost that names the whole hand is paid in full by discarding whatever it holds, including nothing), supported by 5 (the simplest reading consistent with both affected card texts)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Discard your hand
- Affects: 0bddeb19-ff49-4a77-bc06-955377ba3ed1 (Gilded Catalyst, base and amplified), 8e7b0eb9-4933-420b-abee-413bf5b3d2fc (Liminal Dreamer, base); the `discardHandCost()` DSL form (`{ cost: "discard", count: "hand" }`)
- Why: rules.md described chosen discard costs ("Discard a card", "Discard X cards") and, for effects, that a discard with fewer cards available discards the whole hand and does nothing with an empty hand, but said nothing about a cost naming the whole hand: whether the player chooses, whether an empty hand can pay it, and which cards count once other costs and targets have taken some. Step 1 has no decision on it. Step 2 has no prototype cost of this shape (Fortune's Wheel's whole-hand discard is a Dreamwell effect, not a cost). Step 3 does not decide it: read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 parses `Cost::DiscardHand` (`rules_engine/src/ability_data/src/cost.rs:20`, `rules_engine/src/parser/src/parser/cost_parser.rs:173`) but `battle_queries/src/battle_player_queries/costs.rs:8–11` implements only energy in `can_pay` and leaves every other cost `todo!`, and `docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md` is silent. Step 4 decides it: in MTG "Discard your hand" is one cost paid by discarding the hand's cards, chosen by nobody, and a player with an empty hand can still pay it, as the rulings on the cards' MTG sources state. Gilded Catalyst is Diamond Lion, which is designed to be activated after emptying the hand, so an empty-hand prohibition would contradict its own design; Liminal Dreamer draws a fixed number of cards per stored ⧗, which is meaningful whatever the hand held. The hand is read as the costs are chosen, before any is paid: like any discard cost it excludes the card being played and cards already chosen as targets or for earlier costs (rules § Costs, Requirements, and X), which keeps a revealed card in hand and never discards a card twice; a card an earlier cost of the same ability returns to hand was not in the hand when the costs were chosen, so it stays. A cost listed after it that needs a hand card has none, so it cannot be paid. Nothing becomes public beyond the discards themselves, which are shown as they enter the void as every discard is.
- Review provenance: the cold review of hv-umm2.61 (8cafea877, "support filtered discard payment values and complete hand choices") found that a free variable-count discard cannot represent mandatory whole-hand payment for these two cards.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/rules/costs.ts` `planCosts` fixes a whole-hand discard's cards with no prompt as the hand less the source, the excluded cards, and cards used by earlier costs; nested affordability drops hand cards from later costs' pools in the same list; `payCosts` discards exactly those cards through `discardCard`. Contracts: `src/engine/rules/costs-discard-hand.test.ts`.
