# RD-hv-umm2.490-1: An activated ability that moves its own card out of your void works only from that void, activated by the card's owner

- Ladder: 4 (MTG 113.6k: an ability whose cost or effect moves its own object out of a zone functions only in that zone; MTG 602.1 with 108.4: a card in a graveyard is controlled by its owner, who activates its abilities; MTG 602.5b/118.3: a cost that cannot be paid cannot start an activation; MTG 400.7 and 608.2b: the effect does nothing to an object that has changed zones), adapted to Dreamtides. C15 (decisions.md) is the normative anchor for "you win the game": it is checked "while the card is in play". The Rust engine lists activations only for characters on the battlefield (`can_activate_abilities.rs`), so step 3 is silent; rules.md § Ability Types already fixed the trigger order of cards outside play (void, in the order they were created for the battle) and that "this character from your void" names its card in your void as the ability resolves (§ When you play a card from your void).
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Where activated abilities work; § Objective (win conditions in other zones).
- Affects: ecf77f27-df0f-47c4-99c2-48d7fa46a137 (Revenant Automaton, "Abandon a character: Materialize this character from your void."), fd355778-abea-4513-844c-91bf75929ebe (Immortal Reaper, "2●, Banish a card from your void: Materialize this character from your void."). Both are authored by their composition beads.
- Why: rules.md said activated abilities belong to cards in play and was silent on these two cards, whose ability only makes sense from the void. Reading them as working only from the void, as MTG 113.6k does, keeps one ability from offering two meanings (it would do nothing in play). The owner activates because a void holds only its owner's cards, as with Reclaim and void play permissions. ☾ and "abandon this character" need a character in play, so they stay unpayable, as costs.ts already enforced. Battlefield Capacity forbids activating any ability that would put a character into play with a full back rank, and the rule names no exception for a cost that frees a position; keeping it uniform keeps legality independent of cost order. Listing void sources after characters in play, in creation order, reuses the fixed source order triggers already use. C15 says a "you win the game" ability works in play, so the victory check reads only emblems and characters in play, and a void card with a void activation never wins from there.
- Mechanics: `ActivatedAbility.zone?: ActivationZone` (`"play" | "void"`, absent meaning play), set by `activated(costs, effect, { zone: "void" })`. `abilitySources(state, catalog, side)` in `src/engine/rules/activation.ts` lists `inPlaySources` then the side's void cards with a void ability, by instance number; `canActivate` checks the ability's zone against its card's zone. `winConditionSources` in `src/engine/rules/victory.ts` reads `inPlaySources` only. The activate step, its stack item, origin snapshot and `abilityActivated` event are unchanged; the `engine.action` log record names the void source instance and ability index.
- Contracts: `activated-from-void.contracts.ts`.
