# RD-hv-7x4l.6-5: Triggered abilities choose on resolution, resolve without their source, and count once-per-turn when they trigger
- Ladder: 1 (D14: triggered abilities do not use the stack), then 4 (MTG 603.3d chooses targets when an ability goes on the stack, the analog of resolution here; MTG 113.7a for independence from the source; MTG 603.2h for "triggers only once each turn")
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Choices, Once per turn, Floating and delayed triggers, Disabled triggers
- Affects: general; every triggered ability with a target or mode (e4ab6a18-c3b2-4149-a446-c7c1e8d66714), "Once per turn, when …" abilities (a42fbd1b-7629-48c9-90da-e9a4246867d7, e2f542eb-090f-4a22-a42c-a120eb6caaa3), and delayed triggers (fb967cc1-4199-4a08-8070-724e07cebea5)
- Why: Triggered abilities never sit on the stack, so their targets and modes are prompts inside their own `resolveTrigger` step, and a required choice with no legal option makes that part do nothing (rules § Targeting); a modal ability with no choosable mode does nothing. A queued trigger captures its ability's definition when it triggers, as activated abilities do (RD-hv-7x4l.5-1), so it resolves even if its card has since moved. "Once per turn" limits triggering, so a once-per-turn ability that matches twice in one effect queues once; the count resets as each turn begins, extra turns included (C8). Disabling a character's triggers stops matching only, so an ability already waiting still resolves. A delayed trigger with no stated duration lasts until it triggers, and its controller is the controller of the effect that created it.
