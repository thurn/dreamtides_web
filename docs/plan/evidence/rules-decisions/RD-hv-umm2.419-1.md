# RD-hv-umm2.419-1: A card cost setter replaces the selected route's ordinary energy component

- Authority: current rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X → A card's own cost and RD-hv-umm2.101-1. Their stated outcome pays chosen alternative and optional energy on top of the set value; this clarification retains that outcome.
- Rule: normal hand plays, void play permissions and plain Reclaim have the card's copiable ordinary ● component. Explicit printed or granted Reclaim and Offering replace that component with 0. An "It costs N●" setter replaces that ordinary component with N, while selected route energy, X and additional costs remain in the total. Increases, reductions and the zero floor apply to that total. The latest applicable explicit Reclaim grant determines both route costs and its ordinary component; a plain grant preserves the applicable cost.
- Examples: a printed 2● card set to 0● with explicit Reclaim 0● and an accepted optional 1● payment costs 1●. With explicit Reclaim 5● it costs 5●. A permission to play the same card for normal costs uses the set ordinary component. With Offering and a set of 2●, an accepted optional 1● payment costs 3● and its hand banishment is paid.
- Implementation: one private explicit-Reclaim selection seam supplies route costs and route cost modifiers. The modifier uses a caller-provided ordinary energy component for replacement routes and copiable cost by default for display and normal routes. The actual route is chosen before computing payment and X affordability. No persisted route metadata or new state transition is needed.
- Evidence: public synthetic engine plays in modify-card-cost-routes.contracts.ts, including route alternatives, optional/X/composed payments, grants and fold cancellation/reload. The unchanged-source four-case regression and the retained prior hv-umm2.104 public probe establish the cause. Cost filters continue to use copiable characteristics, as covered by the existing modifyCardCost contracts.
- Ladder: existing normative outcome supplies the decision. The official MTG Comprehensive Rules effective 2026-09-25, CR 118.9d and 601.2f, support totaling alternative and additional payments with increases and reductions; they are an analogy for cost composition, not authority for Dreamtides' card-specific setter wording. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt (verified 2026-10-11). Prototype and unchanged-text Rust research supplies no card-setter rule deciding this component.
