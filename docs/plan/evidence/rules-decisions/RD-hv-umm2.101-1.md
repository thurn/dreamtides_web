# RD-hv-umm2.101-1: "It costs N●" sets the card's base energy in place of its ● cost, ahead of increases and reductions; the latest set applies
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: CR 601.2f totals a spell's cost from its mana cost or an alternative cost, then adds additional costs and increases, then subtracts reductions; a characteristic set by a later effect overrides an earlier one, as in layer timestamp order)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → A card's own cost
- Affects: 5aaf13de-25bc-467f-a4a0-fe2c4373cc9f (The Bondweaver), 7f550d4e-c6a1-4e5e-ae0c-1b3edd6f0f4a (Ethereal Courser); general for every "It costs N●" change
- Why: The set value defines the card's normal ● base before increases and reductions. Normal hand plays, play permissions and plain Reclaim use the latest applicable set; explicit Reclaim and Offering choose their replacement base. X and additional mandatory, optional and alternative-choice costs are paid on top of the chosen base. Every energy increase and reduction still applies, including an "It costs N● more" adjustment. For a normal route, the engine joins set-minus-copiable-cost to its increases or reductions; for an explicit replacement route it excludes own base setters. It floors the total at 0● once. A card's cost for conditions stays its copiable cost. The full replacement precedence follows RD-hv-umm2.422-1 and rules.md's Reclaim/Offering route descriptions.
