# RD-hv-umm2.65-1: A "when … is dissolved" ability sees the character as it last was in play, its own card and abandons included
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 603.10a: leaves-the-battlefield abilities look back in time, so "Whenever a creature you control dies" triggers on the creature's own death and reads its characteristics and controller as it last existed on the battlefield; Undead Augur and Midnight Reaper, the MTG analogs of Vigil Keeper and Watcher in the Ruins, trigger on their own deaths), then 5 for abandons (rules § Keywords and Effects → Abandon: an abandon fires ▸Dissolved, so it is a dissolve)

- Why: rules.md named ▸Dissolved, which concerns the ability's own card, and said "leaves play" abilities see the card as it was just before it left, but not what an ability watching other characters' dissolves sees. By the time a character is dissolved it is in its owner's void (or has ceased to exist), where it has lost the types static abilities gave it in play and is controlled by its owner. The ability therefore matches the character as it was just before it left play — its controller then, its types including any it had from effects in play, and whether it is a figment — and triggers only once the dissolve completes, so a banish, a return to hand, Veil turning the opponent's dissolve into losing Veil, or Reclaim turning it into a banish never trigger it. None of the texts says "another", so the ability's own card is one of the characters it watches; it sees its own dissolve because its ability worked in play as it left, while an ability that works only from the void never sees its own card leave play. An abandon fires ▸Dissolved (rules § Abandon), so it is a dissolve for these abilities too. "Non-figment" excludes figments and figment copies, the cards rules § Figments calls figments.
