# RD-hv-umm2.52-2: A card played by a granted Offering stays an Offering play if it loses the keyword while its costs are paid
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 601.2b and 601.2f–h: the alternative cost is chosen as the spell is announced, the total cost is locked in before payment, and a permission that disappears during payment does not undo the choice)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Offering
- Affects: 6cf545ba-b014-4333-a0da-573e8f97d734 (with any card whose additional cost can remove that character from play, such as "abandon a character")
- Why: with Offering granted by a character in play ("Cards in your hand have Offering"), a card whose additional cost abandons a character can abandon that very character while it is played by Offering, so the card loses the keyword part way through its payment. rules.md said only that the player chooses between Offering and the card's own costs as they play it. The engine already chooses the route before the commit point and pays every cost after it (engine-design § Costs), as MTG locks the total cost before payment; rules.md now states that the choice stands, so the card is played for 0● with its banish, gets the Offering status, and is banished during Ending, whatever happens to the keyword meanwhile.
