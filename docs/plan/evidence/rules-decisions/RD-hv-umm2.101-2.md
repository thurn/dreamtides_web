# RD-hv-umm2.101-2: "It" in "It costs N●" is the card the preceding instruction put into a hand; with no such card nothing changes
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with every affected card text), with rules.md § Zone Changes → Cards an effect moved and § Durations
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → A card's own cost
- Affects: 7f550d4e-c6a1-4e5e-ae0c-1b3edd6f0f4a (Ethereal Courser), dbd014b1-1ec8-4762-bd96-48a2a4824de2 (Repulsion Detail), 5aaf13de-25bc-467f-a4a0-fe2c4373cc9f (The Bondweaver)
- Why: every affected text pairs "It costs …" with an instruction that returns or draws exactly one card, so "it" names the card that instruction put into a hand as it resolved (`modifyCardCost` runs the nested instruction and changes the cards that entered a hand meanwhile). When the instruction moved nothing there (no legal target, a target that left play in response, Fatigue, a deck with no match, or a created card that ceased to exist instead), there is no card to change, and no effect starts. A card that reached a hand some other way, such as a target its controller returned in response, is not "it". Once made, the change follows rules § Durations: it lasts until its duration ends wherever the card is, and with no stated duration it is permanent, so Repulsion Detail's tax and The Bondweaver's 0● stay with the card through later zone changes, while Ethereal Courser's "this turn" ends in step 3 of the Ending phase.
