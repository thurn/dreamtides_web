# RD-hv-umm2.107-1: "When you materialize your second spirit animal in a turn" counts every materialization of a matching character under your control this turn, the one just announced included, one at a time, by type as it materialized
- Ladder: 4 (MTG "your second spell each turn" abilities count spells cast before the permanent with the ability was on the battlefield, and the triggering spell itself), then 5 for simultaneous entries (Dreamtides announces each materialization on its own, so they count one at a time in the order they enter). rules.md § Ability Types and § Keywords and Effects → Materialize and Rematerialize supply the normative play-count and entry rules. Step 2 is silent (no prototype behavior counts materializations); step 3 is silent (read-only Rust at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 declares `TriggerEvent::MaterializeNthThisTurn(Predicate, u32)` but its matcher is `todo!("Implement MaterializeNthThisTurn")` in `rules_engine/src/battle_queries/src/card_ability_queries/trigger_queries.rs`).
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Counting materializations
- Affects: cd2d4eaf-6295-451d-aaa2-ea74188bac3b (Paleclaw Panther), f38ab9b6-bea7-4c6e-b5db-2609796ba46b (Dawnprowler)
- Why: rules.md defined the nth count only for plays. A materialization count reads the same way: the matching characters you materialized this turn, the triggering one included, so the ability triggers once, for the one that brings the count to exactly N. "Materialize" covers every way into play and every rematerialization, so each counts each time, figments and figment copies included; a character that left play or ceased to exist still was materialized, and a count that is the turn's, not the card's, includes materializations from before the ability's card entered play (so Paleclaw Panther sees itself as the second). A character's type is read as it materialized, as a play's is as it was played: one with all character types then is a spirit animal, and a later change does not rewrite the count. "You" are the player who controls the character as it enters, whose turn-log record it joins; the opponent's materializations, responses on your turn included, count only toward theirs.
- Mechanics: the `Trigger` member `{ on: "materialize", subject: MaterializedSelector, nth: MaterializeNth }` (`src/engine/dsl/types.ts`), built by `whenMaterialize(selector, nth)` (`src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`), which accepts only a selector of controller, subtype and notSubtype, the fields a turn-log record answers, and a positive integer count. The matcher (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`) matches a `materialized` event whose character matches the subject, then counts `materializedThisTurn(state, event.side)` records matching the selector by recorded type (`allTypes` matching every subtype and never a `notSubtype`); `matchEvent` records the event before matching, so the count includes it. Paleclaw Panther authors `triggered(whenMaterialize(characterYouControl({ subtype: "Spirit Animal" }), 2), drawMatching({ subtype: "Spirit Animal" }))`.
