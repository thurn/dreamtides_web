# RD-hv-7x4l.9-3: An optional loop stops at the last point it reached, and a changed choice is made where it arises
- Ladder: 1 (D12; rules § Optional Loops lists when repeating stops early), then 5 for where play resumes
- rules.md: § Infinite Loops → Optional Loops
- Affects: no catalog card yet; any repeatable sequence
- Why: rules.md said repeating "stops early, returning control to the player" without saying from which point. Each repetition consists of the same actions and the same resolutions as the original sequence, and every point between two of them is a complete game position, so repeating keeps everything done up to the last such point. When the battle ends, it ends there. When the opponent gains a legal response, they respond there. When a choice now offers different options, the action or resolution that raised it is not repeated. If it was one of the player's actions, the player decides from the point before it, where they may take the same action and make the new choice. If it was a resolution, such as a triggered ability or the top of the stack, it resolves normally and its chooser makes the choice, as rules.md's "so the player makes it" says. Repeat ×N accepts any N from 1 to the 10,000-repetition limit, and the shortcut stays on offer after a completed Repeat ×N or after the limit, because the position is again the loop's equivalent position.
