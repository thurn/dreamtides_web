# RD-hv-umm2.93-1: "When you play a card from your void" triggers on every play from the void, by Reclaim or a play permission, as the play is announced, and a character's "return this character to play" ability works from the void without seeing its own card's play
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 601.2i: abilities that trigger when a spell is cast trigger once it becomes cast, with the spell on the stack; MTG 113.6k: an ability that can only work from a zone, as one that returns its own card from a graveyard, functions there), then 5 for counting every route of play from the void, the simplest reading consistent with the printed text. rules.md § Playing Cards and the Stack → Playing from the void, § Ability Types → "when you play" abilities and Where triggered abilities work, and § Keywords and Effects → Materialize, with RD-hv-umm2.91-1, supply the normative play, Reclaim-route and materialization rules. Read-only Rust at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 corroborates the route-independent stack timing: `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/play_cards/play_card.rs` `from_void` serves Reclaim-style and static `PlayFromVoid` plays and pushes `Trigger::PlayedCardFromVoid` with the card already on the stack.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When you play a card from your void, Where triggered abilities work
- Affects: 3a59cd3d-08a9-4a75-a5ab-c91b19d2d8c1 (Graywatch, base and amplified)
- Why: rules.md defined plays from the void and "When you reclaim …" but no trigger on any play from the void. Unlike "reclaim", "play a card from your void" names the zone, not the route, so Reclaim and a play permission both count, and a card both cover triggers it whichever way it is played. The play is the trigger, announced once the card is on the stack, as "when you play" and "when you reclaim" see it; a materialize or a copy is not a play. "Return this character to play" can only mean its own card in the void, so the ability works there; when Graywatch itself is played from the void it is on the stack and does not see its own play, as an in-play "When you reclaim a card" ability does not see its own reclaim. "This character" is read as the ability resolves: if the card has left the void first, the ability does nothing, and a full back rank leaves it in the void (§ Battlefield Capacity).
- Mechanics: the `Trigger` member `{ on: "play", player, filter, from: "void" }` (`src/engine/dsl/types.ts`; a play trigger with `from` counts no `nth`) with builder `whenPlayFromVoid(player, filter)` (`src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`). `moveToStack` (`src/engine/rules/zones.ts`) records `playedFromVoid: true` on the card's stack item when the card leaves a void for the stack, which only a play does; the matcher's `play` case (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`) then also requires that record on the stack item of the card the `cardPlayed` event announces, never on another card waiting on the stack. Graywatch authors `triggered(whenPlayFromVoid(), materialize(thisCardIn("void")), { zone: "void" })`.
