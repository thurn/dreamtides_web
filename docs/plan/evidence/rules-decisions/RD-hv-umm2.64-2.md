# RD-hv-umm2.64-2: "Shuffle N cards from your hand into your deck" chooses the cards as it resolves, and a shuffled-in card is shown only to players who could see it as it left
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Brainstorm chooses the cards to put back as it resolves, after its draws; a card put into a library from a public zone was seen by everyone, one from a hand only by its holder), then 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the text) for a short hand
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Shuffle into a deck
- Affects: 3725379c-676d-4efd-81ee-7e45d80db6d0 (base and amplified), 28f8d95a-be99-4b4c-b9ef-92a4b40fffe7
- Why: "Draw 3 cards, then shuffle 2 cards from your hand" can only be answered after the draws, so the hand cards are a resolution-time choice (a `chooseCards` prompt, role `shuffleInto`), not play-time targets. A hand holding fewer cards shuffles all of them, as for discard (§ Zones). Void cards ("up to 3 cards from your void") remain targets chosen as the card or ability is played, as every void reference is (RD-hv-umm2.59-1). For Frostbound Defiant, the targets are chosen before its abandon cost is paid, so it cannot shuffle itself. Hidden information: once the deck is shuffled, neither side can place any card in it. The `shuffledIntoDeck` event therefore cannot judge identification from the state it arrives with. It records the zone each card left and the sides that could identify it there, and names a void card to both sides but a hand card only to its holder (or to a side that had looked at that hand). A card the opponent owns goes into the opponent's deck, which is shuffled too (§ Zones → Hand).
