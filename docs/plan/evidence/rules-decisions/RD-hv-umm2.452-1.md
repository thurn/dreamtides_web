# RD-hv-umm2.452-1: A ✦ criterion on a card outside play compares the spark the card has in its zone; an event never meets one
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Recruiter of the Guard, the card's `mtgName`, searches by the toughness a library card has in that zone), adapted by rules.md § Spark and § Zone Changes, which keep gained spark and unexpired spark with a duration on a card across zones
- rules.md: § Spark → Spark outside play; § Keywords and Effects → Discover
- Affects: 5e1b943f-9fa2-4055-b613-5dd09a048c99 (Rift Pilgrim, base; it has no amplified text); the `CardFilter` `sparkAtMost` / `sparkAtLeast` bounds and every consumer of `matchesFilter`
- Why: rules.md defined a cost criterion outside play (the copiable cost, because cost changes apply only to playing a card) but no ✦ criterion. Spark differs from cost: rules.md says a card keeps the spark it gained, and spark with a duration, in every zone, and that effective spark is what rules read. In MTG a library search reads the characteristics the card has where it is. So a ✦ bound reads the layer evaluation's effective spark of the card in its current zone. Outside play that is the printed spark after variant and deck-entry modifications, plus gained spark and unexpired spark with a duration; static "have +N✦" abilities apply only in play. The same reading is what `CharacterSelector` ✦ bounds use for characters in play, so one ✦ means one thing everywhere. X in a printed ✦ is 0 until the card resolves with its X (`resolve-top` sets `status.x`), as X in a cost counts as 0 for cost criteria. An event has no spark (`null`), so it meets neither an upper nor a lower ✦ bound, matching "a ≤2✦ character" and the printed form's implied character. Bounds stay constants: no printed CardFilter text needs a computed ✦ bound.
- Played records: RD-hv-umm2.464-1 decides them: a "when you play" filter with a ✦ bound reads the spark the turn log recorded as the card was played. A static ability's ✦ bound on a card outside play reads its final spark (RD-hv-umm2.464-1).
- Mechanics: `src/engine/continuous/characteristics.ts` `FilterSubject` carries `spark` from `filterSubjectOf`'s full layer evaluation; `matchesFilter` fails a ✦ bound on `null` spark; `matchesEveryCard` treats a ✦ bound like a cost bound; `playedMatchesFilter` reads the record's spark. Discover's `matchingDeckCards`, hand and void costs, permissions, cost modifiers, keyword grants, take-from-among, zone counts and draw-matching all match through it.
- Contracts: `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/card-filter-spark.contracts.ts`.
