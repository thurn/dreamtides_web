# RD-hv-umm2.243-1: Rematerializing a void-play subject follows ordinary trigger timing

- Ladder: not invoked; existing normative rules settle the interaction.
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Rematerialize; § Ability Types → When you play a card from your void; § Ability Types → Trigger timing and order.
- Affects: d1fdbe21-56f6-43c0-aaac-1e4683964da5.
- Why: A play from the void announces the card on the stack. Its triggered abilities resolve before any player receives priority, while the played character is on the stack. Rematerialize repeats the materialization of a character in play. Bell therefore triggers for a qualifying play and resolves without rematerializing its off-play subject. The played character subsequently enters play through normal stack resolution. A play from hand, an event play, and a card put into play without being played do not satisfy Bell’s trigger.

The authored ability preserves this timing, the character-only filter, and the triggering physical identity. It does not create a delayed trigger or move the subject from the stack into play. The immutable printed text and its ineffective ordinary interaction are recorded in the corresponding card issue.
