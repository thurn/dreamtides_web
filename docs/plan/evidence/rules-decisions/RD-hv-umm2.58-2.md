# RD-hv-umm2.58-2: A played card's mode is chosen as it is played, even when an instruction precedes "Choose one:"
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 700.2a and 601.2b: a modal spell's modes are chosen as it is cast, before targets, and its instructions are then followed in printed order as it resolves), consistent with rules.md § Ability Types → Choices, which chooses only triggered abilities' modes as they resolve, and with § Playing Cards and the Stack → Copies on the stack, where a copy's modes are chosen as it is created
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Modal abilities
- Affects: 01d53e49-7833-4122-a970-b72c73ad2c28
- Why: rules.md defined "Choose one:" but not when a played card's mode is chosen, and Crimson Pilgrimage puts "Look at the top 4 cards of your deck." before its "Choose one:". The engine chooses every modal node's mode with the play-time choices (`chooseOne`, engine-design § Ability DSL), where legality by search checks each mode's continuation; a mode chosen mid-resolution would make the same words mean two timings. So the mode is chosen as the card is played, before it goes on the stack, and the look happens as it resolves, followed by the chosen mode's draw. A triggered ability still chooses its mode as it resolves. The wording mismatch is logged as a card issue (`docs/plan/evidence/card-issues/01d53e49-7833-4122-a970-b72c73ad2c28--hv-umm2.58.md`).
