# RD-hv-umm2.84-1: "Cards you reclaim are not banished" removes the reclaimed status from its controller's own cards, events on the stack included
- Ladder: 1 (rules.md § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim: such abilities "remove the reclaimed status", the status that also banishes a reclaimed event as it resolves), confirmed by 3 (`~/dreamtides` battle_rules.md: a reclaimed card is banished as it "would later leave the stack or battlefield")
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim; § Zone Changes (replacement 2)
- Affects: 9adbe3db-d872-4b75-92b5-d7f2798c22ce (Clockwork Conductor), 738a5af0-f848-4d48-bceb-9a43c9b11066 (Seeker of Lost Paths)
- Why: rules.md uses "leave play" for the reclaimed replacement itself, and that replacement banishes events leaving the stack, so the ability that undoes it covers the same moves: a reclaimed event resolves or is Prevented into the void (or the Prevent's destination), and a reclaimed character goes to the void when dissolved, firing ▸Dissolved, or wherever an effect sends it. The printed "Cards" (not "characters") agrees. "You" is the card's owner: only a card's owner can play it from the void by Reclaim, so the owner is the player who reclaimed it, and the ability covers those cards whoever controls them now and never the opposing player's. A card that leaves normally is not reclaimed afterwards, so it is not banished later from a void, hand, or deck or when played again normally; how it was played (`playedViaReclaim`) is unchanged.
