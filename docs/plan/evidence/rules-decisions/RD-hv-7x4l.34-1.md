# RD-hv-7x4l.34-1: "When you play" filters and counts read a card's effective types as it was played
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: "whenever you cast a [quality] spell" checks the spell's characteristics, with every characteristic-changing effect applied, as it becomes cast; a later change does not revisit a spell already cast, and counts of spells cast this turn count each spell as it was cast)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Triggered abilities
- Affects: general; every "when you play" or "when the opponent plays" ability with a type or subtype filter, including 7f550d4e-c6a1-4e5e-ae0c-1b3edd6f0f4a, 90a6a7a0-b739-475e-8379-647741aab423, 886c9d49-b25f-4ddd-97f0-2ec4b42eda89, e2f542eb-090f-4a22-a42c-a120eb6caaa3, 3fab0780-59d4-45f1-8aac-a870ad8729ac, 7ecc1da1-dc39-4596-9a1d-262a261ffeea, fb41a90e-279c-419c-80fc-7c3469ba0c74 (Warrior and Spirit Animal play triggers)
- Why: § Card Types makes a card that has all character types count as every subtype a card refers to, and every other card filter reads effective characteristics. "When you play" abilities fire as the card is played and "your second X in a turn" counts earlier plays, so the type that matters is the one the card had at that moment. A "has all character types" effect with a duration travels with the card across zones, so a card can carry one into its play. The turn log records the effective type, subtype, and "has all character types" of each play as it is played; filters and counts match those records, so a type change that ends later in the turn leaves earlier plays counted as they were.
