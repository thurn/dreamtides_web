# RD-hv-umm2.113-1: "Reclaim – costs" plays the card from the void for exactly those costs, in place of its ● cost
- Ladder: 3 (`~/dreamtides` Rust engine: `build_named_abilities` turns `NamedAbility::ReclaimForCost(cost)` into a play-from-void ability with an energy cost of 0● and the printed cost as its additional cost, while plain `Reclaim` and `Reclaim N●` keep an energy cost); the Rust rules text for Reclaim, like rules.md before this decision, names only plain Reclaim and Reclaim N●
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: 3995100d-cdde-4c6a-9bf9-87167c8deb57, cd7ff671-2317-4a54-b9da-acfefb90e6b0; and the earlier fbf32510-0bad-4567-859f-b3db6d7be5d5, which already plays this way
- Why: rules.md defined plain Reclaim (the normal ● cost) and Reclaim N● (N● in place of it) but not the dash form "Reclaim – <costs>", which follows the – keyword marker with a full cost list. Read as an addition to the printed cost, "Reclaim – 2●, Abandon two characters" would make an 8● character cost 10● and two abandons from the void, and the separately printed 2● would serve no purpose; read as a replacement, the printed list is the whole price, exactly as "Reclaim N●" is. The engine's `reclaim(...costs)` ability already pays its costs in place of the printed cost (`src/engine/rules/timing.ts`), so the decision is encoded once there: Immolate and Rise reclaims for one abandon and no energy, The Rising God for 2● and two abandons. Additional costs the card prints elsewhere still apply on top, as for any play route.
