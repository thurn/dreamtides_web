# RD-hv-7x4l.6-4: Duration boundaries: next turn before its Dreamwell, the next Day of either player, late end-of-turn effects, and absent sources
- Ladder: 1 (C8: extra turns are that player's turns for "until your next turn" and "this turn"; C7 for "until the opponent pays"), then 4 (MTG 611.2b: an effect lasting "for as long as" a condition holds that is already false when it would begin does nothing; MTG 514.2 for the late end-of-turn case)
- rules.md: § Durations; § Spark
- Affects: 7be2e6d7-abff-4c44-a0c3-35460da1693c (amplified "until your next turn"), 1bae969b-0bc4-4777-86f3-1e860d601c5c, 70716dd8-1133-4966-b01b-757ea627874c ("until this character leaves play", "while this character is in play"), the "banish until the next Day phase" variant in rules § Keywords and Effects, and every "this turn" effect
- Why: rules.md named the durations but not their exact boundaries. "Until your next turn" ends as the controller's next turn begins, before its Dreamwell phase, so nothing in that turn sees the effect; an extra turn of that player counts (C8) and the opponent's extra turns do not. "Until the next Day phase" ends as the next Day phase begins, whichever player's turn it is, which is the literal reading. An "until end of turn" effect that begins during the Ending phase after step 3 (for example from a hand-limit discard trigger) ends as the turn ends instead of lasting a further turn. A "while this is in play" effect whose source is not in play when it would begin does not happen; an avatar or dreamsign is always in play. Every boundary applies wherever the changed cards are, as § Spark already requires for spark gained with a duration.
