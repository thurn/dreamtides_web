# RD-hv-umm2.318-1: A card's cost for cost conditions is its copiable cost
- Ladder: 1 (C4: a cost check uses the card's cost after its variant, transfigurations and D39 deck mods; C13: a figment's cost is 0● for cost selectors)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Cost changes; § Keywords and Effects → Discover
- Affects: 331e2846-85d1-4667-8c64-dc6c21a418ed, 20fd609a-7529-4582-a362-b8151b0ae011, 0e1209c3-1041-4079-9b1c-f96816a7deb5, 470bbf9d-a73c-4e00-bc66-cb0fa767c8de, 00cc7e7f-4245-4447-b9dc-f647dc6241a2, 47081bde-f35d-4b5c-ba17-53e5dbf5b419, and every other card whose text compares a card's cost ("≤2● cost character", "with cost X●")
- Why: rules.md gave two readings. § Cost changes called a card's cost for conditions its printed cost, while § Discover compared the copiable cost after variant and deck-entry modifications. C4 decides it: the cost a condition reads is the card's cost after its variant and deck mods, with X counting as 0, as every cost selector and Discover already read it (`copiableCostOf`). Cost changes ("Events cost you 1● more") change only what a player pays, so they never affect it. § Cost changes states the rule once, and § Discover refers to it.
