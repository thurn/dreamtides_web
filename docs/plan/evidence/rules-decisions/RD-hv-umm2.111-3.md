# RD-hv-umm2.111-3: "This character gains those abilities" gains the abilities the enemy had as it lost them: its card's or figment type's, then the gains it still had
- Ladder: 4 (MTG: a token's abilities are abilities like any card's, and an effect that grants the abilities another object has copies them as they are when it resolves, independent of that object afterwards, as 611.2c fixes the objects a resolving continuous effect affects), adapted through rules.md § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities (a gained card's printed abilities, none of the changes other effects made to it)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Losing abilities; § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities; RD-hv-umm2.110-1, RD-hv-umm2.110-2, RD-hv-umm2.110-3
- Affects: 890970bd-475f-4da4-b835-2fb75882a84d
- Why: "Those abilities" are the ones the enemy loses. Read as it loses them, they are its own (its card's for its variant, or its figment type's, since figments have abilities such as Awakened, Vengeful, or a static spark ability) and those of each card or figment type it had gained and not already lost, in its ability-list order. Each becomes an `abilities` change on the source, one gained group each, so the gained group of a figment is `{ figment }` beside the existing `{ cardId, variant }`. Keyword grants and losses other effects made to the enemy are changes, not abilities of its card or of a card it gained, as rules § Gaining abilities already says of a gained card, so they are not taken. The gains are definitions, not links: they last while the source is in play (RD-hv-umm2.110-2), whatever then happens to the enemy (its loss ending, its own gains ending, its leaving play). The enemy loses its abilities whether or not the source is still in play as the ability resolves; the source gains only while it is in play (RD-hv-umm2.110-3), and only from a character whose loss began. The target is chosen as the triggered ability resolves, as for every triggered ability; with no enemy, nothing happens.
