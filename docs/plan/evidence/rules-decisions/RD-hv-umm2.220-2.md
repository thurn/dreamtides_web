# RD-hv-umm2.220-2: Tensho copies the last matching actual play's copiable values

- Ladder: 1 (C5 and RD-hv-7x4l.1-11 settle the copied values; current rules settle actual play history).
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Last current-turn references; § Figments → Figment Copies; RD-hv-7x4l.1-11.
- Affects: bf72adff-7d74-4be8-9b93-1db7ba13a1db.
- Decision: Select the latest actual character play matching Warrior as played, including captured all-types, during the current active turn for the effect's controller. Use its immutable copiable Printing/Variant even after cessation. A prevented committed play qualifies; a stack copy, unplayed materialization or cancelled play does not. With no matching play, no copy is produced. Ordinary capacity and C5 zero-spark construction apply.
- Why: C5 copies subtype, abilities, cost and base spark after variant transforms, excluding gained layers, spark, counters and statuses. `CardInstance.printing` and `variant` are immutable, so recording this pair at play preserves live copying semantics while retaining the values after the physical card ceases. Current rules already preserve effective played type and subtype. The Rust same-UUID text instead describes a once-per-turn play trigger making a 1-spark copy (`client/Assets/StreamingAssets/Tabula/dreamcallers.toml:494`, SHA-256 `0f9eaf2ee2dd8bccc2d126204dc420aa520e3e04916d9aaa3d6ae30e85a6c86b`); it is changed text, not a D10.3 precedent. The [current official MTG Comprehensive Rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf), 608.2h–i, offer supporting information/history analogues; stable Dreamtides physical identity and C5 remain authoritative.
- Legacy saves: A record lacking the optional pair can use the same live physical card. A ceased card with unknown copiable history supplies no copy, and no earlier play is substituted. Views retain qualified unknown legacy history without identifying a hidden sampled card.
