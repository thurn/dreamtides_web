# RD-hv-7x4l.8-2: Every "banish … until" returns the card like "until end of turn", at its own boundary
- Ladder: 1 (F3 for "until end of turn"), then 5 for the other variants rules.md names
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Banish
- Affects: 9954cede-8a16-4053-b6e9-da745f4540f5, 7be2e6d7-abff-4c44-a0c3-35460da1693c (and its amplified "until your next turn")
- Why: rules.md names "banish until the banishing card leaves play" and "banish until the next Day phase" besides "until end of turn", and amplified text adds "until your next turn", but only F3's variant had a return rule. Each variant returns exactly as F3 does — to the leftmost open back-rank position of the player who controlled the card when it was banished, as a materialize, staying banished if that back rank is full — at the boundary rules § Durations gives its duration: as the banishing card leaves play, as the next Day phase begins, or as that player's next turn begins. "Until this leaves play" whose source is already gone when it would begin does nothing, as § Durations says for "while this is in play", so the card is not banished at all. A created card or figment ceases to exist when banished and never returns, and a reclaimed card never leaves the Banished zone.
