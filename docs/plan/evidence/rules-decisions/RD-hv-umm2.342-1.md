Includes base and amplified named ▸Dissolved abilities and their figment copies.
- Why: The named ability triggers from wherever its card went, but that location does not determine the ability's controller. The character's last in-play controller controls it, with the source ordered by destination among that side's abilities. A catalog card goes to its owner's void and is controlled by its owner there. A dissolved figment or other created character triggers for its last controller before ceasing to exist. Veil and Reclaim replacements that prevent a dissolve trigger no ▸Dissolved ability. Descriptive "when … is dissolved" abilities retain the armed-listener rule of RD-hv-umm2.333-1; ordinary functional-zone abilities use the current source controller.
