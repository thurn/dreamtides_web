# RD-hv-umm2.471-1: Optional payment selects one target restriction

- Ladder: not invoked (normative rules settle the clause)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Optional additional costs; § Targeting → Targets are chosen before costs are paid, Payment-dependent restrictions, Bounds that count; § Playing Cards and the Stack → Copies on the stack
- Affects: 11b4ae1d-ae2a-41a6-9cdc-70c6d69803d6 (base and amplified), 8a971ba7-9dfb-45be-bfbb-bf5e4f11974a (base and amplified)
- Why: Each printed clause names one enemy and says the expanded restriction applies instead when the optional cost is paid. Choosing a payable optional cost prepares a decision and its physical payment cards; target selection precedes every debit and zone move. The retained decision determines the restriction when resolving and when choosing a copy's targets. A declined cost remains declined even if resources are available later; a copy retains actual paid costs without paying them again. The target's current type, controller, bounds, and targetability are checked normally on resolution. Physical payment cards and targets remain distinct.
