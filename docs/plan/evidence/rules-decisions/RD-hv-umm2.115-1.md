# RD-hv-umm2.115-1: "You may X to Y" in a resolving effect is offered only when X can be paid in full, and Y happens only if X is paid
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: "you may [cost]. If you do, [effect]" makes the effect depend on the cost being paid, and CR 118.3: a player can't pay a cost without the resources to pay it fully); steps 1–3 are silent: no D-, C-, or P-entry covers it, the prototype implements no resolution-time "to" cost (`docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md` lists only "then" sequences), and the `~/dreamtides` Rust engine's `trigger_cost` is unimplemented (`todo!("Implement trigger cost effects")` in `apply_effect.rs`)
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Costs while resolving
- Affects: 5f4bc2cf-4464-409f-acd5-fe4d6df28a05 (base and amplified); the same wording appears on the later 88994286-96db-4ba6-a660-b30ae9b52cff, c11eb833-a45b-4983-949d-848039d0504c, and fcb830e5-85ff-48f3-be1e-46e102a5c8e8
- Why: rules.md defined optional costs paid as a card is played ("You may X to play this event") but not a cost named inside a resolving effect. Read as "You may discard a card, then draw a card", an empty hand would still draw, and the discard would no longer be a price; read as a cost, the draw is what the discard buys, exactly as the play-time optional cost is. The content composes it from existing primitives with no engine change: `ifThen(conditionCardCounts("hand", N), optional(sequence(discard(N), Y)))` offers the `youMay` confirm only when the hand holds the N cards, and on a yes pays the discard and then resolves Y.
