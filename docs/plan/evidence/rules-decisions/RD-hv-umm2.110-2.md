# RD-hv-umm2.110-2: A character keeps the abilities it gained while it stays in play and loses them as it leaves play
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 400.7 and 611.2c: an object that leaves the battlefield becomes a new object, so an undated "gains" effect lasts until then), adapted through rules.md § Ability Types (a character's triggered and static abilities work while its card is in play)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities
- Affects: e72245f2-c153-4e79-ba8e-956d18202b48; general for every "gains the abilities of" effect
- Why: rules.md § Durations makes an undated effect permanent and § Zone Changes keeps card identity across zones, which settle spark and statuses but not abilities granted to "this character": a character is a card in play, and a gained ability carried into a hand, deck, or void would make ability kinds that work there (Reclaim, additional costs, void triggers) depend on gains no player sees once the card is hidden. MTG ends such a grant as the permanent leaves the battlefield, and that adapts cleanly: the gain is a floating `abilities` change expiring as its character leaves play (`sourceLeavesPlay`), so the existing expiry, view, and redaction machinery ends and hides it. Gaining control is not a zone change and keeps the gains. Because the expiry happens as the card departs, after `leftPlay` is announced, a gained "When this character leaves play" ability sees the character as it was just before it left. The dissolve itself is announced once the card has left (rules § Ability Types: ▸Dissolved triggers from the zone it went to, and the trigger matcher reads every ability that triggers on a card's own dissolve from the card as it is then), so no gained ability triggers on the character's own dissolve, ▸Dissolved or "when a character you control is dissolved"; a created character loses its gains as it departs too, before its dissolve is announced. MTG would let such abilities look back; matching that would mean reading armed dissolve abilities from the origin captured as the card left (src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts `matchListener`), which this bead leaves to the matcher's owner. Gained "when … is dissolved" abilities still trigger for other characters while the character is in play. Activated abilities on the stack and queued triggers snapshot their origin with its gained list, so they still resolve after the character leaves.
