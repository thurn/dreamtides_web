# RD-hv-umm2.110-2: A character keeps the abilities it gained while it stays in play and loses them as it leaves play
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 400.7 and 611.2c: an object that leaves the battlefield becomes a new object, so an undated "gains" effect lasts until then), adapted through rules.md § Ability Types (a character's triggered and static abilities work while its card is in play)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities
- Affects: e72245f2-c153-4e79-ba8e-956d18202b48; general for every "gains the abilities of" effect
- Why: rules.md § Durations makes an undated effect permanent and § Zone Changes keeps card identity across zones, which settle spark and statuses but not abilities granted to "this character": a character is a card in play, and a gained ability carried into a hand, deck, or void would make ability kinds that work there (Reclaim, additional costs, void triggers) depend on gains no player sees once the card is hidden. MTG ends such a grant as the permanent leaves the battlefield, and that adapts cleanly: the gain is a floating `abilities` change expiring as its character leaves play (`sourceLeavesPlay`), so the existing expiry, view, and redaction machinery ends and hides it. Gaining control is not a zone change and keeps the gains. Because the expiry happens as the card departs, after `leftPlay` is announced, a gained "When this character leaves play" ability sees the character as it was just before it left. Its gained abilities that trigger on its own dissolve look back the same way (MTG 603.10a; rules.md § When a character is dissolved: an ability that worked where its card was as the character left play sees its own card dissolved, and one that triggers when a card leaves play sees it as it was just before it left). As the character leaves play for a void or for nowhere, the trigger matcher records the origin its abilities came from then, its gained list included, beside the dissolve abilities it arms (src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts `armDissolve`); the `dissolved` event reads the dissolved card's abilities from that origin, so a gained ▸Dissolved ability and an armed gained "When a character you control is dissolved" both trigger and resolve from it, though the gains ended as it left (hv-umm2.450). A gained ability that works only from a void was not working as the character left play, so it is not armed and does not see its own character dissolved. Gained "when … is dissolved" abilities still trigger for other characters while the character is in play. Activated abilities on the stack and queued triggers snapshot their origin with its gained list, so they still resolve after the character leaves.
