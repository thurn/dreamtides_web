# RD-hv-umm2.72-1: An equivalent scoring quantity uses the points awarded by that challenge

- Ladder: 5, the simplest reading consistent with § Objective and
  § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring. C14 and RD-hv-7x4l.1-17 floor loss
  at 0; RD-hv-7x4l.6-3 limits scoring triggers to positive character
  challenge awards.
- rules.md: § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring.
- Affects: `7697da0e-d759-4c75-8c9c-477e9058b035`.
- Why: "an equivalent quantity" refers to the points awarded by the
  triggering challenge. An unpaired 3✦ challenger awards 3⍟; a 3✦
  challenger winning against a 2✦ blocker awards 1⍟. The triggered loss
  therefore requests 3⍟ or 1⍟ respectively, subject to C14. The quantity is
  captured at scoring, so a change to spark before the ability resolves
  does not change it. The opponent is relative to the triggered ability's
  controller at the time it triggered.
