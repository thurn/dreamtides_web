# RD-hv-7x4l.1-2: Triggered abilities queue until the current effect finishes and resolve FIFO in a fixed order
- Ladder: 1 (D14 trigger timing and order)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Trigger timing and order; § Playing Cards and the Stack → "Triggered abilities cannot be responded to"
- Affects: general
- Why: rules.md said triggered abilities "resolve immediately when their trigger condition is met", which contradicts D14's queue (a trigger during an effect waits until the effect finishes, then resolves before priority passes). Both sentences are replaced with D14: FIFO by triggering event, new triggers appended, simultaneous triggers ordered active player first and avatar → dreamsigns → characters B0→B9 then F0→F8, no ordering prompts. Cards in other zones follow each player's characters in void, hand, deck order, as engine-design § Triggers specifies; this bead places them within each player's block because D14's "active player's first" governs the whole order.
