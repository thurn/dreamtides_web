# RD-hv-umm2.495-1: Archive of the Forgotten picks X before its void targets, bounds them by copiable cost against X as they are offered and as it resolves, may be played for X = 0, and a copy chooses its targets with the original's X

- Ladder: 1 (D15: X carries over to a copy), 4 for target bounds checked at choice and resolution as established by RD-hv-umm2.76-1 (MTG 608.2b), and 5 for meaningful X = 0, the simplest reading consistent with the printed text. rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X, § Targeting → Bounds that count and § Playing Cards and the Stack → Copies on the stack, with RD-hv-umm2.142-1 and RD-hv-umm2.86-2, supply the normative X, cost and copy rules; no D-, C-, or P-entry names this card.
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X (X costs); § Playing Cards and the Stack (Copies on the stack).
- Affects: 69552516-14bc-42b9-b4b6-3a0cdc29686b (Archive of the Forgotten), and every later entity whose void target is bounded by X.
- Why: "Choose up to two ≤X● cost events in your void to gain reclaim 0●" uses its paid X only as the copiable-cost bound of its void targets. As for Annular Recall, the play step picks X first, so the target prompt offers only the events in your void whose copiable cost (printed cost after variant and deck-entry modifications, an X in it counting as 0) is at most the picked X, up to two, none required. Like a character selector's bound, it is checked again as the effect resolves: a chosen event whose copiable cost is then above X, or that left the void, gains nothing, and a part with no legal target left reports `noLegalTarget`. Giving 0● events Reclaim 0● is a meaningful effect, so X = 0 is legal (`xMinimum: 0`). A copy uses the original's X (rules § Copies on the stack), so its new void or character targets are offered against that X even though the copy's own stack item is created after its choices. Encoded as `grantReclaim(fromVoid({ cardType: "event" }, { count: 2, upTo: true, costAtMost: x() }), { costs: [energy(0)] })` (`ZoneTargetSpec.costAtMost`, `voidTargetMatching` in `src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts`; the copy's X reaches `chooseForCopy` from `copyOnStack`), pinned by `void-target-x-bound.contracts.ts`.
