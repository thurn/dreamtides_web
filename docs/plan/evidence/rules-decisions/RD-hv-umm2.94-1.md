# RD-hv-umm2.94-1: "Store N⧗" stores nothing on a card that is not in play as it resolves
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: an ability that puts counters on its source does nothing once that object has left the battlefield, CR 400.7 and 113.7a)
- rules.md: § Counters
- Affects: 33874387-2490-48ed-93bc-a47a16e399ad (base and amplified), 47b1d6b7-0388-4bb9-abfc-ed832d902e9f, f4082c01-fdc8-4583-afdb-76bac980b3bf, and every later "store N⧗" card
- Why: rules.md says counters are local to a card and reset to 0 when it leaves play, but not what a store does to a card that has already left. Storing on a card in a void, hand, or the Banished zone would either be erased later or carry counters back into play, contradicting the reset. Soulbinder's "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗." may abandon itself, and an activated ability resolves after its source left play (rules § Activated abilities), so the case is reachable from printed text and from an opponent's response.
- Outcome: `storeCounters` (`src/engine/effects/primitives/store-counters.ts`) adds N⧗ and emits `countersChanged` with the new total only while the source is in play; otherwise it changes nothing and emits nothing. An emblem holding it is a definition error, since emblems store no counters (`rules/costs.ts`). Spending ⧗ is unchanged: it is a cost paid as the ability is activated, so an ability whose ⧗ were paid still resolves after its source leaves play.
