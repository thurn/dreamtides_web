# RD-hv-umm2.502-1: "You may put two of those cards into your hand" puts none or exactly two, or every matching card when fewer were moved
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: "you may" governs the whole instruction, and "up to N" is the wording that allows fewer than N; an instruction that cannot be performed in full does as much as possible, CR 609.3), supported by rules.md's own fewer-than-N readings
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Erode
- Affects: 650b2ed4-f016-46c0-815a-f53fffe08a9a (Part the Veil, "Erode 4. You may put two of those cards into your hand."); the `takeFromAmong(among, { count })` option
- Why: rules.md described a take of one card only. Steps 1–3 are silent: decisions.md has no entry on numbered takes; the prototype implements no such card; the Rust engine at `~/dreamtides` 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 leaves every optional effect unimplemented (`rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/effects/apply_effect.rs:240`, `todo!("Implement optional effects")`). In MTG, "you may" is a single yes-or-no choice about performing the instruction as written, and card text that allows any smaller number says "up to N" — Dreamtides keeps that distinction ("Banish up to two cards", "Shuffle up to 3 cards"). So "You may put two" puts none or exactly two, never one. When fewer matching cards were moved than the number, the instruction does as much as it can and puts all of them, as rules.md already reads "draw K of the cards" after looking at fewer than K ("draw all of them") and "Shuffle 2 cards from your hand" with fewer cards ("shuffle all of them"). A mandatory numbered take reads the same without the yes-or-no choice.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/effects/primitives/take-from-among.ts`. With `count` N > 1 and `optional`, the controller first answers a `confirm` with role `youMay`; on Yes, a `chooseCards` prompt (`chooseAmong`, or `chooseFromOpponentHand` privately) takes min = max = min(N, matching cards), which answers itself when every matching card must be taken. Without `optional` only the `chooseCards` prompt is raised, with the same bounds. A count of one keeps the single prompt (min 0 when optional, else 1; max 1), so existing takes are unchanged. No matching card raises no prompt.
