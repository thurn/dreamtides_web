# RD-hv-umm2.59-2: References to cards in a deck, hand, or void name cards without targeting or revealing them
- Ladder: 1 for "your" zones (RD-hv-umm2.52-1 reads "your hand" as the hand you hold and "your deck/void" as the ones you own for statics; the same reading applies to resolving effects), then 5 (simplest reading consistent with every affected text) for the top-of-deck count, the self reference, and hidden reads
- rules.md: § Zones → References to cards in a zone
- Affects: 0d848dc8-a1a2-410b-b19a-b5f65375e141, 50267984-eaa3-415e-a23f-581c54b0ad2d, f46490a5-7030-4ade-93e9-bedb5813f590 ("cards in your void", "your hand and void"); 1521c06c-f5ac-4d29-a6bd-7030b63a3c3e, 28c3ef90-c0dc-4b85-92c8-40758b99e0ce ("the top card of your deck"); 1ce0f0f2-4b1a-483d-b93b-74c36e946a08, af739e3d-bc61-4fab-bf81-a22e78d7e524 ("this character from/in your void"); and every other entity the content inventory routes to zoneCardRefs
- Why: rules.md defined the zones but not what an effect naming cards in them covers. "Cards in your void" and "your hand" name every matching card there, chosen by nobody, in zone order. "The top card of your deck" names that card only when it matches the text's filter, so "if the top card is a ≤2● character" and "you may play characters from the top of your deck" read the same way; a shorter deck names the cards it has, and an empty deck names none and causes no Fatigue, since nothing is drawn. "This character from your void" names its own card only while it is in its controller's void, so it does nothing while the card is on the stack, in play, or elsewhere. Every reference reads current characteristics, as the static `grantCardKeyword` filter does. A reference is not a look: naming a card in a hidden hand or deck reveals nothing and emits no event; only what the effect then does with the card can show it, through that primitive's own events and knowledge rules.
