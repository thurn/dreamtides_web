# RD-hv-7x4l.17-3: "Until your next turn" ends before anything triggers at the start of that turn, and start-of-turn abilities resolve before its Dreamwell phase
- Ladder: 4 (MTG: an effect lasting "until your next turn" ends as that turn begins, before its untap step and before anything triggers "at the beginning of your upkeep"), then 1 (D14: triggered abilities resolve right after the event that triggered them, before the game moves on)
- rules.md: § Durations; § Ability Types → Triggered abilities
- Affects: 7be2e6d7-abff-4c44-a0c3-35460da1693c (amplified "until your next turn"), 5931e388-1856-4e5a-85f8-a5aa34f867b1 and avatar c72cfd7b-408b-47f6-adf1-1e486a7e20d3 ("At the start of your turn"), and every floating trigger lasting "until your next turn"
- Why: RD-hv-7x4l.6-4 ends "until your next turn" as the controller's turn begins and rules.md triggers "at the start of your turn" abilities then too, but neither said which comes first, nor whether those abilities resolve before the Dreamwell phase begins. The effect ends first, so a floating "until your next turn, at the start of your turn" trigger never fires at its own boundary, as "until" excludes the boundary itself. The abilities that triggered as the turn began then resolve before the Dreamwell phase begins, so nothing in that phase (the ● refill or the Dreamwell draw) happens before them, as the rules text "before its Dreamwell phase" says. The engine announces the turn, and while triggers wait the turn is beginning: its Dreamwell phase begins in the next automatic step once the queue is empty.
