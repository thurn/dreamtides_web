# RD-hv-umm2.514-1: A resolving top-card condition reads one card without revealing it
- Ladder: not invoked; the normative zone-reference reading in RD-hv-umm2.59-2 settles the top-only, current-characteristic and empty-deck clauses.
- rules.md: § Zones → References to cards in a zone; RD-hv-umm2.59-2.
- Affects: 1521c06c-f5ac-4d29-a6bd-7030b63a3c3e; resolving top-card conditions and their empty-deck-safe draw composition.
- Why: A deck's first card is the sole subject. The condition is false when the deck is empty and compares effective type, subtype and spark, plus copiable cost and speed. Checking the condition emits nothing and changes no knowledge. A subsequent materialization or draw uses its ordinary observable events and knowledge rules. The public `topCardMatches(filter, player)` builder is distinct from the `topOfDeck(count, filter, player)` zone reference. `ifThen(topCardMatches(filter), materialize(topOfDeck(1, filter)), ifThen(topCardMatches(), draw(1)))` draws the otherwise-branch top card only when one exists, so the empty branch calls no draw and causes no Fatigue. The new condition is excluded from `StaticCondition`, whose continuous conditions use public inputs. Existing generic triggered, target-restriction, suppression and victory condition consumers retain their own timing and observable gameplay consequences; this form's intended content use is a resolving `ifThen`. The original Prism Caller content bead owns its production ability and full content acceptance.
