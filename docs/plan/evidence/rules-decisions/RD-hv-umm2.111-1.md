# RD-hv-umm2.111-1: "Loses all abilities" removes every ability the character has as of the loss's timestamp; abilities gained later survive it
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 613.1f puts ability removal in layer 6 with ability grants, ordered by timestamp under 613.7: a "loses all abilities" effect removes the printed abilities and those granted earlier, and a grant with a later timestamp survives it), adapted through rules.md § Ability Types (the order of continuous effects)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Losing abilities; § Ability Types; RD-hv-7x4l.7-2 (ties: static abilities first, then floating records in creation order); RD-hv-umm2.110-1 (gained abilities follow the character's own)
- Affects: 9e9efbc0-d438-48a9-9551-85c93fb33f3e (base, amplified), 890970bd-475f-4da4-b835-2fb75882a84d; general for every "loses all abilities" effect
- Why: decisions.md has no entry for losing abilities, and neither the prototype nor the Rust engine implements one. MTG's layer 6 adapts directly to layer 3 here, where keyword grants and losses and gained abilities already apply in timestamp order. The loss is a floating layer-3 `loseAbilities` change: in the layer evaluation it clears every keyword the character has at its timestamp, printed, granted (floating or static), or from a gained card, so a later keyword grant, a later gain, or a static grant whose source entered its zone after the loss adds its keywords again. Its non-keyword abilities stop through the instance's ability list: rules/floating.ts mirrors the loss onto `status.lostAbilities` (the count of gained groups that began before the latest loss), and `originAbilities` leaves the own and earlier-gained positions as holes, so the trigger matcher, activation and legality, static collection (which bypasses the per-printing statics memo for such an instance), victory, discover, and determinization see none of them, and the indexes of every ability, which once-per-turn records, stack items, and queued triggers name, never change as a loss begins or ends. Floating records are created in clock order, so creation order is timestamp order and the lost gains are always a prefix of the gained list. Several changes can share one clock value, so a static ability the character gained orders, on a tie, just after the floating record that gained it rather than with the static abilities that tie before floating records: a loss and a later gain at the same clock (a queued hijack resolving after its own source lost its abilities) leave the gained static keyword in place. A figment loses its type's abilities the same way. Spark, character type, and statuses are not abilities and are kept.
