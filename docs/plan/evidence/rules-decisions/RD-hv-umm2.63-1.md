# RD-hv-umm2.63-1: Cards put on top or on the bottom of a deck together keep their zone order, without a prompt, and stay known to whoever saw them go there
- Ladder: 4 (MTG CR 401.4 lets the owner order cards put into one position of a library at once; Endurance, Augur Crow's analog, orders them randomly), adapted by 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the text)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Put on top or on the bottom of a deck
- Affects: ffa4fa56-8897-465d-b812-3798e3cf5a1f
- Why: "Put all cards in a player's void on the bottom of their deck" names no order, and Augur Crow does not say "in a random order" as Endurance does. Arranging a whole void (often ten or more cards) on the bottom of a deck is a long prompt whose answer rarely matters, while a random order would hide public cards for no reason the text gives. The cards therefore keep the order of the zone they leave, the first nearest the top; a void lists its most recent card first, so the card that has been in the void longest ends up deepest. Each card goes to its owner's deck through the ordinary zone change (a created card ceases to exist, a reclaimed card is banished). The deck is not shuffled, so a card stays known to every player who saw it go there (both for a void or play, the looker for a looked-at card) until the deck is shuffled; the `cardsPlaced` event names each card only to those players. "A player's void" is chosen as the trigger resolves (§ Playing Cards and the Stack → Choosing a player), and "their deck" is that player's.
