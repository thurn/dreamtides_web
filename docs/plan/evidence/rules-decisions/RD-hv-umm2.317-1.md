# RD-hv-umm2.317-1: Looking at the opponent's hand shows the looker every card in it, whatever restriction limits the card it may take
- Ladder: 2 (prototype behavior: Ember Cavern eae99eb2-0fa8-4d12-b7b2-3f5387cb6d3a shows every card in the opponent's hand to its controller alone; `docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md`), with 4 (MTG analog: "look at target opponent's hand … you may choose a nonland card from it" shows the whole hand, and the restriction limits only the choice)
- rules.md: § Zones → Hand
- Affects: eae700c9-5005-4627-b8d6-a5c2c273e61a
- Why: "Look at the opponent's hand" names the whole hand as what is looked at; a restriction such as "a ≤2● cost character from it" limits which card may be taken, not which cards are seen. So the looker learns every card in the hand as the effect resolves, before choosing, including when the restriction leaves one card, none (no choice is asked), or all of them; the opponent learns nothing from the look. The knowledge is the private look's (RD-hv-umm2.56-2): it lasts while each card stays in that hand. A chosen discard from the opponent's hand without "look at" (§ Zones → Hand → Chosen discard) still shows only the matching cards. Mind's Door, the one current card, has no restriction, so its behavior is unchanged.
