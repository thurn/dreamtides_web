# RD-hv-7x4l.6-1: Triggers match as each event happens; each player's block ends with other zones, then floating and delayed triggers
- Ladder: 1 (D14 trigger timing and order), then 5 (engine-design § Triggers orders other zones by instance ID)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Trigger timing and order; § Ability Types → Where triggered abilities work
- Affects: general; every card whose ability works from the void, hand, or deck (4edf2d8d-61e4-4c3a-a388-4b52b2ebd005, 831bc7cf-e8c9-4943-8156-91b7397ef849), every ▸Dissolved and "leaves play" ability (e4ab6a18-c3b2-4149-a446-c7c1e8d66714), and every floating or delayed trigger (d24ea640-638d-4ce2-b15d-b3f4588bda6b, c7c95f67-a838-4e39-879c-cfbf0591540c)
- Why: RD-hv-7x4l.1-2 placed cards in other zones inside each player's block, after that player's characters, and flagged the placement for confirmation. D14's "the active player's first" governs the whole order, so the placement stands: the active player's avatar, dreamsigns, characters B0→B9 then F0→F8, void, hand, and deck, then the opponent's block in the same order. Within a zone, cards are ordered by when they were created for the battle (the engine's instance number), as engine-design specifies, so the order never depends on how a zone happens to be arranged. Floating and delayed triggers belong to no card; each sits at the end of its controller's block in creation order. Events are matched against abilities the moment they happen, and the matches only wait in the queue, so a ▸Dissolved ability sees its own dissolve, "leaves play" and "leaves your void" abilities see the card as it last was, and a card that has just left play is ordered by the zone it went to. Nothing triggers before the first turn begins (the opening deal) or after the battle ends.
