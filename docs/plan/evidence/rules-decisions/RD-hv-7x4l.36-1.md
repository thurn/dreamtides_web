# RD-hv-7x4l.36-1: The cards played and drawn this turn are not part of an optional loop's equivalent position
- Ladder: 1 (D12: offer the shortcut on "an equivalent state with a monotonic gain"; engine-design § Loops lists turn counters among the quantities a loop signature abstracts), then 5 for what a card that counts them does to an offer
- rules.md: § Infinite Loops → Optional Loops
- Affects: no catalog card yet (every catalog entry is pending); any "your second card in a turn" or "nth draw" trigger, and any effect that counts the cards played this turn, such as "When you play a card, gain 1⍟ for each other card you've played this turn"
- Why: the turn log records every card each player plays and how many they draw, and it only grows until the turn ends. Hashing it into the loop signature would make every sequence that plays or draws a card unrepeatable, since no later position in the window could equal an earlier one: the loop of a character that returns itself to hand, and the loops that "for each card you've played this turn" cards are built for, would never be offered, against D12. Once-per-turn uses are hashed instead because they fill up: each ability is used once, so hashing them delays an offer by at most one pass rather than preventing it. A capped count of the turn log would also fill up, but no single cap fits: the threshold that matters is the largest count any trigger in the battle names, granted abilities included, and "for each" effects name none. So the turn log stays out of the comparison and counts neither as a gain nor as a loss, as RD-hv-7x4l.9-1 treats void sizes. The cost is offer quality, never correctness: a card that counts the turn log can trigger in the sequence on offer and not in a repetition, or the reverse. Every repetition replays the real rules and checks each automatic step against the recording, so that repetition stops where it first differs (`diverged`), keeping everything before that point, and the player's next pass of the sequence is offered as it now repeats. rules.md names the quantity as the cards each player has played and drawn this turn, and lists that stop with the others.
