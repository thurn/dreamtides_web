# RD-hv-umm2.61-1: Discard costs capture their count or copiable card cost

- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the printed clauses and the normative rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X and § Keywords and Effects → Discover rules).
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X; § Keywords and Effects → Discover.
- Affects: 94818862-2875-4ee1-8f72-7c0cdc624202, 435ed83c-f7a6-4a73-a684-67fcc4e49bc4, 4f0f0811-1a4c-4d42-a804-77dba8707792.

A filtered discard cost reads the card's current type and subtype while it is held in the payer's hand. A cost used by Discover is the discarded card's copiable cost before payment, including variant transforms and deck-entry reductions and counting an X energy part as zero. This is the same comparison value the Discover rule uses. Lumineth adds exactly one to that captured value; Curio Dealer uses exactly that value and pays its separate 1●. Later characteristics and zone changes do not change the captured value.

Abyssal Plunge's X is a discard count, independent of energy: paying its printed 1● and discarding zero cards draws one card, or two when amplified. Zero has a meaningful effect under the contextual X rule. Selecting the payment cards determines X; the chosen cards are distinct instances, and the played event cannot pay its own cost. The effect uses the captured count even if the payment cards later leave the void.

One ability has one X binding. If an energy-X cost and a discard-count or captured-cost binding occur together, every binding agrees; affordability search withholds incompatible choices. A value already captured by an earlier cost sets later variable discard counts and optional-cost affordability. This composition rule supports the existing declarative cost model without changing the affected cards' printed text.

The final top-level noncapturing discard in an official play or activation accepts every field-legal selection once its fixed resources are payable. Its proof preserves raw prompt fingerprints, cancellation and replay; a following guarded precommit prompt fails explicitly. Earlier, nested, manually planned and captured-cost choices use affordability search. Fixed and filtered discard builders share the canonical filtered representation. Ability definitions supply these costs through their origins and indexes.
