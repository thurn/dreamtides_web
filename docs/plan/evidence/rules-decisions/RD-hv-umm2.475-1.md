# RD-hv-umm2.475-1: "Banish this card" moves the resolving event from the stack to its owner's Banished zone as that instruction resolves, and it then skips the void
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Time Spiral's "Exile Time Spiral" and the Beacon cycle's "Shuffle CARDNAME into its owner's library" move the spell as that instruction resolves; CR 608.2n puts an instant or sorcery into the graveyard only as the final part of its resolution, which a spell that has already left the stack never reaches; Wheel of the Heavens' `mtgName` is Timetwister, Time Spiral's ancestor). Steps 1–3 are silent: no D-, C- or P-entry names a self-banishing event, the prototype played this card text-less, and the read-only Rust engine at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 has only `BanishWhenLeavesPlay`, its Reclaim mark, while `docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md:47,79,100` say only that events go to the void after resolving.
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Stack resolution; § Keywords and Effects → Banish; § Ability Types → Event abilities
- Affects: 25d00336-5ad7-433b-8ced-71720a9f074a (Wheel of the Heavens, base; it has no amplified text); the `banishSelfOnResolve` primitive and the resolved-event destination in `resolveTop`
- Why: rules.md sent every resolved event to the void and said nothing of an event that names itself. Reading "Banish this card" as an ordinary instruction in printed order is the simplest reading and the MTG one: Wheel banishes itself after "each player … draws 5 cards", and since an event resolving is on the stack, never in the void, its own "shuffle their hand and void into their deck" cannot move it. Having left the stack, the event is not then put into its void as it finishes resolving. Paths that never reach the instruction keep their existing rules: a Prevented event never resolves and goes where the Prevent sends it (the void by default); a copy on the stack ceases to exist instead of being banished (§ Created Cards); a reclaimed event is banished either way, announced once, and stays reclaimed (§ Reclaim). The instruction moves nothing when its source is no longer on the stack.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/effects/primitives/banish-self-on-resolve.ts` banishes `env.source` through `rules/zones.ts` `banish` (announced by a public `banished` event) when that instance is on the stack; `src/engine/steps/kinds/resolve-top.ts` moves a resolved event to the void only while it is still on the stack. Contracts: `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/banish-self-on-resolve.contracts.ts`.
