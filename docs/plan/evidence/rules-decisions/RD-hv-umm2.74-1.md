# RD-hv-umm2.74-1: "Banish a chosen card from the opponent's hand" is chosen like a chosen discard and is not a discard
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with every affected text), following rules.md § Zones → Chosen discard, which already defines "a chosen card from the opponent's hand" for discard; the `~/dreamtides` Rust engine has no banish-from-hand effect to consult
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Banish; § Zones → Chosen discard
- Affects: 23a7aca4-3c64-4963-a9a0-d3638822df48, and every other entity whose text banishes "a chosen <card> from the opponent's hand"
- Why: rules.md defined banishing cards in play and the chosen discard, but not when a banished hand card is chosen, what its chooser sees, or whether it counts as a discard. The hand is hidden, so the card cannot be a play-time target; the wording matches the chosen discard, so the controller chooses as the effect resolves, in a prompt private to them that shows only the cards matching the restriction (the `banishChosen` role), and a hand with no matching card makes that part do nothing. The card leaves its owner's hand for the Banished zone, never a void, so it fires no "When you discard a card" abilities. The Banished zone is public, so both players see the card as it enters it. Banishing every card of a hand, deck, or void ("Banish your hand and void") and void cards chosen as targets ("Banish a card from the opponent's void", RD-hv-umm2.59-1) need no decision beyond the zone references (RD-hv-umm2.59-2); rules.md § Banish names them too.
