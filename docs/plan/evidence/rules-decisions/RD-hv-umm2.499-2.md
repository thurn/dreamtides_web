# RD-hv-umm2.499-2: "Draw a card for each character returned" counts the returned characters that reached a hand, whoever owns them

- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the printed text) for counting an opponent-owned character that went to its owner's hand. The ladder is not invoked for the replaced moves: rules.md § Keywords and Effects → When you return a character to hand already settles that a created character that ceases to exist and a reclaimed character that is banished "are not returned", and § Zone Changes orders the replacements. Step 1 is silent (no D-, C-, or P-entry names this card); steps 2 and 3 are silent (no prototype or Rust implementation of the effect).
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Return to hand (an opponent-owned character you control goes to the opponent's hand); § Keywords and Effects → When you return a character to hand; § Zone Changes; § Created Cards.
- Affects: f7ffe999-685d-4ea2-85de-2e567eb2a042 (Key to the Moment, authored by composition bead hv-umm2.156), and every later entity whose draw counts the characters its own return moved.
- Why: The text counts "each character returned", not "returned to your hand", so a character the opponent owns that you controlled counts once it reaches the opponent's hand, the ordinary destination of a return. A character a zone replacement sent elsewhere was not returned: a created character ceases to exist instead and a reclaimed character is banished instead, so neither draws. A reclaimed character whose owner's "Cards you reclaim are not banished when they leave play" applies as it leaves returns normally and counts. The count is neither the size of the cohort, nor the number of characters on the board as the card was played, nor the change in hand size, nor the generic moved-card binding, which also covers ceased and replaced moves. The controller draws after every return, once per counted character, through the ordinary draw (fatigue included).
- Mechanics: `returnAllButOne` counts a cohort member when its instance is in a hand right after its own `returnToHand` zone change, then calls `drawCard` that many times for its controller when `drawForEach` is set.
- Contracts: `return-all-but-one.contracts.ts`.
