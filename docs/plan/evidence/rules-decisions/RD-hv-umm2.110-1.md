# RD-hv-umm2.110-1: "Gains the abilities of that character" gains every printed ability of that card for its variant, after the character's own, each one an ability of the character
- Ladder: 5 (rules.md § Ability Types lists the ability kinds a card prints; decisions.md names no clarification for this card; the Rust engine and the prototype have no such effect)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Gaining abilities
- Affects: e72245f2-c153-4e79-ba8e-956d18202b48; general for every "gains the abilities of" effect
- Why: "The abilities of that character" reads most simply as everything its card prints, for the variant it was in the void (an amplified card's amplified text), keywords included, and not the changes other effects made to it, which are not abilities. The gained abilities follow the character's own (rules/activation.ts `originAbilities`), so the indexes of its printed abilities, which once-per-turn records, stack items, and queued triggers name, never change. Abilities that work only outside play (Reclaim, additional costs) are gained but inert in play, so no ability kind needs an exception. A gained activated ability is activated from the character and paid by it, ☾ exhausting the character, as any ability of the character is. A gained "Once per turn" ability is a separate ability of the character, tracked by its own position, and its use record is dropped when the gained abilities end (rules/floating.ts), so a later gain in the same turn starts unused. Gained keywords apply in layer 3 at the gain's timestamp, so "where effects conflict, the one applied last wins" holds between a gain and a keyword change.
