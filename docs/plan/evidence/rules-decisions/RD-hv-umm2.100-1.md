# RD-hv-umm2.100-1: Current-turn occurrence counts and current-zone physical membership
- Ladder: 5 for the count-versus-membership and predeparture-controller interpretation (simplest reading consistent with the original clauses); not invoked for existing discard/play/materialization and extra-turn rules.
- rules.md: § Current-turn occurrences and physical membership; § Zones (When you discard / Discarding one or more cards); § Ability Types (played type / Counting materializations); § Reclaim; § Abandon; § Turn Structure.
- Affects: 72debe2e-1f47-4a66-83c3-71bb72876663; f7c68226-def4-4eb0-bdfe-b028482e1351; 2a230602-a39b-4ec2-a467-97b92b389aff. The named current-turn readers also support the admitted 606 consumers after this source is certified.
- Why: "For each character you controlled that dissolved" counts each successful occurrence under the predeparture controller; "all characters in your void which dissolved" instead names unique physical current-zone members. Enemy-owned controlled characters therefore count for the departing controller while residing afterwards in their owner's void. Copies/cancelled plays follow existing play rules; replaced/prevented dissolves follow existing zone rules. Abandoned reclaimed cards count abandonment and departure, with no dissolution. Silent figment merging is not an announced departure. Successful ordinary and created departures use the same seam without altering zone order or triggers.

The abandonment reader therefore requires both the selected current-zone holder and that selected player's actual abandonment record, whereas dissolution membership accepts either predeparture controller. Those declarations provide no behavior precedent for the new distinction, so the ruling uses step 5. No MTG analog is needed.
