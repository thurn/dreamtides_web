# RD-hv-umm2.109-1: "Choose a card in the opponent's hand" is a private choice among the whole hand; both players see the chosen card, and a restriction whose duration is already over still makes the choice
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: Kitesail Freebooter's ruling that its controller still looks at the hand and chooses when the source has left the battlefield, but nothing is exiled; CR 611.2b for a "for as long as" effect whose condition is already false), with rules.md § Zones → Chosen discard as the in-rules model for a private choice from the opponent's hand
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Play restrictions; § Zones → Chosen discard; § Durations
- Affects: 70716dd8-1133-4966-b01b-757ea627874c; general for every "choose a card in the opponent's hand" without a discard or banish
- Why: rules.md defined private choices from the opponent's hand only for discards and banishes. This choice has no restriction, so every card in that hand is shown privately to the chooser (the engine's `privateTo` prompt, role `restrictChosen`, which keeps the chooser knowing them while they stay there, as for a chosen discard). The holder sees the restricted card because it is in their hand, and the chooser because they chose it; no other card is revealed to anyone. An empty hand gives nothing to choose, so the effect does nothing. The choice is the first instruction and the restriction the second, so, following the MTG ruling and RD-hv-7x4l.6-4, a "while this character is in play" restriction whose source has left play before it resolves still chooses and restricts nothing.
