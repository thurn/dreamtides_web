# RD-hv-7x4l.45-1: A chosen target that cannot be targeted by effects when the card or ability resolves is no longer a legal target
- Ladder: 1 (C17: "cannot be targeted by effects" covers every effect, its controller's included), then 4 (MTG 608.2b: as a spell or ability resolves, each target is checked again and an illegal target is not affected; Spellskite, Giver of Runes and Sylvan Safekeeper, these cards' MTG analogs, grant protection or hexproof in response to a targeted spell)
- rules.md: § Targeting
- Affects: 516dbd2d-dae4-4873-937e-adfeeee4444d, 2708c635-9332-4cfb-b43d-889ba2e329b6, bd43b120-2503-406c-8b72-ea3dc55198a0 (each also in amplified text)
- Why: rules § Targeting said a card that cannot be targeted by effects cannot be chosen as a target, and that a required choice with no legal option when the card resolves does nothing, but not what happens to a target chosen before the card gained the keyword. All three cards grant it "this turn" through an activated ability, which is useful mainly in response to an opponent's targeted card on the stack; a reading that kept an already-chosen target would make that response do nothing. A chosen target that cannot be targeted as the card or ability resolves is therefore no longer a legal target: it is not affected, other legal targets of the same choice still are, and a part of the effect left with no legal target does nothing while the rest still happens, as RD-hv-7x4l.8-4 describes for illegal targets. The engine already applies this (hv-7x4l.40): `resolveCharacters` and `resolveStackTargets` (`src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts`) skip a chosen target that is no longer targetable and report noLegalTarget when none remain.
