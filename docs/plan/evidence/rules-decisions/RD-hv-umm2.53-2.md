# RD-hv-umm2.53-2: A static ability working from a hand or a deck does not reveal its card
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 400.2 and 113.6b: a hand and a library are hidden zones, and an ability that functions from one applies without revealing its card unless its text says to reveal it)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Static abilities ("Where static abilities work")
- Affects: every card whose static ability works from a hand or a deck ("hand" or "any"), such as the self-cost cards 6497d8b1-85b8-486d-99e2-5c141486d508, 8bf53fe6-8a25-401e-9a99-84343a026bb0, bbdc6ee7-e9b1-488b-90b9-19337b65fee6, and f7c68226-def4-4eb0-bdfe-b028482e1351 as their beads author them
- Why: a static ability working from a hidden zone changes what the opponent can see (a character's spark, a card's cost as it is played), and rules.md did not say whether the card must be shown. No card text asks for a reveal, and the MTG analog applies such abilities without one. The engine evaluates static abilities live and records nothing about them, so no event or view names their card: the opponent's view shows the changed characteristics and the hidden zone's count only, and determinization (D22) samples the hidden card from the view like any other. The opponent may still infer the card from what changed.
