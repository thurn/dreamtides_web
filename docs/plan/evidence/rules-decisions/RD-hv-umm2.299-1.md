# RD-hv-umm2.299-1: Foresee's look is private to the foreseeing player; the opponent sees how many cards were looked at, how many went back on top, and which went to the void
- Ladder: 2 (prototype behavior: the Foresee prompt revealed the top cards to the controller only, `REVEAL_DECK_TOP` then the `FORESEE` edit, recorded in `docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md` for Skypath and Ringwatcher), then 5 for what the opponent sees
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Foresee
- Affects: 647f5150-b2e0-424b-9480-27557642524e; every card, avatar, and Dreamwell card whose text says "Foresee", among them 2162742c-09d0-4e62-ae49-0f8f79b45adc and f9b479cf-02cb-40e1-bb64-70b29977bf15
- Why: rules.md said "look at the top N cards of your deck, reorder them in any order, and optionally send any of them to the void" without saying who sees what. The prototype showed the looked-at cards to the controller only, and "look at" reads as private wherever rules.md uses it (Discover). The cards sent to the void enter a public zone, so the opponent identifies them there, and the opponent can count how many cards left the deck and how many stayed, so the simplest reading consistent with every Foresee card is that both of those are public while the identity and order of the cards put back on top stay private to the foreseeing player. The engine encodes this in the public `foresaw` event, which lists the cards kept on top and the cards voided and names each one only to a side that can identify it (`events/kinds/foresaw.ts`).
