# RD-hv-umm2.56-2: Looking at the opponent's hand is private to the looker, who keeps knowing those cards while they stay there; the opponent sees which card is taken
- Ladder: 2 (prototype behavior: Ember Cavern eae99eb2-0fa8-4d12-b7b2-3f5387cb6d3a reveals every card currently in the opponent's hand to its controller alone, later draws are not revealed, and the reveal never expires; `docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md`), then 5 for the take
- rules.md: § Zones → Hand
- Affects: eae700c9-5005-4627-b8d6-a5c2c273e61a
- Why: "Look at the opponent's hand" names no reveal to both players, unlike the `reveal` effect; the inventory's suggested `reveal` would publish the hand to both sides and log it as the opponent revealing it. The looker alone sees the cards (a prompt private to the looker, which teaches it every card it offers) and keeps knowing each one while it stays in that hand, as the prototype's hand visibility did, but not the cards drawn later. The opponent already knows its own hand, so it sees which card left it and keeps knowing that card in the looker's hand; the take is public (`cardTaken`). "Put a card from it" is mandatory: with a card in the hand the looker must take one, a lone card is taken without asking, and an empty hand gives nothing to choose. Ownership follows § Zones → Hand: the card stays the opponent's and goes to its owner's zones when it leaves the looker's hand. A created card ceases to exist instead of moving to a hand (§ Zone Changes), so it is not taken.
