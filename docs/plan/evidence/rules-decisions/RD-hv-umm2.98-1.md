# RD-hv-umm2.98-1: "Prevent a played card unless the opponent abandons a non-figment character" asks the played card's controller to accept or decline, then to choose
- Ladder: 2 (prototype Dreamwell rule for accepting an abandonment, then choosing it), supported by 3 (Rust "unless the opponent pays" asks the effect controller's opponent and prevents without a prompt when the cost cannot be paid) and 4 (MTG Mana Leak, the card's `mtgName`: "unless its controller pays")
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Prevent; § Keywords and Effects → Abandon
- Affects: 795a7b54-40d5-42f9-bc3a-9e92bc938c2d (Cragfall, base; it has no amplified text); the `prevent(selector, { unlessAbandons })` extension
- Why: rules.md named the "unless the opponent pays" form only, without saying who decides, when, or what a player without the cost does. "The opponent" is the opponent of the Prevent's controller, and in this conditional form "a played card" is a card that opponent played, as engine-design's worked "Prevent a played event unless the opponent pays 2●" encodes it (`stackItem({ controller: "opponent" })`): the player who could save the card is always the one who played it, which also matches MTG's "its controller". The decision is made as the Prevent resolves and is public. Accepting comes first and the character second, the shape the prototype used for its one "you may abandon a character to …" rule effect, so the declining answer never exposes a list of characters, the policy accepts or declines an "unless" cost the same way for energy and characters, and the follow-up choice reuses the resolution-time `abandon` choice of RD-hv-umm2.75-1: a non-targeting choice among the matching characters that player controls, so `cannotBeTargeted` does not exclude one, and figments never match "non-figment". With no matching character the player cannot pay and the card is prevented without a prompt, as the Rust engine prevents directly when the opponent cannot pay. A card that left the stack first leaves the Prevent with no legal target (`noLegalTarget`), so nobody is asked.
- Prototype evidence: `docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md`, The Bastion (`20be0fdd-d691-40a9-b4f8-15689ea7ebaa`) at `8b1d9e824`: a confirm (`confirm-abandon-and-draw`) then, on Yes, a pick prompt (`choose-character-to-abandon`) over the chooser's characters in both ranks.
- Reference evidence: read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12, `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/effects/counterspell_unless_pays_cost.rs:18–55` asks `source.controller().opponent()` with a pay-or-decline choice when `costs::can_pay` holds and otherwise counters immediately; `docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md:271–273` is otherwise silent on conditional Prevents.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/effects/primitives/prevent.ts` raises a `confirm` prompt with role `preventUnlessAbandons` for `opponent(controller)`, then a `chooseCards` prompt with role `abandon` (min 1, max 1) over `matchingCharacters` of the predicate relative to that player, and abandons the chosen instance through the guarded `abandonCharacter` rule; declining prevents through `preventCard`.
