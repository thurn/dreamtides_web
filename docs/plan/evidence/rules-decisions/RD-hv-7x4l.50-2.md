# RD-hv-7x4l.50-2: A triggered ability is seen to trigger without showing the hidden card it concerns
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: a triggered ability is announced as it is put on the stack, but a "whenever you draw a card" trigger does not reveal the card drawn)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → Triggered abilities
- Affects: 7e2f2ac0-4e3c-4520-80c6-c830581b40de (Dark Lord's Champion), 9a18d375-1b6d-4df4-95ec-c7c5c3f36379 (Pattern Seeker), c86c1364-6ac4-4c90-8053-4e49441a2c83 (Key Sifter); any "when you draw" or "when you discard" ability on a card the opponent can see
- Why: rules.md did not say what a player learns from a triggered ability. A player sees an ability trigger and resolve when they can see its card, avatar, or dreamsign, matching the view's trigger queue. The card the triggering event concerns (its subject) is shown only to a player who could identify it as the ability triggered and still can. A card drawn into a hidden hand and then discarded into the void in the same effect is public in the void, but naming it as the draw trigger's subject would tell the opponent which card was the one drawn. The engine records the sides that could not identify the subject as the ability triggered (`QueuedTrigger.subjectHiddenFrom`). The view and the `triggerQueued` event show those sides a `null` subject (`seesSubject`, `eventSeenBy`).
