# RD-hv-umm2.80-1: Materialize the discovered physical character after completing Discover

- Ladder: 5 for the pronoun binding; existing normative rules establish the underlying zone, targeting and capacity behavior. Steps 2 and 3 provide supporting Discover and non-targeting precedent.
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Materialize and Discover; § The Play Area → Battlefield Capacity; § Targeting → Cards in a void.
- Affects: ae90a3e1-c650-4f21-83af-de3208151b11, 3e83cbe2-2b21-4804-9906-4b4735db9869, f10a9ba9-f5eb-4011-9426-a89c5de3b550 (base; these entities have no amplified text).

Discover puts the chosen physical character into the controller's hand and shuffles the deck. The following materialization uses exactly that character, excluding other characters already held or drawn by an earlier part of the effect. With no discovery there is no materialization. If capacity is exhausted after the source entered play or while a resolving effect runs, the discovered character stays in hand. It is not played, spends no energy and fires materialization triggers rather than play triggers.

The current rules define automatic leftmost back-rank placement, exhausted entry unless Awakened, and partial output in source-zone order. The void choice follows RD-hv-umm2.59-1 and the current § Targeting rule: targets are chosen before costs and checked again as the effect resolves. A character entering from another player's void keeps its owner and enters under the effect controller. Existing materialization events and capacity events announce these results.

The actual prototype Discover rule is `src/rules/battle/dreamwell-effects-table.ts` `discoverResolution` (lines 130–157): move the chosen card into hand, then reorder the remaining deck. It supplies the ordinary Discover intermediate zone and shuffle, but implements no Discover-then-materialize combination. Prototype sandbox edits supply no additional rule.
