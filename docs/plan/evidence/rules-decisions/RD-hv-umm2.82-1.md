# RD-hv-umm2.82-1: "When you discard this card" works from the void and triggers on every discard of the card from its owner's hand
- Ladder: 1 for which discards count (RD-hv-umm2.116-1 and rules.md § Zones → Hand "When you discard": every discard from your hand, whatever caused it), then 3 (read-only `~/dreamtides` at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12: `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/effects/discard_cards.rs:36–37` moves the card from hand to void and only then pushes `Trigger::Discarded(VoidCardId)`, and `rules_engine/src/ability_data/src/predicate.rs:11–19` names the "this card"/"it" predicates the trigger matches against the owning card; the Rust engine registers no void-zone listeners, and `docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md` is silent), agreeing with 4 (MTG 603.10a: a discard is not among the look-back triggers, so the ability is checked after the event, with the card in its new zone, and must function there)
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When you discard this card
- Affects: 9d14885c-a757-40cc-b43e-87dbec7ec379 (base and amplified), 4fe9f53e-149a-4b9f-8fac-f75560c986f2, 8f69312f-4bec-4671-99da-e752ec6d4cbc; the `whenDiscardSelf()` trigger and every later entity that uses it ("When you discard [or erode] this card")
- Why: rules.md defined "When you discard a card" but not a card's trigger on its own discard. The discard is announced once the card is in the void, so the ability is matched there and works from the void, not from the hand it left. Which discards count follows RD-hv-umm2.116-1: the player whose hand the card leaves discards it, whether their own effect, the opponent's effect (a chosen discard included), a cost, or the Ending hand-limit discard caused it. "You" is the ability's controller in the void, the card's owner, so a card discarded from a hand by a player who does not own it (it goes to its owner's void) does not trigger it. A created card ceases to exist instead of entering the void, so its ability never works and it does not trigger. Entering the void any other way (dissolve, abandon, erode) is not a discard. "It" and "this card" name the card only while it is in its owner's void ("This character from your void", rules.md § Zones → References to cards in a zone), so a card that leaves the void before the ability resolves gains nothing. The void is public, so both players see the subject even when it was discarded from a hidden hand.
- Mechanics: the `discard` trigger gains `self: true` (`whenDiscardSelf()` in `src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`); the matcher (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`) also requires the `discarded` event's card to be the listener's own card. `discardCard` (`src/engine/rules/resources.ts`) announces a non-created card's discard once it is in the void, so the ordinary functional-zone check sees it there; `triggered()` rejects the trigger unless the ability works in the void (`zone: "void"`). The cards author "it gains reclaim [0●]" as `grantReclaim(thisCardIn("void"), { costs })`.
