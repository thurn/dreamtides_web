# RD-hv-umm2.108-2: A card created in a hand is a base-printing created card, hidden from the opponent like any card in that hand
- Ladder: 1 (rules § Created Cards: a created card is used like a normal card and ceases to exist instead of moving to a deck, a hand, the void, or the Banished zone; § Zones: a hand is hidden from the opponent), consistent with the engine's created copies in a hand (`cardCreated` is private to the hand holding it; `zone-card-refs.contracts.ts`)
- rules.md: § Created Cards
- Affects: 09e17f29-8ee1-477f-8175-ff37eb1f254a (Seer of the Fallen, both variants), 69964b8e-0f38-4fb7-a0cd-89430ef04c2d (Contemplation, C1); the `createCard` primitive
- Why: rules.md defined created cards and hidden hands but not what "create 'X' in your hand" makes or who learns it. The created card has the named card's printed characteristics: a card's transfiguration belongs to its deck entry, and a created card has none. It joins the hand like a drawn card, so its holder sees it and the opponent sees the hand count rise and the public trigger that created it, but no event or view names the card to the opponent. The trigger's text names the card, so the opponent can infer it, as they could infer a known effect's result; the engine does not reveal it. Playing it, discarding it, or any other move out of play or the stack ends it (rules § Created Cards), so it never reaches a void.
- Mechanics: `src/engine/effects/primitives/create-card.ts` checks the named card in the catalog, then `createInstance` (status `created`) and appends to the holder's hand; `cardCreated` is `privateTo` the holder for a hand (`src/engine/events/kinds/card-created.ts`).
