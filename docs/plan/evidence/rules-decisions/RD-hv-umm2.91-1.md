# RD-hv-umm2.91-1: "When you reclaim …" triggers as a play by Reclaim is announced, and "When you reclaim this character" works from the stack, where "it gains +N✦" gives the card gained spark it keeps in play
- Ladder: 1 (D14: triggered abilities resolve before priority passes), then 4 (MTG 601.2i: abilities that trigger when a spell is cast trigger once it becomes cast, with the spell on the stack, and a spell's own "when you cast this spell" ability works there). rules.md § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim, § Ability Types → "when you play" abilities and Trigger timing and order, and § Spark supply the normative route, play-characteristic, timing and persistent-spark rules. Read-only Rust at 0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12 corroborates the stack timing: `rules_engine/src/battle_mutations/src/play_cards/play_card.rs` `from_void` pushes `Trigger::PlayedCardFromVoid` as the card moves to the stack and applies a play-from-void ability's `if_you_do` effect to the card on the stack; `ability_data/src/trigger_event.rs` has no reclaim trigger.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When you reclaim a card, When you reclaim this character, Where triggered abilities work, Trigger timing and order
- Affects: c6d8a7cc-3856-4030-96eb-450e406dec33 (Thornwood Delver, "Once per turn, when you reclaim a card, …"); fa7dadd8-c72f-42e7-b203-3d22aa36122f (Reforged Construct, base "it gains +1✦" and amplified "it gains +2✦")
- Why: rules.md defined Reclaim and "when you play" abilities but no trigger on a reclaim. "Reclaim" is playing a card from the void by its Reclaim ability, so the trigger is that play, announced once the card is on the stack, as "when you play" abilities see it; a play from the void by a play permission is not a reclaim, and a card both cover triggers it only when its player chooses Reclaim. A card with "When you reclaim a card" in play cannot see its own reclaim, since it is on the stack. "When you reclaim this character" can only be about the card's own play, so it works where the card then is, the stack, and matches only that card: another card reclaimed while it waits there does not trigger it. Its ability resolves before the opponent receives priority, so "it" is the card on the stack; the gain is ordinary gained spark, which travels with the card and so applies as it enters play, and again on each later reclaim when a "not banished" ability returns it to the void. A card that leaves the stack first gains nothing. "When a character gains ✦" abilities see characters in play only, so they do not see that gain.
- Mechanics: the `Trigger` member `{ on: "reclaim", player, filter, self? }` (`src/engine/dsl/types.ts`) with builders `whenReclaim(player, filter)` and `whenReclaimSelf()` (`src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`). `TRIGGER_EVENTS.reclaim = ["cardPlayed"]`; the matcher (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`) matches a `cardPlayed` by the named player whose card the play step marked `playedViaReclaim`, by the turn log's record of the play. Each side's listeners end with the cards it controls on the stack, bottom first; only the self form matches there, whatever its ability's zone. `resolveCharacters` (`src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts`) names a character card on the stack for "this character" and "it" only in its own ability that triggered on its own play. The cards author `triggered(whenReclaimSelf(), gainSpark(triggeringCard(), N))` with `reclaim(energy(0))`, and `triggered(whenReclaim(), sequence(gainPoints(thisCharacterSpark()), gainSpark(self(), 1)), { oncePerTurn: true })`.
