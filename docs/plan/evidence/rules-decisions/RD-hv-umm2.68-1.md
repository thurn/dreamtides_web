# RD-hv-umm2.68-1: "Banish X cards from your void" binds X to the banished count, at least 1
- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the card text and the normative contextual-X and shared discard-X binding rules in rules.md § Costs, Requirements, and X and RD-hv-umm2.61-1).
- rules.md: § Costs, Requirements, and X → Banish X cards from your void; § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: 06f8cfff-92ec-4668-afbc-f33a274c3b1a
- Why: Fractured Veil prints "To play this event, banish X cards from your void. Look at the top X cards of your deck. Put two of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom." The rules and the Rust engine define only fixed-count void banish costs. As with "discard X cards", the player chooses the cards that pay, and their number is X; no energy is paid for it, and an X● on the same ability would be the same value. Banishing zero cards looks at no cards and does nothing, which the contextual X rule disallows ("an event cannot generally be played for no effect"), so X is at least 1 and the card cannot be played while its controller's void holds no card that could pay. The card being played is never one of its own cost cards, including when it is played from the void by Reclaim. Cards banished this way are in the Banished zone, out of Reclaim's reach. X travels with the stack item. The final void banish, like the final discard, proves every selection without search, so a large void never truncates the player's choice.
