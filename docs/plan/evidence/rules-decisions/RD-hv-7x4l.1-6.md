# RD-hv-7x4l.1-6: Avatars and dreamsigns are not characters; an avatar has its own exhausted status for ☾ costs, cleared at Ending
- Ladder: 1 (P4 avatars and dreamsigns are not characters)
- rules.md: § Card Types (after Dreamsign); § Exhaust and Awaken; § Turn Structure → Ending step 4
- Affects: general (every avatar and dreamsign)
- Why: rules.md said the avatar "starts each battle already in play" and that exhaust clears "from every character in play", leaving open whether avatars count as characters and how an avatar's ☾ cost works. P4 settles both: they are never characters (no position, spark, Support, character counts, or character targeting), and each avatar has an exhausted status for its ☾ costs, cleared during every Ending with the characters'.
