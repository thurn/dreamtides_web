# RD-hv-7x4l.8-3: Offering is a choice made as the card is played; Ephemeral ends when the card leaves the hand
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with every Offering and Ephemeral card), with MTG 601.2f for cost changes on an alternative cost
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Offering, Ephemeral
- Affects: a424b91a-8c3c-4f96-8ac9-8bbbbbbd28b5, 7ad10754-690c-45fa-abe6-28944da6eef6, ce84b8af-b802-42d3-8a31-36827cff17b2, b56ef7e8-c634-4d40-ac08-fab591dfbc4a, c96c6c7f-c0fe-4272-b856-a54ace01f596, 4eb42989-7498-4995-a3ab-89eb026b0c13, 128d1878-a505-4766-91a2-e5a29498bc4e, 15b63630-d9f8-473b-9717-15ad91ff2f16 (Offering); 9a18d375-1b6d-4df4-95ec-c7c5c3f36379, b8a66998-f2d8-401d-8262-d5a658563936, e3193b23-2002-40a9-91fc-e0713200355e, b957466e-f748-4a95-89b2-8509dc762223 (Ephemeral)
- Why: rules.md says an Offering card "may be played for 0● by banishing a card from your hand" without saying when the player chooses or how cost changes apply. The player chooses Offering or the card's own costs as the first play-time choice, with only the ways they can pay offered; the card banished from hand is chosen with the other cost choices, so it is never the card being played. Cost changes apply to the 0● energy cost as to any play, so an increase can make it cost more. The Offering card is banished during every Ending wherever it is (not the stack or the Banished zone), so returning or replaying it never avoids the banishment. Ephemeral describes how a card was drawn ("a card drawn with Ephemeral is banished … if it is still in hand"), so the status ends when the card leaves the hand.
