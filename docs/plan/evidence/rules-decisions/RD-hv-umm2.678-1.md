# RD-hv-umm2.678-1: These two last-turn clauses read the preceding global turn

- Ladder: 5, the simplest reading of both immutable clauses, informed by the bounded step-4 previous-turn analogy below. Step 1 supplies C3’s copy meaning and C8’s extra-turn membership/order, but neither selects a last-own-turn history. Steps 2–3 supply no applicable same-text implementation.
- rules.md: § Turn Structure → Settled last-turn meaning; § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring; § Playing Cards and the Stack → Copies.
- Affects: `7ec00da2-2b2d-4613-9a2a-8611d38199ca` and `9204fbc8-ba40-4e33-9fbc-f62ccc4a43d1`.

## Ruling and ladder evidence

The canonical texts in `src/content/dreamsigns/dreamcatcher-7ec00da2.ts:9` and `src/content/dreamsigns/powder-bowl-9204fbc8.ts:8` say “last turn”, with “you” qualifying the play or character control. Both therefore select the immediately preceding completed global turn; neither says “your last turn”. Their filters differ: the first clause asks who played the event then, while the second asks which currently controlled in-play character itself scored then. “You control” remains present tense rather than being rewritten as “you controlled when it scored”. The histories rotate on every actual turn transition, including consecutive turns of the same player and opponent extra turns. This ruling is bounded to these two clauses rather than a blanket interpretation of every future use of “last turn”. The ordinary alternation consequence is explicit: a Dawn copy can use an Interrupt its controller played during the preceding opponent turn; the scoring condition after an opponent turn can become true if that player gains control of a character that scored in that opponent turn. A scorer that the player has lost or that is absent from play cannot satisfy the present condition. Card utility supplies no permission to substitute an older own turn.

`docs/plan/decisions.md` C3 defines the first UUID’s ethereal copy as an ephemeral created copy. C8 and RD-hv-7x4l.1-13 make an extra turn a complete turn outside the round count and include it for last-turn language; they do not say to skip intervening opponent turns. Current § Turn Structure makes Dawn controller-relative, § Card Types and the battlefield rules place controlled characters in play, and § Challengers, Blockers, and Scoring defines positive character scoring independently of a player’s arbitrary point gains. § Zones → References to cards in a zone reads current characteristics, while § Zone Changes preserves a physical card’s identity across zones. Thus the historical-scoring lookup and the current controlled-character predicate are separate: ordinary departure and return preserve the scorer’s identity, but absence from play, current enemy control, and cessation prevent qualification. Distinct physical copies never inherit that scoring identity. D15 and § Copies establish that a stack copy is not played.

The current plan record `docs/plan/evidence/legacy-behavior.md` catalogs the prototype’s automated Dreamwell and card rules frozen at source commit `8b1d9e8243b0ed114d47872dc03c6d97b0e1f7c6`, with neither affected UUID. A direct read of that commit’s `src/rules/battle/battle-card-effects-table.ts` also has no affected UUID or last-turn rule. The prototype offers no actual implementation to settle these Dreamsign clauses.

The read-only Rust source at HEAD `0d6afa7dc2eef6048264ffd634da1c2b97886b12` has different same-UUID text: `client/Assets/StreamingAssets/Tabula/dreamsigns.toml:352` asks Judgment to play the last event for 0●; `:837` reduces all character costs by 1● after scoring during that turn. Its Turn Structure also scores during start-of-turn Judgment (`docs/battle_rules/battle_rules.md:143–159`), unlike the current eight-phase rules. Its actual `TurnHistory` retains action-local hand-limit/abandon facts only (`rules_engine/src/battle_state/src/battle/turn_history.rs`); `TurnData` assigns consecutive IDs to individual players’ turns (`turn_data.rs`). A bounded source search of docs/rules_engine found no applicable previous-turn resolver. The relevant files have no working-tree diff; whole-repository cleanliness is not claimed. These changed clauses do not qualify as D10 step-3 unchanged-rule precedent.

The [official Magic Comprehensive Rules effective September 25, 2026](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt), CR502.2 and CR500.7, distinguish the preceding active player’s individual turn and extra turns inserted in sequence. Official [Innistrad Remastered release notes, Double-Faced Werewolves](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/innistrad-remastered-release-notes) expressly interpret last-turn spell checks as the entire previous turn and filter spells by a single player. This is a window/filter analogy, not a Dreamtides Dreamsign ruling; no day/night, transformation, mana or Magic object-lifetime rules are imported. D10 step5 chooses the same unqualified window in the two affected texts after the higher ladder checks.

Before the first completed global turn there is no history. Before a particular player’s first own turn, the preceding opponent turn can already contain that player’s Interrupt play; it is not automatically empty merely because `sideTurns` was zero. At transition after all Ending actions, the completed turn becomes the fixed previous window before new-turn callbacks. Phase advances and round changes do not alter recorded play/scorer identities. Changes of character controller or presence do not retroactively alter who scored, but they can change the present “you control” cost condition. A scorer returning to play keeps its identity by Dreamtides § Zone Changes; Magic’s new-object-on-zone-change rule is not imported. New battles start empty. The first-character cost-use ordinal is independent: per controller within the current actual global turn, including off-turn play, and resets every actual turn.

## Observed current API constraints and consumer obligations

At base `dfa72163e91071d2315f06f94f8941782933713b`, `initialState` (`src/engine/state/create.ts`) begins `turnNumber:0`, both `sideTurns:0`, and an empty `turnLog`. `beginFirstTurn`, `beginNextTurn`, `resetForTurn`, and `startTurn` in `src/engine/rules/turn.ts` increment actual turn clocks, empty both sides’ current turn log before start-of-turn callbacks, then enter Dreamwell. The extra-turn stack is most recent first and round advancement occurs only at a normal-turn boundary. Existing `turn.test.ts` and `take-extra-turn.contracts.ts` describe these boundaries; they were inspected, not executed for this documentation-only ruling.

`TurnLog.played[side]` and `recordPlayed` capture ordered current-turn plays before play-trigger matching; `playedThisTurn` includes all routes and excludes copies. They are current-turn facts, not retained preceding-turn facts. `laneResolved.scored > 0` and its challenger identify a genuine character-scoring occasion (`src/engine/rules/challenge.ts`, `src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts` scores cases). `pointsScored` carries a side/amount/cause rather than a character, and flat effect/Fatigue awards are unsuitable substitutes. A historical boolean keyed only by the scoring side would also be insufficient for the present-control clause. The current `conditionHoldsWith` controls case uses live `matchingCharactersWith` (`src/engine/dsl/conditions.ts` and `selectors.ts`), supporting the current battlefield predicate rather than historical ownership; it supplies no previous-turn scorer lookup. Dreamsign listeners bind `controller:side` in the matcher. None of these observed APIs is claimed to implement either pending Dreamsign’s last-turn behavior.
