# RD-hv-umm2.501-1: A target bound by "this character's ✦" reads the source's spark each time it is checked, its last-known spark once it has left play

- Ladder: 4 (MTG 608.2b: targets are checked again on resolution against the current information; MTG 608.2h: last-known information of a source that has left its zone), adapted to Dreamtides. rules.md § Targeting → Targets are chosen before costs are paid and Bounds that count (a numeric target restriction is read as targets are chosen and again as the effect resolves) and § Spark → This character's ✦, with RD-hv-umm2.91-2 and RD-hv-umm2.460-1 (the source's effective spark while it is in play, else the effective spark immediately before it left play, retained by its pending ability), are normative rule anchors.
- rules.md: § Spark → This character's ✦ as a bound; § Targeting → Bounds that count.
- Affects: 95711262-8510-475c-a572-c1cd144d54cb (Pastward Wanderer, "▸Dawn: Choose an event in your void with cost less than or equal to this character's ✦. That event gains reclaim 0●."), 75bf1aab-dce0-4bbc-8ab0-a3c84b66ae9a (Cloaked Sentinel, "Abandon this character: Dissolve an enemy with ✦ less than or equal to this character's ✦."). Printed catalog data belongs to their content beads, hv-umm2.157 and hv-umm2.143.
- Why: rules.md established both halves, the re-check of every bound and the departure value of "this character's ✦", but no bound read the source's spark, so it did not say which spark a bound reads at each check. Reading the bound by the same rule as the value keeps one meaning for "this character's ✦". Cloaked Sentinel chooses its target before paying its abandon cost, so the offer reads the in-play spark and the resolution check reads the spark captured as the cost removed it; reading 0 there would make its own cost defeat every activation. A triggered ability such as Pastward Wanderer's chooses as it resolves, so a source dissolved by an earlier Dawn trigger offers and checks against the same retained spark, and the targets it offers are always ones its resolution keeps. Where nothing resolves or chooses targets (a static ability, the layer evaluation, a trigger's subject or condition, a cost), the bound has no source spark to read and is rejected, as the value is; the resolving character choices of the `abandon` and `prevent` primitives also supply no reader yet, so a bound by the source's spark there is rejected rather than read as 0.
- Mechanics: `SourceSparkValue` (`{ value: "sourceSpark" }`, builder `thisCharacterSpark()`) is a `BoundValue` member, accepted by character selector bounds and by a void target's `costAtMost` (`fromVoid(filter, { costAtMost: thisCharacterSpark() })`). `boundOf` in `src/engine/dsl/selectors.ts` reads it through a `SourceSparkReader` and throws without one. `src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts` supplies the reader as targets are offered (play time, copies, and choices made on resolution, which receive the queued trigger's `lastKnownSourceSpark` from `src/engine/triggers/resolve.ts`) and as chosen targets, "all" and Support references, and counts are checked on resolution; it prefers current in-play spark, then the step-local departure capture, then the retained value, then 0. The bound adds no state, event or view field: it reads the departure value RD-hv-umm2.460-1 already retains, redacts and validates. The card-lab solver stages such targets at the played card's printed spark, 0 for an event.
- Contracts: `bound-source-spark.contracts.ts` covers the in-play Dawn offer with Support, the Dawn trigger whose source an earlier Dawn trigger dissolved (offer and check against the last-known spark), the void target re-check after the source's spark falls, the character offer before the abandon cost, illegality with no enemy in bounds, the post-abandon re-check against the retained spark rather than 0 or the void spark, a target whose spark rose above the retained bound, saved-state reload, views and determinization, prompt reload with other-side, out-of-bound and stale answers, and rejection without a reader in a selector and in the layer evaluation. `lab-preflight.test.ts` covers the solver's staging.
