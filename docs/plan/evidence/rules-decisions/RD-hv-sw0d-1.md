# RD-hv-sw0d-1: Hand-empty and discard abilities follow their occasions in order

- Ladder: 5, the simplest reading consistent with the affected text and existing discard rules. D14 fixes FIFO resolution after the enclosing effect or action, but does not choose the relative position of different triggering occasions. The prototype and unchanged-text Rust comparison recorded in [RD-hv-6yev-1](RD-hv-6yev-1.md) supply no independent hand-empty sequencing rule. At step 4, the [official Magic Comprehensive Rules effective September 25, 2026](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt), rule 603.3b, permits a player's chosen stack order for simultaneous triggers; it cannot directly dictate Dreamtides' fixed FIFO cross-kind position. Step 5 therefore chooses the current completed-move order while preserving the established discard rules.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When your hand becomes empty; § Ability Types → Trigger timing and order; § Zones → Hand; § Ability Types → When a card is put into a void and When you discard this card. Related decision: [RD-hv-6yev-1](RD-hv-6yev-1.md), whose step-4 post-event listener matching concerns a separate question.
- Affects: ef25fba0-58a0-4b69-be06-3668679fcdc7 and the full production acceptance retained by hv-umm2.237. This decision settles the shared hand-empty/discard order; original237 retains production authoring, the global first-time condition, both filed UUIDs, and every recipe/QA requirement.
- Why: A completed last-card move makes the hand empty before that move's announcement. An ordinary discard announces the card after its arrival in the void, allowing an ability that works in the void to see its own discard. Its hand-empty occasion therefore precedes its per-card discard occasion. A created discard is announced while the card still exists, allowing discard abilities to read it before cessation; that per-card discard occasion precedes hand-empty. These established functional-zone and readability requirements both stand. Earlier cards in a whole-hand discard announce their discards before the final hand-empty occasion. The completed one-or-more discard occasion follows every individual move and discard announcement. All these callbacks wait until the enclosing effect or action finishes, then resolve FIFO.
- Public proof: Eight synthetic engine cases cover both sides × ordinary last card, created last card, two ordinary cards, and ordinary then created last card. They assert public queued/resolved sources, null subjects for hand-empty and batch occasions, real per-card subjects, substantive move/occasion order, the positive completed batch count, one empty occasion, and actual point/energy effects. The existing 21 hand-empty contracts remain intact. Production already implements this order; this decision and coverage require no runtime edit or old-source-failure claim.
