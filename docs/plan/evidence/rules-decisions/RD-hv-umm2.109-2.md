# RD-hv-umm2.109-2: A play restriction stops every way of playing a card, and "that card cannot be played" follows the card across zones and holders
- Ladder: 5 (rules.md § Zone Changes: card identity persists across zones; § Playing Cards and the Stack defines playing a card from a hand, by Offering, by a void permission, and by Reclaim)
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Play restrictions
- Affects: 70716dd8-1133-4966-b01b-757ea627874c, d09a6760-6630-4964-9c77-decc641df713, 9629a58a-9f30-40f0-9199-835ea2dddc76; general for every play restriction
- Why: Every listed route is "playing" the card, so a restriction applies to all of them, and to nothing that puts a card into play without playing it (materialize, a copy) or to activating abilities. The restriction is checked as a player would begin a play (rules/timing.ts `canPlay`), which both legality and the play step's own validation read, so a restricted play is neither offered nor accepted, and a card already on the stack is unaffected. Because identity persists across zones, a chosen card that leaves the hand and returns, goes to a void and could be reclaimed, or reaches the chooser's hand is still that card, and no player can play it until the source leaves play; the restriction ends early only if the card ceases to exist. A type restriction ("events") reads current characteristics, as play permissions do.
