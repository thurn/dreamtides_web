# RD-hv-7x4l.1-13: Extra turns are full turns that do not advance the round count, taken last-in, first-out
- Ladder: 1 (C8 extra turns are full turns outside the round count; MTG 500.7 for the order)
- rules.md: § Turn Structure → Extra turns, Dreamwell phase, Ending; § The Dreamwell and Energy
- Affects: a911ef71-799c-4240-ad13-8fabd3caeafa
- Why: rules.md had no extra-turn rules. C8: an extra turn runs all eight phases including the Dreamwell draw and the Draw, counts as that player's turn for "this turn", "next turn", "last turn", "once per turn", and "until your next turn", does not advance the round counter (so not the turn limit), and several pending extra turns are taken most recent first. Because rules.md now says the Dreamwell draw starts in round 2 (RD-hv-7x4l.1-22), the Dreamwell text names extra turns explicitly so that an extra turn always draws, as C8 requires.
