qualifying held copy. The conditional essence minimum is inclusive. An offer
already prepared is a quoted choice: its alternatives and spent pool are exact
persistent state, and administrative changes preserve them. The next gameplay
pick prepares a fresh offer using current holdings and essence; a debug reroll
preserves its requested count. The existing pool engine supplies uniqueness,
within-visit exclusion and deterministic draws. Count arithmetic is a safe
integer; a failed preparation commits no offer, pool spend or modifier log.

Rosemary's static wording projects enhancement while held onto unvisited,
unprepared free Reward and Revelation sites. Losing it before preparation ends
that projection. Successful preparation snapshots the site's enhanced mode and
exact existing runtime, which persist through loss and reload. Natural
enhancements persist, and multiple targeted sources share the one enhanced
mode. Bazaar is a paid shop outside the settled correspondence. Additional
random enhancements exclude these effectively enhanced sites; each such grant
keeps its own existing source/dreamscape history and entitlement.

random enhancement sees held targeted descriptors in either order. It changes
candidate eligibility without changing guide eligibility, RNG, history or
entitlement arithmetic. Current Revelation counts come from its economy
catalog, and Reward counts and strict immutable choice behavior come from the
settled capability supplied by hv-umm2.598 and hv-umm2.607.
