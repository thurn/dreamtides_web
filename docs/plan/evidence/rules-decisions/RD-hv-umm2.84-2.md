# RD-hv-umm2.84-2: Whether a reclaimed card is banished is decided as it would leave, against the abilities applying then
- Ladder: 4 (MTG analog: a static ability applies only while its source is on the battlefield, and a replacement effect is checked as the event it modifies happens; a leaving permanent's own leaves-the-battlefield replacement applies to itself), with 5 for the one-at-a-time dissolve rules.md already gives
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: 9adbe3db-d872-4b75-92b5-d7f2798c22ce, 738a5af0-f848-4d48-bceb-9a43c9b11066
- Why: the card text is a static ability, so it covers a reclaimed card whenever that card would leave play or the stack while the ability applies, including a card reclaimed before the source entered play. A source that is itself reclaimed is still in play as its own departure is decided, so it covers itself. A card that leaves after the source has left play is banished; rules.md § Ability Types (▸Dissolved) already dissolves "each character" one at a time in a fixed order, so a reclaimed card dissolved after its source by the same effect is banished, and one dissolved before it is not. The status is read from the card's owner's layer-6 `reclaimNotBanished` changes only for a card in play or on the stack; a card the reclaimed status already banished stays reclaimed in the Banished zone and never returns. While the ability applies, the battle view shows the covered card without the reclaimed status.
