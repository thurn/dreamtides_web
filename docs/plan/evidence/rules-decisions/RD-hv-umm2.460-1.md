# RD-hv-umm2.460-1: A resolving ability reads its departed source's retained effective spark

- Ladder: 4, MTG Comprehensive Rules 608.2h, adapted to Dreamtides' physical card identity across zones. Steps 1–3 give no answer to the departure question: no D-, C-, or P-entry in decisions.md decides which spark a resolving ability reads after its source leaves play; the prototype has no resolving source-spark implementation; and the read-only Rust quantity and variable-value expression implementations provide no source-spark or last-known-source rule for unchanged text. rules.md § Spark establishes effective spark and resolution-time values, and § Zones establishes identity across zones; RD-hv-umm2.91-2 identifies the departure question these anchors leave open.

- Why: the [official June 19, 2026 Comprehensive Rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260619.pdf) § 608.2h uses current information when the object remains in the relevant zone and last-known information when it has left that zone. Dreamtides reads effective spark immediately before departure ends duration, static, Support, or counter-dependent changes. A queued, stacked, delayed, or currently resolving ability retains this numeric characteristic even if its source ceases to exist. Abandoning a source to pay for an activation captures it before payment removes the source, then attaches it to the activated item. The same physical card returned to play supplies current information, consistent with Dreamtides identity across zones; the MTG object-lifetime rule is not part of this adaptation. An ability with no relevant in-play source information reads 0, as an emblem does.
- Mechanics: `rules/zones.ts` captures before departure and expiry, with a step-local map rebuilt by replay for current resolution and same-step trigger enqueue. Optional `lastKnownSourceSpark` values persist on queued triggers, activated stack items, and floating/delayed trigger changes; the load boundary validates finite nonnegative numbers. The evaluator prefers current in-play spark, then step-local capture, then the pending ability's retained value. Public departure events carry the captured spark. Views and queued events omit the retained characteristic when their source is unidentified; visible queued and delayed records preserve it through determinization. Existing JSON clone and structural hashing preserve plain record fields. Static source-spark expressions fail the synthetic content gate.
- Contracts: `last-known-source-spark.contracts.ts` exercises current reads, before-expiry capture, reentry, Dissolved enqueue, created and silent departures, current resolution and abandonment costs, waiting activations, delayed triggers, saved-state validation, prompt replay, state hashes, and source privacy. The silent lifecycle case invokes public isolated step boundaries to exercise capture; it does not assert that the normal automatic scheduler offers a card play while triggers are pending.
