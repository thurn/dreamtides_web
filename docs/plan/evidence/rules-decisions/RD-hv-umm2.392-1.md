# RD-hv-umm2.392-1: "Them" after a materialize names the characters it put into play, each while it is in play
- Ladder: 5 (simplest reading consistent with rules.md § Zone Changes, where card identity persists across zones, the deferred zone-checked precedents of F3 and C3, and the affected text), after 4 (MTG 400.7 and Rally the Ancestors: a creature that left the battlefield is a new object, so "exile those creatures" spares it even once it returns)
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Materialize
- Affects: 2a6af0b8-11da-4d4b-8217-3bd7ed399dfa (Borrowed Minutes)
- Why: "Materialize each ≤X● cost character in your void. Banish them at end of turn" refers back to the materialized characters from a later, delayed clause. rules.md did not say which cards "them" names or what happens to one that moves before the clause resolves. Steps 1–3 are silent: no D-, C-, or P-entry covers a deferred reference to moved cards, the prototype and the Rust engine do not implement the text. "Them" is exactly the characters that materialize put into play, never one that stayed in its zone because the back rank was full, one already in play, or one another part of the effect put into play. The clause acts on each of them that is in play as it resolves, whoever controls it then: the materialized cards are characters in play, and the deferred actions already settled act on their card only while it is still where the effect put it (a card banished until a boundary returns only from the Banished zone, F3; an ephemeral copy is banished only from hand, C3). MTG would also spare a card that left and returned to play, but Dreamtides keeps a card's identity across zones (§ Zone Changes: "Banishing a card and returning it to play does not protect a character"), so a member that left play and entered it again is banished. A member in a void, hand, deck, or the Banished zone, or one that ceased to exist, is untouched. With no member in play, the clause does nothing.

## Encoding

`withMaterialized(materialize(from), effect)` binds the instance IDs that entered play while its materialize resolved; a `delayed` trigger inside `effect` stores them as its floating record's `captured`, which its queued trigger carries to resolution. `capturedCards()` resolves to those still in play, and `banishCards([capturedCards()])` banishes them through the ordinary zone change (`src/engine/effects/primitives/with-materialized.ts`, `resolveCards` in `src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts`). A view shows a member the viewer cannot identify, in a hidden hand or deck, by its owner, printing, variant, and a member number shared by every trigger naming it, and a determinization deals a different hidden card of that owner, printing, and variant for each member number, so a member shuffled into a deck and materialized again is banished in a sample as in the battle. The contracts in `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/with-materialized.contracts.ts` pin the cohort, the return-and-replay and left-play cases, and the empty cohort; `with-materialized-view.contracts.ts` pins the views, the hidden member's stand-in, and a hidden source.
