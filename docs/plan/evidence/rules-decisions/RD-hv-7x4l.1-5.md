# RD-hv-7x4l.1-5: A player with no legal response passes priority automatically, but phases are never skipped
- Ladder: 1 (P1 response windows)
- rules.md: § Playing Cards and the Stack → Automatic passing
- Affects: general
- Why: P1 gives a response window only to a player holding a legal response; otherwise the pass is automatic. The phase windows stay explicit: the active player always ends their own Day and Night, and the opposing player always ends Dusk, matching the existing "explicitly passes to end this phase" text in § Turn Structure. The pay-to-end special action (RD-hv-7x4l.1-12) is never a response, so it does not open a response window.
