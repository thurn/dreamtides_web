# RD-hv-7x4l.1-21: Created cards cease to exist instead of moving to a deck, hand, void, or the Banished zone
- Ladder: 1 (D15: a copy "ceases to exist on resolution or prevention"; C1 and C3 read created cards as ceasing to exist; § Figments already says figments cannot enter the Banished zone)
- rules.md: § Created Cards
- Affects: general
- Why: rules.md § Created Cards said a created card "ceases to exist" but then defined that as being banished ("a created event … is banished on resolution"), which contradicts D15 and § Figments and would leave created cards in the Banished zone for effects that read it. The section now says the card ceases to exist instead of moving to a deck, hand, the void, or the Banished zone, and is then in no zone, matching engine-design § Zones and zone changes replacement 1.
