# RD-hv-6yev-1: A hand becomes empty on each settled positive-to-zero transition

- Ladder: 4, adapted from the [official Magic Comprehensive Rules effective September 25, 2026](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt), rules 603.2c, 603.2e and 603.10. Each actual occurrence triggers once; a “becomes” condition requires a transition; ordinary listeners and continuous effects are checked immediately after the event. Dreamtides D14 independently supplies its FIFO and whole-effect timing. Magic's stack and state-trigger repetition rules are not imported.
- rules.md: § Ability Types → When your hand becomes empty; § Zones → Hand; § Ability Types → Trigger timing and order.
- Affects: ef25fba0-58a0-4b69-be06-3668679fcdc7. This foundation supplies the descriptive trigger; its global first-time condition and production authoring belong to hv-umm2.237.
- Why: The hand its holder controls is public by size. Playing, discarding, banishing, transferring or otherwise moving its last card, and a created card ceasing to exist, can make it empty. Count the completed move, including replacements: detaching and reinserting in the same hand is not an occasion. Match listeners in their settled zones; a character put directly into play from the last hand position can observe the occasion. Later draws in the same effect preserve the earlier occurrence. Empty-to-empty and positive-to-positive changes trigger nothing. Existing source-local once-per-turn limits retain their ordinary meaning.
- Prior ladder evidence: Current rules settle holder, visibility, functional zones and FIFO. The current prototype/engine contains no implementing hand-empty rule. The unchanged-text Rust comparison supplies no hand-empty trigger in `rules_engine/src/ability_data/src/trigger_event.rs` or corresponding rules text. The parent session verified the primary Wizards rules through its official rules landing page before this decision; no lower ladder step is needed.
- Mechanics: `whenHandBecomesEmpty(player = "you")` matches a public `handEmptied` event with only its former hand holder and a null trigger subject. The four central successful hand-departure routes publish after settlement. The event remains reconstructible through `engine.handEmptied` logs even without listeners. Movement bookkeeping retains existing deck-top-revelation boundaries. No persisted history or first-occurrence counter is part of this form.
