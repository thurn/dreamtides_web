# RD-hv-umm2.533-1: Abandon the captured materializations the delayed controller still controls in play

- Ladder: 4 for the control requirement (Magic Comprehensive Rules, 2026-09-25, 701.21a); 5 for the figment cohort, applying the printed reference to the actual materializations and the existing capacity rule.
- rules.md: Keywords and Effects → Materialize and Abandon; Creating Figments at Capacity.
- Affects: 5a27e236-86d7-442f-81d2-fb29a2b7f12a (Pyre Wight, both variants).
- Why: The D-, C-, and P-entries contain no ruling for abandoning a captured materialization cohort. The prototype's `abandonCard` in `src/rules/battle/apply-debug-edit.ts` is a sandbox operation, excluded as rule precedent by P10; it supplies no delayed cohort implementation. The read-only Rust reference has `StandardEffect::AbandonAtEndOfTurn` in `ability_data/src/standard_effect.rs` and target/serializer metadata, but no implementing delayed captured instruction in `battle_mutations`. Its `character_mutations/abandon.rs` moves the selected character using that character's controller and does not settle which player a captured instruction instructs. Rust's Abandon text agrees that it concerns a player's own character. Step 4's [official rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt), 701.21a, permit a player to sacrifice only a permanent they control. Adapted to Dreamtides' Abandon instruction, the delayed ability instructs its retained controller, and therefore skips a named member another player controls. The source changing control does not change the controller of its existing delayed ability.

The cohort contains each new physical figment that actually entered play while the materialization instruction resolved. `createFigments` (`src/engine/rules/figments.ts`) fills available positions with new instances and distributes the group's combined spark among those new instances when fewer than the requested number fit. Existing figments are not destinations for that distribution. Two open positions produce two captured members; one position produces one combined-spark member; a full rank at resolution produces an empty cohort. Manual merging is a separate action: a captured member that ceases on a merge is absent, and an existing destination outside the cohort is not captured by receiving its spark. This follows the simplest reading of “them” consistent with the printed text and the established capacity rule.

RD-hv-umm2.392-1 supplies the existing card-identity behavior across zones: a captured ordinary card that leaves and returns to play is again named; members outside play and members that ceased to exist are skipped. It is an already normative identity ruling, not step 1 authority in this ladder. RD-hv-umm2.86-1 supplies the established end-of-turn timing. This ruling uses that timing directly. Members are abandoned once each in their materialization order, through the ordinary abandonment operation, so each fires its own Dissolved and applicable When-you-abandon triggers.

## Encoding

`withMaterialized` accepts either `materialize` or `materializeFigments`, retaining the existing entry-clock cohort and delayed-trigger binding. `abandonCards([capturedCards()])` resolves that binding, deduplicates members and checks play zone and the delayed controller immediately before calling `abandonCharacter` for each. It introduces no persisted field, prompt or event kind. The existing public `abandoned`, `dissolved`, `ceasedToExist`, trigger and effect events name the physical members. Synthetic contracts cover both controllers, capacity, duplicate references, departure, changed control, source control, ordinary-card reentry, determinization, failure atomicity, suspended reload and stale/concurrent answers.
