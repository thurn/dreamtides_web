# RD-hv-umm2.86-1: "At end of turn" triggers as the Ending phase begins and resolves before its cleanup; one created later waits for the next end of turn
- Ladder: 4 (MTG 513.1–513.2 and 514: "at the beginning of the end step" abilities trigger and resolve before cleanup's hand-size discard and "until end of turn" expiry, and one created during the end step waits for the next turn's end step), after 1 (C8: an extra turn is one of its player's turns), 2 (the prototype implements no end-of-turn trigger), and 3 (the Rust engine fires its `Trigger::EndOfTurn` before clearing "until end of turn" effects, but its Ending phase text differs from rules.md's four-step Ending, so step 3 only agrees)
- rules.md: § Turn Structure → Ending; § Ability Types → Triggered abilities
- Affects: 2a6af0b8-11da-4d4b-8217-3bd7ed399dfa (Borrowed Minutes), 5a27e236-86d7-442f-81d2-fb29a2b7f12a (Pyre Wight, "Abandon them at end of turn", base and amplified), and the "At the end of your turn" dreamsigns b765f6bb-0b8a-4847-ada4-477b9493e3ad and d76684c3-797e-44ae-a2ba-333e77d46a4d
- Why: rules.md's Ending phase listed its cleanup (hand-limit discard, Ephemeral and Offering banishes, "until end of turn" expiry with returns, exhaust clear) but not when "at end of turn" abilities happen. They trigger as the Ending phase begins and resolve before step 1, as MTG's end step precedes its cleanup step: a cohort Borrowed Minutes borrowed is banished while "until end of turn" changes still apply and before any hand-limit discard, so a card those abilities move is not discarded or returned in the same cleanup by accident of order. "At end of turn" is the end of every turn; "At the end of your turn" only of the controller's turns, extra turns included (C8), so a cohort borrowed in a turn followed by an extra turn is banished at the end of the turn it entered, not after the extra turn. The end-of-turn moment happens once per turn: a delayed "at end of turn" trigger created later in that Ending phase (by an ability that resolved at end of turn, or one that triggered during the cleanup) and a character entering play then wait for the next turn's end, as MTG 513.2 rules. Borrowed Minutes itself cannot resolve during an Ending phase, which has no priority window, so only a trigger can create such a late delayed trigger. "Until end of turn" effects that begin during the end-of-turn abilities end in step 3; those beginning after step 3 still end as the turn ends (RD-hv-7x4l.6-4).

## Encoding

`atEndOfTurn(player?)` (`src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`) is `{ on: "endOfTurn", player? }`, matching the `phaseChanged` event that enters the Ending phase (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`). `enterPhase("ending")` (`src/engine/rules/turn.ts`) runs the cleanup in the same step when nothing triggered; otherwise it sets the optional persisted marker `turn.endingCleanup: true`, the queued triggers drain one step each, and the next automatic `advancePhase` clears the marker and runs the cleanup. Games with no end-of-turn trigger keep their step structure and state hashes. The contracts in `src/engine/effects/primitives/contracts/at-end-of-turn-trigger.contracts.ts` pin the order, the unchanged single-step cleanup, the player filter, extra turns, the late trigger, JSON and cold fold reloads mid-Ending, and a failing trigger step.
