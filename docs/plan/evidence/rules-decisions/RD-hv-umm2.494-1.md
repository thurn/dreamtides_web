# RD-hv-umm2.494-1: "When you discard a card, it gains reclaim" grants the discarded card permanent plain Reclaim while it is in a void as the ability resolves
- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the printed text) for the reference "it". The ladder is not invoked for the settled normative rules.md discard and Reclaim rules: "When you discard a card" triggers once per card discarded from your hand by any cause, RD-hv-umm2.78-1 establishes a permanent plain gain keeping the card's cost, and "When you discard this card, it gains reclaim" names its card only while it is in your void. Those established anchors supply normative support, rather than a new prototype-behavior claim. Step 1 is silent (no D-, C-, or P-entry names this card), and step 3 is silent (the `~/dreamtides` Rust engine parses `GainsReclaim` but implements no mutation for it).
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Reclaim
- Affects: d755cfa4-d7c0-46d8-a836-909c45c68890 ("When you discard a card, it gains reclaim"), and every later entity whose trigger grants Reclaim to the card the triggering event concerns
- Why: "It" is the card the trigger saw discarded, as "it" names the triggering card elsewhere. The grant is a characteristic of the card, not a permission to its controller, so it names the card in whichever void it entered: a card the opponent owns that you discard from your hand goes to the opponent's void and gains Reclaim there, which only its owner can use, as with any void card. A card that is no longer in a void as the ability resolves, such as one another trigger put into the deck first, gains nothing, matching "When you discard this card"; a discarded created card ceased to exist and gains nothing. The text states no cost or duration, so the gain is plain and permanent. The card is public in the void, so the trigger and its `effectStarted` name it to both players. The engine resolves `triggeringCard()` to the trigger's subject and keeps it only while it is in a void (`grantReclaim`), so the grant is announced, survives a reload as a floating change, and needs no new state.
