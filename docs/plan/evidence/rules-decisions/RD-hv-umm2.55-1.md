# RD-hv-umm2.55-1: Free Discover cost choices use a public outcome-complete range

- Ladder: 5 (the simplest reading consistent with the affected printed text).
- rules.md: § Keywords and Effects → Discover; § Costs, Requirements, and X.
- Affects: 331e2846-85d1-4667-8c64-dc6c21a418ed; 20fd609a-7529-4582-a362-b8151b0ae011.
- Why: “Choose a value of X” in the effect is a resolution-time choice, distinct from an X energy cost. Beacon pays its printed 1● only. The prototype has no implemented precedent for this effect; the Rust effect catalog has Discover predicates but no equivalent free-number effect under unchanged text. The rules distinguish cost choices from resolving effects but do not specify an upper bound for this free choice.

For a free choice used solely to Discover an exact cost, the canonical offered integers are 0 through the public catalog's greatest fixed card cost plus one. Every attainable Discover cost is represented. The greatest cost plus one represents every larger choice: each has the same empty candidate set, consumes no Discover randomness, and does not shuffle. This range depends on public catalog definitions, never hidden deck contents or available energy. The ceiling is metadata derived from the catalog, rather than an energy cap or balance tunable. Copiable deck-entry cost reductions and Empowered reduce costs; they preserve the ceiling. X in a candidate card's cost counts as 0 for the comparison, as for other cost selectors.

This rule is limited to a free choice used solely by an exact-cost Discover effect. Other free-X effects have their own printed context. Rite's paid X allows 0 because discovering a zero-cost card is meaningful; its paid X retains the existing affordability range. Exact spark requirements use current effective spark, including modifications and the zero clamp.
