- Why: rules.md defined Erode but no trigger on it. "You erode" names the player whose deck the card leaves, as "you discard" names the player whose hand it leaves (RD-hv-umm2.116-1): an opponent's "The opponent erodes 2" makes you erode. A deck holds only cards its player owns (a created card ceases to exist instead of entering a deck, and `createCard` creates into a hand), so the eroded card always goes to its eroder's void and is never created. Only the Erode keyword erodes: Foresee's "send to the void", a discard, and a dissolve are other routes into the void, which "When a card is put into your void" covers. Erode moves its cards one at a time, so each card triggers on its own, and Fatigue erodes nothing. The trigger reads the eroded card in the void, where it is when `eroded` is announced, matching "When a card is put into your void" (RD-hv-umm2.77-1). "When you erode this card" is then the discard-self rule with Erode in place of the discard: the card is in its owner's void as `eroded` announces it, so the ability works from the void and only there, a card already in the void sees nothing, "it" names the card only while it stays in the void, and both players see it although the deck was hidden. "When you discard or erode this card" triggers on either occasion.
- Mechanics: the `Trigger` member `{ on: "erode", player, filter, self? }` (`src/engine/dsl/types.ts`) with builders `whenErode(player, filter)` and `whenErodeSelf()` (`src/engine/dsl/triggers.ts`); `triggered()` requires `zone: "void"` for a self erode trigger, alone or as an `either` branch, as for `whenDiscardSelf()`. The matcher (`src/engine/triggers/matcher.ts`, `TRIGGER_EVENTS.erode = ["eroded"]`) matches the `eroded` event that `erodeCard` (`src/engine/rules/resources.ts`) emits once the card is in the void, for the player whose deck it left, and with `self` only the listener's own card. The cards author "When you discard or erode this card" as `triggered(either(whenDiscardSelf(), whenErodeSelf()), …, { zone: "void" })`.
