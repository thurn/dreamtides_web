# Pre-existing issues found by hv-umm2.87

- **Keyword grants other than Reclaim drop out of views and determinizations when their source is hidden or ceased to exist.** `carriesLegality` (`src/engine/rules/floating.ts`) now keeps a floating Reclaim change whose source a side cannot see (this bead's fix, found by the fuzz through a resolved copy of From the Barrow), but `view/view.ts` `floatingVisible` still omits every other `keyword` change from such a source, such as a granted `cannotBeTargeted` or `veil` from a copied event. The view's characteristics still show the keyword, but a determinization rebuilds characteristics from the floating effects it receives, so a target or prevent legality that reads those keywords could differ from the real state. No fuzz game has shown it yet. Smallest fix: return `true` for every `keyword` change in `carriesLegality` (a keyword change names only its changed card, which the view already requires to be visible), with a determinization-fidelity contract for a copied keyword grant.
