# Pre-existing issues found by hv-umm2.392

- `src/engine/view/determinize.ts:222-232` (`deal` → `mint`), with `src/engine/view/view.ts:287` (`hiddenZone`) and `src/engine/view/knowledge.ts:40` (`knows`): a determinization deals every card its viewer cannot identify from the decklist remainder (`remaining`, `determinize.ts:95`) with `variantOf(entry)` and `freshStatus(created)`. That keeps the hidden cards' variants as a multiset, but every persistent status a card carried publicly before it became hidden is lost. Persistent statuses travel with a card across zones (rules § Zone Changes: gained spark, lost Veil, playedViaReclaim, and so on). Scenario: a character gains permanent spark or loses Veil in play, is shuffled into its owner's deck, and is later drawn or materialized. In the battle it keeps that status; in every sample its dealt stand-in has fresh status, so search misjudges it. This holds whether or not a `withMaterialized` cohort names the card. It is not a view leak, since the view shows nothing about an unidentified hidden card. A fix needs the view to carry what the viewer knows publicly about hidden cards that left public zones (for example per owner: the printing, variant, and persistent status of each card known to have entered a hidden zone, without position), and `deal` to assign those descriptors to dealt hidden cards of matching printing and variant. The determinization-fidelity invariant (`src/engine/testing/determinization-fidelity.ts`) would then compare such cards once they return to a public zone. hv-umm2.392 only matches a hidden captured member's stand-in on owner, printing, and variant (`standIns`), not on status.
