# Pre-existing issues found by hv-umm2.70

- `src/engine/rules/challenge.ts` (`designateChallengers`, `designateBlockers`, `resolveLane`): designations are kept by instance id, and an instance keeps its id when it leaves play and returns, so a designated character that left play and came back into a front lane during Night would still challenge or block. No effect reaches that today (cards enter play in the back rank and Night allows no repositioning); the Phase 5 `move` primitive should decide whether leaving play clears a designation. The challenger and blocker selectors (`src/engine/dsl/selectors.ts`) read the same designations, so they follow whatever `resolveLane` does.
- `src/engine/rules/challenge.ts` (`resolveLane`): a challenger or blocker exhausted by an effect after its designation still challenges or blocks, while rules § Exhaust and Awaken says an exhausted character cannot challenge or block; the rules do not say whether exhaustion after designation removes the role. The selectors follow `resolveLane`.
- `src/battle/components/EngineDebugPanel.tsx` (`window.__engineProbe`): probe events record `instance` only for events with an `instance` field, so `triggerQueued` and `triggerResolved` carry `null` and a QA scenario cannot tell which source's trigger resolved without reading the committed state.
