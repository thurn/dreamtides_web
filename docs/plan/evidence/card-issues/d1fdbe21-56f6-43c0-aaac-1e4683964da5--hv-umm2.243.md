# d1fdbe21-56f6-43c0-aaac-1e4683964da5: Void-play rematerialization has an off-play subject

- Bead: hv-umm2.243.
- Printed text: “When you play a character from your void, rematerialize it.”
- Rules decision: RD-hv-umm2.243-1, existing normative rules; ambiguity ladder not invoked.

A qualifying play puts its physical character card on the stack and triggers Bell. Bell resolves before the opponent receives priority. Its subject is on the stack at that time, and Rematerialize applies to a character in play, so the ability has no rematerialization effect in this ordinary workflow. The character’s eventual stack resolution produces one normal materialization. Both Reclaim and a permission to play from the void follow this sequence.

The authored composition implements the printed trigger and instruction under the current rules. The printed text stays unchanged. A semantic change that delays Bell until the character enters play requires a separate authorized design decision; it is not inferred here.

A typed public synthetic probe covers both seats, qualifying Reclaim and distinct permission plays, hand and event exclusions, a reload of the played state, and a positive in-play rematerialization control. All eight normative cases pass; all six in-play controls emit rematerialization. Four hypothetical delayed-benefit assertions fail and reject that inferred exception, rather than identify an engine defect.
