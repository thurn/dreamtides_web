- Problem: the text reads "Look at the top 4 cards of your deck. Choose one: …", which suggests choosing the mode after seeing the cards, but a played card's mode is chosen as it is played, before the look; the text also leaves the undrawn cards' destination unstated.
- Implemented as: the mode is chosen as the card is played (RD-hv-umm2.58-2); as it resolves its controller looks at the top 4 cards, draws 1 of them, or 2 that gain Ephemeral, and the rest stay on top in their order (RD-hv-umm2.58-1).
- Suggested fix: "Choose one: Look at the top 4 cards of your deck and draw 1 of them; or look at the top 4 cards of your deck and draw 2 of them with ephemeral."
