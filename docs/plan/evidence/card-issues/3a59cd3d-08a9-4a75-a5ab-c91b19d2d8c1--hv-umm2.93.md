- Problem: "When you play a card from your void, return this character to play" names no zone for the ability or the return. Other cards with the same return name it ("return this character from your void to play", "materialize this character from your void"), and the rules make a triggered ability work in play unless it names another zone.
- Implemented as: an ability that works while Graywatch is in its owner's void and returns it from there; it does nothing in play or elsewhere, and does not see Graywatch's own play from the void (RD-hv-umm2.93-1). The amplified "with awakened" returns it without the exhausted status (RD-hv-umm2.93-2).
- Suggested fix: "When you play a card from your void, return this character from your void to play." (amplified: "… from your void to play with awakened.")
