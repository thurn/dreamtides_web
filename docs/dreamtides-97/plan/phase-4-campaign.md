# Phase 4: The Campaign (M3)

**Status: outline.** Phase 3's filing bead for this phase turns it into a full
page.

**Goal:** the whole campaign:

- five assembled acts;
- Limbo and Apollyon;
- the reward bag's layers;
- refills;
- Avatar characters, Avatar quests and switching;
- difficulty;
- a catalog of about 40 dreams, so runs stop repeating.

## Earliest start

The phase starts at the Phase 3 gate. Content beads may start as soon as the
Phase 3 dream catalog schema and tooling have landed, since dreams are
act-agnostic.

## Task outline

**Systems:**

- **Multi-act assembly** (Appendix B §B3–B4): the acts' exit doors, tiers by
  act, incarnation draws, and Limbo placement.
- **Bag layers by act** (Appendix A §A2), with logging.
- **Refills** (§B8).
- **Avatar characters:**
  - placement, at most one appearance each (§B7);
  - no self-encounters, with replacements when an Avatar joins the roster
    (§B16);
  - Avatar quests in Acts 2 and 3;
  - switching at waypoints.
- **Amulet changers and high-ante challengers** (§A9–A10).
- **Difficulty:** Standard, Hard and Nightmare, with the Nightmare unlock
  (§A18), and the full tier knob table (§B9).
- **Score targets by act,** and the D97 score (§A19).
- **Limbo:** its fixed map, the five Limbo incarnations, and Apollyon built
  from the five act incarnations (Appendix C §C3–C4).
- **The campaign victory** and the run summary.

**Content:**

- **More dreams.** About 32 more catalog dreams, as concept-and-map and
  residents beads with batched QA, until each category has at least 8. The
  elven realm, snowfield and pirate island tilesets need their art sourced
  first.
- **The Limbo map,** and the incarnations' and Apollyon's dialogue.
- **Through-line dialogue** for the Dream Guides across acts.

**Filing later phases.** Two `filing` beads run inside this phase, so the
next phases overlap it:

- **File Phase 5** (`filing=d97-phase-5`), after the multi-act assembly and
  bag-layer beads;
- **File Phase 6** (`filing=d97-phase-6`), after the assembly and economy
  beads (bag layers, refills, amulets, difficulty and score targets), so the
  simulator can start.

**Phase end:**

- the refactor-and-tooling pass;
- the retrospective;
- the gate review;
- the gate.

## Exit gate (draft)

- A campaign is playable from Avatar select to Apollyon's defeat.
- Every golden and milestone fixture loads, migrated, and the gate commits
  the M3 milestone fixture.
- The catalog holds about 40 dreams.
- Reviews are resolved.
