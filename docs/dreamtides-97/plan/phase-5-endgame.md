# Phase 5: The Endgame (M4)

**Status: outline.** Its filing bead turns it into a full page.

**Goal:** the endgame of keys and key dreams after Apollyon, and from Act 4
on (Appendix C §C5–C8, Appendix A §A16).

[Back to the plan](README.md)

## Earliest start

The phase starts once Phase 4's multi-act assembly and bag-layer beads have
landed. It may overlap the Phase 4 content waves.

## Task outline

- **Keys.** Sources (challenger extras from Act 4, the Act 4 and 5
  incarnations, finished key dreams), the rolled contents, and the key UI.
- **Key dreams.** Instancing a catalog dream with no guardians, one attempt
  per challenger, the open-key-dream limit (as decided in 2.1), and the
  finish and close rules.
- **Prizes:**
  - Legendary cards;
  - Legendary Dreamsigns, after a curation bead that picks the ten (S12);
  - locked Avatars, with a key dream's Avatar prize appearing as its
    guardian;
  - Perfected supply cards.
- **The endgame Atlas:** open key dreams, remaining challengers and the prize.
- **Phase end:**
  - the refactor-and-tooling pass;
  - the retrospective;
  - the gate review;
  - the gate.

## Exit gate (draft)

- After Apollyon, keys open key dreams, clears pay prizes and keys, and the
  endgame never runs dry.
- Every earlier golden save loads.
- Reviews are resolved.
