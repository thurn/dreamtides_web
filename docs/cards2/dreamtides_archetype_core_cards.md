# Dreamtides — Core Cards by Archetype

A companion to [dreamtides_archetypes.md](dreamtides_archetypes.md). Where
[dreamtides_archetype_cards.md](dreamtides_archetype_cards.md) lists *every* card
connected to an archetype, this document picks only the **core** cards: the 1–4
cards that most uniquely define what each archetype is trying to do. Smaller
archetypes get one or two; the broadest get four.

Each card appears under exactly one archetype — its home, where it is most
defining — even when it sees play across several decks. Archetypes follow the
order in `dreamtides_archetypes.md`.

## Aggro and Tempo

### Spirit Animals

- **Spirit of the Greenwood** — the 1● "mana dork" that turns a wide board into
  energy and powers explosive starts.
- **Dreamborne Leviathan** — each spirit animal you play spawns another, giving
  the deck its signature board flood.
- **Spiritbound Alpha** — the tribal anthem/finisher that pushes a flooded board
  through as lethal damage.
- **Spirit Bond** — the overrun payoff: unstoppable plus +X✦ scaling with your
  character count closes the game.

### Outsiders

- **Vanishing Inquisitor** — the cheap engine for the outsider bounce loop that
  re-buys ▸Materialized triggers and draws when it scores ⍟.
- **Driftrider** — the workhorse two-drop bouncer that converts every outsider
  score into foresee + a card.
- **Thronebound Arbiter** — the top-end bouncer whose ⍟ trigger draws, turning
  the grind itself into card advantage.

### Warrior Aggro

- **Tethered Hollow** — makes the first warrior each turn cost 3● less, enabling
  the explosive multi-warrior turns the deck is built on.
- **Sundown Ronin** — a global "warriors cost 1● less" that compounds the flood.
- **Skyflame Commander** — the Support anthem that scales every supported
  character with your warrior count.
- **Nocturne Strummer** — a mobile +2✦ Support body that amplifies the cheap
  board.

### Events

- **Gateweaver** — the canonical "when you play an event" payoff, spawning a
  figment every event.
- **The Waking Titan** — stores ⧗ per event and bounces the enemy board, the
  marquee events-matter engine.
- **Somber Flockmaster** — recurs events from the void with reclaim, keeping the
  event chain flowing.

### Survivors

- **Kindred Sparks** — the premier enabler: with another survivor it replays
  from hand or void for 1●.
- **Sorrow Watcher** — makes survivor figments and draws cards as the survivor
  count climbs.
- **Dustborn Veteran** — turns survivor deaths into fresh survivors, fueling the
  recursion engine.

## Midrange and Value

### Discard / "Madness"

- **Shadowcaller** — the iconic madness card: gains reclaim when discarded *or*
  eroded and makes a figment on every death.
- **Part the Veil** — erodes for value and gains reclaim when discarded, the
  archetypal "good to throw away" card.
- **Minstrel of Falling Light** — the discard outlet/draw engine that loots
  events and scales off the void.
- **Wheel of the Heavens** — the Wheel effect that refills the hand and floods
  the void in one shot.

### Abandon

- **Saltless Mariner** — the cheap, repeatable abandon outlet that converts
  fodder into ⍟.
- **Silent Avenger** — the premier abandon payoff: every dissolve becomes a
  point.
- **Obliterator of Worlds** — stores ⧗ per abandon and turns it into removal.
- **Skull Weaver** — gains ⍟ whenever any ally leaves play, rewarding a board
  built to die.

### Characters with Cost 2● or Less

- **Clockwork Conductor** — lets you replay cheap characters straight from the
  void, the recursion backbone.
- **Pathwalker** — a cheap survivor that buys back events and erodes from the
  void.
- **Abomination of Memory** — a 1● beater that swells to 5✦ off a void full of
  cheap characters, the deck's payoff.
- **Ferryman of the Falls** — draws a card whenever a cheap character hits the
  void and abandons for value, an 8✦ finisher.

### Blink

- **Passage Through Oblivion** — the cheap, reclaimable rematerialize spell that
  re-fires a ▸Materialized ability.
- **Starlight Guide** — a ▸Challenge body that rematerializes an ally on demand.
- **Duskmount** — an awakened engine that rematerializes an ally every turn.

## Combo

### Storm

- **Broadcast Array** — the unpreventable "for each card you've played this
  turn" payoff that converts a chain into ⍟.
- **Intermezzo Balladeer** — gains ⍟ for every card already played as you keep
  chaining.
- **Call of the Lost** — turns the chain into a wide figment board.
- **Echoes of the Journey** — refills the chain for free, drawing a card per
  card played at 0● each.

### Celestial Reverie Combo

- **Celestial Reverie** — draws a card on each character you play, the namesake
  refill engine.
- **Luminous Ascent** — draws on each materialize, deepening the loop and
  reclaiming itself.
- **Worlds Await** — the deterministic deck-out finisher the combo assembles
  toward.

### Warrior Combo

- **Spirit Reaping** — the abandon outlet that loops warriors into energy.
- **Aftermath Bloom** — the warrior tutor: abandon a small warrior to dig up and
  deploy a larger one.
- **Dreaming Obelisk** — the "welder" that recurs warriors from the void for
  near-infinite value.

### Reclaim Combo

- **Reclaimer of Lost Paths** — strips the reclaimed-banish rule so a Reclaim 0●
  body can be replayed endlessly, and hands out reclaim itself.
- **Titan of Forgotten Echoes** — the redundant copy of that key static.
- **Starrunner** — the Reclaim 0● engine body the combo loops for energy.

### Wake the Fallen / Shadow March Combo

- **Wake the Fallen** — mass-returns every character that dissolved this turn,
  re-firing all their triggers.
- **Shadow March** — the cheap-character variant of that mass return.
- **Keeper of Forgotten Light** — draws a card on each cheap materialize, turning
  the mass return into a card engine.

### Fading Farewell

- **Fading Farewell** — returns allies to hand as they dissolve, the loop's
  namesake centerpiece.
- **Conduit of Ashes** — the 0● body that, with a free abandon outlet, makes the
  loop infinite.

### Cindermarch / Shadow Soloist Combo

- **Cindermarch** — awakens your whole board on every materialize, going
  infinite with zero-cost character producers.
- **Shadow Soloist** — awakens each ally whenever you play an event, the
  event-driven engine.
- **Conduit of Resonance** — re-triggers every ally's ▸Dawn on each character
  you play.
