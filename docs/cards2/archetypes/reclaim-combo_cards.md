# Reclaim Combo — Card Pool

A companion to [reclaim-combo.md](reclaim-combo.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Reclaim Combo archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## The Static — the combo's heart

1. Reclaimer of Lost Paths — carries "Cards you reclaim are not banished when they leave play," the line that turns the loop on, and also grants reclaim to a void character for a second way to feed it.
2. Titan of Forgotten Echoes — the redundant copy of the static; running both makes drawing into the engine far more consistent.

## Reclaim 0● Bodies — the loop pieces

3. Starrunner — Reclaim 0● self-contained engine: reclaim for 0●, self-abandon for 1●, return under the static, net +1● per cycle.
4. Torn Circuit Feeder — Reclaim 0● body whose shrinking spark never matters; pure looping fuel.
5. Enginespeaker — Reclaim 0● body with all character types, relevant to the type-anthem package while it cycles.
6. Nocturne — Reclaim 0● body that erodes 1 every time it materializes, making the loop itself an erosion finisher.
7. Rusted Monolith — Reclaim 0● body that gains 1⍟ on every materialize, scoring a point each pass through the loop.

## Reclaim-granting engines (beyond the static)

8. Revenant of the Lost — "A character in your void with cost 2● or less gains reclaim until end of turn," a redundant way to hand reclaim to a loop body.
9. Path to Redemption — "Until end of turn, cards in your void gain reclaim," a mass grant that turns every body in the void into a loop piece under the static.

## Abandon Outlets

10. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●," the premier outlet that nets +1● per loop.
11. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●," the same job for double the energy banked each cycle.
12. Stargazer Adrift — "Abandon another character: Foresee 1," trades energy for sculpting the top of your deck while you loop.
13. The Dread Sovereign — "X●, ☾, Abandon another character: Discover a character with cost X●, then materialize it," an outlet that also tutors the turn's missing piece.

## Drain Payoffs — score ⍟ off each abandon

14. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟," scoring once per looped abandon for unbounded points.
15. Saltless Mariner — "Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟" behind Veil 2●, an outlet and payoff in one body.
16. Twilight Suppressor — banks a ⧗ on each character played, then spends it as "Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟."
17. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟," a trigger-side drain firing on every loop abandon.
18. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟," a cheap redundant copy of the Silent Avenger drain.
19. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟," scoring off every body the loop sends to the void.
20. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟," another redundant dissolve drain.
21. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟," an outlet that scores directly.

## Erosion Payoffs — mill the opponent into Fatigue

22. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X," looping a body grinds the opponent's deck to empty and into doubling Fatigue.
23. Ashen Remnant — "Abandon another character: Erode 1," adding erosion to every loop abandon.
24. Ashborn Necromancer — "Abandon a character: Erode 2," a stronger erosion outlet for the mill kill.
25. Obliterator of Worlds — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗" feeding "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," erosion-adjacent and repeatable removal.
26. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗" feeding "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," banking removal off the loop.

## Reclaim-specific and energy payoffs

27. Thornwood Delver — "Once per turn, when you reclaim a card, gain X⍟... then this character gains +1✦," a payoff that scales with the very act of reclaiming.
28. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●," a second free-energy source banking off every looped abandon.
29. Eclipse Herald — ⧗ removal that converts the loop's banked energy straight into dissolves.
30. Horizon Follower — "4●: Gain 1⍟," a flat energy sink that turns infinite energy into points.

## Tutors and Re-entry

31. Door to Possibility — "Discover an event with cost X," digging toward the missing combo half.
32. Curio Dealer — "1●, Discard a character with cost X●: Discover a character with cost X●," tutoring while discarding a Reclaim 0● body straight into the void.
33. Borrowed Minutes — "Materialize each character with cost X● or less in your void," a mass re-entry that fires every ▸Materialized trigger (a void of Nocturnes) at once.

## Bodies That Earn Their Place

34. Momentum's Edge — picks a type and gives it +1✦, leaning on Enginespeaker and Ashen Harbinger for an anthem on the loop bodies.
35. Ashen Harbinger — "This character has all character types," supporting the type-anthem package.
36. Cloudmantle Ray — ▸Dawn energy dork with Reclaim 3●, recurring fuel if not free.
37. Selfless Rescuer — "❖❖ – Abandon another character: An ally cannot be targeted by effects this turn," an Interrupt outlet that protects the static mid-loop.
38. Gateway Defender — the same protection on "❖❖ – Abandon this character," shielding the static when the opponent reaches for it.
39. Blade of Unity — an X/X threat to round out the curve and close a fair game.
40. Molten Duel — cheap fast removal to clear blockers and protect the engine.
