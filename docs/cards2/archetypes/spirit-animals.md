# Spirit Animals — Deep Dive

A companion to the [Spirit Animals](../dreamtides_archetypes.md#spirit-animals)
entry in the archetype overview. Spirit Animals is the fast, redundant character
deck: the curve tops out low, the board floods, and the game ends on an explosive
turn driven by a wall of 1● bodies. The plan plays out like a Storm deck a lot of
the time — your characters are simultaneously your energy and your card draw — but
it can also win a perfectly fair game on speed alone, which makes it one of the
fastest decks in the format. There is heavy overlap with the
[Celestial Reverie Combo](../dreamtides_archetype_cards.md#celestial-reverie-combo),
and the two share most of their core, but Spirit Animals can win without ever
drawing the combo piece.

The cards that anchor the deck are **Celestial Reverie** (the draw engine),
**Spirit of the Greenwood** (the energy engine), and **Spirit Bond** (the
finisher). Everything else is built to chain those three together.

## The Spirit Animal core

When the deck is at its most focused it wants to be wall-to-wall 1● spirit
animals. Keeping the curve flat is the whole point: the more cheap bodies you
draw in a row, the more your engines snowball, and a flooded back rank converts
directly into both energy and ⍟.

Two characters do the heavy lifting for grinding through a fair game.
**Dreamvale Monarch** is the redundant card-advantage body — "▸Materialized: Draw
a card" — so every copy you play replaces itself and keeps you digging. **Moonbound
Wolf** is the recursion engine: it enters Awakened, and its activated ability is
"☪, Return an ally to hand: Trigger the '▸Dawn' ability of an allied spirit
animal." Bouncing a body to hand means you get to replay it for another
▸Materialized trigger, so once you have a Wolf and a Monarch you do not need to
keep finding fresh characters in your deck — you recycle the same one for repeated
draws. The Wolf also re-fires ▸Dawn abilities on demand, which matters a great
deal once **Cloudmantle Ray** ("▸Dawn: Gain 1●") and **Sky Voyager** ("▸Dawn: Gain
1●") are on the board.

**Stoneborn Eternal** is the cheapest possible self-recurring body: "When you play
a spirit animal, return this character to hand. It costs 0● this turn." Every
spirit animal you play after it bounces it back to your hand for free, so a single
copy keeps re-entering play all turn, feeding every "when you play a spirit animal"
and "when you materialize a spirit animal" trigger in the deck. It is the spirit
animal you never run out of.

## Energy

The obvious question for a deck that wants to dump its whole hand in one turn is
how it pays for all of it. The answer is that the characters pay for themselves.

**Spirit of the Greenwood** is the engine the deck is named around — it enters
Awakened, so it produces the turn it lands, and its ability is "1●, ☪: Gain 1● for
each allied character." With a flooded board it is a net-positive energy faucet,
and because each 1● spirit animal you have already played makes its activation
bigger, every cheap body you commit is effectively "free" once the Greenwood is
online. **Blazing Emberwing** ("☪: Gain 1● for each allied character") is the same
engine without the up-front 1●, and it counts every character, not only spirit
animals.

The tribal energy producers scale with how wide you have gone.
**Mountainwatch Alpha** ("☪: Gain 1● for each allied spirit animal") and
**Ghostlight Wolves** ("☪: Gain 1● for each allied spirit animal", plus "Support –
Supported spirit animals have +2✦") both reward a board stuffed with the type, and
the Wolves double as a back-rank anthem that pushes the front rank's spark up. The
key with all of these is to play as many bodies as you can before you tap them, so
you extract the maximum from each ☪.

A second set of producers triggers off the act of playing characters rather than
off a ☪. **Melodist of the Finale** is "Veil 2●. When you play a character, gain
1●", so it refunds energy on every card you chain — the closer the deck gets to a
Storm turn, the more it produces, and the Veil keeps it alive through removal.
**Seedling Sage** ("When you play a character, store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●") and
**Sunshadow Eagle** ("When you materialize a spirit animal, store 1⧗. 3⧗: Gain
3●") bank counters as you go wide and cash them back into energy mid-combo.
**Cloudmantle Ray** is the early accelerant — a 1● body that produces 1● each Dawn
and carries Reclaim 3●, so it comes back from the void when you need another
producer.

The single most explosive energy card is **Nexus Wayfinder**: "Characters with
cost 2● or less cost you 0●." With it in play your entire low curve is free, and
since the deck is mostly 1● and 2● bodies, a Wayfinder turns a full hand into a
single uninterrupted deployment. The same effect on a smaller scale comes from
**Heavenward Penitent** ("The first character with cost 2● or less you play each
turn costs you 0●"), which makes the first cheap drop of each turn free even
without the full lockout.

A handful of these producers double as the engine for the combo build. With a
**Celestial Reverie** active ("Until end of turn, when you play a character, draw a
card"), every free body off **Nexus Wayfinder** is also a card, so the deck draws
into more bodies and more energy at the same time — the loop only stops when you
choose to stop or run out of board space.

## Win conditions

There are many ways to close, and which you reach for depends on what enablers you
have assembled.

The default finisher is **Spirit Bond**: "Until end of turn, allied characters
have +X✦ where X is the number of allied characters." On a wide board this is an
alpha strike — every front-rank challenger swings for its spark plus the full
board count. With a back rank
also stacked, repositioning fresh bodies forward before the strike turns the whole
flood into points in a single Challenge phase.

**Mystic Runefish** ("5●, ☪: Each allied spirit animal's ✦ becomes 7") is the
tribal nuke: it sets every spirit animal to 7✦ at once, which a wide board
converts into a crushing front rank. It is also resilient — once the board is big
enough, a single activation is lethal, so the deck keeps energy open through the
energy engines to fire it the moment it is safe. **Field Reverent** ("X●: Each
allied character's ✦ becomes X until end of turn") does the same job scaled to your
energy total, hitting every character rather than only spirit animals, so it is the
finisher when the board is mixed rather than pure tribe.

**Dreamborne Leviathan** ("Once per turn, when you play a spirit animal,
materialize a 1✦ spirit animal figment") and **Spiritbound Alpha**
("❖ – 5●: Allied spirit animals gain +3✦ and vengeful until end of turn") both turn
the deck into an army. The Leviathan widens the board for free every turn — and
because spirit animal figments stack into one shared position, it builds a single
tall stack that absorbs spark from the top down in a challenge while still scoring
its full total. The Alpha is the Fast finisher: +3✦ and Vengeful across the tribe
during the opponent's window means even unfavorable challenges trade up, since a
Vengeful character that loses still dissolves its opponent.

For the all-in builds, **Crumbling Behemoth** ("Other allied characters have 10✦")
is the top-end payoff — a single resolution makes the entire rest of your board a
10✦ wall — and it can be found rather than drawn. **Beacon of Tomorrow** ("Choose a
value of X. Discover a character with cost X●") is the deck's tutor, fetching
whichever finisher the board wants, while **The Dread Sovereign**
("X●, ☪, Abandon another character: Discover a character with cost X●, then
materialize it") both finds and deploys it in one activation, converting a spare
body directly into the missing piece.

If you cannot win on your turn, hold energy and a producer open to protect the
engine and re-activate **Mystic Runefish** or **Spiritbound Alpha** the following
turn. With a strong enough board you do not need the Celestial Reverie loop at all.

## Going wider with figments

The deck leans on created bodies to outpace a fair opponent. **Endless Projection**
("When you play a character, materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment") makes a figment
every time you deploy, so a Storm-style chain leaves a row of figments behind it.
**Radiant Trio** ("Materialize X 1✦ ethereal figments") is the burst version —
paid out for a large X it floods the board in one card, and every figment counts
toward both your energy engines and your **Spirit Bond** count. Because ethereal
figments of the same type share a single stacked position, these effects build tall
stacks rather than filling all nine slots, which keeps room open for your named
bodies.

**Boundless Wanderer** ("This character has all character types. 1●: Allied
characters have all character types until end of turn") and **Sky Voyager** ("This
character has all character types") make every body count as a spirit animal, so
your figments and off-type characters all feed the tribal producers and the
Runefish. **Momentum's Edge** ("▸Materialized: Choose a character type. This
character is that character type. Characters of the chosen type have +1✦") names
the tribe and turns into a spirit animal anthem on top.

## Awaken: attacking the turn you flood

A board that materializes exhausted cannot challenge the turn it lands, so the
deck carries ways to wake itself up and swing immediately. **Verdant Pilgrim**
("☪: Awaken an ally") wakes one body at a time, while **Cindermarch** ("When you
materialize a character, awaken each other allied character") wakes the whole board
on every deployment — so a Storm turn that floods bodies also un-exhausts them,
letting the entire flood challenge the same turn it was played. **Pyrestone Avatar**
("Offering. Allied characters have awakened") and **Pinnacle Ascendant** ("Allied
characters have awakened. When you discard or erode this card, it gains reclaim
0●") are static versions: while either is in play every character you materialize
enters ready to fight. The Avatar's Offering line lets you drop it for 0● by
banishing a card from hand, so it can come down free on the turn you go off.

**Conduit of Resonance** ("When you play a character, trigger the '▸Dawn' ability
of each other allied character") turns a wide board of ▸Dawn producers into a
deployment engine — every character you play re-fires every **Cloudmantle Ray** and
**Sky Voyager** for a wave of energy. **Unquenched** ("When you materialize an ally,
trigger its '▸Dawn' ability") does the same on materialization, so a fresh
Cloudmantle Ray pays you the moment it enters.

## Card advantage and tutors

Beyond **Dreamvale Monarch** and the Celestial Reverie loop, the deck has dedicated
diggers. **Eternal Stag** ("Allied spirit animals have +1✦. 2●, ☪: Draw a spirit
animal") is both an anthem and a tribal draw engine, refilling on exactly the cards
the deck wants. **Oathbound Pair** ("▸Materialized, Dawn: Give an ally +1✦") grows a
threat a little every turn and again the moment it lands, a steady spark gain that
adds up across a long game.

**Beacon of Tomorrow** and **The Dread Sovereign** are the tutors covered above;
**Curio Dealer** ("Veil 2●. 1●, Discard a character with cost X●: Discover a
character with cost X●") trades a stranded card for the exact body you need at the
same cost, and survives removal behind Veil. **Winterbough Monk** ("▸Materialized:
Return a card from your void to your hand") rebuys a spent finisher or a discarded
spirit animal, and pairs with **Moonbound Wolf** or **Stoneborn Eternal** to re-fire
its trigger repeatedly.

## Disruption and interaction

A deck that wants to play a fairer game still needs answers. **Molten Duel**
("Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less") clears a blocker out of a key lane so your
challengers go unpaired and score. **Voidcaller** ("▸Materialized: Banish an enemy
warrior") is targeted hate against the Warrior decks, removing their best body the
moment it lands and leaving a 2✦ body behind. **Thundercatcher** ("2●, Abandon a 1✦
ally: Draw 2 cards") turns a spent 1● body — a figment, an exhausted energy dork,
a **Stoneborn Eternal** — into two fresh cards, smoothing out the grind.

## Reach into combo and value

The deck overlaps with several others and can be pushed in their direction.

Toward **Celestial Reverie Combo**: the engine pieces are already in the list.
**Nexus Wayfinder** plus **Celestial Reverie** plus self-bouncing bodies
(**Stoneborn Eternal**, **Heavenward Penitent**) turns into a draw-and-energy loop;
**Melodist of the Finale** keeps the energy positive while you chain. Closing the
loop is a matter of adding **Terminus** ("If you have no cards in your deck, you
win the game") for the empty-deck finish or **Hatching Ground** ("For each card
you've played this turn, chosen player erodes X") to Fatigue the opponent out.

Toward **Storm**: every "when you play a character" trigger in the deck —
**Melodist of the Finale**, **Endless Projection**, **Seedling Sage**,
**Conduit of Resonance** — counts the same cards, so the more bodies you chain the
more of everything you get. **Passage Through Oblivion** ("Rematerialize an ally.
Reclaim 1●") re-fires a ▸Materialized body for another draw or another figment and
comes back from the void to do it again.

Toward Abandon: **Ashen Remnant** ("This character has +1✦ for each character in
your void. Abandon another character: Erode 1") and **The Dread Sovereign** both
want to feed on your own spent bodies. These play differently from a pure tribal
list and want a void-centric shell rather than a slot or two in the all-in deck,
but the wide board of cheap, expendable spirit animals is exactly the fuel they ask
for. **Soulkindler** ("If this card is in your void, allied characters have +1✦")
rewards getting itself into the void early, becoming a permanent anthem from
outside play.

Toward a flicker/value plan: **Roots of Rebirth** ("Materialize two random
characters with cost 2● or less from your deck") deploys two bodies straight from
the deck for one card, and since the deck is wall-to-wall cheap characters it almost
never whiffs. With the curve already this low, the cards a slower value deck plays
on later turns land far ahead of schedule here, which is what lets Spirit Animals
turn the corner before the opponent has set up.
