# Events — Splash Cards

A companion to [events.md](events.md), listing the portable core of the Events archetype: the cards that stay good when mixed into a neighboring deck at low density. Most Dreamtides decks blend two or more archetypes, so this picks out the self-contained, flexible pieces — the cheap interactive events that are strong in any deck, plus the event-payoffs whose trigger still earns its keep at a moderate event count. The density-hungry payoffs that need the whole deck built around them are left to [events_cards.md](events_cards.md). Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Removal

1. Molten Duel — "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less" for 1● at fast/interrupt speed; premier cheap removal that is excellent in literally any deck regardless of event count.
2. Flashpoint Detonation — 2● fast "Dissolve an enemy with cost 3● or less"; a clean, unconditional answer to most of a low curve.
3. Scorched Reckoning — 2● "Dissolve an enemy with 3✦ or less"; reliable spot removal with no setup.
4. Shatter the Frail — 1● "Dissolve an enemy with 1✦ or less" with Reclaim 1●; cheap early-game removal that buys itself back.
5. Unleashed Destruction — 1● "Dissolve an enemy with cost 2● or less"; efficient answer to the cheap bodies that dominate the format.
6. Grounded — X-cost "Banish an enemy with cost X●" at fast/interrupt speed; a scalable catch-all that answers whatever the opponent commits, good in any interactive shell.
7. Fell Swoop — 1● "Dissolve an enemy with 1✦ or less" with Reclaim – Discard a card; cheap removal that recurs through a discard outlet.
8. Maelstrom Denial — 1● unconditional "Dissolve an enemy" with an abandon-or-discard cost; premium removal for any deck that can pay the small cost.
9. Ferryman's Tithe — Offering "Dissolve an enemy" with no ✦ or cost restriction; a flexible kill spell that can be cast for cheap by banishing a card.

## Prevents (Interrupts)

10. Toll of Passage — 1● "Prevent a played card with cost 2● or less"; held up cheaply, it stops a large fraction of a low curve in any matchup.
11. Defiant Parry — Offering counter, "Prevent a played card unless the opponent pays 1●", castable for 0● by banishing a card; protects your plan without tapping out.
12. Echoing Denial — Offering counter, "Prevent a played card" outright, castable for 0●; a free, flexible answer that any deck can hold up.
13. Infernal Rest — 1● "Prevent a played card unless the opponent pays 1●"; a cheap soft counter usable in any interactive build.
14. Abolish — 2● "Prevent a played card"; clean, unconditional interaction with no archetype requirement.
15. Keeper of the Tides — 2● body that, on ▸Materialized, prevents a played card with cost 2● or less; a counter attached to a repositionable character, good as a standalone tempo piece.

## Hand disruption

16. Lurking Dread — 1● "Discard a chosen card with cost 2● or less from the opponent's hand"; strips a key answer or threat for next to nothing.
17. Vertiginous Leap — 1● "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand"; unconditional hand disruption that fits any proactive deck.
18. Peak Plunder — 2● "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand" with Reclaim 1●; takes any card and comes back for a second pass.
19. Break the Veil — 1● "The opponent discards a card" with Reclaim – Discard a card; grinds the opponent's hand turn after turn and feeds your own void.
20. The Thinning — 1● "Banish a chosen character from the opponent's hand"; cheap, permanent removal of a threat from the opponent's plan.

## Card draw / dig

21. Epiphany Unfolded — 1● fast/interrupt "Draw 3 cards"; the premier cheap refuel, excellent card advantage in any deck.
22. Fragments of Vision — 1● fast/interrupt draw-3-then-shuffle-2; smooths and refills the hand for a single energy.
23. Gleam Below — 1● "Draw a card" plus Foresee 2; a cheap cantrip that digs toward what you need.
24. Guiding Light — 1● "Foresee 1. Draw a card." with Reclaim 4●; a recurring cantrip that smooths draws.
25. Glimpse of What Was — 1● fast/interrupt "Foresee 1. Draw a card."; a flexible cheap cantrip for any deck.
26. Sunken Radiance — 1● "Draw 2 cards, then discard 2 cards" with Reclaim 2●; digs while filling the void, valuable in any value or discard shell.
27. Data Pulse — 2● "Gain 2●. Draw a card."; a ritual cantrip that replaces itself and ramps a turn.

## Event tutoring

28. Door to Possibility — 1● "Discover an event with cost X"; fetches the exact piece of interaction you need, good even at low event counts.
29. Inverted Reflection — 1● fast "Discover an event"; smooths the interactive package for any deck running a handful of events.
30. Barrage Specialist — 2● body that, on ▸Materialized, Discovers an event with cost 2● or less; a fast standalone body that finds interaction.

## Event payoffs that splash

31. Gateweaver — 2● body, "When you play an event, materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment"; even at moderate event density it quietly builds a board, and it is a fine 2-drop on its own.
32. Oracle of Shifting Skies — 1● body, "When you play an event, foresee 1"; a cheap, low-floor payoff whose trigger smooths draws and costs nothing to include.
33. Twilight Troubadour — 2● body, "When you play an event, materialize a 1✦ warrior figment"; goes wide off the cheap interaction you were already casting.
34. Starcatcher — 3● body, "When you play an event, gain 1●"; turns interaction into tempo, helpful in any deck running several events.
35. Somber Flockmaster — 2● body, on ▸Dawn a void event with cost ≤ its ✦ gains reclaim 0●; recurs a spent removal spell or prevent for free, a standalone value engine even with few events.
36. Gearwright — 2● body, "When you play an event, draw a card, then discard a card"; loots off interaction, digging and filling the void in any value or discard build.
