# Discard / Madness — Card Pool

A companion to [discard-madness.md](discard-madness.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Discard / Madness archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## Discard outlets

1. Evacuation Enforcer — 1● awakened "☪: Draw 2, then discard 3"; the premier repeatable outlet and the archetype's signal.
2. Pulse of Sacrifice — 0●: discard your hand, gain 3●; the explosive enabler that dumps the hand and pays to replay it.
3. Curio Dealer — "1●, Discard a character: Discover a character with cost X●"; tutors while it fills the void.
4. Chronicle Reclaimer — 2● looter, "☪: Draw a card, then discard a card"; cheap repeatable outlet.
5. Manufactured Abomination — "☪, Discard a card: Draw a card"; repeatable looter body.
6. Liminal Dreamer — stores 1⧗ each Dawn and "Discard your hand: Draw one per stored ⧗"; empties the hand into the void for a refill.
7. Sunken Radiance — draw 2, discard 2, Reclaim 2●; loots and recurs itself.
8. Spiral Offering — "discard 2 cards" to draw 3; card advantage that fills the void.
9. Shadowpaw — "☪, Discard a card: Erode 2"; doubles each discard with milled cards.
10. Salvage Engine — "Discard a card: +2✦"; cheap repeatable discard outlet on a body.
11. Pathfinder Adrift — "play your second card: draw a card, then discard a card"; free incidental looting each turn.
12. Seeker for the Way — "▸Materialized: discard a card to draw a card," Reclaim 2●; outlet that recurs.
13. Grotto Seer — "▸Challenge: Discard your hand, then draw 3"; a wheel-on-attack outlet.
14. Urban Cipher — "▸Materialized: Discard your hand, then draw 3"; self-contained wheel on a body.
15. Void Pilgrim — "▸Challenge: Draw a card, then discard a card"; loots while attacking.
16. Dreadcall Warden — "▸Night: Draw a card, then discard a card"; recurring incidental outlet.
17. Iconoclast — "Discard a card: Draw two cards with ephemeral"; high-rate discard outlet.
18. Astral Angler — "2●, Discard a character: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less"; points discards at removal.
19. Blade of Oblivion — "☪, Discard your hand, Abandon this character: Gain 3●"; a Pulse-style hand-dump on a body.
20. Skies of Change — "discard a card" to draw 2; cheap looting cantrip.
21. Call of Calamity — "discard X cards" to draw X+1; scalable hand-into-void converter.
22. Seaside Requiem — "abandon a character or discard a card" to draw 2; flexible incidental outlet.
23. A New Adventure — draw 2, discard 2, gain 3●; loots and ramps.
24. Shared Revelation — draw 2, then discard a card; incidental loot.
25. Sanctum Approach — "Draw X, then discard X"; scalable looter that also makes figments per event discarded.
26. Lone Castaway — "To play, discard a character"; a 0● body that dumps a madness creature into the void.
27. Maelstrom Denial — "abandon a character or discard a card" to dissolve an enemy; discard-as-removal.
28. Pyrokinetic Surge — "discard a card or pay 5⍟" to dissolve an enemy; discard powers removal.
29. Wandering Archivist — "2●, ☪: Draw a card, then discard a card"; outlet that also drains the opponent (see payoffs).
30. Tidewreck Navigator — "☪, Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ survivor figment"; outlet that builds a board.
31. Harborwarden — "☪, Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment"; outlet for aggressive builds.
32. Hope's Vanguard — "Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ survivor figment"; cheap repeatable discard outlet.
33. Shoreline Penitent — "3●, ☪, Discard a card: Draw a card" plus reclaim-on-discard; outlet and payoff in one.
34. Gleamharvester — "▸Challenge: discard a card to draw"; outlet that also spikes on discard (see payoffs).
35. Rubble Diviner — "▸Challenge: pay 2● and discard a card to draw two"; pulls cards while attacking.
36. Shadowprowler — "▸Challenge: discard a card to give +2✦"; turns a discard into a swing.
37. Wraith of Twisting Shadows — "☪, Discard a card: Draw a character with cost 2● or less"; outlet that also scales on the void.
38. Lumineth — "☪, Discard a character: Discover a character with cost 1● higher"; outlet that tutors up.
39. Shatterpoint Agent — "▸Materialized: discard a card to discover a costlier character"; one-shot outlet/tutor.

## Wheel / mass outlets

40. Starfall Communion — each player discards their hand and draws 5; mass refill that fills the void.
41. Wheel of the Heavens — shuffle both hands and voids into decks, draw 5; mass reload.
42. Reunion — shuffle hand and void into deck, draw 5, gain 5●; mass refill and ramp.
43. Across the Void — shuffle hand and void into deck, draw 5; Reclaim – 2●, Abandon a character; the recurring wheel.
44. Aurora Confluence — discard X from a player's hand, draw X; flexible mass discard/refill.
45. Cascade of Reflections — copies events you play; doubles a wheel into a draw-10.

## Discard payoffs

46. Minstrel of Falling Light — loots events for free and "+1✦ for each event in your void"; the strongest discard payoff.
47. Key Sifter — "when you discard one or more cards, draw that many"; makes each turn's first discard free.
48. Flagbearer of Decay — "When you discard a card, you may play it from your void this turn"; open-ended madness payoff.
49. Wandering Archivist — "When you discard a card, gain 1⍟"; turns every discard into a drain.
50. Gleamharvester — "When you discard a card, +2✦ until end of turn"; spark/tempo on every discard.
51. Pattern Seeker — "When you discard a card, draw a card with ephemeral"; free card per discard.
52. Torchbearer of the Abyss — "Once per turn, when you discard a card, draw a card and gain 1●"; loots and ramps on discard.
53. Unbroken — "When you discard a card, this character gains +1✦"; cheap growing discard payoff.
54. Radiants' Captain — "When you discard a card, store 1⧗" then makes figments; converts discards into a board.
55. Emberwatch Veteran — "Discard a card: gains vengeful"; turns a discard into a combat trick / removal.
56. Duneveil Vanguard — "Discard a character with cost 2● or less: Dissolve an enemy"; points discards at the board.
57. Gloomantler — "When you discard your third card in a turn, materialize this from your void," plus a void-play engine; a discard-built threat.

64. Shadowcaller — survivor figment engine; "When you discard or erode this, it gains reclaim 0●"; the keystone bridge piece.
65. Cradle of Storms — materialize all characters discarded this turn; reclaim 0● when discarded; a madness payoff and outlet cash-in.

68. Break the Veil — opponent discards, "Reclaim – Discard a card"; reclaimed by discarding.

76. Sanctum Awakened — draw 2, Reclaim 2●; recurring draw from the void.
77. Shatter the Frail — dissolve a 1✦ enemy, Reclaim 1●; recurring removal.
78. Speaker for the Forgotten — "5●: Erode 1, that card gains reclaim 0●"; turns the void into replayable cards.
79. Ashen Avenger — "☪: a void card gains Reclaim – X●, Discard a card"; hands madness-style reclaim to anything.
80. Ashlight Caller — "Discard a card: a void event gains reclaim"; discard outlet that recurs spells.
81. Dune Reaper — "2●, Banish a card from void: Materialize this from void"; a recurring body the deep void enables.
