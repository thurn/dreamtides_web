# Storm — Card Pool

A companion to [storm.md](storm.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Storm archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Per-cast payoffs (the count cash-outs)

1. Broadcast Array — Unpreventable 4● event that gains 2⍟ for each card you've played this turn; the marquee storm capstone.
2. Intermezzo Balladeer — Scores 1⍟ per other card played this turn on every play; resolve early and the whole chain pays you.
3. Call of the Lost — Materializes a 1✦ awakened ember figment for each card played this turn, with Reclaim 2● for a second wave.
4. Hatching Ground — X-cost: chosen player erodes X for each card played this turn; the Fatigue close.
5. Packcaller of Shadows — Materializes a 1✦ ethereal figment for each card played this turn; a second go-wide count payoff at 2●.

## Per-cast engines (pay as the chain runs)

6. Intermezzo Balladeer — (see above) the premier score-as-you-go engine.
7. Starcatcher — Gain 1● on each event played; energy that refunds the chain per cast.
8. Gearwright — Loots on each event played, filtering the chain toward payoffs.
9. Shadow Soloist — Awakens each ally on each event, turning a cheap-event chain into an attacking board.
10. Moonlit Dancer — +1✦ to allies on each event, pushing the awakened board toward ⍟.
11. Oracle of Shifting Skies — Foresee 1 on each event, smoothing draws across the chain.
12. Gateweaver — Materializes a 1✦ ethereal figment on each event, building a board mid-chain.
13. Twilight Troubadour — Materializes a 1✦ warrior figment on each event; another per-event token engine.
14. Celestial Reverie — Until end of turn, draw a card when you play a character; a per-cast refill for character-heavy chains.
15. Keeper of Forgotten Light — Draws an ephemeral card on each cheap non-figment character materialized; per-cast fuel for cheap-creature storms.

## Energy engines (rituals and free-play enablers)

16. Genesis Burst — Doubles your current ●; the most explosive ritual once a pool exists.
17. Flash of Power — Gain 5● for 3●; net +2● and a card on the count.
18. Arc Gate Opening — Gain 4● for 1●; net +3●.
19. Glimpse of Infinity — Gain 3● for 0●; free energy and a free count.
20. Canopy of Stars — Gain 2● for 1●; cheap chain fuel.
21. Starfall — Gain 3● for 1●; flat ritual.
22. Nebula's Wake — Gain 5● for 2●, trivially online once events pile in your void mid-chain.
23. Data Pulse — Gain 2● and draw a card; ritual that replaces itself.
24. A New Adventure — Draw 2, discard 2, gain 3●; smooths the hand while paying for plays.
25. Reunion — Shuffle hand and void into deck, draw 5, gain 5●; a mid-chain reload.
26. Pulse of Sacrifice — Discard hand, gain 3● for 0●; the all-in burst that feeds void recursion.

## Card flow (keep the chain going)

27. Echoes of the Journey — Draw an ephemeral 0● card for each card played this turn; the dedicated late-chain refill.
28. Epiphany Unfolded — Draw 3 for 1●; cheap three-for-one that fires extra triggers when copied.
29. Iconoclast — Discard a card to draw two ephemeral cards; refills with must-spend-this-turn fuel.
30. Pattern Seeker — Draws an ephemeral card whenever you discard; turns loot/discard into chain fuel.
31. Prophet of the Consumed — On materialize, draw a cost-2●-or-less card that costs 0●; a free body to add to the chain.

## Copy effects (the heart of the engine)

32. Cascade of Reflections — Until end of turn, copy each event you play; doubles every ritual, cantrip, and payoff.
33. Echo Architect — Passive permanent copy of each event (events cost 1● more); a surviving body makes the next turn a storm turn.
34. Cascading Detonation — Next event this turn copies twice; Reclaim 5● for a second use.
35. Cosmonaut of Tides — On materialize, the next cost-2●-or-less event this turn copies twice.
36. The Ringleader — On materialize, makes a 0● ephemeral copy of the next event you play this turn.
37. Echoes of Eternity — Next cost-2●-or-less event this turn copies; a cheap single-shot copy enabler.
38. Twice-Lit Portal — Next event this turn copies (Offering); another single-shot doubler.

## Void recursion (replay the spent chain)

39. Path to Redemption — Until end of turn, all void cards gain reclaim; replay every spent ritual and cantrip.
40. From the Barrow — Until end of turn, void events gain reclaim; carries its own reclaim 3● to survive discard.
41. Archive of the Forgotten — Up to 2 cheap void events gain reclaim 0● until end of turn; hands back two free plays.
42. Dreadwood Emissary — On materialize, a void event gains reclaim until end of turn; a cheap body that buys back a piece.

## Cheap point and removal fuel

43. Derelict Voyage — Gain 3⍟ for 1● (6⍟ if reclaimed, Reclaim 4●); a cheap play that is its own payoff.
44. Fleeting Reunion — Gain X⍟, reclaim by discarding X cards; converts a flooded hand into points late.
45. Burst of Obliteration — X-cost: gain X⍟ or dissolve with X✦; dumps leftover energy into points.
46. Molten Duel — Dissolve a 3✦-or-less character for 1●; cheap removal that nudges the count.
47. Shattering Gambit — Banish an enemy for 1● (opponent gains 5⍟); cheap fast removal on the count.
48. Vertiginous Leap — Strip a chosen card from the opponent's hand for 1●; cheap disruption that counts.

## Free/cheap chain pieces and recursion bodies

49. Searcher in the Mists — Erode 4 on materialize and when dissolved; fills the void to fuel reclaim and empty-deck/Fatigue plans.
50. Silent Gatherer — Returns itself from void to hand on ▸Dawn; a recurring cheap play that never runs out.
51. Dreaming Obelisk — On ☪ and an abandon, materializes a warrior from void; recurs bodies into the chain.
52. Signal Resonant — 1✦ synth with all character types and a Fast reposition; a cheap body that slots any count.
53. Boundless Wanderer — Grants all character types so type-payoffs reach the whole team; cheap glue for a wide storm board.
54. Field Reverent — X● sets each ally's ✦ to X until end of turn; converts a wide figment board into a real clock.
55. Terminus — If your deck is empty you win; the deterministic finish for a mill/draw-to-empty storm line.

## Abandon / drain overlap

56. Silent Avenger — Gain 1⍟ whenever any character is dissolved; drips points as a wide board trades.
57. Fathomless Maw — Gain 1⍟ each time you abandon a character.
58. Kindlehorn — Stores 1⧗ on each abandon; spends X⧗ to dissolve an enemy with X✦.
59. Infernal Ascendant — Abandon another character: gain 1●; turns abandons into chain energy.
60. Spirit Reaping — Abandon a warrior: gain 2●; an abandon ritual feeding the count.
