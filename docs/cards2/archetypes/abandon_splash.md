# Abandon — Splash Cards

A companion to [abandon.md](abandon.md), listing the portable core of the Abandon archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density, so a neighboring deck can splash 5-10 Abandon pieces without building around them. These are the flexible outlets, self-sufficient bodies, and overlapping removal that carry their own value rather than needing a full sacrifice engine to function. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Free and cheap outlets

1. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●"; a free, uncapped outlet on a 3-cost body. Even with a handful of fodder it converts a doomed or stalled body into energy and a ▸Dissolved trigger every turn, with no engine required around it.
2. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●"; the best ramp outlet in the pool. At any density it turns a single spare body into a two-energy refund, which any deck running cheap characters can use.
3. Saltless Mariner — "Veil 2●. Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟"; a self-contained outlet that carries its own point payoff, so you need no second card to profit from an abandon. Veil 2● makes it sticky in a deck that isn't otherwise protecting it.
4. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X (the cost)"; pairs an outlet with an erosion clock on a 3✦ warrior body. One spare body a turn quietly grinds the opponent's deck toward Fatigue without needing a dedicated abandon loop.
5. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●"; a cheap 2-cost body whose repeatable energy outlet is useful in any deck with small characters to feed it.
6. Nightmare Manifest — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. ☪, Abandon a character: Gain 1●"; a 1-cost body that ramps on its own and offers an extra energy outlet, fine even without other abandon pieces.
7. Soulbinder — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●"; a cheap, repeatable outlet that slowly converts spare bodies into energy without any payoff support.
8. Ashborn Necromancer — "Abandon a character: Erode 2"; a 2-cost outlet that doubles as self-mill and an erosion source, standalone-useful in void-leaning decks.

## Self-abandoning fodder (works alone)

9. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●"; a 0● 0✦ body that is both fodder and a free outlet for itself. It is a Lotus-Petal-style ritual any deck can run, and it self-triggers every "when an ally is dissolved" effect you do have.
10. Starrunner — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. Abandon this character: Gain 1●. Reclaim 0●"; a recurring self-outlet that ramps, abandons itself for energy, and comes back from the void for free.
11. Junkfield Renegade — "1●, Abandon this character: Gain 2⍟ or Draw a card"; a 1-cost body that cashes itself in for points or a card on demand, with no support needed.
12. Ochre Prospector — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; a self-replacing outlet body that nets a card whenever it dies.
13. Blightmaw — "Abandon this character: Draw a card"; a 1-cost self-cycling warrior that replaces itself, splashable as a free card-flow body.
14. Rebirth Ritualist — "Abandon this character: Draw a card with ephemeral"; 0-cost self-cycling fodder that any deck can use to thin and trigger a death payoff.
15. Dread Arbiter — "Abandon this character: Reveal the opponent's hand. Draw a card with ephemeral"; 0● 0✦ self-cycling fodder that adds a peek at the opponent's hand.
16. Starshot Gunner — "1●, Abandon this character: Return a character to hand"; a self-outlet with a flexible bounce, useful even without an abandon shell.
17. Pyrewatcher — "Abandon this character: Gain 3●"; a self-abandoning warrior that bursts out three energy, a clean ritual body for any tempo turn.
18. Marrow Mimic — "Abandon this character: Erode 1. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; cheap self-feeding fodder that erodes and replaces itself.

## Cheap fodder that wants to die

19. Entropy Spike — "▸Dissolved: Gain 1● or Gain 1 maximum ●"; the premier 1-cost fodder, paying energy or permanent ramp whenever it leaves play. Good in any deck that occasionally trades or abandons a body.
20. Spent Courier — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card with ephemeral"; card-neutral on both entry and exit, so it never costs you a card to run as fodder.
21. Ossuary Overlord — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card"; card-neutral fodder that replaces itself both ways.
22. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟"; a 2-cost warrior that hands you two points whenever it dies, whether traded or abandoned.
23. Overstory Explorer — "▸Dissolved: Gain 1 maximum ●"; permanent ramp every time it dies, valuable even as an occasional trade.
24. Searcher in the Mists — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Erode 4"; cheap body that fills the void on both ends, fueling any void or erosion plan.
25. Ashwalker — "▸Materialized: Erode 3. ▸Dissolved: Return a character from your void to your hand"; self-mill fodder that recurs a body on death, self-sufficient at any count.

## Recurrent bodies

26. Cinderfang — "▸Dissolved: You may pay 1● to return this character to hand"; a 2-cost body that bounces back whenever it dies, making it a renewable feed without any engine around it.
27. Duskwall Delver — "When an ally is dissolved, you may pay 1● to materialize this character from your void"; climbs back out of the void off any death, including its own, so it survives a low-density splash.
28. Harvester of Despair — "Abandon another character: This character gains +1✦"; a 1-cost growth body that turns any spare character into a standalone threat.
29. Kindred Sparks — "With an allied survivor, play this card from hand or void for 1●"; a renewable survivor body that ignores zones, splashable as recurring fodder in any survivor-touching deck.

## Death-trigger payoffs (individually strong)

30. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; the core point payoff. It counts every dissolve on both sides, so even at low density it scores off trades and removal, not just your own abandons.
31. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟"; scores off bounces and banishes as well as dissolves, so it fires in nearly any deck that cycles its own bodies.
32. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟"; prices each of your abandons at a point with no second card, a clean payoff for even a few outlets.
33. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●"; refunds energy on any death, helping any deck that trades or abandons.
34. Sunset Chronicler — "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card"; turns every loss into a card, keeping the splash self-sustaining.

## Overlapping removal

35. Molten Duel — "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less"; flexible cheap removal that can also point at your own small body to fire a ▸Dissolved trigger.
36. Together Against the Tide — "Play this card only if you abandoned a character this turn. Dissolve an enemy with cost 3● or less"; removal that the abandon you were already making turns on for free.
37. Maelstrom Denial — "Abandon a character or discard a card: Dissolve an enemy"; flexible removal that uses a spare body as its cost, slotting into any deck with fodder to spare.
38. Forsaken Skyline — "Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less. Reclaim 4●"; recurring small-body removal that comes back from the void, useful regardless of abandon density.

## Selection and tutors

39. Rite of Summoning — "Discover a card with cost X●"; a flexible tutor that finds whichever Abandon piece your splash is missing, useful in any deck.
40. Door to Possibility — "Choose a value of X. Discover an event with cost X"; digs up a missing event — a removal spell or an outlet event — and is generically good selection.
