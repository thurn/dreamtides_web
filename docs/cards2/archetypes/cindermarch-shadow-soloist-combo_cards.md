# Cindermarch / Shadow Soloist Combo — Card Pool

A companion to [cindermarch-shadow-soloist-combo.md](cindermarch-shadow-soloist-combo.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Cindermarch / Shadow Soloist Combo archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/cards.toml`.

## Backbone awakeners and ▸Dawn re-triggers

1. Cindermarch — the materialize-side backbone: "When you materialize a character, awaken each other allied character" turns every body into a full-board untap.
2. Shadow Soloist — the event-side backbone: "When you play an event, awaken each allied character" untaps the whole board on each event.
3. Conduit of Resonance — "When you play a character, trigger the '▸Dawn' ability of each other allied character" re-fires every ▸Dawn engine per play, the third backbone.
4. Unquenched — "When you materialize an ally, trigger its '▸Dawn' ability" runs each entering body's Dawn engine on the spot, stacking with the materialize loop.

## Awaken / untap engines and redundancy

5. Lanternhearted — enters awakened, "☾: Awaken an ally", Reclaim 1●; the on-demand spot untapper, recurs cheaply.
6. Verdant Pilgrim — "☾: Awaken an ally" is a redundant cheap copy of Lanternhearted's untap.
7. Mother of Flames — Awakened, "☾, Return an ally to hand: Awaken an ally" is another redundant awakener that also resets a materialize trigger.
8. Moonbound Wolf — Awakened, "☾, Return an ally to hand: Trigger the 'Dawn' ability of an allied spirit animal" re-fires a Dawn engine and recycles a body.
9. Rootbound Witness — Awakened, "Abandon this character: Allied characters gain awakened this turn" is a one-shot mass untap that primes the loop.
10. Duskmount — Awakened, "☾: Rematerialize an ally" both untaps itself and re-materializes a body to fire Cindermarch each activation.
11. Surge of Fury — "Trigger the '▸Dawn' ability of each allied character. Awaken each allied character" is a one-shot Cindermarch-plus-Conduit in event form.
12. Pyrestone Avatar — Offering, "Allied characters have awakened" makes every body arrive ready to challenge and pay ☾ the turn it lands.
13. Pinnacle Ascendant — "Allied characters have awakened", and gains reclaim 0● when discarded or eroded, so the loot/erode engines replay it for free.
14. Scrap Reclaimer — free Awakened body (Veil 2●) that also recurs cheap characters from the void to refuel the loop.

## ☾ payoffs (net-energy taps the untap loop abuses)

15. Wolfbond Chieftain — enters awakened, "☾: Gain 1● for each allied warrior"; the canonical net-energy faucet the loop re-taps.
16. Crescendo Channeler — "▸Dawn: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" widens the board and "2●, ☾: Gain 1● for each allied warrior" taps for a pile of energy.
17. Spirit of the Greenwood — Awakened, "1●, ☾: Gain 1● for each allied character" is a net-energy tap that scales off the whole awakened board.
18. Blazing Emberwing — "☾: Gain 1● for each allied character" is another whole-board energy faucet for the untap loop.

## Repeatable materialize producers (Cindermarch loop)

19. Dreaming Obelisk — "☾, Abandon a warrior: Materialize a warrior from your void"; the core repeatable producer that fires Cindermarch every activation.
20. Pit Descender — "☾, Abandon a warrior: Materialize a warrior with cost 2● or less from your void"; second repeatable producer to ping-pong with Obelisk.
21. Worldbreacher — "☾: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" is a cheap repeatable materialize trigger for Cindermarch.
22. Shadowbinder — Awakened, "☾: Materialize two 1✦ ember figments with awakened" fires two materializes per activation.
23. Spellweaver — "1●, Abandon a non-figment warrior: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" converts the loop's bodies into fresh materializes.
24. Simulacra — "2●, ☾: Materialize a figment copy of an ally until end of turn" is a repeatable materialize the awakened board keeps firing.
25. Mirrorlight Architect — "▸Dawn: Store 1⧗; ❖ – ☾: Materialize a character from hand with cost ≤ stored ⧗"; a repeatable materialize whose Dawn is re-fired by Conduit.

## Figment producers (extra materializes per play)

26. Voidsire — "When you play a warrior, materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" drops a figment and fires Cindermarch each warrior.
27. Twilight Troubadour — "When you play an event, materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" turns every event into an extra materialize.
28. Forge-Twin — ▸Materialized makes a 'Blade of Unity' figment copy, two materializes from one play plus a scaling threat.
29. Embersummoner — "When you materialize a figment, materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment" chains materializes, multiplying Cindermarch triggers.
30. Inferno's Herald — ▸Dissolved "Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment for each ✦ this character has"; fed to an abandon outlet it floods the board with materializes.

## Finishers and payoffs

55. Paradox Enforcer — 7✦ body playable from the void for "banish 7 cards from your void"; a void-flood finisher.

57. Wandering Archivist — "2●, ☾: loot" and "When you discard a card, gain 1⍟"; a repeatable ☾ drain off the loot triggers.
58. Rubble Diviner — "▸Challenge: pay 2● and discard to draw two" turns the awakened board's challenges into card and discard fuel for the drain.
59. Dreadlord — costs 1● less per allied warrior; a free 5✦ beater the wide warrior loop deploys to close.
