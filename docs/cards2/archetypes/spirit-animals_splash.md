# Spirit Animals — Splash Cards

A companion to [spirit-animals.md](spirit-animals.md), listing the portable core of the Spirit Animals archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density when mixed into a neighboring deck. These are the flexible, self-contained pieces: the 1● energy dorks that accelerate any deck, the individually strong value bodies, and the finishers and tutors that close any wide board. The tribal payoffs that demand a critical mass of spirit animals live in the full pool list, not here. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Energy dorks (accelerate any deck)

These 1● and 0● bodies produce energy on their own, so they ramp a host deck the turn they land regardless of how many other spirit animals you run. They are the single most splashable group in the archetype.

1. Cloudmantle Ray — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 3●; a self-contained accelerant that returns from the void, good in any deck that wants more energy.
2. Starrunner — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●", "Abandon: Gain 1●", Reclaim 0●; produces standalone and recurs cheaply, no tribe required.
3. Starchaser — 1● body, Veil 2●, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; a resilient faucet that ramps any host and survives removal.
4. Worldsong Behemoth — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; pure redundant acceleration.
5. Ethereal Trailblazer — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; pure redundant acceleration.
6. Hallowed Stag — 1● body, Vengeful, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; ramps and trades up on defense, fine in any deck.
7. Blazebound Sentinel — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Gain 1●"; refunds energy even when it dies, so it accelerates without being a liability.
8. Lunar Hart — 1● body, "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 2●"; the densest standalone energy on entry and each Dawn.
9. Emberwolf Triad — 1● body, "▸Materialized: Foresee 1. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; ramp plus a small dig, useful anywhere.
10. Driftcaller Sovereign — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" plus a spark-pump activated ability; standalone faucet with late-game reach.
11. Lone Castaway — 0● body (discard a character to play), "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 1●"; near-free recurring acceleration.
12. Fangbound — 0● body (banish a card to play), Veil 3●, "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 1●"; a free, resilient faucet that asks nothing of the rest of the deck.
13. Sky Voyager — 1● body, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" and "has all character types"; a standalone faucet that also slots into any type-matters host.
14. Dawnhorn Elder — 1● body, "2●, ☪: Store 1⧗. ▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each stored ⧗"; banks counters into recurring energy with no tribal requirement.

## Board-counting energy faucets

These scale off your total characters rather than off spirit animals specifically, so they ramp in any creature deck that goes even moderately wide.

15. Spirit of the Greenwood — Awakened, "1●, ☪: Gain 1● for each allied character"; a net-positive energy faucet on any populated board, producing the turn it lands.
16. Blazing Emberwing — "☪: Gain 1● for each allied character"; the same faucet with no up-front cost, counting every character.
17. Skyborne Jellyfish — "▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each allied character with cost 2● or less"; rewards a flat, cheap curve, which most splash hosts already run.

## Acceleration enablers

18. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; turns a low-curve host's whole board free, the deck's most explosive enabler and a fit for any cheap-creature deck.
19. Heavenward Penitent — Veil 2●, "The first character with cost 2● or less you play each turn costs you 0●"; a resilient per-turn free cheap drop that needs no tribe.
20. Melodist of the Finale — Veil 2●, "When you play a character, gain 1●"; refunds energy on every body you chain in any creature deck, kept alive by Veil.

## Self-replacing value bodies

21. Dreamvale Monarch — "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a body that replaces itself, good in any deck at any density.
22. Emerald Guardian — "▸Materialized: Draw a card. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; replaces itself and ramps, one of the cleanest standalone splash bodies.
23. Oathbound Pair — "▸Materialized, Dawn: Give an ally +1✦"; steady, non-tribal spark growth on any board.
24. Lumineth — "☪, Discard a character: Discover a character with cost 1● higher"; turns a stranded card into the body you need, useful in any creature deck.
25. Stoneborn Leviathan — "1●, Return an ally to hand: Give an ally +1✦"; recycles a body for spark and re-fires any ▸Materialized trigger, no tribe required.

## Recursion and value

26. Passage Through Oblivion — "Rematerialize an ally. Reclaim 1●"; re-fires a ▸Materialized body and returns from the void, generic blink value in any creature deck.
27. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand"; rebuys any spent card, a standalone value body.

## Finishers and tutors

28. Spirit Bond — "Until end of turn, allied characters gain unstoppable and +X✦ where X is the number of allied characters"; a wide-board alpha strike that counts every character, so it closes any go-wide host.
29. Field Reverent — "X●: Each allied character's ✦ becomes X until end of turn"; a finisher scaled to your energy that hits every character, ideal for a mixed board.
30. Beacon of Tomorrow — "Choose a value of X. Discover a character with cost X●"; a generic tutor for whatever finisher or body the board wants, splashes anywhere.
31. The Dread Sovereign — "X●, ☪, Abandon another character: Discover a character with cost X●, then materialize it"; finds and deploys a piece off a spare body, no tribe required.
32. Curio Dealer — Veil 2●, "1●, Discard a character with cost X●: Discover a character with cost X●"; trades a dead card for the exact body at the same cost and survives removal.
33. Through the Rift — "Discover a spirit animal"; a cheap tutor that fetches whichever dork or payoff you splashed, good even when the splash is small.

## Going wide and interaction

34. Roots of Rebirth — "Materialize two random characters with cost 2● or less from your deck"; two bodies from one card, which almost never whiffs in a low-curve host.
35. Radiant Trio — "Materialize X 1✦ ethereal figments"; a burst flood that widens any board and pays toward Spirit Bond and the board-counting faucets.
36. Marked Direwolf — 1● body, "☪: Awaken an ally"; a cheap body that wakes a fresh drop to swing the turn it lands, useful as standalone tempo.
37. Molten Duel — Fast, "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less"; cheap, flexible removal that clears a blocker in any deck.
38. Thundercatcher — "2●, Abandon a 1✦ ally: Draw 2 cards"; turns a spent 1● body — a figment or a tapped dork — into two cards, generic value alongside cheap bodies.
39. Celestial Reverie — "Until end of turn, when you play a character, draw a card"; a draw engine for any deck that chains characters, the bridge toward the combo build.
