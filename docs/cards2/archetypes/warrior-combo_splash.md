# Warrior Combo — Splash Cards

A companion to [warrior-combo.md](warrior-combo.md), listing the portable core of the Warrior Combo archetype — the recursion bodies, abandon outlets, tutors, and self-contained warriors that stay good even at low density, so a neighboring deck can splash 5-10 of these pieces without assembling the full loop. These carry their own value rather than needing a welder, a wide figment board, or a critical mass of warriors online to function. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Recursion bodies (good on their own)

1. Starbound Striker — "▸Dissolved: Return another warrior from your void to your hand"; a 2● warrior that quietly turns any trade or abandon into a free card back. It needs nothing but a warrior in the void, so a handful of warriors makes it a self-sufficient value body.
2. Infernal Cavalier — "▸Dissolved: Return another warrior with cost 4● or less from your void to your hand"; the same death-recursion on a 3✦ body that covers nearly the whole curve, fine to splash as a body that replaces itself on death.
3. Tranquil Duelist — "Vengeful. ▸Dissolved: Return another warrior from your void to your hand"; a Vengeful warrior that trades up in combat and refills you when it dies — a strong standalone body whose recursion is pure upside.
4. Scorched Crusader — "When you materialize a non-figment character, store 1⧗. 3⧗: Return a warrior from your void to your hand"; a self-paced recursion engine on a 2● body that needs only a few materializations a turn, not a built loop.
5. Dune Reaper — "2●, Banish a card from void: Materialize this character from your void"; a 3✦ warrior that recurs itself, giving any deck a renewable body for the cost of a void card.

## Abandon outlets (standalone)

6. Spirit Reaping — "Abandon a warrior: Gain 2●"; the gold-standard outlet with no ☪ and no per-turn cap. Even with a few cheap warriors it turns spare bodies into a steady energy refund, no engine required.
7. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●"; the best ramp outlet in the pool, taking any spare body for two energy and a ▸Dissolved trigger at any density.
8. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●"; a free, uncapped outlet that converts a doomed or stalled body into energy and a trigger every turn.
9. Pyrewatcher — "Abandon this character: Gain 3●"; a self-abandoning warrior that bursts three energy on demand, a clean ritual body for any tempo turn that also fires a warrior dissolve.
10. Forsworn Champion — "❖❖ – Abandon a warrior: This character gains +1✦"; a spark-paying Interrupt-speed outlet that grows into a lethal challenger in an aggressive shell, good on its own without a recursion loop.
11. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●"; a cheap repeatable energy outlet whose small body is easy to feed in any deck with low-cost characters.
12. Nightmare Manifest — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. ☪, Abandon a character: Gain 1●"; a 1● body that ramps on its own and adds a free abandon outlet, fine even without other sacrifice pieces.
13. Soulbinder — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●"; a cheap repeatable outlet that banks spare bodies into energy with no payoff support.

## Self-cycling warrior fodder

14. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●"; a 0● 0✦ body that is both fodder and a free outlet for itself, a Lotus-Petal ritual any deck can run that also triggers your dissolve payoffs.
15. Dread Arbiter — "Abandon this character: Reveal the opponent's hand. Draw a card with ephemeral"; 0● 0✦ self-cycling fodder that replaces itself and peeks at the opponent's hand.
16. Rebirth Ritualist — "Abandon this character: Draw a card with ephemeral"; 0● self-cycling warrior fodder that thins your deck and triggers a death payoff at no card cost.
17. Blightmaw — "Abandon this character: Draw a card"; a 1● warrior that cashes itself in for a card, splashable as free card flow.
18. Ochre Prospector — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; a self-replacing outlet body that nets a card whenever it dies.
19. Marrow Mimic — "Abandon this character: Erode 1. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; cheap self-feeding warrior fodder that fills the void and replaces itself.
20. Starshot Gunner — "1●, Abandon this character: Return a character to hand"; a 1● self-outlet with a flexible bounce, useful even outside an abandon shell.
21. Ironclad Marksman — "Abandon this character: Shuffle up to 3 cards from your void into your deck. Draw a card"; self-cycling fodder that refuels your deck against decking out and replaces itself.
22. Spent Courier — "▸Materialized, ▸Dissolved: Draw a card with ephemeral"; card-neutral on both entry and exit, so it never costs a card to run as a warrior body.
23. Ossuary Overlord — "▸Materialized, ▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; card-neutral warrior fodder that replaces itself in both directions.
24. Sandglider — "(no ability)"; a 0● fast warrior with no text, pure cheap fodder to fill the curve and feed any abandon or dissolve trigger.

## Warrior card advantage

25. Sigil Analyst — "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards"; a fat ▸Materialized warrior payoff that draws two whenever it lands, strong on a plain cast and great if a recursion piece returns it.
26. Crucible Warlord — "▸Materialized: Draw a warrior"; a 3✦ warrior that digs up another warrior on entry, keeping a low-density warrior splash flowing.
27. Reforged Automaton — "▸Materialized: Draw a warrior" plus growth on each non-figment warrior; refuels the warrior count and scales with the bodies you play.
28. Invoker of Myths — "Once per turn, when you materialize a warrior, draw a card"; turns each warrior you play into a card, useful in any deck running a warrior package.
29. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand"; a non-abandon way to recur a key event or body, generically good ▸Materialized value.

## Tutors and assembly

30. Aftermath Bloom — "To play this event, abandon a warrior. Discover a warrior with higher cost, then materialize it"; trades a small warrior for whichever larger piece you want straight into play, a flexible tutor and fail-safe even in a light warrior splash.
31. Mirror Protocol — "☪, Abandon a warrior: Discover a warrior with cost 1● higher and materialize it"; a tutor on a stick that climbs the curve and finds your best warrior, repeatable without a built engine.

## Cost reduction

32. Tethered Hollow — "The first warrior you play each turn costs 3● less"; a 1● body that discounts a warrior every turn, valuable in any deck deploying warriors.
33. Sundown Ronin — "Warriors cost you 1● less"; flat reduction that quietly pays off across a whole warrior package.

## Drain and point payoffs (individually strong)

34. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; the core point payoff, counting every dissolve on both sides, so it scores off trades and removal even at low warrior density.
35. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟"; a 2● warrior that hands you two points whenever it dies, whether traded or abandoned, folding back into recursion as a warrior.
36. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X (that character's cost)"; an erosion clock on a 3✦ warrior body, grinding the opponent toward Fatigue one spare body at a time without a dedicated loop.
37. Twilight Suppressor — "When you play a character, store 1⧗. 1⧗, Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟"; a warrior that banks plays into stored ⧗ and cashes spare bodies for points, self-contained at any density.
38. Junkfield Renegade — "1●, Abandon this character: Gain 2⍟ or Draw a card"; a 1● warrior closer that cashes itself in for points or a card on demand and doubles as recursion fodder.
39. Saltless Mariner — "Veil 2●. Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟"; a self-contained abandon-to-points outlet with a sticky Veil body, profitable from a single abandon.

## Burst energy and removal

40. Glimpse of Infinity — "Gain 3●" for 0●; explosive fast energy that lets a combo turn fire early, generically useful in any energy-hungry deck.
41. Pulse of Sacrifice — "Discard your hand. Gain 3●"; pays for a big turn and dumps recursion targets into the void, splashable as burst energy plus void setup.
42. Eclipse Herald — "3●: Store 1⧗. X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with ✦ X or less"; a warrior that banks toward repeatable removal, keeping challengers off your bodies without any combo support.
