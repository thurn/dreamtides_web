# Celestial Reverie Combo — Splash Cards

A companion to [celestial-reverie-combo.md](celestial-reverie-combo.md), listing the portable core of the Celestial Reverie Combo archetype — the cards that stay good at low density, so a neighboring deck can splash a handful of them without committing to the full loop. These are the flexible, self-contained pieces; the deterministic win-pieces and chain-length payoffs that need the whole engine assembled are left out. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Draw-on-play triggers

1. Celestial Reverie — the namesake 1● event, "Until end of turn, when you play a character, draw a card"; a single copy turns any hand of cheap characters into a wave of cantrips, so it pays off the moment you have a few bodies to unload, no loop required.
2. Luminous Ascent — 2● "Until end of turn, when you materialize a character, draw a card," firing on figments and returns as well as hard casts, with Reclaim 4● for a second go; broad enough to reward any creature-dense turn on its own.

## Self-bouncing and recurring bodies

3. Still Dreamer — 2● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; even without a free-cast enabler it is a flexible body that re-buys an ally's ▸Materialized trigger or rescues a body from removal, and it folds straight into the loop when one shows up.
4. Forgotten Factory Titan — 2● Awakened with the same self-returning ▸Materialized; the Awakened body makes it an immediate challenger, so it earns its slot as a tempo play and doubles as a loop piece.
5. Nomad of Endless Paths — 2● Unstoppable 5✦ that returns another ally each materialize; a strong standalone threat that re-buys a second body's ▸Materialized trigger, good in any creature deck regardless of loop density.
6. Ethereal Courser — 1● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," a cheap self-bouncing body that re-buys a trigger or saves an ally, useful at any density.
7. Stoneborn Eternal — 1● spirit animal that returns itself for 0● whenever you play a spirit animal; in any cheap spirit-animal base it quietly chains free replays off whatever draw or play trigger you have.
8. Wondrous Clearing — 1● "▸Materialized: you may materialize a cost-2●-or-less character from hand," a low-curve body that deploys a second body, a fine standalone tempo play that adds to any character-count synergy.

## Free-play and energy enablers

9. Nexus Wayfinder — 4● "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; the clearest combo signal, but it asks nothing of the rest of your board and turns any low-curve hand into a free dump, so it splashes into any cheap-character shell.
10. Heavenward Penitent — 1● Veil "The first character with cost 2● or less you play each turn costs you 0●," a cheap recurring tempo discount that survives removal and is good even as a one-of.
11. Melodist of the Finale — 2● Veil "When you play a character, gain 1●," refunding a point on every character; a self-contained energy body that pays off in any creature deck.
12. Spirit of the Greenwood — 1● Awakened "1●, ☪: Gain 1● for each allied character," a large energy refund on any wide board and an early Awakened body besides.
13. Blazing Emberwing — 3● "☪: Gain 1● for each allied character," a big standalone energy burst that scales with whatever board you build.
14. Bloomweaver — 1● Veil "When you play a character with cost 2● or less, gain 1●," a low-curve refund per play that is good in any deck running cheap bodies.
15. Skyborne Jellyfish — 2● spirit animal "▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each allied cost-2●-or-less character," scaling energy that rewards a low curve without any combo commitment.
16. Lord of Hidden Paths — 2● spirit animal anthem, "Allied spirit animals have +1✦" plus "when you play a spirit animal, pay 1● to draw a card," a flexible value body for any spirit-animal splash.

## Card flow and digging

17. Beacon of Tomorrow — 1● "Choose a value of X. Discover a character with cost X●," a toolbox tutor that finds whatever cheap body you need; good in any creature deck for smoothing draws.
18. Dreamcatcher's Call — 1●,X "Discover a character with cost X and materialize it," a flexible fetch-and-deploy that adds a body on demand at any density.
19. Call to the Unknown — 3● "▸Materialized: Discover a character with 2✦ or less," a body that also digs for a cheap follow-up, useful standalone.
20. Winterbough Monk — 3● "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand," a value body that recurs any spent event or body, and a cantrip under a draw trigger.
21. Key to the Moment — 3● "Return all but one allied character to hand. Draw a card for each character returned," a one-card refill that reloads a stalled board and re-buys ▸Materialized triggers on replay, strong in any wide creature deck.
22. Dreamvale Monarch — 2● spirit animal "▸Materialized: Draw a card," a body that replaces itself with no support and draws twice with a trigger up.
23. Doorlight Foundling — 2● fast that materializes or draws the top card when it is a cheap character, a self-contained smoother for a low curve.
24. Lumin-Gate Seer — 2●,X Veil "when you materialize a cost-X● character, draw a card," a second draw-on-materialize body tuned to the curve, good as a standalone engine.
25. Keeper of Forgotten Light — 2● "when you materialize a non-figment cost-2●-or-less character, draw a card with ephemeral," a redundant draw-on-cast body that quietly cantrips off the cheap base.

## Board and ⍟ payoffs

26. Standard Bearer — 3● "When you play a character, materialize a 1✦ warrior figment," a go-wide engine that builds a board on any character-dense turn, no loop needed.
27. Endless Projection — 3● the same figment engine on ethereal figments, widening the board on every character you play.
28. Dreamborne Leviathan — 2● spirit animal that drops a spirit-animal figment once per turn per spirit animal played, a cheap body that widens any spirit-animal board.
29. Angel of the Eclipse — 3● "When you materialize another character, gain 1⍟," dripping points on any materialize-heavy turn, a self-contained payoff at low density.
30. Mystic Runefish — 2● spirit animal "5●, ☪: each allied spirit animal's ✦ becomes 7," a flexible go-wide finisher for any spirit-animal splash.
31. Spiritbound Alpha — 3● that gives allied spirit animals +3✦ and Vengeful, a standalone pump that pushes any spirit-animal board through.

## Drain and dissolve payoffs

32. Silent Avenger — 3● "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟," a self-contained drain that drips points whenever a board trades, useful in any creature deck.
33. Sunset Chronicler — 3● "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card," reloading the hand off combat, good standalone in any board that trades.
34. Final Witness — 3● "When a non-figment ally is dissolved, draw a card," the same combat-reload on a survivor body.
35. Soulrender — 3● "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X (its cost)," a flexible erosion outlet that works off any spare body, not just a full loop.

## Spirit Animal awaken and replay support

36. Marked Direwolf — 1● "☪: Awaken an ally," a cheap spirit animal that re-readies a body for combat, useful at any density.
37. Moonbound Wolf — 1● "☪, Return an ally to hand: Trigger the 'Dawn' ability of an allied spirit animal," a cheap body that adds bounce and a re-fired ▸Dawn to any spirit-animal splash.
38. Lunar Hart — 1● "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 2●," a cheap energy body that pays off on its own ▸Dawn even without re-fire support.
