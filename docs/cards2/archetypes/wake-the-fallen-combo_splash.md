# Wake the Fallen / Shadow March Combo — Splash Cards

A companion to [wake-the-fallen-combo.md](wake-the-fallen-combo.md), listing the portable core of the archetype: the cards that stay good at low density and can be mixed into a neighboring deck without building around the mass-return loop. The mass-return enablers themselves and the density-dependent loop engines are absent here because they only pay off with the whole deck assembled; what travels is the generically-useful abandon outlets, the cheap self-paying value bodies, and the individually-strong drains the combo happens to share with the Abandon and Cheap Characters decks. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Abandon outlets — the front half travels anywhere

1. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●." No cost on the ability and no use limit, so it converts any dying body into energy plus a ▸Dissolved trigger. The cleanest free outlet in the pool and good in any deck that runs even a handful of expendable bodies.
2. Saltless Mariner — "Veil 2●. Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟." Outlet and point payoff in one self-protecting body, so it earns its slot on the strength of "abandon for a point" alone, before any loop exists.
3. Ruptured Dynamo — "Abandon a character: Return a character from your void to your hand." A 1● body that turns a doomed character into a fresh card in hand — pure value at any density, no mass-return required.
4. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟." Both outlet and payoff on a 3● body: every cheap body you feed it is a point, which is a fine standalone rate without looping anything.
5. Spirit Reaping — "Abandon a warrior: Gain 2●." A high-rate energy outlet that splashes wherever you already run warriors.
6. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●." A 2● energy outlet tuned to the cheap bodies most decks already play.
7. Soulbinder — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●." A 1● outlet that banks abandons into energy, flexible enough to slot anywhere fodder dies.
8. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●." A straightforward repeatable energy outlet at a strong rate, useful in any deck with bodies to spare.
9. Stargazer Adrift — "Abandon another character: Foresee 1." A 1● body that turns each abandon into deck sculpting — good standalone smoothing, not loop-dependent.
10. Ashborn Necromancer — "Abandon a character: Erode 2." An outlet that fires a ▸Dissolved trigger while filling your own void, useful to any void or self-mill shell.
11. Harvester of Despair — "Abandon another character: This character gains +1✦." A 1● repeatable outlet that also grows into a real threat on its own.
12. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X, where X is that character's cost." A premium outlet whose product is opponent erosion; strong in any abandon or mill direction with no loop attached.
13. Obliterator of Worlds — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦." Turns your expendable bodies into repeatable removal, valuable in any deck regardless of whether it ever mass-returns.
14. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗. X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦." The same abandon-to-removal converter triggered passively, so any incidental abandons feed it.
15. Specter of Silent Snow — "▸Dawn: Abandon a character. When you abandon a character, draw a card and this character gains +1✦." A self-feeding 4● body that draws and grows off the abandon stream, a strong standalone value engine.

## Cheap self-paying bodies — fodder that is fine on its own

16. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●." A 0● 0✦ body that is its own outlet and a free replay, useful as a Lotus-Petal-style energy burst in any deck.
17. Pyrewatcher — "Abandon this character: Gain 3●." A 2● body that cashes for a 3● ritual when you want burst energy, fine as a standalone accelerant.
18. Spent Courier — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card with ephemeral." Replaces itself on entry and exit, so it is card-neutral fodder in any deck that puts bodies in the void.
19. Ossuary Overlord — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card." The same draw-coming-and-going body without the ephemeral clause, generically card-neutral.
20. Marrow Mimic — "Abandon this character: Erode 1. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card." A 1● outlet-and-cantrip in one body that erodes the opponent on the way out, self-contained value.
21. Ochre Prospector — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card." A 2● body that pays energy on the way out and a card when it dies, fine in any void deck.
22. Dread Arbiter — "Abandon this character: Reveal the opponent's hand. Draw a card with ephemeral." A 0● body that abandons itself for information and a card, a self-contained scry-and-cantrip.
23. Rebirth Ritualist — "Abandon this character: Draw a card with ephemeral." A 0● 1✦ body that cantrips itself away, cheap flexible fodder.
24. Blightmaw — "Abandon this character: Draw a card." A 1● body that replaces itself, splashable wherever you want an extra expendable cantrip.
25. Junkfield Renegade — "1●, Abandon this character: Gain 2⍟ or Draw a card." Cashes a single body for two points or a card, a flexible self-contained outlet body.
26. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟." A 2● 2✦ warrior that banks two points whenever it dies, strong fodder for any abandon or trade-heavy deck.

## Drains and dissolve payoffs — strong at any density

27. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟." The cleanest drain in the pool: every abandon, every trade, and every opposing dissolve is a point, so it scores in any deck that sees characters die.
28. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟." A Blood-Artist drain that banks a point on every one of your bodies dying, generically strong in abandon and trade decks.
29. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟." A drain that scores on every outlet activation, valuable in any deck that abandons at all.
30. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟." Drains on abandons, dissolves, and bounces alike, the broadest of the point triggers.
31. Cinderheart — "When you abandon a character, this character gains +1✦. ▸Dissolved: Gain ⍟ equal to this character's ✦." A 2● body that grows on the abandon stream and cashes a pile of points when it dies, a self-contained payoff.
32. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "▸Materialized: Materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment. When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟." A drain that also brings a body, fine on its own in any sacrifice deck.
33. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●." Turns the dissolve stream into energy, a standalone refuel for any deck that trades or abandons.
34. Sunset Chronicler — "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card." Refills your hand off every dissolve, generically useful card advantage.

## Energy and recursion the deck shares

35. Glimpse of Infinity — "Gain 3●." A 0● ritual that fits any deck wanting a burst of energy for a big turn, completely loop-independent.
36. Genesis Burst — "Double your current ●." A 1● ritual that explodes any sizable energy pool, useful in any ramp or combo direction.
37. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand." A 3● body that fishes back any spent card, a generically-good value-and-blink body that happens to recur your mass-return spells when you have them.
38. Starrunner — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. Abandon this character: Gain 1●. Reclaim 0●." Cheap energy fodder that comes back from the void for free, useful as a recurring accelerant body in any deck.
