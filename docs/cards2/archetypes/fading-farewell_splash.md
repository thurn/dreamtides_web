# Fading Farewell — Splash Cards

The portable core of [fading-farewell.md](fading-farewell.md) — the cards that stay good in a neighboring deck even at low density, so you can mix a little dissolve-and-return synergy into whatever you are already drafting. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`. Fading Farewell is a small, dedicated combo, so its splash list leans on the free bodies and free outlets it shares with Abandon rather than the loop-only finishers; the centerpiece event and the dedicated loop payoffs (the erosion, figment, and ⧗ sinks that only pay once a loop is running) stay home with the all-in build.

## Free bodies

1. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●"; a 0● 0✦ body that turns itself into energy. Even with no combo around it, it is a free Lotus Petal that ramps a turn, and it is the ideal fuel for any abandon outlet you happen to run.
2. Aspiring Guardian — 0● 1✦ no-text Warrior; a genuinely free body that fills a board slot, feeds an outlet, and costs nothing to deploy regardless of how much synergy surrounds it.
3. Sandglider — 0● 0✦ no-text Warrior; another free passenger that any abandon outlet or board-count effect can use, with zero deck-building cost.
4. Pyrewatcher — "Abandon this character: Gain 3●"; a 2● Lotus Petal body that banks a big energy burst on demand. Strong standalone ramp that any deck with an extra turn-of-need will happily run.
5. Blightmaw — "Abandon this character: Draw a card"; a 1● body that cashes in for a card whenever you want, useful as cheap interaction-bait and card flow without any combo.
6. Rebirth Ritualist — "Abandon this character: Draw a card with ephemeral"; a 0● body that replaces itself, a free cantrip-on-legs that slots into any list.
7. Ochre Prospector — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●" plus "▸Dissolved: Draw a card"; a 2● Warrior that ramps and replaces itself when it dies, good value in any deck that abandons or trades bodies.
8. Starrunner — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 0●; a 2● spirit animal that ramps, comes back from the void for free, and rewards any abandon-leaning shell.

## Free abandon outlets

9. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●" with no cost and no cap; the workhorse outlet. It is a fine ramp engine in any deck running a few expendable bodies, turning excess board into energy at will.
10. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●"; a higher-rate outlet that converts a spare body into a real energy swing. Strong standalone ramp even without a loop.
11. Spirit Reaping — "Abandon a warrior: Gain 2●"; the warrior-flavored 2●-per-abandon outlet, an easy include in any warrior shell that wants on-demand energy.
12. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●"; a cheap 2● outlet that pairs naturally with the cheap-body shells most decks already run.
13. Nightmare Manifest — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" plus "☪, Abandon a character: Gain 1●"; a 1● body that ramps on its own and doubles as a repeatable outlet, useful at any density.
14. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟"; a self-contained outlet that turns spare cheap bodies directly into points, good in any value or abandon deck.
15. Ruptured Dynamo — "Abandon a character: Return a character from your void to your hand"; a 1● outlet that recurs a body each abandon, a flexible value engine that works without any loop.
16. Saltless Mariner — "Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟"; a 2● Veil body that converts any spare character into a point, a clean standalone outlet for grindy decks.
17. Stargazer Adrift — "Abandon another character: Foresee 1"; a cheap 1● outlet that smooths draws while feeding off expendable bodies, useful at low density.

## Per-dissolve and abandon payoffs

18. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; the premier payoff, and strong on its own because it banks points off *any* dissolve, including the opponent's losses and your own trades.
19. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; a redundant per-dissolve point engine that quietly turns every trade or abandon into score in any deck that loses bodies.
20. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟" plus a free ethereal figment on entry; a second Silent Avenger that also leaves a body, so it carries its weight standalone.
21. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟"; a broad leaves-play payoff that profits from bounces and returns as well as deaths, generically useful in any board-churning deck.
22. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟"; pays a point per abandon, a natural include in any deck already running abandon outlets.
23. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●"; turns every lost body into energy, a fine ramp payoff in any deck that trades or abandons, with no combo required.
24. Sunset Chronicler — "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card"; converts board attrition into card flow, a standalone value engine for grindy lists.
25. Cinderheart — "When you abandon a character, this character gains +1✦" plus "▸Dissolved: Gain ⍟ equal to this character's ✦"; a self-scaling 2● body that grows in any abandon shell and pays off when it dies.
26. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟"; a 2● body that pays you for its own death however it dies, a solid value body in any deck that trades bodies.

## Recursion bodies

27. Burning Revenant — "When an allied warrior is dissolved, return a warrior with lesser cost from your void to hand"; a self-contained warrior-recursion engine that grinds value in any warrior deck, returning a body every trade.
28. Scrapyard Custodian — "▸Materialized: Gain 1⍟" plus "Abandon a spirit animal: Materialize this character from your void"; a 1● body that pays on entry and recurs itself off any spirit-animal abandon, good value standalone.
29. Duskwall Delver — "When an ally is dissolved, pay 1● to materialize this from your void"; a self-recurring 2● body that climbs back out of the void whenever a body dies, useful in any attrition deck.
30. Ashen Harbinger — "This character has all character types"; an X-cost flex body that is a legal target for every tribal outlet and payoff and a free passenger at 0●, slotting into whatever shell you are running.
