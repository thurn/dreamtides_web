# Cheap Characters — Splash Cards

A companion to [cheap-characters.md](cheap-characters.md) listing the portable core of the Cheap Characters (cost 2● or less) archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density, so you can splash a handful of them into a neighboring deck without committing to the full engine. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Acceleration and cost reducers

1. Heavenward Penitent — "The first character with cost 2● or less you play each turn costs you 0●"; a 1● 1✦ body with Veil 2● that pays for itself the moment you have any cheap characters at all, so it slots into any deck running a few of them.
2. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; a single resolution makes every cheap body in your deck free, and even five or six of them turn into a sizable tempo swing.
3. Bloomweaver — "When you play a character with cost 2● or less, gain 1●"; a 1● 1✦ Veil 2● body that refunds energy on each cheap play, good even when only a fraction of your deck triggers it.
4. Melodist of the Finale — "When you play a character, gain 1●"; refunds energy on every character rather than only the cheap ones, so it splashes into any creature deck and rewards a cheap curve without needing one.

## Recursion and replay bodies

5. Clockwork Conductor — "You may play characters with cost 2● or less from your void"; a self-contained 4● 3✦ body that turns whatever cheap characters end up in your void into a second hand, valuable even at low density.
6. Starsea Traveler — "Once per turn, you may play a character with cost 2● or less from your void"; a fair 3● 3✦ body that quietly buys back one cheap body per turn for grindy value in any deck that runs a few.
7. Scrap Reclaimer — "Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand"; a 0● 0✦ body that enters Awakened with Veil 2● and rebuilds your hand two bodies at a time, useful in any void-leaning deck.
8. Steel Abomination — "▸Dissolved: Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand" plus growth as cheap characters hit the void; a resilient 3● 3✦ body that pays out a two-card refund on its own when it trades.
9. Wreckage Prowler — "1●: Return a character with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand" plus "1●, ☪, Abandon a character: Draw a card"; a flexible 2● 2✦ body that rebuys one body at a time and converts surplus into cards.

## Cheap-body materialize payoffs

10. Keeper of Forgotten Light — "When you materialize a non-figment character with cost 2● or less, draw a card with ephemeral"; a 2● 2✦ card engine that turns each cheap body into a card, and a handful of cheap drops is enough to make it worthwhile.
11. Doorlight Foundling — "▸Materialized: If the top card of your deck is a character with cost 2● or less, materialize it. Otherwise, draw that card"; a self-contained 2● 2✦ body that either advances your curve for free or cantrips, with no setup required.
12. Wondrous Clearing — "▸Materialized: You may materialize a character with cost 2● or less from your hand"; a 1● 1✦ body that gives a free extra cheap drop and trigger whenever you have a cheap character in hand.
13. Prophet of the Consumed — "▸Materialized: Draw a card with cost 2● or less. It costs 0●"; a 3● 3✦ body that digs up a cheap character and plays it free, self-contained value that does not need a stocked deck of them.
14. Lumin-Gate Seer — "When you materialize a character with cost X●, draw a card"; a 2✦ Veil 2● body that draws off the cheap drops you are already making, rewarding the curve without depending on density.

## Tutors and selection for cheap bodies

15. Dragonward — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 2● or less"; a 3● tutor body that finds whichever cheap character your splash wants, useful even when you run only a few of them.
16. Verdant Pioneer — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 1● or less and materialize it"; a 2● 2✦ body that tutors and plays a cheap body for free, strong on its own card-advantage merits.
17. Forsaken Pact — "To play this card, abandon a character with cost 2● or less" then "Discover a character with cost 2● or less and materialize it"; a 1● event that folds an abandon outlet and a fresh body into one self-contained card.

## Abandon outlets and void fill

18. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●"; a 0● 0✦ body that costs nothing, ramps when abandoned, and feeds the void, splashable into essentially any deck.
19. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●"; a 2● 2✦ repeatable outlet that converts any cheap body into energy turn after turn.
20. Evacuation Enforcer — "☪: Draw 2 cards, then discard 3 cards"; a 1● 1✦ body that enters Awakened so it digs the turn it lands, and the discard loads the void for whatever recursion you run.

## Removal that doubles as fuel

21. Duneveil Vanguard — "Discard a character with cost 2● or less: Dissolve an enemy with cost 2● or less"; a 3● 1✦ removal body that trades a cheap card from hand — a resource your void wants anyway — for a kill.
22. Desolation's Edge — "☪: Store 1⧗" plus "Abandon this character: Dissolve each character with cost less than or equal to the stored ⧗"; a 2● 2✦ body that banks counters and then one-sidedly sweeps a cheap board when abandoned.
23. Astral Angler — "2●, Discard a character: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less" plus "1●: Return an ally to hand"; a 3● 3✦ removal body whose bounce also re-triggers your cheap ▸Materialized abilities.
24. Ordained Collapse — "Dissolve each character with cost X●"; a tunable sweeper that clears a chosen point on the opponent's curve while leaving your cheap allies intact, flexible in any deck.

## Mill-and-recover utility

25. Depthwalker — "▸Materialized: Erode 4. You may return a character with cost 2● or less from among those cards to your hand"; a 2● 1✦ body that fills the void and recovers a cheap body in one self-contained package.
26. Wistful Angler — "▸Challenge: Erode 3. You may put a character with cost 2● or less from among those cards into your hand"; a 3● 3✦ body that does the same mill-and-recover on a recurring challenge trigger.
