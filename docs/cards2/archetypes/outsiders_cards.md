# Outsiders — Card Pool

A companion to [outsiders.md](outsiders.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Outsiders archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/cards.toml`.

## The Outsiders and their bounce loop

1. Vanishing Inquisitor — 1● Outsider with the bounce line; "when this character scores ⍟, draw a card, then discard a card" filters while it grinds.
2. Northlight Maestro — 2● Outsider; bounces an ally on entry and draws cleanly whenever it scores ⍟.
3. Driftrider — 2● Outsider whose payoff fires off *any* allied outsider scoring: "foresee 1, then draw a card," so it scales with the team.
4. Thronebound Arbiter — 4● top-end Outsider; board-wide "draw a card when an allied outsider scores" on a sturdier 2✦ payoff.
5. Breach Artist — 4● Outsider with the bounce line plus "when you return an ally to hand, reduce its cost by 2●," cheapening every rebuy.
6. Nomad of Endless Paths — carries the exact "▸Materialized: Return another ally to hand" bounce, a body that re-buys ▸Materialized value.

## Outsider payoffs and card-advantage engines

7. Last Beacon — makes 1✦ outsider figments and turns each scoring outsider into a card plus ⍟ equal to its ● cost; the central payoff stack.
8. Dreadcall Warden — "▸Night: Draw a card, then discard a card unless you have an allied challenger," a clean draw every turn the deck commits a threat.
9. Oracle of Shifting Skies — "when you play an event, foresee 1," sculpting draws off the dense prevent-and-removal base.

## All-types bodies (count as Outsiders)

10. Signal Resonant — 1● body with all character types and the reposition ability; counts as an Outsider for payoffs and is a prime bounce-and-recast target.
11. Sky Voyager — 1● all-character-types body that counts as an Outsider for the scoring triggers and splashes into the bounce loop.
12. Enginespeaker — 2● all-types body with Reclaim 0●; counts as an Outsider and recurs cheaply for the payoffs.
13. Boundless Wanderer — all types itself and "1●: Allied characters have all character types until end of turn," turning the whole board into Outsiders for the payoffs.
14. Ashen Harbinger — X/X with all character types, scaling outsider body for the scoring triggers.

## Repositioning challengers (the evasion / scoring engine)

15. Saffron Trailblazer — enters Awakened with "❖ – 1●: Move this character to an unoccupied character position," so it can slide into an open lane and score the turn it lands.
16. Planetgazer — repositioning body with ▸Materialized Foresee 2 to smooth draws while it threatens a lane.
17. Keeper of the Tides — ▸Materialized prevents a played card with cost 2● or less and carries the reposition ability; a disruptive body that slides forward.
18. Last Light Herald — 1● reposition body that also supports +2✦, a cheap evasive challenger for the scoring plan.
19. Headtaker Wurm — reposition body whose ▸Materialized can bounce an enemy blocker out of a lane.

## ▸Materialized value bodies (rebuy on bounce)

20. Glimmerwood Scout — ▸Materialized banishes an enemy with cost 4● or less; the premier bounce-rebuy removal body (time the bounce, since it hands a card on leave).
21. Voidcaller — ▸Materialized banishes an enemy warrior, a re-buyable answer to Warrior Aggro.
22. Cosmic Puppeteer — ▸Materialized gains control of an enemy with cost X● or less, stealing a threat and swelling your board each cycle.
23. Pilgrim of Old Stones — ▸Materialized hand-discard on a 2✦ body that both disrupts and becomes a bounce target to re-fire.
24. Wreckland Maverick — ▸Materialized banishes a card from the opponent's hand until it leaves play; bounce and replay to strip another card.
25. Riftwalker — ▸Materialized banishes a chosen card from the opponent's hand until it leaves play, a re-buyable disruption body.
26. Ethereal Courser — 1● ▸Materialized "Return an ally to hand," a cheap bounce enabler that also shuffles void cards when you bounce.
27. Still Dreamer — fast 2● ▸Materialized "Return an ally to hand," a clean bounce body to re-fire ▸Materialized triggers.

## Rematerialize / blink package

28. Duskmount — enters Awakened, "☾: Rematerialize an ally. This character gains +1✦," re-firing Glimmerwood Scout or Voidcaller every turn while it grows.
29. Starlight Guide — fast ▸Challenge "Rematerialize an ally," re-buying a ▸Materialized body without spending a card.
30. Dreadwood Emissary — ▸Materialized gives an event in your void reclaim until end of turn, re-buying a spent prevent or removal event.

## Prevents (the interaction base)

31. Toll of Passage — fast/interrupt 1● prevent of a played card with cost 2● or less, answering a huge share of this low-curve pool.
32. Defiant Parry — Offering soft-tax prevent (unless the opponent pays 1●), firable for 0● while you keep ● open for Outsiders.
33. Echoing Denial — Offering hard prevent of a played card, the 0●-castable counter for tempo turns.
34. Ripple of Defiance — Offering hard prevent for events specifically.
35. Infernal Rest — fast/interrupt 1● tax prevent unless the opponent pays 1●.
36. Cragfall — prevents a played card and puts it into your hand, a clean two-for-one that converts the threat into your card.
37. Break the Sequence — prevents a played card, returns it to the opponent's hand, and draws you a card; a full turn of tempo plus a card.
38. Threadbreaker — Offering body with "▸Materialized: Prevent a played card," doubling as a bounce-rebuy prevent.
39. Clockwork Prodigy — "Cards in your hand have offering," fueling Defiant Parry / Echoing Denial / Ripple of Defiance for 0●.

## Hand disruption

40. Lurking Dread — discards a chosen card with cost 2● or less from the opponent's hand; hits a large fraction of the low curve.
41. Dreaming Groves — discards a chosen event from the opponent's hand, pulling the answer before you commit a threat.

## Removal

43. Grounded — fast/interrupt banish of an enemy with cost exactly X●, scaling to what they commit.
44. Standoff — 0● fast/interrupt dissolve of any enemy at the cost of 5⍟, affordable once the grind plan is ahead.
45. Veil Shatter — Offering fast/interrupt banish of an enemy with cost 3● or less.
46. Ambush Operative — Offering ▸Materialized "Banish an enemy," premium banish on a body the deck can deploy for cheap.

## Tempo bounce

47. Ridgecutter — fast/interrupt "Return a character to hand. Gain 2●," bouncing an enemy blocker out of a lane while netting ● for your next play.
