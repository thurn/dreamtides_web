# Outsiders — Splash Cards

A companion to [outsiders.md](outsiders.md), listing the portable core of the Outsiders archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density, so you can mix a handful of them into a neighboring deck. These are the flexible, self-contained pieces: the cheap interaction this tempo shell runs on plus the individually-strong bounce bodies and ▸Materialized value characters. The type-specific payoffs that need a board built of outsiders are left to the full pool. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Prevents (the interaction base)

1. Toll of Passage — "Prevent a played card with cost 2● or less" for 1●; in this low-curve pool it answers a huge share of plays, and it is great in any deck that wants cheap interaction.
2. Defiant Parry — Offering soft-tax prevent ("Prevent a played card unless the opponent pays 1●"); the Offering keyword lets you fire it for 0● by banishing a card from hand, so it never competes with your own ● on a given turn.
3. Echoing Denial — Offering hard prevent of a played card; the 0●-castable counter that slots into any tempo or control shell.
4. Ripple of Defiance — Offering hard prevent for events specifically, a flexible 0●-castable answer against event-heavy opponents.
5. Infernal Rest — 1● tax prevent ("unless the opponent pays 1●"); cheap, generic disruption that holds up at any density.
6. Cragfall — "Prevent a played card, then put that card into your hand," a clean two-for-one that converts the opponent's threat into your card and needs no archetype support.
7. Break the Sequence — "Prevent a played card, then return it to the opponent's hand. Draw a card," a full turn of tempo plus a card in any deck.
8. Null Sphere — 1● "Prevent a played character with 2✦ or less," a cheap, self-contained counter to early bodies.
9. Ripple Through Reality — 2● "Prevent a played card. Put it on top of the opponent's deck," denying the play and their next draw with no setup.
10. Abolish — 2● "Prevent a played card," an unconditional hard counter that works anywhere.
11. Threadbreaker — Offering body with "▸Materialized: Prevent a played card," doubling as a standalone prevent that happens to be a bounce-rebuy target.
12. Clockwork Prodigy — "Cards in your hand have offering," a 2● body that turns every Offering prevent into a 0● cast and remains useful in any Offering-leaning shell.

## Hand disruption

13. Lurking Dread — "Discard a chosen card with cost 2● or less from the opponent's hand"; with the low curve it hits a large fraction of their deck, and stripping a key card is good in any matchup.
14. Dreaming Groves — "Discard a chosen event from the opponent's hand," pulling a prevent or removal answer before you commit, useful in any proactive deck.
15. Vertiginous Leap — 1● "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand," unconditional hand attack that splashes everywhere.
16. Peak Plunder — "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand" with Reclaim 1●, recurring disruption that is good at any density.
17. The Thinning — 1● "Banish a chosen character from the opponent's hand," a permanent answer to a threat before it lands.

## Removal

18. Molten Duel — 1● "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less," cheap interaction that clears a blocker in any deck.
19. Grounded — "Banish an enemy with cost exactly X●," scaling removal that fits any curve.
20. Standoff — 0● "Dissolve an enemy" at the cost of 5⍟; a flexible answer once you have spark to spend, usable in any board-stalling deck.
21. Veil Shatter — Offering "Banish an enemy with cost 3● or less," 0●-castable removal that splashes into any interactive deck.
22. Ambush Operative — Offering body with "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy," premium banish on a flexible body the deck can deploy cheaply.
23. Dusk Duelist — "▸Materialized: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less," a standalone removal body that also re-buys on bounce.
24. Lightningborn — 2● body, "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 2● or less until this character leaves play," cheap tempo removal that is fine on its own.
25. Gatebound Warden — "▸Challenge: Banish an enemy until end of turn," a recurring soft answer that clears a lane to let a challenger through.

## Bounce bodies and ▸Materialized value (rebuy on bounce, strong standalone)

26. Glimmerwood Scout — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 4● or less," premier removal on a body; strong on its own and a prime bounce-rebuy target (time the bounce, since it hands a card on leave).
27. Voidcaller — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior," a sharp standalone answer to Warrior Aggro that also re-buys on bounce.
28. Cosmic Puppeteer — "▸Materialized: Gain control of an enemy with cost X● or less," a two-for-one swing that is excellent at any density.
29. Pilgrim of Old Stones — "▸Materialized: Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand" on a 2✦ body; disrupts and joins the board with no support needed.
30. Wreckland Maverick — "▸Materialized: Banish a chosen character from the opponent's hand until this character leaves play," self-contained hand disruption on a body.
31. Riftwalker — "▸Materialized: Banish a chosen card from the opponent's hand until this character leaves play," a cheap disruptive body good in any tempo deck.
32. Still Dreamer — fast 2● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," a clean self-bounce body that re-fires any ▸Materialized trigger you pair it with.
33. Ethereal Courser — 1● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," a cheap bounce enabler that also shuffles a void card away when you return a character.
34. Dreadwood Emissary — "▸Materialized: An event in your void gains reclaim until end of turn," re-buying a spent prevent or removal event, valuable in any interaction-heavy deck.
35. Ridgecutter — "Return a character to hand. Gain 2●," bouncing an enemy blocker out of a lane while netting ● for your next play, a flexible tempo swing anywhere.
36. Abyssal Enforcer — "▸Materialized: Return an enemy to hand. It costs 2● more," a tempo body that sets the opponent back a full turn with no support.
37. Veilpiercer — "▸Materialized: Return a played card with cost 3● or less to the opponent's hand. It costs 2● more," soft removal plus a tax on a self-sufficient body.
