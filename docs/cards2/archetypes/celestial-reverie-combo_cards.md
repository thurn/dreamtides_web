# Celestial Reverie Combo — Card Pool

A companion to [celestial-reverie-combo.md](celestial-reverie-combo.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Celestial Reverie Combo archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## Combo core: draw-on-play triggers

1. Celestial Reverie — the namesake 1● trigger: "when you play a character, draw a card," turning every cheap body into a cantrip for the turn.
2. Luminous Ascent — the broader 2● trigger firing on every materialize (figments and returns included), with Reclaim 4● for a second go-off turn.
3. Conduit of Resonance — "when you play a character, trigger every other allied ▸Dawn," re-firing the board's value engines on each loop replay.
4. Cindermarch — "when you materialize a character, awaken each other allied character," so the loop ends with the whole team ready to challenge.

## Self-bouncing and recurring bodies

5. Still Dreamer — 2● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; returns itself, so with a free-cast enabler it replays for 0● indefinitely. The core loop piece.
6. Forgotten Factory Titan — 2● Awakened body with the same self-returning ▸Materialized, doubling as an immediate threat when the loop stops.
7. Nomad of Endless Paths — 2● Unstoppable that returns another ally each materialize, re-buying a second body's trigger and leaving a 5✦ threat behind.
8. Ethereal Courser — 1● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," another cheap self-bouncing body to keep the chain going.
9. Stoneborn Eternal — 1● spirit animal that returns itself for 0● whenever you play a spirit animal, chaining draws off the trigger with no enabler.
10. Wondrous Clearing — 1● "▸Materialized: you may materialize a cost-2●-or-less character from hand," deploying an extra body that itself draws under the trigger.

## Free-play and cost-reduction enablers

11. Nexus Wayfinder — 4● "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●," the clearest combo-online signal; pours the whole low hand onto the board for free.
12. Heavenward Penitent — 1● Veil "first cost-2●-or-less character each turn costs 0●," a cheap enabler that stacks with an energy source to extend the chain.
13. Prophet of the Consumed — 3● "▸Materialized: Draw a cost-2●-or-less card; it costs 0●," handing you a free body to feed the loop.
14. Clockwork Conductor — 4● "you may play cost-2●-or-less characters from your void," recurring spent loop bodies for replays.
15. Starsea Traveler — once per turn play a cost-2●-or-less character from void, re-buying a looped body each turn.

## Energy engines

16. Melodist of the Finale — 2● Veil "When you play a character, gain 1●," refunding a point per play to make the loop net-positive on energy.
17. Spirit of the Greenwood — 1● Awakened "1●, ☪: Gain 1● for each allied character," a large refund on a wide loop board.
18. Blazing Emberwing — 3● "☪: Gain 1● for each allied character," a big energy burst to restart a stalled loop.
19. Mountainwatch Alpha — 2● "☪: Gain 1● for each allied spirit animal," the spirit-animal energy backbone.
20. Skyborne Jellyfish — 2● "▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each allied cost-2●-or-less character," scaling energy on a flooded board.
21. Sunshadow Eagle — 1● stores 1⧗ per spirit animal materialized, spending 3⧗ for 3● to refuel the loop.
22. Dawnprowler Panther — 1● gains 1● on the second-and-later spirit animal each turn, turning a flurry of cheap bodies into energy.
23. Bloomweaver — 1● Veil "When you play a cost-2●-or-less character, gain 1●," a low-curve energy refund per play.
24. Dawnblade Wanderer — 1● "once per turn, when you materialize a character, gain 1●," a cheap body that pays back a point.
25. Lord of Hidden Paths — 2● "when you play a spirit animal, pay 1● to draw a card," adding draw and a spirit-animal anthem to the engine.
26. Seedling Sage — 1● "when you play a character, store 1⧗; 2⧗: gain 1●," converting a long chain into energy.

## Card flow and digging

27. Beacon of Tomorrow — 1● "Discover a character with cost X●," the toolbox tutor that finds Nexus Wayfinder, Still Dreamer, or the missing piece.
28. Dreamcatcher's Call — 1●,X "Discover a character with cost X and materialize it," fetching and deploying a loop piece in one card.
29. Call to the Unknown — 3● "▸Materialized: Discover a character with 2✦ or less," digging for the loop while adding a body to the count.
30. Winterbough Monk — 3● "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to hand," buying back a spent Celestial Reverie or looped body, and a cantrip under the trigger.
31. Key to the Moment — 3● "Return all but one ally to hand, draw a card per character returned," a one-card refill that re-buys every ▸Materialized trigger on replay.
32. Dreamvale Monarch — 2● spirit animal "▸Materialized: Draw a card," a body that draws even without a trigger and twice with one up.
33. Doorlight Foundling — 2● fast that materializes or draws the top card if it is a cheap character, smoothing toward the loop.
34. Lumin-Gate Seer — 2●,X Veil "when you materialize a cost-X● character, draw a card," a second draw-on-materialize engine tuned to the deck's curve.
35. Keeper of Forgotten Light — 2● "when you materialize a non-figment cost-2●-or-less character, draw a card with ephemeral," another redundant draw-on-cast piece.

## Dedicated finishers

48. Terminus — 1● "If you have no cards in your deck, you win," the deterministic empty-deck close after the loop draws the deck.
49. Hatching Ground — X● "For each card played this turn, chosen player erodes X," turning a long loop into a Fatigue kill on the opponent's deck.

## Drain and dissolve payoffs

51. Soulrender — 3● "Abandon a character: chosen player erodes X (its cost)," feeding the same empty-deck Fatigue plan from the abandon side.
52. Sunset Chronicler — 3● "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card," reloading the hand off combat when the draw trigger stalls.
53. Soulreaver — 3● "When a non-figment ally is dissolved, draw a card," the same combat-reload as a survivor body.

## Spirit Animal awaken/replay support

54. Verdant Pilgrim — 1● "☪: Awaken an ally," re-buying materialization-style readiness on the loop's bodies.
55. Moonbound Wolf — 1● "☪, Return an ally to hand: Trigger a spirit animal's Dawn," adding bounce and re-fired ▸Dawn value to extend the chain.
