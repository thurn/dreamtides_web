# Cindermarch / Shadow Soloist Combo — Splash Cards

A companion to [cindermarch-shadow-soloist-combo.md](cindermarch-shadow-soloist-combo.md), listing the portable core of the Cindermarch / Shadow Soloist Combo archetype — the cards that stay good when you splash a thin awaken package into a neighboring deck rather than building the full engine. Each entry is a flexible, self-contained piece that earns its slot at 5–10 cards of density: an awaken enabler that helps any board deck, a standalone energy producer, a cheap ☪ body that is individually fine, or a payoff that does not need the infinite loop assembled. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Backbone awakeners that stand alone

1. Shadow Soloist — "When you play an event, awaken each allied character"; a 3✦ body for 3● that is a strong event-shell payoff on its own, untapping your board for free in any deck already chaining cheap events.
2. Conduit of Resonance — "When you play a character, trigger the '▸Dawn' ability of each other allied character"; a 3✦ body that doubles your ▸Dawn output in any deck running a few Dawn producers, with no loop required.

## On-demand awaken enablers (good in any board deck)

3. Lanternhearted — enters awakened, "☪: Awaken an ally", Reclaim 1●; a flexible spot untapper that pushes one exhausted body to the front rank or re-readies a tapped producer, and buys back for 1●.
4. Marked Direwolf — a 1● body with "☪: Awaken an ally"; a cheap, self-contained spot awakener that untaps one ally each turn even with no other synergy.
5. Pyrestone Avatar — Offering, "Allied characters have awakened"; a static that makes every body you deploy arrive ready to challenge and pay its ☪ cost, useful in any board deck, and bankable for 0● via Offering.
6. Pinnacle Ascendant — "Allied characters have awakened", and gains reclaim 0● when discarded or eroded; a standing untap anthem that the loot and erode engines replay for free.
7. Scrap Reclaimer — a free awakened body (Veil 2●) whose "Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand" recurs cheap bodies in any deck that runs them.
8. Rootbound Witness — awakened, "Abandon this character: Allied characters gain awakened this turn"; a 1● body that is a one-shot mass untap on demand, fine as a cheap awakened beater otherwise.
9. Duskmount — awakened, "☪: Rematerialize an ally. This character gains +1✦"; a self-untapping body that re-fires any ▸Materialized trigger and grows, valuable in any blink or board shell.

## Standalone energy producers and ☪ taps

10. Wolfbond Chieftain — enters awakened, "☪: Gain 1● for each allied warrior"; a 1● body that taps for real energy in any warrior-leaning board even without the re-untap loop.
11. Crescendo Channeler — "▸Dawn: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment" and "2●, ☪: Gain 1● for each allied warrior"; widens the board on Dawn and taps for a pile of energy, a fine standalone warrior engine.
12. Spirit of the Greenwood — awakened, "1●, ☪: Gain 1● for each allied character"; a 1● ramp dork that scales off any wide board, a staple in creature decks regardless of the awaken plan.
13. Blazing Emberwing — "☪: Gain 1● for each allied character"; a whole-board energy faucet that is a strong tap in any go-wide deck.
14. Driftcaller Sovereign — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" and "4●, ☪: This character gains +1✦"; a 1● recurring producer whose Dawn energy is good in any deck and whose pump closes games.
15. Cloudmantle Ray — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●", Reclaim 3●; a cheap recurring ramp body that fits any deck wanting per-turn energy.
16. Melodist of the Finale — Veil 2●, "When you play a character, gain 1●"; a protected ramp body that refills the pool in any character-dense deck, no events required.
17. Seedling Sage — "When you play a character, store 1⧗" and "2⧗: Gain 1●"; a 1● body that banks energy across a long turn in any deck that deploys several characters.
