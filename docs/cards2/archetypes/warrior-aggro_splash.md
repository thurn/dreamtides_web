# Warrior Aggro — Splash Cards

A companion to [warrior-aggro.md](warrior-aggro.md) listing the portable core of the archetype — the cards that stay good at low density and slot cleanly into a neighboring deck that wants to splash a handful of warriors. These are the flexible, self-contained pieces (efficient cheap bodies, flat anthems, energy producers, individually-strong value warriors) rather than the tribal payoffs that need a critical mass of warriors to function. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Cheap aggressive bodies that stand alone

1. Sandglider — 0● fast vanilla warrior; a free, immediate front-rank attacker that asks nothing of the rest of the deck.
2. Aspiring Guardian — 0● 1✦ vanilla warrior; a free body that beats down on its own and pads any board.
3. Pyre Challenger — 1● Awakened warrior, "▸Challenge: This character gains +1✦"; a cheap self-growing attacker that needs no support.
4. Saffron Trailblazer — 1● Awakened warrior with "❖ – 1●: Move this character to an unoccupied character position"; an evasive cheap body that picks an open lane regardless of the rest of the deck.
5. Dawnblade Wanderer — 1● 2✦ warrior, "Once per turn, when you materialize a character, gain 1●"; an aggressive body that refunds a deployment every turn in any creature deck.
6. Blight Weaver — 1● warrior, "▸Materialized: Gain 3●"; a body that pays for itself the turn it lands ("▸Dawn: The opponent gains 1⍟" keeps it to fast builds).

## Flat anthems and self-contained pumps

7. Nocturne Strummer — "Support – Supported characters have +2✦" plus "❖ – 1●: Move this character to an unoccupied character position"; a flat back-rank pump that boosts any front-rank attacker, warrior or not, and slides to the lane that needs it.
8. Charnel Seraph — "Support – Supported characters have +3✦" with an abandon-reposition; a big flat back-rank pump that helps any board, not just a wide warrior one.
9. Momentum's Edge — "▸Materialized: Choose a character type. ... Characters of the chosen type have +1✦"; name whatever you are running and it is a team-wide +1✦ that also counts itself, so it works at any density.
10. Company Commander — "When you play a warrior, this character gains +1✦"; permanent growth that turns even a light warrior count into a self-made finisher over a few turns.
11. Meadowlight Charger — 1● 2✦ warrior, "When an ally gains ✦, it gains an additional +1✦" plus "2●, ☪: Give an ally +1✦"; an aggressive body that amplifies any ✦-granting effect already in the deck.
12. Field Reverent — "X●: Each allied character's ✦ becomes X until end of turn"; converts leftover energy into a board-wide swing on any board, independent of types.
13. Sorrowful Prince — 2● warrior, "X●: This character gains +X✦ until end of turn"; a body that turns surplus energy into reach with no support required.

## One-shot reach and combat-trade value

14. Ironclad Holdout — 1● warrior, "▸Challenge: Each challenging ally gains +1✦"; even with two or three attackers it adds a meaningful ⍟ swing, and it does not care whether the allies are warriors.
15. Flamestride Rider — "Allied warriors cannot be dissolved while challenging"; protects whatever warriors you do run, so the opponent's blockers trade nothing into your attackers.
16. Nineborn Specter — 2● 2✦ warrior, "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟"; a cheap beater that still scores when it trades, good in any aggressive shell.
17. Wired Duelist — "▸Dawn: This character gains +1✦" then "▸Dissolved: Give an ally +X✦"; a growing body that dumps its accumulated spark onto an attacker on the trade.
18. Apocalypse Vigilante — 3● 3✦ warrior, "When this character scores ⍟, the opponent loses an equivalent quantity of ⍟"; a standalone finisher that doubles every swing it lands.
19. Demonbane — 4● warrior, "4●: This character gains +1✦" plus "▸Dissolved: Draw a card for each ✦"; a mana sink beater that refunds itself in cards when it dies.

## Energy and curve enablers

20. Tethered Hollow — "The first warrior you play each turn costs 3● less"; even at low density it discounts one warrior per turn, and the discount is best saved for the most expensive warrior in hand.
21. Sundown Ronin — 4● 4✦ warrior, "Warriors cost you 1● less"; a solid body that quietly shaves the cost of whatever warriors a splash brings, with no board requirement.
22. Lunar Hart — 1● spirit animal, "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 2●"; a cheap repeatable energy accelerant for any fast start.
23. Pyrewatcher — 2● warrior, "Abandon this character: Gain 3●"; a body that doubles as a ritual to power out a bigger turn.

## Card flow that travels

24. Invoker of Myths — "Once per turn, when you materialize a warrior, draw a card"; even a handful of warriors turns each turn's first deployment into a card.
25. Spent Courier — 1● warrior, "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card with ephemeral"; a cheap body that cantrips on both ends regardless of the surrounding deck.
26. Ossuary Overlord — 2● warrior, "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card"; the durable cantrip body that always replaces itself.
27. Frost Visionary — 2● warrior, "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a cheap warrior that replaces itself on entry.
28. Crucible Warlord — 3● 3✦ warrior, "▸Materialized: Draw a warrior"; a beater that digs to your next threat (still useful with only a few warriors to find).
29. Sigil Analyst — 5● 5✦ warrior, "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards"; a top-end body that refills the hand in any deck.
30. Thundercatcher — 2● warrior, "2●, Abandon a 1✦ ally: Draw 2 cards"; cashes a spent cheap body or figment into cards, an outlet any creature deck appreciates.
31. Key to the Moment — "Return all but one allied character to hand. Draw a card for each character returned"; a board-into-cards refuel that re-fires every ▸Materialized, valuable in any wide creature deck.

## Recursion and staying power

32. Aftermath Bloom — "To play this event, abandon a warrior. Discover a warrior with higher cost, then materialize it"; with even one spent warrior it trades up into a bigger threat straight into play.
33. Starbound Striker — 2● warrior, "▸Dissolved: Return another warrior from your void to your hand"; a cheap body that buys back a warrior when it trades.
34. Tranquil Duelist — Vengeful warrior, "▸Dissolved: Return another warrior from your void to your hand"; an attacker that recurs a warrior on the trade.
35. Infernal Cavalier — 3● 3✦ warrior, "▸Dissolved: Return another warrior with cost 4● or less from your void to your hand"; a mid-cost beater with built-in recursion.
36. Pit Descender — "▸Materialized: Erode 3" plus "☪, Abandon a warrior: Materialize a warrior with cost 2● or less from your void"; a self-contained value loop that re-buys a cheap warrior each turn.

## Type-flexible bodies and tribal removal

37. Enginespeaker — 2● "This character has all character types" with Reclaim 0●; counts as a warrior for every payoff and as anything else the deck wants.
38. Sky Voyager — 1● "all character types" body with "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; a flexible body that also ramps.
39. Signal Resonant — 1● "all character types" body with "❖ – 1●: Move this character to an unoccupied character position"; a flexible body that slides into any lane.
40. Voidcaller — 2● 2✦, "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior"; on-color removal that clears a blocker and is a fine standalone tempo play.
41. Summons of the Bonded — 1● fast event, "Dissolve an enemy warrior"; cheap, efficient removal that needs nothing from your own board.
