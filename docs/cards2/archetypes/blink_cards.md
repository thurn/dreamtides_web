# Blink — Card Pool

A companion to [blink.md](blink.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Blink archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## Rematerialize enablers

1. Passage Through Oblivion — 1● "Rematerialize an ally" with Reclaim 1●; the cheapest, cleanest re-fire, and it buys itself back for a second trigger.
2. Duskmount — Awakened "☾: Rematerialize an ally. This character gains +1✦"; the build-around engine that re-fires a trigger every turn and grows into a finisher.
3. Starlight Guide — 2● body with "▸Challenge: Rematerialize an ally"; the rematerialize comes free off committing it to a lane.

## Bounce-and-replay enablers

4. Still Dreamer — "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; picks up a spent value body to recast, and can return itself to keep cycling.
5. Forgotten Factory Titan — Awakened "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; same bounce loop, can act the turn it lands and bounce itself.
6. Ethereal Courser — 1● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," with a void-shuffle rider whenever you bounce; a cheap repeatable bounce enabler.
7. Nomad of Endless Paths — "▸Materialized: Return another ally to hand"; a 5-spark body that doubles as a bounce setup.
8. Key to the Moment — "Return all but one allied character to hand. Draw a card for each"; mass bounce that refuels and resets every ▸Materialized at once.
9. Stoneborn Leviathan — "1●, Return an ally to hand: Give an ally +1✦"; a repeatable bounce activator that folds into the replay loop.

## Outsider bounce package

10. Northlight Maestro — fast "▸Materialized: Return another ally to hand. Move to its position"; built-in bounce enabler that draws when it scores.
11. Vanishing Inquisitor — 1● fast bounce-on-entry that draws (and filters) on scoring; the cheapest Outsider enabler.
12. Thronebound Arbiter — fast bounce-on-entry; draws whenever any allied outsider scores, rewarding the value board.
13. Breach Artist — fast bounce-on-entry that reduces the returned ally's cost by 2●, making the replay loop cheaper.

## ▸Materialized payoffs — card advantage

14. Frost Visionary — "▸Materialized: Draw a card" on a cheap 2● body; the bread-and-butter re-fire target.
15. Dreamvale Monarch — "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a second copy of the core draw engine.
16. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand"; the value standout, recurs your best card every re-fire and can rebuy Passage.
17. Verdant Wayfarer — "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards"; a heavy refuel that dedicated enablers turn into four-plus cards.
18. Sigil Analyst — "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards"; another bulk-draw body to loop with Duskmount.
19. Urban Cipher — "▸Materialized: Discard your hand, then draw 3 cards"; a repeatable hand-refill on entry.
20. Prophet of the Consumed — "▸Materialized: Draw a card with cost 2● or less. It costs 0●"; a re-fire that draws and ramps a free play.
21. Looming Oracle — Veil "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a cheap, hard-to-kill draw body for the loop.

## ▸Materialized payoffs — selection and recursion

22. Call to the Unknown — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with 2✦ or less"; each re-fire digs three cards deep to feed the engine.
23. Barrage Specialist — fast "▸Materialized: Discover an event with cost 2● or less"; fetches a prevent or cheap removal, the interaction this deck wants.
24. Rift Pilgrim — "▸Materialized: Look at the top 4 and banish one. ▸Dissolved: put it in hand"; repeatable card selection that pays off later.
25. Dragonward — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 2● or less"; another discover-on-entry to re-fire.
26. Verdant Pioneer — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 1● or less and materialize it"; each re-fire stamps out another free body.
27. Doorlight Foundling — fast "▸Materialized: materialize the top card if a cheap character, else draw it"; card advantage or a free body every re-fire.

## ▸Materialized payoffs — removal and disruption

28. Dusk Duelist — "▸Materialized: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less"; recurring removal stapled to a body.
29. Glimmerwood Scout — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 4● or less"; rematerialize (don't bounce) for repeat removal.
30. Voidcaller — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior"; a sharp re-firing answer against Warrior decks.
31. Cosmic Puppeteer — "▸Materialized: Gain control of an enemy with cost X● or less"; a backbreaking steal to re-fire.
32. Ambush Operative — Offering "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy"; drop it for 0● to banish a threat, and re-fire it if you can.
33. Blazepath Traveler — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 2● or less"; another permanent removal-on-entry to loop.
34. Lightningborn — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 2● or less until this leaves play"; recurring soft removal.
35. Abyssal Enforcer — "▸Materialized: Return an enemy to hand. It costs 2● more"; repeatable tempo bounce on a body.
36. Pilgrim of Old Stones — "▸Materialized: Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand"; each re-fire strips their best card, and a fine 2✦ body to bounce.
37. Veilpiercer — fast "▸Materialized: Return a played card to hand, costs 2● more"; an interrupt body whose bounce you want to re-buy.

## ▸Materialized payoffs — board building

38. Featherlight Summoner — "▸Materialized: Materialize a 4✦ monstrosity figment"; re-fire to stamp out big scoring bodies, a way for the value engine to actually win.

## Going wider and looping

39. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; lets you dump strings of cheap ▸Materialized bodies for free, firing trigger after trigger.
40. Cindermarch — "When you materialize a character, awaken each other allied character"; un-exhausts the board on many-materialize turns, keeping Duskmount and ☾-bodies ready.
41. Conduit of Resonance — "When you play a character, trigger the '▸Dawn' of each other ally"; turns each body into a board-wide ▸Dawn pulse that can chain energy into more plays.
42. Roots of Rebirth — "Materialize two random characters with cost 2● or less from your deck"; slams two cheap bodies into play and fires both entry triggers at once.

## Energy enablers

43. Cloudmantle Ray — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 3●; an energy dork that accelerates value plays and comes back from the void.
44. Sky Voyager — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●," all character types; energy plus a flexible bounce/rematerialize target and tribal filler.

## Named removal

45. Bladefall — 1● fast "Banish an enemy"; the clean cheap answer for slots where you just need to remove a threat.
