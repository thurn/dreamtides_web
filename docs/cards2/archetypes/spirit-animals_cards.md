# Spirit Animals — Card Pool

A companion to [spirit-animals.md](spirit-animals.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Spirit Animals archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## Tribal energy engines

1. Spirit of the Greenwood — Awakened spirit animal, "1●, ☾: Gain 1● for each allied character"; the namesake net-positive energy faucet on a wide board.
2. Mountainwatch Alpha — "☾: Gain 1● for each allied spirit animal"; tribal faucet that scales with how wide you have gone.
3. Ghostlight Wolves — "☾: Gain 1● for each allied spirit animal" plus a Support anthem giving supported spirit animals +2✦.
4. Blazing Emberwing — "☾: Gain 1● for each allied character"; the Greenwood engine with no up-front cost, counting every character.
5. Skyborne Jellyfish — spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each allied character with cost 2● or less"; rewards the flat cheap curve every Dawn.
6. Sunshadow Eagle — spirit animal, "When you materialize a spirit animal, store 1⧗. 3⧗: Gain 3●"; banks tribal materializations into energy mid-combo.
7. Seedling Sage — "When you play a character, store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●"; refunds energy as you chain bodies.
8. Cloudmantle Ray — 1● spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 3●; the early accelerant that returns from the void.
9. Dawnhorn Elder — spirit animal, "2●, ☾: Store 1⧗. ▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each stored ⧗"; banks counters into recurring Dawn energy.
10. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; turns the whole low curve free, the deck's most explosive enabler.
11. Heavenward Penitent — "The first character with cost 2● or less you play each turn costs you 0●"; behind Veil, a per-turn free cheap drop.
12. Melodist of the Finale — Veil 2●, "When you play a character, gain 1●"; refunds energy on every body chained, kept alive by Veil.

14. Starrunner — spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●", "Abandon: Gain 1●", Reclaim 0●; recurring cheap producer.
15. Starchaser — 1● spirit animal, Veil 2●, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; resilient redundant faucet body.
16. Emberwolf Triad — 1● spirit animal, "▸Materialized: Foresee 1. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; cheap body with dig and energy.
17. Undying Fang — spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●, with 3 allied spirit animals gain 2● instead"; scales with a wide tribal board.
18. Hallowed Stag — 1● spirit animal, Vengeful, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; cheap producer that trades up on defense.
19. Worldsong Behemoth — 1● spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; redundant faucet body.
20. Ethereal Trailblazer — 1● spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; redundant faucet body.
21. Blazebound Sentinel — 1● spirit animal, "▸Dawn: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Gain 1●"; refunds energy even when it dies.
22. Ebonwing — 1● spirit animal, "▸Materialized: Erode 2. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; faucet body with a self-mill rider.
23. Lunar Hart — 1● spirit animal, "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 2●"; double energy on entry and each Dawn.
24. Lone Castaway — 0● spirit animal (discard a character to play), "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 1●"; near-free recurring producer.
25. Fangbound — 0● spirit animal (banish a card to play), Veil 3●, "▸Materialized, Dawn: Gain 1●"; free resilient faucet body.
26. Luminwings — 0● spirit animal, "▸Dawn: With 3 allied spirit animals, gain 1●"; free body that rewards going wide.
27. Emerald Guardian — spirit animal, "▸Materialized: Draw a card. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; a body that replaces itself and produces energy.

## Materialize / play-trigger payoffs

28. Young Beastcaller — spirit animal, "When you materialize a spirit animal, gain 1●; on your second each turn, draw a spirit animal"; tribal energy plus draw.
29. Dawnprowler Panther — 1● spirit animal, "When you materialize a spirit animal (not the first this turn), gain 1●"; rewards flooding the tribe.
30. Dreamborne Leviathan — spirit animal, "Once per turn, when you play a spirit animal, materialize a 1✦ spirit animal figment"; widens the board for free.
31. Lord of Hidden Paths — spirit animal anthem, "Allied spirit animals have +1✦; when you play a spirit animal, pay 1● to draw a card"; tribal anthem plus draw.
32. Eternal Stag — spirit animal, "Allied spirit animals have +1✦. 2●, ☾: Draw a spirit animal"; tribal anthem and tribal draw engine.
33. Dreamvale Monarch — spirit animal, "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; the redundant card-advantage body that replaces itself.
34. Lumineth — spirit animal, "☾, Discard a character: Discover a character with cost 1● higher"; tribal body that upgrades stranded cards.
35. Oathbound Pair — "▸Materialized, Dawn: Give an ally +1✦"; steady spark growth on a body every turn.
36. Stoneborn Leviathan — spirit animal, "1●, Return an ally to hand: Give an ally +1✦"; tribal body that recycles a body for spark.

## Recursion / self-bounce engines

37. Stoneborn Eternal — spirit animal, "When you play a spirit animal, return this to hand, it costs 0● this turn"; the body you never run out of.
38. Moonbound Wolf — Awakened spirit animal, "☾, Return an ally to hand: Trigger the ▸Dawn ability of an allied spirit animal"; recursion plus on-demand Dawn re-fires.
39. Passage Through Oblivion — "Rematerialize an ally. Reclaim 1●"; re-fires a ▸Materialized body and returns from the void to do it again.
40. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand"; rebuys a finisher or discarded spirit animal, abusable with bounce loops.

## ▸Dawn / awaken combo enablers

41. Conduit of Resonance — "When you play a character, trigger the ▸Dawn ability of each other allied character"; turns a board of Dawn producers into a deployment engine.
42. Unquenched — "When you materialize an ally, trigger its ▸Dawn ability"; pays you the moment a fresh Dawn producer enters.
43. Cindermarch — "When you materialize a character, awaken each other allied character"; wakes the whole flood so it can challenge the turn it lands.
44. Verdant Pilgrim — spirit animal, "☾: Awaken an ally"; wakes a body to swing the turn it floods.
45. Pyrestone Avatar — Offering, "Allied characters have awakened"; static awaken enabler that can drop for 0●.
46. Pinnacle Ascendant — "Allied characters have awakened" with reclaim when discarded/eroded; static awaken anthem.

## Going wider with figments

47. Endless Projection — "When you play a character, materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment"; leaves a row of figments behind a Storm chain.
48. Radiant Trio — "Materialize X 1✦ ethereal figments"; burst flood that feeds energy engines and Spirit Bond count.
49. Boundless Wanderer — "Has all character types. 1●: Allied characters have all character types until end of turn"; makes figments and off-type bodies count as spirit animals.
50. Sky Voyager — "Has all character types. ▸Dawn: Gain 1●"; a spirit-animal-by-type Dawn faucet.
51. Momentum's Edge — "▸Materialized: Choose a type, this character is that type, that type has +1✦"; names the tribe and becomes a spirit animal anthem.

## Finishers

52. Spirit Bond — "Allied characters have +X✦ where X is the number of allied characters"; the default wide-board alpha strike.
53. Mystic Runefish — spirit animal, "5●, ☾: Each allied spirit animal's ✦ becomes 7"; the tribal nuke a wide board makes lethal.
54. Spiritbound Alpha — spirit animal, "❖ – 5●: Allied spirit animals gain +3✦ and vengeful"; the Fast tribal finisher that trades up in the opponent's window.
55. Field Reverent — "X●: Each allied character's ✦ becomes X until end of turn"; the finisher for a mixed board scaled to energy.
56. Crumbling Behemoth — "Other allied characters have 10✦"; top-end payoff that makes the whole board a 10✦ wall.

## Tutors

57. Beacon of Tomorrow — "Choose X. Discover a character with cost X●"; the deck's tutor for whichever finisher the board wants.
58. The Dread Sovereign — "X●, ☾, Abandon another character: Discover a character with cost X●, then materialize it"; finds and deploys in one activation off a spare body.
59. Curio Dealer — Veil 2●, "1●, Discard a character with cost X●: Discover a character with cost X●"; trades a stranded card for the exact body, survives removal.
60. Through the Rift — "Discover a spirit animal"; cheap tribal tutor for the missing piece.

## Disruption and interaction

61. Molten Duel — Fast, "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less"; clears a blocker so challengers go unpaired and score.
62. Voidcaller — spirit-animal-supporting body, "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior"; targeted hate against Warrior decks, leaving a 2✦ body.
63. Thundercatcher — "2●, Abandon a 1✦ ally: Draw 2 cards"; turns a spent 1● body into two fresh cards.

## Combo / value reach

64. Celestial Reverie — "Until end of turn, when you play a character, draw a card"; the draw engine that turns each free body into a card.
65. Terminus — "If you have no cards in your deck, you win the game"; the empty-deck finish for the combo build.
66. Hatching Ground — "For each card you've played this turn, chosen player erodes X"; Fatigues the opponent after a Storm turn.
67. Roots of Rebirth — "Materialize two random characters with cost 2● or less from your deck"; almost never whiffs in a wall-to-wall cheap deck.
68. Soulkindler — "If this card is in your void, allied characters have +1✦"; a permanent anthem from outside play.
69. Ashen Remnant — "+1✦ per character in your void. Abandon another character: Erode 1"; feeds on the wide board of expendable bodies for the abandon reach.
