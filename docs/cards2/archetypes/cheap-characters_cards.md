# Cheap Characters — Card Pool

A companion to [cheap-characters.md](cheap-characters.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Cheap Characters (cost 2● or less) archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## Acceleration and cost reducers

1. Heavenward Penitent — "The first character with cost 2● or less you play each turn costs you 0●"; the premier free-body accelerant, and Veil 2● keeps the discount online.
2. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; the uncapped free-play engine that turns the whole archetype into pure profit.
3. Bloomweaver — "When you play a character with cost 2● or less, gain 1●"; refunds energy as you chain cheap bodies, pulling ahead alongside a discount.

## Cost-≤2 play / materialize payoffs

4. Keeper of Forgotten Light — "When you materialize a non-figment character with cost 2● or less, draw a card with ephemeral"; turns every cheap body into a card.
5. Doorlight Foundling — materializes the top card for free when it is a cheap character, advancing the curve off the top of the deck.
6. Wondrous Clearing — "▸Materialized: You may materialize a character with cost 2● or less from your hand"; a free extra cheap body and trigger.
7. Skyborne Jellyfish — "▸Dawn: Gain 1● for each allied character with cost 2● or less"; ramps off a wide cheap board.
8. Lumin-Gate Seer — "When you materialize a character with cost X●, draw a card" (set to the cheap cost); rewards the cheap-body churn with cards.
9. Prophet of the Consumed — "Draw a card with cost 2● or less. It costs 0●"; digs for cheap bodies and plays one free.

## Recursion / replay engines

10. Clockwork Conductor — "You may play characters with cost 2● or less from your void"; the uncapped recursion engine that turns the void into a second hand.
11. Starsea Traveler — "Once per turn, you may play a character with cost 2● or less from your void"; the fair, repeatable void replay.
12. Scrap Reclaimer — "Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand"; bulk recursion that enters awakened with Veil 2●.
13. Steel Abomination — grows as cheap characters hit the void and returns 2 of them on death; the deck's resilient recursion body.
14. Wreckage Prowler — "1●: Return a character with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand" plus a draw outlet; one-at-a-time rebuy that converts surplus to cards.
15. Reclaimer of Lost Paths — grants reclaim to a void character and removes the reclaimed-banish rule, looping cheap bodies through your outlets indefinitely.
16. Revenant of the Lost — "A character in your void with cost 2● or less gains reclaim until end of turn"; an on-demand reclaim grant for the chosen bodies.
17. Pathwalker — returns events from the void and fills the void from the discard each Dawn; the event-side recursion the deck slots in to be cast from the graveyard.
18. Phantom Flotilla — "Materialize up to two characters with cost 2● or less from your void"; a burst of two free bodies and triggers.
19. Radiant Convergence — "Materialize a character with cost 2● or less from your void"; a 1● replay with an abandon-fueled reclaim.
20. Veilseeker — "Return all characters with cost 2● or less from your void to play"; a mass void-to-play recursion payoff.
21. Borrowed Minutes — "Materialize each character with cost X● or less in your void" (banished end of turn); a one-turn mass cheap-body swarm.
22. Pit Descender — "Materialize a warrior with cost 2● or less from your void"; abandon-fueled recursion of cheap warriors.
23. Prism Caller — "When you play a warrior, you may materialize a character with cost 2● or less from your void"; repeatable void recursion stapled to warriors.

## Mass return

24. Wake the Fallen — "Materialize all characters in your void which dissolved this turn"; the broadest one-card mass return after an abandon turn.
25. Shadow March — "Materialize all characters with cost 2● or less in your void which dissolved this turn"; the version keyed exactly on the deck's bodies.
26. Heroic Rescue — "Materialize up to three chosen characters in your void which dissolved this turn"; the cheap, selective burst.
27. Roots of Rebirth — "Materialize two random characters with cost 2● or less from your deck"; goes wide off the deck directly into play.

## Abandon outlets and void fill

28. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●"; the cleanest free body that makes energy and feeds the void.
29. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●"; the repeatable outlet that converts cheap bodies to energy turn after turn.
30. Ruptured Dynamo — "Abandon a character: Return a character from your void to your hand"; closes the smallest abandon-replay loop on its own.
31. Forsaken Pact — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less" then "Discover a character with cost 2● or less and materialize it"; folds the outlet and a fresh body into one card.
32. Evacuation Enforcer — enters awakened and "Draw 2 cards, then discard 3 cards"; loads the void with cheap bodies while digging.

## Tutors and selection for cheap bodies

33. Dragonward — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 2● or less"; tutors a cheap body to hand.
34. Verdant Pioneer — "Discover a character with cost 1● or less and materialize it"; tutors and plays a cheap body for free.
35. Light of Emergence — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Materialize a random character with cost 2● or less from your deck"; an abandon-fueled cheap-body engine.
36. Wraith of Twisting Shadows — "+1✦ for each character with cost 2● or less in your void" plus "Draw a character with cost 2● or less"; a scaling body that digs for more cheap characters.
37. Depthwalker — "Erode 4. You may return a character with cost 2● or less from among those cards to your hand"; mills and recovers a cheap body.
38. Wistful Angler — "Erode 3. You may put a character with cost 2● or less from among those cards into your hand"; another mill-and-recover for cheap bodies.

## Payoffs and win conditions

39. Abomination of Memory — "+4✦ if there are three or more characters with cost 2● or less in your void"; a 5✦ body for 1● once the void fills.
40. Specter of Silent Snow — "▸Dawn: Abandon a character" plus draw-and-grow on every abandon; the grinding finisher that outgrows the board.
41. Ferryman of the Falls — "When a character with cost 2● or less is put into your void, draw a card"; the top-end card engine that refills your hand on a big abandon turn.
42. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟"; converts the endless cheap-body supply directly into points.
43. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟"; passively scores off every abandon in the deck.
44. Burst of Obliteration — "Gain X⍟" mode; closes the game off the deck's energy refunds and free plays at a large X.
45. Vaultbreaker — "Abandon a character... Reduce the cost of this card by 1●"; the 8✦ top-end the abandon outlets pay for, fed by a glut of cheap bodies.

## Disruption and removal

46. Desolation's Edge — stores ⧗ and "Abandon this character: Dissolve each character with cost less than or equal to the stored ⧗"; a one-sided sweep of cheap boards plus an abandon trigger.
47. Duneveil Vanguard — "Discard a character with cost 2● or less: Dissolve an enemy with cost 2● or less"; trades a cheap card (which the void wants anyway) for a kill.
48. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗" plus "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦"; scaling removal that grows with the abandon loop.
49. Ordained Collapse — "Dissolve each character with cost X●"; a tunable sweeper that clears the opponent's curve while leaving cheap allies intact.
50. Astral Angler — "Discard a character: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less" plus "Return an ally to hand"; removal that also re-triggers your cheap ▸Materialized abilities.
51. Shatter the Frail — "Dissolve an enemy with 1✦ or less" plus Reclaim 1●; recurring cheap interaction from the void.
52. Whisper of the Past — grants an event reclaim and self-recurs to the top of your deck; keeps cheap interaction flowing every turn.
