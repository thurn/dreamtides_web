# Blink — Splash Cards

A companion to [blink.md](blink.md), listing the portable core of Blink — the cards that stay good when you fold a handful of them into a neighboring creature deck rather than build the whole list around them. Each is either a self-contained rematerialize/bounce enabler or an individually-strong ▸Materialized body that earns its slot at low density. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`. Blink is a value sub-archetype rather than a standalone deck, so this list is deliberately a supporting core.

## Rematerialize enablers

1. Passage Through Oblivion — 1● "Rematerialize an ally" with "Reclaim 1●"; one card that re-fires your best ▸Materialized body twice, and points at any entry trigger your shell already runs, so it needs no Blink density to be good.
2. Duskmount — Awakened "☪: Rematerialize an ally. This character gains +1✦"; a self-sufficient one-card engine that re-fires a trigger every turn and grows into a finisher, fine even if it is the only enabler you run.
3. Starlight Guide — 2● "▸Challenge: Rematerialize an ally"; the rematerialize comes free off committing a body you wanted to send forward anyway, so it slots into any creature deck with a value ▸Materialized to target.

## Bounce-and-replay enablers

4. Still Dreamer — 2● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; picks up a spent value body for a recast, and can return itself when nothing better is around, so it works on a board with only one or two ▸Materialized friends.
5. Forgotten Factory Titan — Awakened "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand"; the same self-bouncing loop on an awakened 2● body that can act the turn it lands.
6. Ethereal Courser — 1● "▸Materialized: Return an ally to hand," with a void-shuffle rider on the bounce; a cheap, repeatable bounce enabler that is a fine standalone tempo body.
7. Northlight Maestro — fast 2● "▸Materialized: Return another ally to hand. Move this character to that ally's position," drawing when it scores; a built-in bounce enabler that is independently strong as a self-moving challenger.
8. Vanishing Inquisitor — 1● fast bounce-on-entry that draws and filters on scoring; the cheapest Outsider enabler and a strong card on its own in any tempo shell.
9. Breach Artist — fast bounce-on-entry that reduces the returned ally's cost by 2●; makes the replay cheaper and is a solid standalone bounce body.

## ▸Materialized value bodies — card advantage

10. Frost Visionary — 2● "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a cheap cantrip body that replaces itself with no synergy required and is the bread-and-butter re-fire target when you do have an enabler.
11. Dreamvale Monarch — 2● "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a second copy of the core cantrip body, equally fine standalone.
12. Winterbough Monk — 3● "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand"; recurs your best spell or character even on a single entry, and becomes a soft resource lock under any rematerialize.
13. Verdant Wayfarer — 5● "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards" with Reclaim; a heavy refuel that is a strong card on its own and turns into four-plus cards under an enabler.
14. Looming Oracle — Veil 2● "▸Materialized: Draw a card"; a cheap, hard-to-kill cantrip body that is a clean inclusion at any density.
15. Prophet of the Consumed — "▸Materialized: Draw a card with cost 2● or less. It costs 0●"; a body that draws and ramps a free play on its own, with extra upside when re-fired.
16. Sigil Analyst — 5● "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards"; bulk draw on a sizable body, good standalone and a fat target to loop.

## ▸Materialized value bodies — selection and recursion

17. Call to the Unknown — 3● "▸Materialized: Discover a character with 2✦ or less"; digs three cards deep for your next play with no synergy needed, and feeds the engine when re-fired.
18. Dragonward — "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 2● or less"; another standalone discover-on-entry that finds whatever your deck wants next.
19. Verdant Pioneer — 2● "▸Materialized: Discover a character with cost 1● or less and materialize it"; nets a free body on entry, value on its own and a board-builder when looped.
20. Rift Pilgrim — "▸Materialized: Look at the top 4 cards and banish one. ▸Dissolved: Put the banished card into your hand"; card selection now that pays off later, fine in any deck and repeatable with an enabler.
21. Doorlight Foundling — fast "▸Materialized: materialize the top card if a cheap character, else draw it"; card advantage or a free body on entry, a flexible standalone include.
22. Barrage Specialist — fast "▸Materialized: Discover an event with cost 2● or less"; fetches a prevent or cheap removal from your own list, so it is good in any deck carrying interactive events.

## ▸Materialized removal and disruption on a body

23. Dusk Duelist — 3● "▸Materialized: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less"; removal stapled to a body, a strong card with zero synergy and recurring removal when re-fired.
24. Glimmerwood Scout — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 4● or less"; premium permanent removal on a body (rematerialize rather than bounce it, since it gives the opponent a card on leaving play).
25. Voidcaller — 2● "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior"; a sharp answer against the format's warrior decks that re-fires cleanly.
26. Blazepath Traveler — 5● "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 2● or less"; another permanent removal-on-entry that is a fine standalone body.
27. Lightningborn — 2● "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy with cost 2● or less until this leaves play"; cheap soft removal on a body, good on its own.
28. Cosmic Puppeteer — "▸Materialized: Gain control of an enemy with cost X● or less"; a backbreaking steal that swings the board even on one entry, and is devastating to re-fire.
29. Ambush Operative — 5● Offering "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy"; drop it for 0● to banish a threat at a key moment, strong as a one-shot answer regardless of density.
30. Abyssal Enforcer — 3● "▸Materialized: Return an enemy to hand. It costs 2● more"; repeatable tempo on a body, a clean standalone play.
31. Pilgrim of Old Stones — 2● "▸Materialized: Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand"; strips their best card on entry, a fine 2✦ body to bounce, and good interaction at any density.

## Board building

32. Featherlight Summoner — 4● "▸Materialized: Materialize a 4✦ monstrosity figment"; stamps out a big scoring body on entry, strong on its own and a way for a value shell to actually close.

## Going wider and accelerating

33. Nexus Wayfinder — "Characters with cost 2● or less cost you 0●"; lets any low-curve creature deck dump strings of cheap bodies, firing entry trigger after entry trigger, and splashes well wherever the curve is low.
34. Cloudmantle Ray — 1● "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 3●; an energy dork that accelerates your value plays and comes back from the void, useful in any low-curve shell.
35. Sky Voyager — 1● "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with all character types; energy plus a flexible bounce/rematerialize target that counts for any tribal payoff your shell already runs.

## Named removal

36. Challenge of the Blade — 1● "Banish an enemy"; the clean, cheap, unconditional answer for the slots where you just need to remove a threat and keep your plan moving.
