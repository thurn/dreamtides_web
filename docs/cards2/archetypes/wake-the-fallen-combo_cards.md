# Wake the Fallen / Shadow March Combo — Card Pool

A companion to [wake-the-fallen-combo.md](wake-the-fallen-combo.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Wake the Fallen / Shadow March Combo archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/cards.toml`.

## Mass-return engines

1. Wake the Fallen — The marquee enabler: 3● to materialize *everything* in your void that dissolved this turn, no cost ceiling, so it returns your whole abandoned board at once.
2. Shadow March — Functionally the second Wake the Fallen at 3●, returning all dissolved-this-turn bodies with cost 2● or less — exactly the half the deck is built from.
3. Heroic Rescue — The cheap surgical version at 1●: materialize up to three chosen dissolved-this-turn characters, enough to re-arm a loop without paying full price and leaving fodder behind on purpose.
4. Veilseeker — A mass-return engine on a body: "5●, ☾, Abandon this character: Return all characters with cost 2● or less from your void to play." Re-arms the whole loop and feeds the void abandoning itself.
5. Phantom Flotilla — Materializes up to two 2● bodies from your void regardless of when they died, and Reclaim 5● lets it come back to re-arm a loop on a turn you did not abandon a full board.
6. Borrowed Minutes — Materializes each character with cost X● or less in your void and banishes them at end of turn — a one-turn army built straight out of the void that you abandon through your outlets before it banishes anyway.
7. Cradle of Storms — Materializes all characters in your void *discarded* this turn; the discard-fed parallel to Wake the Fallen that pairs with Pulse of Sacrifice and other hand-dump outlets, and Reclaims 0● when discarded.
8. Radiant Convergence — Materializes a 2● body from your void, and its Reclaim cost is abandoning two characters — a single-target return that simultaneously loads the void for the next mass-return.

## Abandon outlets — the front half of the loop

9. Infernal Ascendant — The workhorse: "Abandon another character: Gain 1●" with no cost and no use limit, chewing through a board and turning each body into 1● plus a ▸Dissolved trigger.
10. Spirit Reaping — "Abandon a warrior: Gain 2●," the same outlet at a higher rate for the warrior fodder.
11. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●," an energy outlet tuned to the deck's cheap bodies.
12. Soulbinder — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. 2⧗: Gain 1●," a 1● outlet that banks abandons into energy.
13. Saltless Mariner — Both outlet and payoff: "Veil 2●. Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟," no cost ceiling and self-protecting behind Veil.
14. Ruptured Dynamo — "Abandon a character: Return a character from your void to your hand," an outlet that fishes a body back so you can replay it and re-stage a loop without a mass-return.
15. Stargazer Adrift — "Abandon another character: Foresee 1," turning each abandon into deck sculpting toward the next combo piece.
16. Ashborn Necromancer — "Abandon a character: Erode 2," an outlet that fires a ▸Dissolved trigger while milling your own void full of fodder.
17. Harvester of Despair — "Abandon another character: This character gains +1✦," a 1● repeatable outlet whose product is the stream of ▸Dissolved triggers.

## Void fodder / recurring bodies

18. Conduit of Ashes — The centerpiece fodder: a 0● 0✦ body that "Abandon this character: Gain 1●" — fodder, outlet, and free replay all at once, the smallest complete loop the deck has.
19. Pyrewatcher — The burst version: a 2● body that "Abandon this character: Gain 3●" when you want a ritual rather than a slow drip.
20. Starrunner — Reclaim 0● plus "Abandon this character: Gain 1●," cheap energy fodder that comes back from the void for free.
21. Spent Courier — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card with ephemeral," replacing itself on both entry and exit — a mass-return turn hits the void twice per body, so it cantrips repeatedly.
22. Ossuary Overlord — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Draw a card," the same card-neutral fodder coming and going.
23. Marrow Mimic — A 1● outlet-and-payoff in one: "Abandon this character: Erode 1. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card," filling the void and cantripping on each death while eroding the opponent.
24. Dread Arbiter — A 0● body that "Abandon this character: Reveal the opponent's hand. Draw a card with ephemeral," fodder that pays information and a card.
25. Rebirth Ritualist — A 0● 1✦ body that "Abandon this character: Draw a card with ephemeral," cheap self-abandoning fodder.
26. Blightmaw — A 1● body that "Abandon this character: Draw a card," cheap self-abandoning fodder.
27. Ochre Prospector — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●. ▸Dissolved: Draw a card," a 2● body that pays on the way out and again when it dies.
28. Junkfield Renegade — "1●, Abandon this character: Gain 2⍟ or Draw a card," cashing a single body for two points or a card.
29. Enginespeaker — Reclaim 0● with all character types, free recurring fodder that keeps coming back to be abandoned again.
30. Torn Circuit Feeder — Reclaim 0●, a body built to be abandoned and replayed from the void each loop.
31. Nocturne — A 3● body with Reclaim 0● and ▸Materialized erode, recurring fodder that fills the void as it comes back.
32. Rusted Monolith — Reclaim 0● with "▸Materialized: Gain 1⍟," a recurring body that scores a point each time a return brings it back.
33. Scrapyard Custodian — "▸Materialized: Gain 1⍟. Abandon a spirit animal: Materialize this character from your void," self-recurring fodder that scores on each return.
34. Dune Reaper — "2●, Banish a card from your void: Materialize this character from your void," a body that re-stages itself from the void to be abandoned again.

## Self-recurring bodies on death

35. Cinderfang — "▸Dissolved: You may pay 1● to return this character to hand," a body that fishes itself back from each abandon to replay and abandon again.
36. Resilient Wanderer — "▸Dissolved: Draw a card. ▸Dawn: If this card is in your void, you may pay 1● to return it to your hand," a body that cantrips on death and recurs itself.
37. Duskwall Delver — "When an ally is dissolved, you may pay 1● to materialize this character from your void," a body that returns itself off the abandon triggers the deck generates.
38. Wreckheap Survivor — "▸Night: With an allied challenger, you may pay 2● to return this character from your void to play," recurring void fodder.
39. Gloomantler — Plays characters from your void and "When you discard your third card in a turn, materialize this character from your void," a self-recurring body in a discard-heavy build.

## Materialize & death payoffs

40. Keeper of Forgotten Light — "When you materialize a non-figment character with cost 2● or less, draw a card with ephemeral," the dig engine that pays off the return half — a mass-return brings back six bodies and draws six cards, refilling your hand as it re-arms the loop.
41. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟," the cleanest drain: every abandon, every return-and-abandon, every trade is a point, and it counts opposing dissolves too.
42. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟," both outlet and payoff — feed a board through it, mass-return, feed it again, and each lap is the same haul of points.
43. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟," a Blood-Artist drain that banks a point on every abandon and return-and-abandon.
44. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟" plus a figment on materialize, another drain that scales with the doubled dissolve passes.
45. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟," draining on both the leaves-play of abandons and any returns that bounce.
46. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟," a drain that scores on every outlet activation across a mass-return turn.
47. Cinderheart — "When you abandon a character, this character gains +1✦. ▸Dissolved: Gain ⍟ equal to this character's ✦," a body that grows on the abandon stream and cashes huge ⍟ when it dies in the loop.
48. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟," fat death payoff that fires every time it hits the void — twice per mass-return lap.
49. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●," turning the abandon stream into energy to keep the chain alive.
50. Sunset Chronicler — "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card," refilling your hand off every dissolve in the loop.
51. Angel of the Eclipse — "When you materialize another character, gain 1⍟," scoring a point per body on each mass-return.
52. Dawnblade Wanderer — "Once per turn, when you materialize a character, gain 1●," recouping energy on the return half.
53. Wired Duelist — "▸Dissolved: Give an ally +X✦," a warrior death payoff that fires on each abandon.
54. Inferno's Herald — "▸Dissolved: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment for each ✦," rebuilding a board out of every abandon.

## Removal payoffs for the loop

55. Obliterator of Worlds — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗. X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," banking each abandon into removal to machine down the opposing front rank while the loop turns.
56. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗. X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," the same abandon-to-removal converter at a higher body cost.
57. Lanternwood Scout — "Abandon another character: Store 1⧗. Abandon this character: Gain X⍟ where X is the stored ⧗," banking abandons into a points payoff.

## Storm payoffs — doubling the return

58. The Ringleader — "▸Materialized: The next time you play an event this turn, create a 0● ephemeral copy of that event in your hand," handing you a free second mass-return that returns whatever the first round re-abandoned.
59. Cascade of Reflections — "Until end of turn, when you play an event, copy it," making every mass-return resolve doubled and copying the outlets and drains in between.
60. Cascading Detonation — "The next time you play an event this turn, copy it twice. Reclaim 5●," tripling the next return and coming back from the void for another go.
61. Twice-Lit Portal — "The next time you play an event this turn, copy it," with Offering — a cheap copy effect that doubles the next mass-return.
62. Echoes of Eternity — "The next time you play an event with cost 2● or less this turn, copy it" — doubles a Heroic Rescue for cheap.
63. Cosmonaut of Tides — "▸Materialized: The next time you play an event with cost 2● or less this turn, copy it twice," tripling a Heroic Rescue off a body.

## Energy to keep going

64. Glimpse of Infinity — "Gain 3●," a 0● burst that pays for the next return.
65. Pulse of Sacrifice — "Discard your hand. Gain 3●," refueling a stalled storm turn with cards you were planning to abandon for value anyway (and loading Cradle of Storms).
66. Genesis Burst — "Double your current ●," a 1● ritual that explodes a mid-combo energy pool into another lap.

## Tutors & recursion support

67. Forsaken Pact — A tutor that is itself an abandon: "To play this card, abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Discover a character with cost 2● or less and materialize it," trading a doomed body for the exact small character the loop is missing, already on the board.
68. Aftermath Bloom — "To play this event, abandon a warrior: Discover a warrior with higher cost, then materialize it," a warrior outlet that trades up the curve while feeding the void.
69. Winterbough Monk — "▸Materialized: Return a card from your void to your hand," a recurring body that fishes a spent mass-return spell back to hand and re-fires that recovery each loop.
70. Ironclad Marksman — "Abandon this character: Shuffle up to 3 cards from your void into your deck. Draw a card," self-abandoning fodder that recovers banished-risk pieces and cantrips.
71. Wreckage Prowler — "1●: Return a 2● body from your void to your hand. 1●, ☾, Abandon a character: Draw a card," fishing fodder back and acting as a draw outlet.
72. Nightmare — "Gain 2●. Draw a card. If reclaimed, copy it. Reclaim 6●," energy and a card that comes back bigger from the void during a long turn.

## Finishers

73. Vaultbreaker — "Abandon a character, reveal this card from your hand: Reduce the cost of this card by 1●," an 8● 8✦ Ancient that a single combo turn's worth of abandons deploys for almost nothing on top of everything else.
