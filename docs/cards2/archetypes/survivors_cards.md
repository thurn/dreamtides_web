# Survivors — Card Pool

A companion to [survivors.md](survivors.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Survivors archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Core tribal enablers and payoffs

1. Kindred Sparks — THE survivor card; replays from hand or void for 1● with any allied survivor, looping endlessly through abandon and recursion.
2. Sorrow Watcher — survivor enabler that makes a survivor figment on entry and draws a card per 3 allied survivors, feeding and scaling the whole deck.
3. Dustborn Veteran — every non-figment allied survivor that dies leaves a 1✦ survivor figment, turning attrition into a growing army.
4. Deathwalker — draws a card whenever an allied survivor is dissolved, converting each loss into a card.
5. Soulreaver — draws a card whenever a non-figment ally is dissolved, the broad attrition draw payoff.
6. Tidewreck Navigator — survivor figment maker on discard and survivor recursion engine (return a survivor from void by abandoning one).
7. Defiant Holdout — survivor that makes survivor figments equal to its spark when it dies, paying you for its own death.
8. Hope's Vanguard — discard a card to make a survivor figment, bridging the discard build with the survivor count.
9. Seer of the Fallen — survivor that hands you a loot event each time a non-figment ally dies, grinding cards out of attrition.
10. Veil of the Wastes — returns itself from the void to play whenever you materialize a survivor, a recurring body the tribe powers for free.
11. Twilight Reclaimer — its dissolve gives a survivor in your void reclaim, turning each death into permission to replay from the void.

## Recurrent bodies and void recursion

12. Duskwall Delver — survivor that returns itself from the void for 1● whenever any ally dies, the sustainable abandon-fuel body.
13. Cinderfang — survivor that bounces back to hand on dissolve for 1●, a recurrent threat for the abandon loop.
14. Resilient Wanderer — survivor that draws on dissolve and can return itself from the void at Dawn, recurring value body.
15. Vessel of Echoes — 1●/1✦ survivor with +5✦ at six void cards and Reclaim 1●; a full void makes it a cheap 6✦ replayable body.
16. Rooftop Prophet — survivor that gains +1✦ per character in your void, a spark scaler that rewards a stocked void.
17. Ashen Remnant — survivor that scales with the void and abandons others to erode, both a payoff and an outlet.
18. Marrow Drinker — survivor that makes a 2✦ shadow figment each time a character card leaves your void, a recursion payoff.
19. Harvester of Despair — survivor abandon-sink that grows +1✦ per abandoned character, an in-tribe outlet body.
20. Graywatch — survivor that returns to play whenever you play a card from your void, recurring alongside the void plan.
21. Exiles of the Last Light — survivor abandon outlet for 1● (at a small point cost), an in-tribe energy engine.
22. Veil Crosser — survivor that abandons a character to discover and materialize a 2● body, converting fodder into threats.

## Abandon outlets and attrition engines

23. Infernal Ascendant — the workhorse outlet: "Abandon another character: Gain 1●," powering the Kindred Sparks loop.
24. Dreadweaver — top-end engine that abandons two characters for +2✦ and 2 cards, emptying a stalled board.
25. Soulrender — abandon a character to erode the opponent equal to its cost, an outlet that doubles as disruption.
26. Kindlehorn — stores ⧗ on each abandon and spends it as scalable removal, an outlet that kills things.
27. Obliterator of Worlds — abandon a character to store ⧗, then dissolve enemies, another outlet-into-removal body.
28. Saltless Mariner — abandon a character to gain 1⍟, turning the loop directly into points.
29. Volcanic Channeler — gain 1● whenever an ally is dissolved, fueling the next Kindred Sparks replay.
30. Fathomless Maw — gain 1⍟ on every abandon, turning the attrition loop into a clock.
31. Silent Avenger — gain 1⍟ whenever a character is dissolved, the broad death-to-points drain.

## The discard / void-reanimator build

32. Searcher in the Mists — survivor that erodes 4 on entry and on dissolve, the premier void-filler.
33. Ashwalker — survivor that erodes 4 then mines the void on dissolve (return a character from void to hand).
34. Pathwalker — survivor that erodes 3 at Dawn from the void and buys back an event when abandoned, a discard-pile utility piece.
35. Evacuation Enforcer — draw 2, discard 3 each ☪, the heavy discard outlet that loads the void fast.
36. Urban Cipher — discard your hand then draw 3, the burst discard outlet that sets up the void.
37. Soulkindler — a board-wide +1✦ pump that works from the void; actively wants to be discarded or eroded.
38. Shadowcaller — makes a survivor figment whenever an ally dies and gains reclaim 0● when discarded/eroded, bridging both builds.
39. Pinnacle Ascendant — gives allies awakened and gains reclaim 0● when discarded/eroded, upside to pitch.
40. Wasteland Arbitrator — survivor that discards both hands on entry, fills your void, and reclaims for 1●.
41. Wasteland Tamer — survivor that forces both players to abandon a non-figment, disrupting while feeding death triggers.
42. Whisper of the Past — gives a void event reclaim and recurs itself onto your deck for eroding 3, exactly the discard build's resource.
43. Path to Redemption — gives all cards in your void reclaim for the turn, the payoff for a stocked void.
44. Part the Veil — erode 4 (keep 2) and gains reclaim when discarded, a self-recurring void-filler.
45. Grotto Seer — discard your hand then draw 3 on challenge, a discard outlet that loads the void.

## Void finishers

46. Paradox Enforcer — 1●/7✦ Unstoppable cast from the void by banishing 7 cards, the discard build's natural payoff.
47. Verdant Wayfarer — draws 2 and reclaims by banishing 7 void cards, converting a deep void into cards.
48. The Rising God — 8●/7✦ Unstoppable that reclaims for 2● and two abandons, the lategame abandon closer.

## Recursion support and acceleration

49. Lanternhearted — awakens an ally each ☪ and reclaims for 1●, letting a replayed Kindred Sparks or recurrent body act immediately.
50. Ruin Scavenger — grows by banishing the opponent's void, void hate plus a scaling body.
51. Scrap Reclaimer — discard a card to return up to two cheap characters from your void, a recursion engine.
52. Unquenched — retriggers each materialized ally's Dawn ability, multiplying the value of recurred bodies.

## Discard utility and threats fueled by it

53. Immolate and Rise — draws a card with a reclaim mode paid by abandoning a character, an attrition-priced cantrip.
54. Emberwatch Veteran — discard a card to make a body vengeful, a cheap discard outlet that turns surplus cards into pressure.
55. Sunken Radiance — draw 2 discard 2 with Reclaim 2●, a recurring loot that loads the void.

## Incidental disruption

56. Vertiginous Leap — clean 1● discard at the opponent's hand, a game-defining strip.
57. Peak Plunder — hand disruption with Reclaim 1● that doubles as self-discard, recurring from the void.
58. Break the Veil — makes the opponent discard with a reclaim mode paid by discarding, aimed out while re-buying itself.
59. Unleash Ruin — dissolve an enemy with cost 3● or less for 2●, the deck's cheap broad removal.
60. Shatter the Frail — kills small bodies with Reclaim 1●, trading and coming back from the void.

## Misc. role players

61. Sky Voyager — counts as a survivor for every payoff while ramping you at Dawn.
62. Momentum's Edge — names Survivor to become both a body and a board-wide +1✦ survivor pump.
63. Voidcaller — banishes an enemy warrior on entry, a tempo body the deck slots in.
