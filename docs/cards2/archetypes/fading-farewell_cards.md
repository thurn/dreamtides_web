# Fading Farewell — Card Pool

A companion to [fading-farewell.md](fading-farewell.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Fading Farewell archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards.toml`.

## The centerpiece

1. Fading Farewell — The namesake: "Until end of turn, when an ally is dissolved, return it to your hand" installs the return clause the whole loop runs on.

## Free bodies and free outlets

2. Conduit of Ashes — 0● body that abandons itself for 1●; with the return clause it loops infinitely as both fuel and outlet.
3. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●" with no cost or cap; the workhorse outlet that loops a 0● body for net +1● per lap.
4. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●"; the accelerated outlet, banking energy faster every abandon.
5. Spirit Reaping — "Abandon a warrior: Gain 2●"; the warrior-flavored outlet for the deck's warrior bodies.
6. Aspiring Guardian — 0● 1✦ no-text Warrior; the ideal free passenger to shuttle between hand and an outlet.

## Energy to keep the turn going

7. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●"; every loop lap pays energy on top of the outlet, giving margin for the closing play.

## Dissolve / abandon payoffs

8. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; the premier closer, one point per lap, races to the score threshold in a turn.
9. Nineborn Specter — "▸Dissolved: Gain 2⍟"; a body that pays for its own death, looped through the return clause for points.
10. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; a redundant Silent Avenger that also seeds a figment.
11. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟"; another redundant per-dissolve point payoff.
12. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟"; a leaves-play point payoff that profits from every looped abandon.
13. Sunset Chronicler — "When an ally is dissolved, draw a card"; turns each loop lap into card flow.
14. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟"; pays a point for every abandon the loop fires.
15. Cinderheart — Grows when you abandon and "▸Dissolved: Gain ⍟ equal to its ✦"; a self-scaling death-payoff body.

## Figment finishers

16. Inferno's Herald — "▸Dissolved: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment for each ✦"; looped, it buries the board under an ever-growing figment army.
17. Wreckborn — "When a non-figment ally is dissolved, materialize a 1✦ ember figment"; converts every loop death into a figment.
18. Shadowcaller — "When an ally is dissolved, materialize a 1✦ survivor figment"; another per-dissolve figment maker for the board finish.

## Erosion and point sinks

19. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X (its cost)"; an outlet that doubles as a Fatigue finisher, draining the opponent's deck each lap.
20. Eclipse Herald — Stores ⧗ and dissolves enemies; the energy sink that clears the opposing front rank so the figment army connects.
21. Horizon Follower — "4●: Gain 1⍟"; turns the loop's surplus energy directly into points.

## Recursion and tutoring overlap

22. Burning Revenant — "When an allied warrior is dissolved, return a warrior with lesser cost from your void to hand"; a poor-man's Fading Farewell scoped to warriors that loops a descending-cost chain.
23. Scrapyard Custodian — "▸Materialized: Gain 1⍟" plus "Abandon a spirit animal: Materialize this from your void"; rewards re-entry and self-recurs off a Conduit of Ashes abandon.
24. Ashen Harbinger — "This character has all character types"; the flex body that is a legal target for every tribal outlet and payoff and a free X-cost passenger.
25. Duskwall Delver — "When an ally is dissolved, pay 1● to materialize this from your void"; a self-recurring body that feeds itself off the loop's deaths.
