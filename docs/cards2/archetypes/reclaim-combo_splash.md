# Reclaim Combo — Splash Cards

A companion to [reclaim-combo.md](reclaim-combo.md) listing the portable core of the Reclaim Combo archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density, so you can mix them into a neighboring deck without committing to the full character loop. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`. Reclaim Combo is a narrow, dedicated combo, so its splash list is short and leans on the abandon outlets and ▸Dissolved payoffs that double as fair-game value; the specific statics ("Cards you reclaim are not banished when they leave play"), the Reclaim 0● loop bodies, and the reclaim-granting enablers that only matter once the loop is assembled stay home.

## Abandon Outlets — energy and selection on demand

1. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●," a free, repeatable outlet that converts any spare body into energy; great in any deck running cheap fodder, no loop required.
2. Arc Disciple — "Abandon another character: Gain 2●," the same effect for double the energy, a strong standalone tempo and ramp piece.
3. Stargazer Adrift — "Abandon another character: Foresee 1," a 1● body that sculpts your draws off any throwaway character; cheap and self-contained.
4. Virtuoso of Harmony — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1●," a clean free outlet on a 2● 2✦ body that splashes into any cheap-character shell.
5. Conduit of Ashes — "Abandon this character: Gain 1●," a 0● body that is a one-shot ritual on its own and a free abandon target anywhere.
6. Pyrewatcher — "Abandon this character: Gain 3●," a 2● body that cashes itself in for a burst of energy, fine in any tempo deck.
7. Soulbinder — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗" feeding "2⧗: Gain 1●," a 1● outlet that banks abandons into energy at its own pace.

## Drain Payoffs — score ⍟ off dissolves and abandons

8. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟," scores off every body that dies anywhere; strong in any deck with abandon or trading, no combo needed.
9. Duskreaper — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟," a cheap 2● redundant drain that rewards any sacrifice or trade.
10. Skull Weaver — "When an ally leaves play, gain 1⍟," scoring off every body you abandon, bounce, or lose; broadly useful in attrition decks.
11. Avatar of Cosmic Reckoning — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1⍟," also makes a figment on entry, a standalone value body in any sacrifice shell.
12. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟," a flat payoff for any abandon outlet, good wherever you already sacrifice fodder.
13. The Forsaker — "Abandon a character with cost 2● or less: Gain 1⍟," an outlet that scores directly, self-contained in any cheap-body deck.
14. Saltless Mariner — "Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟" behind Veil 2●, an outlet and payoff in one durable body that splashes anywhere you make fodder.
15. Twilight Suppressor — "When you play a character, store 1⧗" plus "1⧗, Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟," banking points off ordinary character drops.

## Erosion and Removal Outlets

16. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X, where X is that character's cost," a strong erosion engine that mills the opponent off any abandon, loop or not.
17. Ashen Remnant — "Abandon another character: Erode 1" on a body that grows with your void, useful in any void-leaning deck.
18. Ashborn Necromancer — "Abandon a character: Erode 2," a stronger erosion outlet that splashes into any mill or self-fill plan.
19. Obliterator of Worlds — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗" feeding "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," repeatable removal that pays for itself off fodder.
20. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗" into "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦," banking removal off every abandon you already make.

## Energy and Value Bodies That Earn Their Place

21. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●," a free-energy engine off any dissolve, strong in any sacrifice or trading deck.
22. Eclipse Herald — "3●: Store 1⧗" into "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with ✦ X or less," a self-contained energy-to-removal threat that needs no support.
23. Horizon Follower — "4●: Gain 1⍟," a flat energy sink that turns surplus energy into points in any ramp shell.
24. Momentum's Edge — "Choose a character type. Characters of the chosen type have +1✦," a 2● 2✦ anthem that is a fine standalone payoff in any typed board.
25. Ember Shepherd — "❖❖ – Abandon another character: An ally cannot be targeted by effects this turn," a cheap Interrupt that protects a key body and uses spare fodder, broadly useful as protection.
26. Gateway Defender — "❖❖ – Abandon this character: An ally cannot be targeted by effects this turn," the same protection on a self-abandoning 2● body.
27. Cloudmantle Ray — "▸Dawn: Gain 1●" with Reclaim 3●, an energy dork that recurs from the void on its own, fine in any ramp deck.
28. Blade of Unity — an Unstoppable X/X warrior, a flexible standalone threat to close a fair game at any curve point.
29. Molten Duel — "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less," cheap fast removal that splashes into essentially any deck.

## Tutors and Filtering

30. Door to Possibility — "Choose a value of X. Discover an event with cost X," flexible event tutoring that helps any event-leaning deck find its key card.
31. Curio Dealer — "1●, Discard a character with cost X●: Discover a character with cost X●," a durable filtering body that digs toward what you need and fills the void on the way.
