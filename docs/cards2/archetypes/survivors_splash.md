# Survivors — Splash Cards

A companion to [survivors.md](survivors.md) that lists the portable core of the Survivors archetype — the cards that stay good when you splash a handful of them into a neighboring void deck rather than building the whole deck around the tribe. These are flexible, self-contained pieces that pull their weight at low density. The tribal payoffs that need a critical mass of survivors live in [survivors_cards.md](survivors_cards.md), not here. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Recurrent bodies that fend for themselves

1. Cinderfang — "▸Dissolved: You may pay 1● to return this character to hand." A 2●/2✦ survivor that simply bounces back to hand whenever it dies; it needs no other survivors to be a sticky, replayable body in any abandon or attrition shell.
2. Duskwall Delver — "When an ally is dissolved, you may pay 1● to materialize this character from your void." Triggers off *any* ally dying, not just survivors, so it self-recurs as cheap fuel in any deck that trades or abandons.
3. Resilient Wanderer — "▸Dissolved: Draw a card." plus a Dawn return from the void for 1●. A 3●/3✦ that cantrips when it dies and brings itself back; pure standalone value.
4. Graywatch — "When you play a card from your void, return this character to play." A vengeful body that recurs for free in any void/reclaim deck, with no survivor count required.
5. Vessel of Echoes — "If there are 6 or more cards in your void, this character has +5✦." plus Reclaim 1●. A 1● replayable 6✦ body that any void-filling deck turns on; it cares about void depth, not survivors.
6. Rooftop Prophet — "This character has +1✦ for each character in your void." A flat 2● spark scaler that rewards any stocked void, no tribe needed.
7. Ashen Remnant — "This character has +1✦ for each character in your void." plus "Abandon another character: Erode 1." A void scaler that doubles as a free abandon-and-erode outlet, useful in any void or abandon deck.

## Abandon outlets and attrition engines

8. Infernal Ascendant — "Abandon another character: Gain 1●." The premier free abandon outlet; it powers any abandon loop and recurs cheap bodies regardless of subtype.
9. Dreadweaver — "2●, ☪, Abandon two other characters: This character gains +2✦. Draw 2 cards." A self-contained engine that empties a stalled board into a pump and two cards in any deck with spare bodies.
10. Soulrender — "Abandon a character: Chosen player erodes X, where X is that character's cost." An abandon outlet that doubles as disruption and void-fill; strong standalone in any abandon shell.
11. Kindlehorn — "When you abandon a character, store 1⧗." plus "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦." Turns any abandon habit into scalable removal, fully self-contained.
12. Obliterator of Worlds — "Abandon a character: Store 1⧗." plus "X⧗: Dissolve an enemy with X✦." A cheap outlet-into-removal body that asks nothing of the tribe.
13. Saltless Mariner — "Abandon a character: Gain 1⍟." plus Veil 2●. Converts any abandon directly into points; a clean standalone drain.
14. Volcanic Channeler — "When an ally is dissolved, gain 1●." Rewards every loss with energy in any attrition deck, with no survivor density required.
15. Fathomless Maw — "When you abandon a character, gain 1⍟." A flat abandon-to-points clock that works in any abandon deck.
16. Silent Avenger — "When a character is dissolved, gain 1⍟." The broadest death-to-points drain; fires on any dissolve, friend or foe.

## Discard and void-reanimator pieces

17. Searcher in the Mists — "▸Materialized, Dissolved: Erode 4." The premier void-filler; an 8-card erosion across its life that loads any void deck, no tribe required.
18. Ashwalker — "▸Materialized: Erode 3." plus "▸Dissolved: Return a character from your void to your hand." Fills then mines the void as a standalone value body in any reanimator shell.
19. Wasteland Arbitrator — "▸Materialized: Each player discards a card." plus Reclaim 1●. A disruptive 3● body that fills your void and re-buys itself, good in any discard or void deck.
20. Evacuation Enforcer — "Awakened. ☪: Draw 2 cards, then discard 3 cards." The heavy discard outlet that loads the void fast in any discard/madness shell.
21. Urban Cipher — "▸Materialized: Discard your hand, then draw 3 cards." A burst discard outlet that sets up the void in one play.
22. Grotto Seer — "▸Challenge: Discard your hand, then draw 3 cards." A repeatable discard outlet attached to a body, standalone in any discard deck.
23. Soulkindler — "If this card is in your void, allied characters have +1✦." A board-wide pump that works from the void; any deck that discards or erodes wants this and it asks nothing of the tribe.
24. Shadowcaller — "When an ally is dissolved, materialize a 1✦ survivor figment." plus "When you discard or erode this card, it gains reclaim 0●." Its death-figment fires off any ally and its reclaim-on-discard makes it free to pitch — a madness staple even outside a survivor deck.
25. Pinnacle Ascendant — "Allied characters have awakened." plus "When you discard or erode this card, it gains reclaim 0●." A board-wide tempo anthem that is pure upside to discard; works in any creature deck.
26. Pathwalker — "Abandon this character: Return an event from your void to your hand." plus "▸Dawn: If this card is in your void, erode 3." A discard-pile utility piece that fills the void and buys back a key event, fine on its own.
27. Part the Veil — "Erode 4. You may put two of those cards into your hand." plus reclaim-on-discard. A self-recurring void-filler with card selection, splashable into any void deck.
28. Sunken Radiance — "Draw 2 cards, then discard 2 cards." plus Reclaim 2●. A recurring loot event that loads the void anywhere.
29. Whisper of the Past — "An event in your void gains reclaim until end of turn." plus a self-recurring erode-3 clause. Keeps your best events flowing in any event-leaning void deck.
30. Path to Redemption — "Until end of turn, cards in your void gain reclaim." A one-turn payoff for any stocked void; the more you milled, the better.

## Void finishers paid for by a deep void

31. Paradox Enforcer — "To play this character, banish 7 cards from your void. You may play this character from your void. Unstoppable." A 1●/7✦ Unstoppable threat any void-filling deck can cast straight out of the void.
32. Verdant Wayfarer — "▸Materialized: Draw 2 cards." plus "Reclaim – 3●, Banish 7 cards from your void." A standalone value body that converts a deep void into cards.
33. The Rising God — "Unstoppable. Reclaim – 2●, Abandon two characters." An 8●/7✦ closer any abandon deck can recur for 2● and two bodies it was happy to feed an outlet anyway.

## Recursion, acceleration, and utility

34. Lanternhearted — "Awakened. ☪: Awaken an ally." plus Reclaim 1●. Fixes tempo by letting a freshly replayed body act immediately, then reclaims itself; useful in any recursion deck.
35. Ruin Scavenger — "☪, Banish a card from the opponent's void: This character gains +1✦." Void hate plus a growing body, good against any recursion opponent.
36. Scrap Reclaimer — "2●, ☪, Discard a card: Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand." A standalone recursion engine for any cheap-character void deck.
37. Sky Voyager — "This character has all character types." plus "▸Dawn: Gain 1●." Counts as a survivor for any payoff while ramping; a flexible 1● body anywhere.
38. Momentum's Edge — "▸Materialized: Choose a character type. This character is that character type. Characters of the chosen type have +1✦." A 2● body and a board-wide pump that names whatever your deck wants.

## Incidental disruption and removal

39. Vertiginous Leap — "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand." A clean 1● hand strike that is strong in any deck.
40. Peak Plunder — "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand." plus Reclaim 1●. Recurring hand disruption that doubles as self-discard for the void.
41. Break the Veil — "The opponent discards a card. Reclaim – Discard a card." Aims at the opponent while re-buying itself from the void; pure self-discard upside.
42. Flashpoint Detonation — "Dissolve an enemy with cost 3● or less." Cheap broad removal that fits any deck.
43. Shatter the Frail — "Dissolve an enemy with 1✦ or less." plus Reclaim 1●. Trades for small bodies and comes back from the void.
44. Voidcaller — "▸Materialized: Banish an enemy warrior." A tempo body against Warrior decks that slots into anything.
