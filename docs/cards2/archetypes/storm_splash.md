# Storm — Splash Cards

The portable core of the Storm archetype: cards that keep a chain humming yet
remain good standalone, so a neighboring deck can run 5-10 of them without
building the whole list around the count. See [storm.md](storm.md) for the full
strategy. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`. The density-dependent
"for each card you've played this turn" finishers stay home — they only pay off
once you have assembled a long chain, so they live in the dedicated build rather
than in a splash.

## Energy rituals (refund the chain, good in any tempo or combo shell)

1. Genesis Burst — "Double your current ●" for 1●; even at low density it is a free explosive ramp turn whenever you hold a pool.
2. Flash of Power — "Gain 5●" for 3●; a flat net +2● ritual that any energy-hungry deck welcomes.
3. Arc Gate Opening — "Gain 4●" for 1●; net +3● at one mana, the cleanest single-card ramp in the pool.
4. Glimpse of Infinity — "Gain 3●" for 0●; free energy and a free play, useful in literally any deck that wants a big turn.
5. Canopy of Stars — "Gain 2●" for 1●; cheap, unconditional chain fuel.
6. Starfall — "Gain 3●" for 1●; a flat ritual that ramps a single turn regardless of the count.
7. Nebula's Wake — "Gain 5●" for 2●, online with 3 events in your void; even a light events shell turns it on, and the payoff is huge.
8. Data Pulse — "Gain 2●. Draw a card"; a ritual that replaces itself, so it is never a dead draw at any density.
9. A New Adventure — "Draw two cards, then discard two cards. Gain 3●"; smooths the hand while ramping, valuable in any deck.
10. Glimpse of What Was — "Foresee 1. Draw a card"; not a ritual but a free cantrip that keeps a chain (or any deck) flowing.
11. Pulse of Sacrifice — "Discard your hand. Gain 3●" for 0●; an all-in burst that also seeds the void for any recursion you splash.

## Copy effects (the engine — each piece is fine on its own)

12. Cascade of Reflections — "Until end of turn, when you play an event, copy it"; doubles whatever events you do run, strong even in a light events shell.
13. Echo Architect — passive "when you play an event, copy it" (events cost 1● more); a single body that doubles every event you play, payoff in any events deck.
14. Cascading Detonation — "The next event this turn copies twice"; point it at a ritual or removal spell for value at any density, with Reclaim 5●.
15. Cosmonaut of Tides — on ▸Materialized, the next cost-2●-or-less event copies twice; a 4● body that bends one cheap event without needing a chain.
16. The Ringleader — on ▸Materialized, makes a 0● ephemeral copy of the next event you play; a standalone body that hands back a free recast.
17. Echoes of Eternity — "The next cost-2●-or-less event this turn copies"; a cheap single-shot doubler good with any cheap event.
18. Twice-Lit Portal — Offering event that copies the next event you play; a flexible doubler that costs no card-economy when offered.

## Card flow (keep the chain going, never dead)

19. Epiphany Unfolded — "Draw 3 cards" for 1●; a cheap three-for-one that any deck is happy to run.
20. Iconoclast — "Discard a card: Draw two cards with ephemeral"; refills with must-spend fuel, fine value in any deck with a discard outlet or just a card to pitch.
21. Pattern Seeker — "When you discard a card, draw a card with ephemeral"; turns any loot or pitch into a free card, a low-commitment engine.
22. Prophet of the Consumed — on ▸Materialized, draw a cost-2●-or-less card that costs 0●; a body that adds a free play in any shell.

## Void recursion (replay what you spent — useful in any value deck)

23. Path to Redemption — "Until end of turn, cards in your void gain reclaim"; replays a spent ritual or removal even outside a dedicated chain.
24. From the Barrow — "Until end of turn, events in your void gain reclaim", with its own reclaim 3●; a self-sustaining buyback for any events deck.
25. Archive of the Forgotten — "Up to 2 cheap void events gain reclaim 0● until end of turn"; hands back two free plays without needing a long chain.
26. Dreadwood Emissary — on ▸Materialized, a void event gains reclaim until end of turn; a cheap body that buys back the exact piece you want.

## Cheap point and removal fuel (good cards that also count)

27. Derelict Voyage — "Gain 3⍟" for 1● (6⍟ if reclaimed, Reclaim 4●); a cheap play that is its own payoff at any density.
28. Fleeting Reunion — "Gain X⍟", Reclaim by discarding X cards; converts a flooded hand straight into points, no chain required.
29. Burst of Obliteration — X-cost "Gain X⍟ or dissolve with X✦"; dumps leftover energy into points or removal, flexible everywhere.
30. Molten Duel — "Dissolve a character with 3✦ or less" for 1●; premium cheap removal that any deck wants.
31. Shattering Gambit — "Banish an enemy" for 1● (opponent gains 5⍟); cheap fast answer to anything, count nudge as a bonus.
32. Vertiginous Leap — "Discard a chosen card from the opponent's hand" for 1●; cheap disruption that slots into any tempo or control shell.

## Per-event bodies (individually fine, scale with whatever events you run)

33. Starcatcher — "When you play an event, gain 1●"; a 2-spark body that refunds the chain and helps any events deck ramp.
34. Gearwright — "When you play an event, draw a card, then discard a card"; a cheap looter that smooths draws in any events-matter deck.
35. Gateweaver — "When you play an event, materialize a 1✦ ethereal figment"; builds a board off ordinary event density.
36. Oracle of Shifting Skies — "When you play an event, foresee 1" for 1●; a 1● body that smooths draws across any events deck.
37. Twilight Troubadour — "When you play an event, materialize a 1✦ warrior figment"; another per-event token maker that needs no count to pay.

## Cheap glue and free bodies

38. Signal Resonant — 1✦ synth with all character types and a Fast reposition; a cheap, typeless body that slots into any board plan.
39. Silent Gatherer — "▸Dawn: If this character is in your void, return it to your hand"; a recurring cheap play that refuels any deck for free.
40. Celestial Reverie — "Until end of turn, when you play a character, draw a card" for 1●; a per-cast refill that any character-heavy turn enjoys.
