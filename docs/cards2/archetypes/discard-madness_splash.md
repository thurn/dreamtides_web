# Discard / Madness — Splash Cards

A companion to [discard-madness.md](discard-madness.md), listing the portable core of the Discard / Madness archetype — the cards that stay good even at low density, so a neighboring deck can splash a handful of discard pieces without building around them. These are the self-contained looters, the madness bodies that turn their own discard into free value, the discard-as-removal that uses a spare card as a cost, and the few payoffs that count any discard from any source. The hard, density-dependent win engines and "for each card in your void" finishers stay home. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Repeatable looters and outlets

1. Evacuation Enforcer — "Awakened. ☪: Draw 2 cards, then discard 3 cards"; a 1● body that filters and digs every turn, the archetype's signal but useful in any deck that just wants to smooth its draws and feed a void.
2. Chronicle Reclaimer — "☪: Draw a card, then discard a card"; a clean 2● rummager that loots every turn with no support, a quiet card-quality body anywhere.
3. Manufactured Abomination — "☪, Discard a card: Draw a card"; a repeatable looter on a 2● body, self-contained filtering at any density.
4. Salvage Engine — "Discard a card: This character gains +2✦ until end of turn"; a cheap repeatable outlet that converts a spare card into board presence on demand.
5. Iconoclast — "Discard a card: Draw two cards with ephemeral"; a high-rate outlet that turns one card into two this turn, strong even as a one-off card-flow body.
6. Pathfinder Adrift — "When you play your second card in a turn, draw a card, then discard a card"; free incidental looting that happens on a normal two-spell turn, no engine required.
7. Void Pilgrim — "▸Challenge: Draw a card, then discard a card"; loots for free off an attack you were making anyway, a fine standalone 2● warrior.
8. Dreadcall Warden — "▸Night: Draw a card, then discard a card unless you have an allied challenger"; recurring incidental filtering on a 2● body.
9. Seeker for the Way — "▸Materialized: You may discard a card to draw a card. Reclaim 2●"; an outlet that recurs itself from the void for a second loot.
10. Liminal Dreamer — "▸Dawn: Store 1⧗. Discard your hand: Draw one card per stored ⧗"; empties a stranded hand into the void and refills it, a self-contained reload that needs no payoff.

## Cheap looting events

11. Sunken Radiance — "Draw 2 cards, then discard 2 cards. Reclaim 2●"; loots and recurs itself from the void, card-neutral filtering anyone can run.
12. Skies of Change — "To play this event, discard a card. Draw 2 cards"; a cheap looting cantrip that nets a card while seeding the void.
13. Shared Revelation — "Draw two cards, then discard a card unless you played another event this turn"; near-free card advantage with an incidental discard.
14. A New Adventure — "Draw two cards, then discard two cards. Gain 3●"; loots and ramps in one card, good in any deck wanting cards and energy.
15. Call of Calamity — "To play this event, discard X cards. Draw X+1 cards"; a scalable hand-into-void converter that always nets a card, flexible at any density.
16. Spiral Offering — "To play this event, discard 2 cards. Draw 3 cards"; pays its discard up front for raw card advantage, so it is just a good draw spell that happens to fill the void.
17. Seaside Requiem — "To play this card, abandon a character or discard a card. Draw 2 cards"; flexible card draw whose cost is a spare body or a spare card, fine in any value deck.

## Madness bodies and events (free value off any discard)

18. Ashmaze Guide — 1● "When you discard this card, it gains reclaim 0● until end of turn"; a body that lands for free after being thrown away, so discarding it costs nothing — the cleanest splashable madness card.
19. Ridge Vortex Explorer — 2● "When you discard this card, materialize it"; the discard is the deployment, a self-contained 2✦ body that turns any discard into a free play.
20. Sorrowful Prince — 2● "X●: This character gains +X✦ until end of turn"; a pumpable threat that comes down off any outlet and can immediately threaten to score, strong as a standalone body.
21. Part the Veil — "Erode 4. You may put two of those cards into your hand. When you discard this card, it gains reclaim 2● until end of turn"; a dig that is fine cast from hand and becomes a discounted dig from the void when discarded.
22. Pinnacle Ascendant — "Allied characters have awakened. When you discard or erode this card, it gains reclaim 0●"; an awaken anthem that you can hard-cast or park in the void for free off a discard or mill.
23. Shadowcaller — 3● Veil 3● "When an ally is dissolved, materialize a 1✦ survivor figment. When you discard or erode this card, it gains reclaim 0●"; a sticky figment engine that is a good body on its own and replays for free after a discard.

## Madness removal (reclaimed by discarding)

24. Forsaken Skyline — "Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less. When you discard this card, it gains reclaim 1● until end of turn. Reclaim 4●"; cheap removal that also recurs from the void, useful regardless of discard density.
25. Fell Swoop — "Dissolve an enemy with 1✦ or less. Reclaim – Discard a card"; cheap removal you can buy back by discarding, pairing interaction with void setup.
26. Maelstrom Denial — "To play this event, abandon a character or discard a card. Dissolve an enemy"; flexible unconditional removal whose cost is a spare card or body, good in any deck with something to spare.
27. Pyrokinetic Surge — "To play this card, discard a card or pay 5⍟. Dissolve an enemy"; premium removal that a routine discard pays for, splashable as cheap interaction.
28. Astral Angler — "2●, Discard a character: Dissolve an enemy with 2✦ or less. 1●: Return an ally to hand"; points a discarded body at removal and carries a flexible bounce, self-contained at any density.
29. Duneveil Vanguard — "Discard a character with cost 2● or less: Dissolve an enemy with cost 2● or less"; turns a spare small character into a removal outlet on a body.

## Payoffs that count any discard

30. Key Sifter — "Once per turn, when you discard one or more cards, draw that many cards"; the first discard each turn replaces itself no matter the source, so it pays off even with a single looter.
31. Wandering Archivist — "2●, ☪: Draw a card, then discard a card. When you discard a card, gain 1⍟"; an outlet that also drains the opponent on every discard from any source, an individually-strong value body.
32. Gleamharvester — "▸Challenge: You may discard a card to draw a card. When you discard a card, this character gains +2✦ until end of turn"; a looter that spikes into a real attacker on any discard, fine standalone.
33. Pattern Seeker — "When you discard a card, draw a card with ephemeral"; a free card on every discard, so it stays live even if a handful of looters are all you run.
34. Torchbearer of the Abyss — "Once per turn, when you discard a card, draw a card and gain 1●"; loots and ramps on your first discard each turn, self-sufficient at low density.
35. Unbroken — "When you discard a card, this character gains +1✦"; a cheap 1● body that quietly grows on every discard, a clean low-density inclusion.
36. Shoreline Penitent — "3●, ☪, Discard a card: Draw a card. When you discard a card, it gains reclaim until end of turn"; both an outlet and a payoff in one, handing reclaim to whatever it discards with no further support.
37. Emberwatch Veteran — "Discard a card: This character gains vengeful this turn"; turns a spare card into a combat trick that trades up, useful even as the only discard payoff.

## Survivor and figment outlets

38. Tidewreck Navigator — "☪, Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ survivor figment"; an outlet that builds a board, a fine 1● survivor in any creature-leaning shell.
39. Harborwarden — "☪, Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ warrior figment"; the warrior-side version, turning a discard into a figment for go-wide decks.
40. Hope's Vanguard — "Discard a card: Materialize a 1✦ survivor figment"; a cheap repeatable discard outlet that pays in board presence.
41. Wasteland Arbitrator — "▸Materialized: Each player discards a card. Reclaim 1●"; an incidental outlet that disrupts the opponent while feeding your own void, and recurs for a second pass.
42. Kindred Sparks — "With an allied survivor, you may play this card from your hand or void for 1●"; a renewable body that is happy to be discarded and bought back cheaply, splashable as recurring fodder in any survivor-touching deck.

## Self-returning discard fodder

43. Silent Gatherer — "▸Dawn: If this character is in your void, return it to your hand"; unlimited free discard fodder you can feed to an outlet every turn at no card cost.
44. Resilient Wanderer — "▸Dissolved: Draw a card. ▸Dawn: If this card is in your void, you may pay 1● to return it to your hand"; a repeatable discard target with a 1● tax that also replaces itself on death.
45. Veil of the Wastes — "When you materialize a survivor, return this card from your void to play"; free recurring fodder in any deck that occasionally makes a survivor.
46. Scrap Reclaimer — "Awakened, Veil 2●. 2●, ☪, Discard a card: Return up to 2 characters with cost 2● or less from your void to your hand"; loots a card and refills the hand with two cheap bodies, a self-contained recursion outlet that keeps the discards card-positive.
