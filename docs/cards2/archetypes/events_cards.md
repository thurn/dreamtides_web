# Events — Card Pool

A companion to [events.md](events.md), listing every CardsV2 card with a specific affinity for the Events archetype — the cards you would prioritize drafting when building this deck. Cards are drawn from `data/tabula/cards_v2.toml`.

## Event payoffs ("when you play an event")

1. Gateweaver — 2● body that materializes a 1✦ ethereal figment each time you play an event; the workhorse engine.
2. Duskshore Virtuoso — stores 1⧗ per event, then spends 3⧗ to gain +5✦ and become a real threat.
3. The Waking Titan — stores 1⧗ per event, then spends 5⧗ to return all other characters to hand for a board-resetting blowout.
4. Echo Architect — events cost 1● more, but each event you play is copied; the marquee payoff and the Storm bridge.
5. Twilight Troubadour — materializes a 1✦ warrior figment whenever you play an event, going wide off cheap interaction.
6. Starcatcher — gains 1● each time you play an event, helping you chain more events the same turn.
7. Oracle of Shifting Skies — foresee 1 on each event, smoothing draws while the engine runs.
8. Gearwright — loots (draw, then discard) on every event, digging and filling the void for reclaim.
9. Shadow Soloist — awakens each allied character whenever you play an event, the awaken backbone for a board-wide swing.
10. Moonlit Dancer — gives allied characters +1✦ until end of turn on each event, pushing spark off a flurry of interaction.

## Event recursion and reuse

11. Somber Flockmaster — on ▸Dawn, a void event with cost ≤ its ✦ gains reclaim 0● until end of turn, recurring spent removal/prevents for free.
12. Keeper of the Lightpath — ☪ gives a void event with cost 3● or less reclaim on demand, buying back a prevent or removal when needed.
13. Whisper of the Past — 1● to give a void event reclaim until end of turn, and it loops itself back onto the deck by eroding.
14. Dreadwood Emissary — ▸Materialized gives an event in your void reclaim until end of turn.
15. Spirit of Smoldering Echoes — 2● to give a void event reclaim until end of turn, a repeatable recursion engine.
16. Ashlight Caller — discard a card to give a void event reclaim until end of turn, a cheap recurring enabler.
17. Weblight Waif — ▸Materialized returns an event from your void to hand, re-buying a key interactive spell.
18. Pathwalker — Abandon to return an event from your void to your hand; recurs your best event on demand.
19. Minstrel of Falling Light — ☪, discard an event: draw an event, and grows +1✦ per event in your void.
20. Echo Technician — ☪ lets you play an event directly from your void, a repeatable recursion outlet.
21. Archive of the Forgotten — up to 2 void events with cost X● or less gain reclaim 0● this turn, recurring a pair of cheap events at once.
22. From the Barrow — until end of turn, events in your void gain reclaim; a mass reclaim turn for the whole event suite.

## Play-count and second-card payoffs

32. Broadcast Array — gains 2⍟ per card played this turn and cannot be prevented; cashes out a chain of cheap events.
33. Intermezzo Balladeer — gains 1⍟ per other card played this turn every time you play a card, scaling with event density.
34. Echoes of the Journey — draws an ephemeral card per card played this turn, all costing 0●, refilling after a busy event turn.
35. Pathfinder Adrift — loots when you play your second card in a turn, rewarding the multi-event turns the deck enables.
36. Harrowing Officiant — materializes itself from the void when you play your second card in a turn, a free body off the event chain.

## Key events the engine wants

### Removal
37. Molten Duel — 1● fast/interrupt dissolve of a character with 3✦ or less; premier cheap removal for the events core.
38. Unleash Ruin — 2● fast dissolve of an enemy with cost 3● or less.
39. Scorched Reckoning — 2● dissolve of an enemy with 3✦ or less.
40. Shatter the Frail — 1● dissolve of a 1✦-or-less enemy, with Reclaim 1● for a second use.
41. Unleashed Destruction — 1● dissolve of a cost-2●-or-less enemy.
42. Grounded — X-cost fast/interrupt banish of an enemy whose cost is exactly X●; the scalable catch-all answer.

### Prevents (Interrupts)
43. Toll of Passage — 1● prevent of a played card with cost 2● or less; hits a large fraction of a low curve.
44. Defiant Parry — Offering counter that prevents a played card unless the opponent pays 1●, castable for 0●.
45. Echoing Denial — Offering counter that prevents a played card outright, castable for 0●.
46. Infernal Rest — 1● soft counter, prevent unless the opponent pays 1●.
47. Momentum of the Fallen — 1● counter that prevents an event unless the opponent pays 2●.

### Hand disruption
48. Lurking Dread — 1● discard of a chosen cost-2●-or-less card from the opponent's hand.
49. Vertiginous Leap — 1● discard of any chosen card from the opponent's hand.
50. Peak Plunder — 2● discard of any chosen card, with Reclaim 1● for a second pass.
51. Break the Veil — opponent discards a card, with Reclaim – Discard a card so it grinds their hand turn after turn.

### Card draw / dig
52. Epiphany Unfolded — draws 3 cards for 1● fast/interrupt, the premier event-engine refuel.
53. Fragments of Vision — 1● fast/interrupt draw-3-then-shuffle-2 that smooths and refills.
54. Gleam Below — 1● draw a card plus Foresee 2, a cheap cantrip that feeds the payoffs.
55. Guiding Light — 1● Foresee 1 and draw, with Reclaim for a recurring cantrip.
56. Ancient Descent — 1● fast/interrupt Foresee 1 and draw, a flexible cheap event.
57. Sunken Radiance — 1● draw 2 / discard 2 with Reclaim 2●, digging while filling the void for reclaim.
