# Dreamtides — Unmapped Synergy Cube Cards

A companion to
[dreamtides_archetype_cards.md](dreamtides_archetype_cards.md). That document's
final section lists every Synergy Cube card from
[../calebgannon/archetype_cards.md](../calebgannon/archetype_cards.md) with no
card carrying its name as an `mtg-name` in `data/cards.toml`. The bulk
of those entries are lands, fetchlands, utility lands, and Moxen — effects the
shared Dreamwell and energy curve absorb, so they have no reason to exist as
cards.

This document narrows that list to the cards that are **not** lands or mana
rocks: the genuine gaps where a Synergy Cube effect referenced in the archetype
catalogue has no Dreamtides counterpart in the current pool. These are the
